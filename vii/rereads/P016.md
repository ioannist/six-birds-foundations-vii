## Anti-overread

- We do not claim a general reconstruction of non-Hilbert interference or a formalization of the full benchmark runtime.
- The claim is narrower: these recombination phenomena arise on fixed support, can be audited exactly, and admit a partial formal backbone on the Six Birds substrate.
- Discussion and Limitations: The paper establishes a substrate-level result. On a fixed-support Six Birds architecture, branchwise predictive structure and recombination-sensitive structure can be separated operationally, and that separation can be audited under exact finite controls. The benchmark suite shows that the same substrate supports a classical-mixture null regime, hidden predictive memory without additional recombination sensitivity, readable-route suppression, erased conditional recovery, bounded cyclic-family generalization, dissipative washout, and artifact controls based on protocol internalization and flattening/completion. The formal contribution is narrower. The Lean development proves an abstract refinement layer under detectability, a factorized no-interference regime in which the recombination quotient collapses to the branchwise quotient, and the corresponding obstruction result that strict refinement rules out global branchwise factorization. It also proves one theorem-backed concrete positive instance for an aligned Lean model of the promoted family n5_d1_w1of4_3of4 . These results support the interpretation of the benchmark suite but do not replace it.
- It does not provide a reconstruction of quantum theory, and it does not claim to be the first general treatment of interference beyond Hilbert-space formalisms It does not prove the paired erasure benchmark in full runtime detail, the full cyclic-family generalization, or the dissipative control.
