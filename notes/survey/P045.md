## Problem and thesis

We ask not merely whether layer birth occurs, but how many primitive kinds of birth are experimentally accessible. Within the Six-Birds framework, birth events can be organized by activation of packaging ( P5 ), directed circulation ( P6_ drive ), and staging anomaly ( P4 ). We show that these primitives support a small canonical taxonomy of four layer-birth classes and that the corresponding classes can be operationally instantiated and separated in a common measured observable space.
