## Problem and thesis

This paper isolates a control-stable intrinsic family inside the fixed-support recombination layer of Six Birds theory. The first three papers of the series established admissible event packages on fixed support, predictive quotients on single histories, and the branchwise and recombination quotients on branch assemblages. Here we identify a two-anchor relative-cycle family in which branchwise predictive agreement does not determine later recombination observables.
