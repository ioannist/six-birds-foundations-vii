## Problem and thesis

Previous demonstrations of contextuality-type obstruction start from quantum mechanics or from externally specified measurement scenarios. We show that the same locally-Boolean-but-globally-obstructed structure can arise from an autonomous emergence framework—Six Birds Theory—without importing Hilbert space, amplitudes, or the Born rule. The formal vehicle is a finite event package: a family of stable-record contexts, each carrying a finite Boolean event algebra, together with a layer-relative shared-event identification and an audit layer.
