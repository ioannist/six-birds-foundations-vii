## Problem and thesis

We present a reproducible computational framework—with Lean 4 mechanized proofs and deterministic Python simulations—that treats multi-scale model reduction as a closure-audit calculus: each ``physics theory'' is a packaging operator built from a lens (retained macro information), a completion (canonical reconstruction), and diagnostics that certify when the reduced description is self-consistent. We instantiate the Six-Birds lens/completion/closure dictionary as a multi-scale layer template, centering three cross-domain certificates: closure coherence (idempotence), audit monotonicity (data-processing inequality), and route mismatch (noncommutation of coarse-graining with dynamics). We operationalize this calculus via a reproducible workflow (Python artifacts + Lean-certified lemmas) and demonstrate it on four instantiations: quantum dephasing, discrete BGK-like kinetic closure, filtered Burgers (LES), and nonlinear averaging (backreaction).
