## Problem and thesis

Certifying multiscale anti-localization only at t=0 is not useful unless legality is forward-closed; we lack general, mechanizable conditions under which SPT-legality propagates under time evolution. We propose a slot-audited channelized-feasibility contract in a Hilbert/band/cell model, where legality is defined by the normalized max-cell concentration audit _j and enforced structurally by feasible representations F _j=Ran( _j) with packaging projectors E_j . We introduce these objects and theorem-grade constants (delocalization, frame bounds, mode growth, and cellwise defect), operationalize them with standardized experiment outputs and an automated audit.json, and evaluate the resulting bounds in toy dynamics together with matched failure-mode controls.
