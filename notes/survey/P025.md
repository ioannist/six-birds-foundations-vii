## Problem and thesis

When must an operator-valued obstruction associated with an involutive system vanish? This paper answers that question inside the Six Birds framework by naming Self-Dual Trace Confinement (SDTC) as the recognition source for formed closures with genuine involutive self-duality. The mechanism is the duality-confinement membrane theorem: for an involutive object ledger with separating anti-invariant readout, a sequence of positive domination records B_n with B_n 0 forces the anti-invariant ledger to vanish and confines visible mass to the fixed locus of the duality.
