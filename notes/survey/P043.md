## Problem and thesis

Many sciences rely on maps of macroscopic behavior—phase diagrams, stability charts, bifurcation sets, and feasibility regions—but emergence itself is rarely treated as a mapping problem. We introduce emergence atlases: subsets and scalar fields on control space indicating where a macro-description is feasible. In the finite-state setting used here, feasibility means that a packaged description is simultaneously closed, nontrivial, audit-safe, and budget-feasible.
