## Problem and thesis

The Zermelo–Fraenkel axioms are usually presented as a flat list, adopted because they work. We show that the list factors, theorem by theorem, through a single staged construction discipline—packaging under a membership lens, audited by rank. We then read, at discussion grade, G\"odel incompleteness and Cohen independence as the saturation and extension faces of that discipline.
