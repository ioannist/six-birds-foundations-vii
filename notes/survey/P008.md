## Problem and thesis

The preceding carrier-exactification paper established a scoped carrier layer on the Route A flagship support: a lawful same-branch witness, a non-faithful public signature shadow, a protocol-indexed carrier surface, scoped quadratic carrying, and two proto-event and proto-test pilots. We ask whether this carrier structure supports admissible event and test structure on fixed support. The central construct is an event bridge record B_E=(C,P,I,L,V, ,S,A) binding a carrier-side predicate, a public/readout-side predicate, an instrument, a selected lift or forgetting map, a visibility and suppression record, a threshold record, an exact-six role profile, and an audit record.
