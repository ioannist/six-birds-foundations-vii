## Problem and thesis

We propose that to be real is to have closed: that reality is not a substance, a substrate, or a set, but a ledger of survived closures. A thing is real when it is a package that earned its objecthood through audited gates and pays, continuously, to keep it. The proposal is grounded in the Six Birds corpus, which has spent the last years doing something Nietzsche predicted and no one else has attempted: worshipping the stone.
