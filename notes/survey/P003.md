## Problem and thesis

Scoped event admissibility on fixed support leaves a specific gap for strict tests: the protocol-indexed flagship-seed bridge family realizes the quadratic-levelset and transition-stability slots, but the protocol-indexed readout-agreement slot remains unrealized, so no accepted strict test family is available at the bridge-native event level. This paper asks whether strict-test acceptance is admissible on fixed support without repairing that bridge level. The method is a layer-local, audit-based PICA route: promotion, internalization of protocol coordinates, carrier exactification, and audited source admission produce a carrier substrate above the bridge-native level.
