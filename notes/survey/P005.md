## Problem and thesis

Formal theories of consciousness often mix mathematical structure, empirical access, and ontological interpretation inside single claims. We present an architecture that separates them by construction: an eight-clause closure package (objecthood, strict promotion, a reflexive self-coordinate, non-exposure, action credit, temporal closure, binding, and viability) specified as a full design and audit contract, with every result graded as a structural theorem, a recognition bridge, a conditional ontological implication, or an open target. The evidence base consists of exact finite studies, meaning exhaustively enumerated rational computations on declared carriers, together with selected machine-checked Lean kernels.
