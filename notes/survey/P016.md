## Problem and thesis

We study recombination-sensitive branch structure on a fixed-support substrate in Six Birds Theory. Building on prior work on event packaging and predictive holonomy, we introduce branch assemblages and two quotient levels at an interface: a branchwise predictive quotient ( K_i ), which captures independent predictive treatment of the branches, and a recombination quotient ( R_i ), which captures admissible recombination observables on branch families. On this architecture we exhibit a benchmark suite separating classical mixture, hidden predictive memory without recombination sensitivity, route-marked suppression, erasure-conditioned recovery, cyclic-family generalization, dissipative washout, and artifact controls based on protocol internalization and flattening/completion.
