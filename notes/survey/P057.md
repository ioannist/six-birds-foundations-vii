## Problem and thesis

Boolean logic is usually treated as a formal starting point. We ask instead when a substrate actually earns a logic layer. In the Six Birds emergence calculus—P1 operator rewrite, P2 gating/constraints, P3 protocol holonomy, P4 sectors/invariants, P5 packaging, and P6 accounting/audit—we define a logic layer as an audited closure of packaged predicates under induced dynamics.
