## Problem and thesis

Many of the objects mathematics uses to solve problems do not live in the layer where the problem is posed. Imaginary numbers are not real, infinitesimals are not real scalars, roots of irreducible polynomials need not lie in the base field, and cohomology classes are not vertex potentials. We study this pattern by separating a lower layer, where a problem and its accepted answers are stated, from a promoted layer, where additional objects may be available.
