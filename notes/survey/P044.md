## Problem and thesis

Next-token prediction is not, by itself, a theory of compositional content: finite predictive statistics can agree while the induced quotients compose differently under larger contexts. We study an alternative, non-gradient formulation in which a language model is an audited ledger of finite theory extensions rather than a parameter vector. Six Birds Theory (SBT) supplies the admissibility calculus: typed closure-package layers, explicit quotient maps, audit records, nonclaim records, and promotion gates based on descent, stability, control, no-smuggling, legibility, and strict factorization failure.
