## Problem and thesis

Near-minimal cosmological bounds on the summed neutrino mass can shift under seemingly benign pipeline/likelihood updates, yet standard presentations rarely include lens-level stability diagnostics that distinguish cosmology from packaging effects. We address the inconsistency highlighted by , where swapping the SPT-3G 2018 TT/TE/EE likelihood for the later SPT-3G D1 release—analyzing essentially the same sky field—tightens constraints and can move posteriors toward the physical boundary =0 . Our approach is to cast this lens swap as a packaging stability problem in the Six Birds / SBT formalism .
