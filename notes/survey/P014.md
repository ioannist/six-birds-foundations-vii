## Problem and thesis

Six Birds Theory constructs layered descriptions of a stochastic substrate without importing Hilbert space or the Born rule. Earlier papers in the series showed that the substrate supports interference-like recombination structure on fixed support and that at least one promoted package survives on the Route A flagship family, but left open the internal status of that survivor. We classify it here on a frozen exact-finite corpus consisting of two flagship anchors, four nearby positives, matched controls, and one support-only comparison row, comparing intrinsic, sectioned, bundle-section-fiber, and protocol-indexed candidates under descended readout, signature-level descended update, second-stage recoarsening, protocol honesty, invariant validation, and exact-finite quadratic probes.
