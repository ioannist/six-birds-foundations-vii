## Problem and thesis

We introduce a fixed-support route-transport framework in which histories can be equivalent at a current event interface yet differ as predictors of later admissible observations. For each interface, we define a current quotient by present-event indistinguishability and a predictive quotient by equality of all later admissible event statistics under continuation. Standard quotient-algebraic results then apply in this setting: future-predictive equivalence refines current-event equivalence, admissible continuations induce well-defined transport on , and the current class after transport is determined by the source predictive class through a canonical mixed map _i _j .
