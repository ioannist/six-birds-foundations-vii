## Problem and thesis

The structural laws of the Six Birds corpus through Foundations IV are stated from the standpoint of an external theorist: the theorist declares a lens, detects an obstruction, installs a repair, prices a residual, and runs the audit. This paper makes the closure apparatus part of the carrier. An endogenous closure system carries its own instruments, ledgers, repair generator, boundary records, and re-audit loop, and its claims of self-repair are admitted only when the relevant records are produced by its own transitions under a closed-loop source policy.
