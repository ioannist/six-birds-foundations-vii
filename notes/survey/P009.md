## Problem and thesis

Many obstruction arguments exhibit a localized failure mode that appears irreducible at the layer where it is first visible; this paper studies one audited route by which such needle obstructions cease to be obstructions, namely passage to a higher layer where additional admissible dissolving probes are available. The framework is the formed-layer membrane setting: zero-energy directions are removed by a legal quotient, native probes are assigned a quadratic native currency, unexplained dissolving currency is measured by an adequacy residual, and predictive membranes, dissolving transport records, and the all-six taxonomy control the transfer. The layer-dissolving endpoint theorem shows that, under predictive native bounds, residual bounds, and transport hypotheses, the dissolving currency is bounded uniformly across accepted stages, so no layer-dissolving predictive needles exist within the declared scope.
