## Problem and thesis

Formal incompleteness explains why no sufficiently expressive effective theory captures all arithmetic truth, but it does not by itself provide a structural language for comparing theory growth. This paper develops such a language by treating a formal theory as a packaged closure object evaluated on a frozen ledger. First, a fixed idempotent package saturates under iteration: repeated closure does not sustain persistent strict growth.
