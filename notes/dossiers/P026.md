## Paper thesis

Earlier work in the Six-Bird program supplies six primitive role families ( P_1, ,P_6 — descent, representability, route mismatch, refinement, packaging, audit), an admissibility and exact-six scope discipline over FATCD , inherited status, threshold, visibility, and audit machinery, and concrete model fragments such as PICA and Cantor. It does not, however, supply a paper-level finite interaction calculus that records, in one finite typed framework, how the six primitive roles interact, how typed directed cells are judged, how defects induce statuses, how promotion is audited, and how model realizations are scoped. This paper constructs , a finite audited typed calculus for six-bird interaction. The calculus carries finite typed schemas of theory packages, instruments, visibility and suppression records, witness and update families, role-channel, directed-cell, pair-observable, promotion, dependency, and no-go judgments, defect records, statuses, gates, audit records, and model-realization maps. Every component is finite, admissibility is checked on finite records, and every claim is recorded under an explicit host or instrument together with a nonclaim register. The main theorem groups are: no total six-symbol algebra; typed non-collapse and status-family separation; finite well-formedness and covered-cell status uniqueness; descent, closure-deficit, completion, saturation, strict-extension, and definability results; graph and cohomology no-drive results; promotion-gate soundness; and no-overreading and finite rotating-audit theorems. The numbered stack comprises theorem-grade Theorems 1–28 and 30–32, model-grade Model Theorems 29, 33, and 34, and audit-grade Proposition 35. The repository includes a Lean 4 validation track, pinned to leanprover/lean4:v4.28.0, whose manifest-driven strict validator audits the paper-to-Lean declaration coverage. The mathematical content of these theorems is standard — descent through a finite quotient, idempotent saturation, the Kullback–Leibler decomposition, finite cycle/cochain duality, the universal property of the image factorization, and so on — and is not claimed as novel; what the paper contributes is the calculus, its scope discipline, the countermodel atlas, and the explicit nonclaim register within which these results are restated and used. Twelve countermodels CM_1 – CM_ 12 each block a specific overclaim, and four scoped model-realization statements cover finite stochastic PICA, audited Cantor shells, finite abstract-interpretation hosts, and finite graph and cohomology supports. Each model realization is host-bound, fragment-scoped, and certified at claim-strength model_realization rather than theorem-grade accepted. The paper does not prove a universal exact-six theorem, a universal six-symbol algebra, a unique decomposition theorem, an empirical realization claim, or complete same-level self-certification.
