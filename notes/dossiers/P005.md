## Paper thesis

Formal theories of consciousness often mix mathematical structure, empirical access, and ontological interpretation inside single claims. We present an architecture that separates them by construction: an eight-clause closure package (objecthood, strict promotion, a reflexive self-coordinate, non-exposure, action credit, temporal closure, binding, and viability) specified as a full design and audit contract, with every result graded as a structural theorem, a recognition bridge, a conditional ontological implication, or an open target. The evidence base consists of exact finite studies, meaning exhaustively enumerated rational computations on declared carriers, together with selected machine-checked Lean kernels. The central exact result: on a declared twelve-state deadline carrier, every public-lens policy has an empty viability kernel, the canonical predictive quotient restores a ten-state kernel, and the unique minimal closure-restoring refinement splits exactly one public fiber; extension beyond the carrier is a conditional schema, not a generic theorem. A structural catalog then recasts privacy, the explanatory gap, zombies, free will, binding, temporal experience, valence, quality geometry, inverted spectra, personal identity, combination, and the meta-problem as interface-relative factorization, quotient, viability, gauge, and audit results. Realization is split into seven graded claims: a mechanized conditional bridge purchases it only from assumed certificate soundness plus an ontic-closure postulate, and there is no integrated certified instance and no empirical validation. An auditable code baseline with executable finite controls and predeclared prospective empirical obligations accompanies the paper.
