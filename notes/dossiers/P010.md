## Paper thesis

We instantiate a certificate-based closure calculus (Six Birds Theory) on an exact-finite class of kinetically constrained glass models, where kernels, stationary measures, witnesses, and zero controls are exact ratios of integers and the remaining certificate evidence is deterministic float64, labeled as such in each artifact. On the declared scope: the thermodynamic lens has a positive closure deficit exceeding its own constrained-equilibrium baseline by a certified margin of 3/2 (achieved 1.64 ) — and that baseline is provably nonzero, since closure is a lumpability property, not an equilibrium property (GT1). The Kovacs protocol yields an exact split-pair witness: two preparation histories current-equal and future-different (GT2). All 512 predicates definable from the declared energy lens agree on a witness pair that a memory readout separates, constructively foreclosing the static-order-parameter search on that declared class (GT3). A kill-test panel correctly executes five constructed artifact regimes (GT4). Resolving the witness pair costs exactly one declared memory scalar — fictive temperature is a lawful memory coordinate, and the buyback ledger demonstrates the mechanism without proving general single-scalar insufficiency (GT5). The predictive-structure layer receives a shell-local strict-promotion certificate via non-factorization, with the fuller completion-dynamics route explicitly not invoked (GT6). Aging readouts frozen on the East model and transferred out-of-sample under a pre-registered no-fitting protocol scored 8 passes and 14 one-sided failures, with the trap family structurally unobservable for a reason we prove: the declared trap weight family's stationary distribution is exactly temperature-invariant (GT7). A vendored Lean core machine-checks the abstract witness/refinement theorems; attempting to encode the numeric witness produced a machine-checked impossibility theorem locating that core's expressiveness boundary. Every claim is hash-pinned to its artifacts, and the full nonclaims ledger is printed unabridged.
