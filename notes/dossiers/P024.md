## Paper thesis

This paper gives a conditional Six Birds closure of the Riemann hypothesis by moving the question from classical analytic number theory to a formed-layer trace setting. The substrate is the saturated completed Selberg trace closure of the completed Riemann zeta function, equipped with the functional-equation involution and with a typed ledger of nontrivial zeros.
