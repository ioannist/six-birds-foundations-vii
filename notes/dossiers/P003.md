## Paper thesis

Scoped event admissibility on fixed support leaves a specific gap for strict tests: the protocol-indexed flagship-seed bridge family realizes the quadratic-levelset and transition-stability slots, but the protocol-indexed readout-agreement slot remains unrealized, so no accepted strict test family is available at the bridge-native event level. This paper asks whether strict-test acceptance is admissible on fixed support without repairing that bridge level. The method is a layer-local, audit-based PICA route: promotion, internalization of protocol coordinates, carrier exactification, and audited source admission produce a carrier substrate above the bridge-native level. On the resulting carriers, the readout-agreement, quadratic-witness, and transition-witness slots are strict-realized natively; the three-slot strict domain closes; and accepted scoped strict test families survive the required control panel. The panel covers null-baseline, memory-only, artifact, boundary, selected-lift, visibility, and claim-boundary checks. The bridge-native level is retained as the : descent from new-layer evidence to old-layer compatibility preserves non-factorization without inducing old-layer strict realization. The final classification is a strict theory extension at the new layer with old-layer real-provisional compatibility intact. The result is bounded: it does not assert event-package construction, shared-event identity, proto-effect or effect-algebra structure, Born valuation, composition readiness, faithful public compression, a lawful public carrier, or canonical selected lifts.
