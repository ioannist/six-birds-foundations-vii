## Paper thesis

The structural laws of the Six Birds corpus through Foundations IV are stated from the standpoint of an external theorist: the theorist declares a lens, detects an obstruction, installs a repair, prices a residual, and runs the audit. This paper makes the closure apparatus part of the carrier. An endogenous closure system carries its own instruments, ledgers, repair generator, boundary records, and re-audit loop, and its claims of self-repair are admitted only when the relevant records are produced by its own transitions under a closed-loop source policy. Six definitions (D1–D6) and sixteen laws (E1–E16) give the resulting catalog: endogenous repair, bounded reflexivity, self-maintenance, repair compilation and collapse; priced exposure, alarm, control prices, and probe acquisition; stack rewrite, individuation, and repair transport; and reconsolidation, offline reclosure, and adaptability. The laws are carrier-side and substrate-independent. Living, cognitive, and social systems therefore enter on equal terms: physiological regulation and organismal maintenance, agent-side learning and control, and institutional reproduction and rewrite are distinct realizations of the same typed hypotheses, not metaphors for one another. Each law is stated in the six-part normal form inherited from Foundations IV, with an obstruction or falsifier, an exactly-one status partition where applicable, a proof spine, interaction discipline, and explicit nonclaims. D1–D6 and the core E1–E16 laws are represented in the companion Lean 4 development and finite Repair-World/law-specific campaign; two named interpretive corollaries, Alarm Fatigue and Operational Selfhood, remain unformalized and are marked accordingly. The finite campaign contains 348 preregistered comparisons across fifteen law-specific result artifacts, all passing. Existing particle and neural substrate experiments supply life-facing null, drive, maintenance, motif, and intervention evidence under explicit nonclaims, while an unrun planarian-regeneration protocol states a concrete biological promotion target and its falsifiers. No definition of biological life, consciousness, death, welfare, or autonomous evolution is claimed.
