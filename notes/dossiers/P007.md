## Paper thesis

Earlier papers in the fixed-support line of Six Birds Theory established admissible event packages, predictive and recombination quotients, a control-stable Route A flagship corpus, promotion criteria, and the intrinsic promoted-package survivor. They left open the internal carrier status of that survivor. We introduce a carrier-level substrate on the same Route A flagship corpus, consisting of three state-like objects and one structural relation. The three objects are a lawful same-branch witness, a refined witness rung on which the same-branch transport and readout law exactifies; a coarse public signature shadow, which retains public signature role but not lawful transport; and a flagship-only protocol-indexed carrier surface, an autonomous lift of the witness on which protocol coordinates are first-class state data. The structural relation is non-faithful public compression: a projection from the witness to the public shadow that preserves public signature role but does not preserve the refined lawful structure. A seven-criterion exactification audit adapted from standard operational-theory methodology—readout sufficiency, transport and readout law, non-reducibility against the lower quotient baselines, section and protocol honesty, denominator and expansion stress, and cross-seed support—together with a refreshed section/protocol honesty rerun on the lifted flagship patch and a controlled adjacent-surface search stabilises the atlas. Three claim-bearing quadratic families and two proto-event/test families survive on the scoped carrier. Carrier law on the public surface, cross-seed carrier exactification, and full-core carrier exactification are not earned; those non-claims are preserved explicitly. A Lean formal layer records the criterion vocabulary and a typed mirror of the final verdict. The carrier atlas, with its scope and compression structure made explicit, is the substrate carried forward.
