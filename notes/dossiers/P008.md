## Paper thesis

The preceding carrier-exactification paper established a scoped carrier layer on the Route A flagship support: a lawful same-branch witness, a non-faithful public signature shadow, a protocol-indexed carrier surface, scoped quadratic carrying, and two proto-event and proto-test pilots. We ask whether this carrier structure supports admissible event and test structure on fixed support. The central construct is an event bridge record B_E=(C,P,I,L,V, ,S,A) binding a carrier-side predicate, a public/readout-side predicate, an instrument, a selected lift or forgetting map, a visibility and suppression record, a threshold record, an exact-six role profile, and an audit record. A deterministic artifact pipeline generates candidate events, constructs bridge records, applies an admissibility gate, runs controls, scores layer placement, attempts finite test-family construction, and records the consequences for shared-event identity, proto-effect structure, quadratic alignment, and exact-six bookkeeping. From a pool of seventeen candidates, two inherited scoped positives — a quadratic level-set event and a transition-stability test — are accepted at the event gate and retained under controls as protocol-indexed event-level objects. No finite admissible test family is accepted: the one serious protocol-indexed bridge-family candidate is non-exhaustive because a required readout-agreement slot is unrealized. The scoped quadratic families organize the accepted q-levelset event boundary at event level but not at test level. The resulting classification is accepted scoped protocol-indexed event-level structure without accepted tests, shared-event identity, or proto-effect operations.
