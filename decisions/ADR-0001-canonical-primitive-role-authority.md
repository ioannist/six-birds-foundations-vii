# ADR-0001 — Canonical authority for P1–P6

**Status:** accepted for Step 1.

Foundations II explicitly fixes the six role meanings for its theorem chain. Therefore its definitions govern the repository. Foundations I’s labels are preserved as concrete operational realizations, not averaged into competing definitions. This prevents generic semantics→P1, generic transitions→P3, and generic paths→P5 misclassification. Directionality uses the narrower P6_drive face and cannot be inferred from P3 holonomy alone.

Sources: P027 lines 254–303 and 1208–1245; P031 lines 1175–1207.
