# ADR-0018 — Make `readiness/` canonical and `vii/` a generated mirror

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

## Context

Later paper-planning tools benefit from a compact `vii/` layout, while delivery review benefits from one coherent canonical dossier with human and machine views. Two independently edited surfaces would drift.

## Decision

`readiness/` is the canonical Step-3 surface. `vii/` is a generated compact mirror. Both are regenerated by `scripts/build_step3_readiness.py`, and the finite result mirror is refreshed by `scripts/step3_reference_model.py`.

## Consequences

Manual edits to `vii/` are invalid. Any discrepancy is repaired by rebuilding from the frozen Step-2 inputs. Validation and delivery evidence cite `readiness/` first.
