# ADR-0017 — Preserve foundational deferrals explicitly

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

## Context

Several wish-list items would require structure not fixed by the current corpus: a category and universal property for join reduction, a nonredundant primitive operation set with relations, and an invariant supporting a conserved contact degree or scalar currency.

## Decision

The following are explicit deferrals rather than implied omissions:

1. unconditional categorical reduction of contact or join;
2. a complete generators-and-relations interaction algebra;
3. a universal scalar interaction currency or conserved contact degree.

Special-case theorems may later be admitted if their objects, maps, hypotheses, countermodels, and proof grades are explicit.

## Consequences

Later authors may not market the Step-3 operational records as already providing these stronger structures. The decision register states the evidence needed to reopen each question.
