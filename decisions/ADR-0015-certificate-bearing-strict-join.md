# ADR-0015 — Require certificate-bearing strict joins

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

## Context

The corpus distinguishes contact, common refinement, composite formation, strict nonfactorization, objecthood, source, budget, retention, and directionality. Treating any one of these as a join would recreate role collapses already prohibited by the Step-2 bridge audit.

## Decision

A later strict-join claim must carry separate witnesses for:

- evidenced contact;
- composite objecthood;
- recoverable parent retention;
- source and licensing posture;
- budget/payment or a certified zero-cost channel;
- anti-product/nonfactorization novelty against the declared baseline;
- append-only audit.

Directionality is not part of the strictness certificate and requires a separate P6-compatible drive witness.

## Consequences

Products, common refinements, co-presentations, relabelings, scheduling artifacts, and one-lineage resemblance do not receive strict-join credit by default. Failed joins and typed obstructions remain auditable outcomes rather than missing data.
