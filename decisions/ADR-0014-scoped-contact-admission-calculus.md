# ADR-0014 — Scope Foundations VII as a contact/admission calculus

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

## Context

The wish lists ask for interaction, join, access, enablement, reachability, observer, temporal, and obstruction machinery. The prior corpus already contains rich closure, access, strictness, transport, holonomy, budget, and no-go results. Calling the missing layer a universal interaction algebra would overstate both the source support and the completed proof work.

## Decision

The proposed Foundations VII center is a **scoped, proof-carrying admission/contact calculus for partially accessible, source-typed theory packages**. It may define and study domain states, admission transitions, commitments, witnessed contact, join certificates, enablement, reachability, observer occupancy, and coverage-qualified obstruction.

A universal interaction taxonomy, complete algebra, or unqualified categorical reduction is not part of the accepted scope.

## Consequences

- Prior F/E/G/BirdInt/no-go machinery is inherited through explicit adapters.
- Candidate claims are scoped to declared carriers/families until stronger proofs are supplied.
- Countermodels and red lines are first-class dependencies.
- Paper drafting must preserve the readiness grades rather than smoothing them into universal prose.
