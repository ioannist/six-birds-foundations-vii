## Anti-overread

- We do not derive continuum theories or prove analytic bounds
- Discussion, limitations, and what breaks: This paper argues that several familiar ``theory transitions'' in physics can be fruitfully organized using the Six-Birds dictionary: a layer is specified by a lens Lens (what is retained), a completion Uf (how missing information is filled in), and a timescale evolution operator T_tau , inducing a packaging operator Etauf = Uf Qf T_tau . The goal is not to replace domain knowledge, but to provide a shared language for comparing closures across domains. This section makes explicit what we are and are not claiming, and records the main failure regimes that prevent overclaiming.
- We do not claim to derive fluid models from particle mechanics, classicality from quantum theory, or backreaction corrections in GR.
- Domain-specific limitations: Each instantiation is intentionally minimal; this is a feature (clarity) and a limitation (fidelity). Quantum. Dephasing is a clean closure, but it is not ``the'' quantum-to-classical limit; basis choice matters, and semiclassical limits involve additional structure. Our point is the closure template (exact idempotence + audit monotonicity + route mismatch with coherent dynamics), not a foundational claim. Kinetic/fluid.
