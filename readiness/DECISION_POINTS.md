**Question:** Is contact surface primitive or reducible to inherited interface mediation?

**Evidence gate:** Exact source/target interface category and equivalence theorem.

## DP02

**Current safe ruling:** Treat the list as scoped to the declared finite interface family.

**Evidence gate:** Completeness theorem or explicit counterexample outside the family.

## DP03

**Question:** Is source independence required for every join?

**Current safe ruling:** Require it only for independence-sensitive claims; preserve non-independent joins as separately typed.

**Evidence gate:** Claim-by-claim source sensitivity criterion.

## DP04

**Question:** Can categorical products or pullbacks represent the join?

**Evidence gate:** Category, morphisms, universal property, and countermodels.

## DP05

## DP06

## DP07

## DP08

## DP09

## DP10
