## Defensible new center

A scoped, proof-carrying admission/contact calculus for partially accessible, source-typed theory packages: operational domain states, prospective commitment, witnessed contact, certificate-bearing strict join, enablement attribution, observer occupancy, reachability, and coverage-qualified obstruction/non-interaction.

## Inherited rather than new

Closure/objecthood, quotient-relative access, strict nonfactorization, F/E/G laws, BirdInt roles, route mismatch, confluence/holonomy machinery, currencies/budgets, and the eight existing no-gos remain inherited and require exact adapters.

## Not presently claimable

A universal join taxonomy, unconditional categorical reduction, a complete primitive algebra, one scalar interaction currency, a conserved contact degree, monotone access growth, automatic totality transfer, or an arrow inferred from holonomy.
