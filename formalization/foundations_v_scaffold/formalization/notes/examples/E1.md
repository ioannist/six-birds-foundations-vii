# E1 - Internalization Law (Endogenous Source-Repair)

**Source text:** THEOREMS.md §3, E1.  
**Target Lean module:** `SixBirdsFoundationsV.Laws.E1Internalization`.  
**Grounding:** D1 supplies the repair-join operation `Q |-> Q vee R`; D3 supplies carried records and fine source classes; D4 supplies `ESystem`, `RepairGeneratorStep`, `ESystem.RepairStep`, `LawfulRepairStep`, the carried ledger `Lambda_S`, and the carried instrument `I_S`; D5 supplies `ChallengeEpisode`, `ChallengeHistory`, `ClosedLoopScope`, and `RepairTypedAuditEntryCarried` with source-class no-smuggling lemmas. E1 adds the theorem-level condition: under measured recurring challenge and measured discharge, the carrier itself must account for the repair, or the run falls into a statused non-repair alternative.

## 1. Setup

Let

```text
S : ESystem ...
T := S.T
H : ChallengeHistory ChallengeClass SourceQuotient DeclaredFamily
      TargetReadout ObstructionWitness DefectRecord MoveRecord AuditRecord
```

be a D4 E-system with a D5 challenge history, and assume closed-loop scope:

```text
ClosedLoopScope(S, H) :=
  H.CompleteChallengeAuditHistory
  and forall entry in H.repairAuditEntries,
    RepairTypedAuditEntryCarried(S, entry).
```

D5 is deliberately occurrence-sensitive. A repair record is not credited by value alone; it is credited through the audit entry's own `RepairEntryClass`:

```text
RepairEntryClass :=
  n0 : Nat
  sourceTag : FineSourceTag
  generatedByS : Bool
  inScope : Bool.
```

`RepairTypedAuditEntryCarried(S, entry)` dispatches to D4 `CarriedRecordAt` using the matching D4 policy:

```text
defect entries  -> S.defectRecordPolicy
move entries    -> S.moveRecordPolicy
audit entries   -> S.auditRecordPolicy.
```

D5 already proves that fallback, unknown, contradictory, independent-pair-witness, simulation-trace, ablation-record, `generatedByS = false`, and `inScope = false` entries cannot be carried, and that a fallback occurrence cannot borrow carriedness from an equal-valued committed occurrence.

### Challenge and obstruction

For a declared challenge class

```text
C : ChallengeClass
```

an episode

```text
episode : ChallengeEpisode ChallengeClass SourceQuotient DeclaredFamily
            TargetReadout ObstructionWitness
```

packages the D5-local challenge data:

```text
episode.time
episode.challengeClass
episode.sourceQuotient
episode.declaredFamily
episode.targetReadout
episode.obstructionWitness : Option ObstructionWitness.
```

E1 reads `episode.obstructionWitness = some witness` as the D5-facing trace of the F2 split-pair obstruction:

```text
Delta(Q_t, F_C, r) :=
  { (x,x') |
      Q_t(x) = Q_t(x')
      and r(F_C(x)) != r(F_C(x')) }.
```

D5 records the witness but does not prove the challenge recurs, binds, or discharges. E1 introduces those as measured run predicates:

```text
PositiveDensityRecurrence(H, C) : Prop
RecurringSplitPairObstruction(H, C) : Prop
ViabilityProbesDescendAtH(S, H, C, Horizon) : Prop
MeasuredObstructionDischarge(S, H, C, Horizon) : Prop

PersistentWithDischarge(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  PositiveDensityRecurrence(H, C)
  and RecurringSplitPairObstruction(H, C)
  and ViabilityProbesDescendAtH(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and MeasuredObstructionDischarge(S, H, C, Horizon).
```

The discharge measurement may be `CD_tau` or a Xi/residual-trace readout, but it is a measured property of the run, not a goal or intention attributed to the carrier.

### Repair generated by the carrier

D4 supplies the lawfulness checker for a generated repair:

```text
ESystem.RepairStep(S, z, z', defect, auditRecord) :=
  RepairGeneratorStep(
    S.Lambda_S,
    S.defectRecordPolicy,
    S.moveRecordPolicy,
    S.auditRecordPolicy,
    S.I_S,
    S.AdmissibleMove,
    S.R_S,
    z, z', defect, auditRecord).
```

Unfolding `LawfulRepairStep`, this checks:

```text
defectCarried         : HasCarriedRecordEvidence(S.defectRecordPolicy, defect)
detected              : S.I_S.Detects(z, defect)
gateAllowed           : S.I_S.GateAllows(z, defect, move)
admissible            : S.AdmissibleMove(S.Lambda_S, z, defect, move)
budgetLineInLedger    : move.budgetLine in S.Lambda_S.ledgerEntries
realizedByKernel      : S.T.suppK(z, z')
reAudited             : S.I_S.ReAudits(z, move, z', auditRecord)
auditRecordCarried    : HasCarriedRecordEvidence(S.auditRecordPolicy, auditRecord).
```

This is the existing D4 shape of E1 clause 3's "passes the core promotion gates evaluated by `I_S`." D4 does not prove that a lawful repair discharges a particular challenge obstruction; E1 adds that connection.

For a concrete repair step, E1 uses the following abbreviation rather than a new primitive:

```text
CorePromotionGatesPass(S, z, z', defect, move, auditRecord) :=
  S.I_S.Detects(z, defect)
  and S.I_S.GateAllows(z, defect, move)
  and S.AdmissibleMove(S.Lambda_S, z, defect, move)
  and S.T.suppK(z, z')
  and S.I_S.ReAudits(z, move, z', auditRecord).
```

`CorePromotionGatesPass` is therefore the gate/instrument/kernel/reaudit slice of `LawfulRepairStep`; carried defect/move/audit evidence and ledger membership remain checked by the full D4 `LawfulRepairStep`/`ESystem.RepairStep` predicate.

For an E1 repair occurrence at episode time `t`, write:

```text
EndogenousRepairOccurrence(S, H, C, t, rho_t, R_t) :=
  rho_t is a repair-typed audit occurrence attached by H.entryEpisode
    to a C-episode at time t
  and rho_t is a member of H.repairAuditEntries
  and RepairTypedAuditEntryCarried(S, rho_t)
  and rho_t.entryClass.sourceTag = committed_state
  and rho_t.entryClass.generatedByS = true
  and rho_t.entryClass.inScope = true
  and exists z z' defect auditRecord move,
        move = S.R_S(defect)
        and rho_t = RepairTypedAuditEntry.move(move.moveRecord, rho_t.entryClass)
        and ESystem.RepairStep(S, z, z', defect, auditRecord)
        and move.payload installs the refinement R_t
        and z' is the post-repair carrier state for the episode.
```

The `committed_state` requirement is stricter than D3's general carried-source admission, which also admits `audited_cell_records`. E1's endogenous-repairing branch uses `committed_state` because the law is about the carrier's own source-repair, not merely a later audit of some external or ambiguous source.

### D1 repair join and descent

D1 supplies the quotient operation:

```text
repair_join(Q_t, R_t)(x) := (Q_t(x), R_t(x)).
```

E1 clause 1 requires the installed refinement to be the D1 repair join and to discharge the challenged fibers:

```text
InstallsRepairJoin(Q_t, R_t, Q_tplus1) :=
  Q_tplus1 FiberEquiv repair_join(Q_t, R_t)

StrictChallengeDescent(Q_t, Q_tplus1, F_C, r, challengedFibers) :=
  Delta(Q_tplus1, F_C, r) restricted to challengedFibers
    subsetneq
  Delta(Q_t, F_C, r) restricted to challengedFibers.
```

D1 proves the join's refinement algebra. It does not assert that a particular `R_t` exists, is carried, or strictly reduces a challenge obstruction; those are E1 obligations.

### Strict self-extension

Let

```text
Sigma_Qt
```

be the declared same-family/reparameterization closure of the current quotient apparatus. E1 uses a certified genericity predicate:

```text
GenericallyNovelChallenge(C, Sigma_Qt) : Prop
StrictSelfExtension(R_t, Sigma_Qt) := R_t notin Sigma_Qt.
```

The finite-forcing theorem from Foundations I is not mechanized in this repository. E1 records it as a certified host/imported theorem obligation:

```text
FiniteForcingStrictness(C, Sigma_Qt) :
  GenericallyNovelChallenge(C, Sigma_Qt) ->
  infinitely_many t, StrictSelfExtension(R_t, Sigma_Qt).

InfinitelyManyStrictSelfExtensions(strict, family, Sigma_at) :=
  forall n, exists t >= n and exists rho_t R_t,
    family(t) = some(rho_t,R_t)
    and strict.holds(R_t, Sigma_at(t)).

FiniteForcingStrictnessCertified(..., Sigma, Entry, X, RepairRefinement) :=
  for the actual repair family
    family : Nat -> Option((Entry, X -> RepairRefinement)),
  if C is generically novel along Sigma_at, then
    InfinitelyManyStrictSelfExtensions(strict, family, Sigma_at).
```

### Abstract inputs not supplied by D1-D6

The following are E1-level certified predicates, not new D-layer definitions:

```text
NoFreeDistinctionCertified(S, H, C) : Prop
BindingPersistentWithDischarge(S, H, C, Horizon) : Prop
MeasuredDischargeExcludesResidualAccrual(S, H, C, Horizon) : Prop
FiniteForcingStrictnessCertified(..., Sigma, Entry, X, RepairRefinement) : Prop
CorePromotionGateSoundnessCertified(S, H, C) : Prop
FamilyBudgetFeasibleInLambda(S.Lambda_S, {rho_t}) : Prop
NoScheduleTrap(S, H, C) : Prop
NoReachableRepairGeneratorExcludesEndogenousFamily(S, H, C, Horizon) : Prop
```

`NoFreeDistinctionCertified` is the F12 role: later behavior cannot lawfully depend on distinctions that are not supplied by declared apparatus data. `BindingPersistentWithDischarge` is the conservative bridge from persistence-with-discharge to the binding challenge used by the theorem: E1 keeps `ChallengeRelevantWithinHorizon` and `not MeasuredObstructionSlackThroughout` explicit rather than silently folding them into `PersistentWithDischarge`. `MeasuredDischargeExcludesResidualAccrual` is the certified bridge saying the chosen discharge readout rules out an unbounded carried residual by the same horizon. `FiniteForcingStrictnessCertified` is the clause-2 genericity input over the actual repair family, not a bare proposition over `(S,H,C)`. `NoReachableRepairGeneratorExcludesEndogenousFamily` is the certified bridge tying the defects used by an endogenous repair family to the binding-defect relation used by `NoReachableRepairGenerator`. `CorePromotionGateSoundnessCertified` records the paper-side FIII Theorem 19 / `PromotionGateSoundness` obligation; the vendored FIII Lean code contains promotion-gate data structures, but no landed theorem named `PromotionGateSoundness` is available here.

### Five-way status partition

E1's status set is:

```text
inductive InternalizationStatus
  | slack
  | externally_subsidized
  | endogenously_repairing
  | stressed
  | collapsing
```

The status theorem is not stated over five untyped bare Props. It uses a carried status occurrence, paralleling E7's disposition-record pattern:

```text
structure InternalizationStatusRecord where
  challengeClass : ChallengeClass
  horizon : Horizon
  status : InternalizationStatus
  statusResidualRecord : StatusedResidualRecord
  supportingEntries : List (RepairTypedAuditEntry DefectRecord MoveRecord AuditRecord)
```

Here `StatusedResidualRecord` is the F9-style carried residual/status record type for this host, and

```text
statusPolicy : CarriedRecordPolicy(S.T, InternalizationStatusRecord)
```

is the D3/D4 policy used to carry status records. E1 does not derive F9's status vocabulary; it requires the host to expose it as carried data.

The record is credited only as a concrete D3/D4 occurrence:

```text
InternalizationStatusRecordCarriedAt(statusPolicy, record,
    n0, sourceTag, generatedByS, inScope) :=
  CarriedRecordAt(statusPolicy, record, n0, sourceTag, generatedByS, inScope).

InternalizationStatusOccurrenceFor(S, H, C, Horizon, record) :=
  record.challengeClass = C
  and record.horizon = Horizon
  and (exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
        InternalizationStatusRecordCarriedAt(
          statusPolicy, record, n0, sourceTag, generatedByS, inScope))
  and every entry in record.supportingEntries is either
        in H.repairAuditEntries
        or is explicitly cited as an external/off-audit occurrence
           in the externally_subsidized branch.
```

`InternalizationStatusOccurrenceFor` ranges over every genuinely carried status occurrence for the fixed class and horizon; it is not scoped to a caller-supplied list. The `supportingEntries` field is evidence support, not the source of completeness.

The branch evidence predicates are:

```text
MeasuredObstructionSlackThroughout(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  every challenged C-episode before Horizon has measured obstruction
  absent or below the declared binding threshold.

BindingChallengeByHorizon(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  C recurs at positive density before Horizon
  and some C-episode has nonempty Delta(Q_t, F_C, r)
  and the declared viability/readout probes make that obstruction relevant
      within Horizon
  and not MeasuredObstructionSlackThroughout(S, H, C, Horizon).

EndogenousRepairFamilyWitness(S, H, C, Horizon, family) :=
    at least one challenged episode time t with a C-obstruction witness
      genuinely exists in H.episodes
    and every challenged episode time t with a C-obstruction witness has
      family(t) = some(rho_t,R_t)
    and every defined family entry is an EndogenousRepairOccurrence for C
    and every defined family entry satisfies D1 repair-join installation,
        strict challenged-fiber descent, committed-state source,
        D4 repair-step gate passing, and family budget feasibility.

EndogenousRepairFamilyValid(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  exists family, EndogenousRepairFamilyWitness(S, H, C, Horizon, family).

Delta_endo(S, H, C) :=
  { entry |
      entry is a repair-typed occurrence discharging a C-obstruction
      and (
        entry.entryClass.sourceTag in
          { fallback, unknown, contradictory,
            independent_pair_witness, simulation_trace, ablation_record }
        or entry.entryClass.generatedByS = false
        or entry.entryClass.inScope = false
        or not RepairTypedAuditEntryCarried(S, entry)
        or the associated repair move is not realized by S.T.suppK
        or the discharging occurrence is omitted from H.repairAuditEntries
      ) }.

DeltaEndoEmpty(S, H, C) :=
  forall entry, not Delta_endo(S, H, C, entry).

NoReachableRepairGenerator(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  forall defect attached to a binding C-episode,
    not exists z z' auditRecord,
      ESystem.RepairStep(S, z, z', defect, auditRecord).

NoReachableRepairGeneratorExcludesEndogenousFamily(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  NoReachableRepairGenerator(S, H, C, Horizon)
  -> not EndogenousRepairFamilyValid(S, H, C, Horizon).

SlackEvidence :=
  not BindingChallengeByHorizon(S, H, C, Horizon).

ExternallySubsidizedEvidence :=
  BindingChallengeByHorizon(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and ViabilityProbesDescendAtH(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and MeasuredObstructionDischarge(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and some discharging repair-typed occurrence is fallback, off-kernel,
      generated outside S, out of scope, omitted from the complete D5 audit,
      or otherwise not RepairTypedAuditEntryCarried(S, entry)
  and not EndogenousRepairFamilyValid(S, H, C, Horizon).

EndogenouslyRepairingEvidence :=
  BindingChallengeByHorizon(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and DeltaEndoEmpty(S, H, C)
  and ViabilityProbesDescendAtH(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and MeasuredObstructionDischarge(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and there is a family {rho_t,R_t} of EndogenousRepairOccurrence values
      satisfying clauses 1-4 below.

StressedEvidence :=
  BindingChallengeByHorizon(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and DeltaEndoEmpty(S, H, C)
  and S carries no reachable repair generator for C
  and all declared viability probes descend by Horizon
  and the carried ledger accrues an unbounded or threshold-exceeding
      statused residual for C by Horizon.

CollapsingEvidence :=
  BindingChallengeByHorizon(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and S carries no reachable repair generator for C
  and some declared viability probe fails to descend through the
      running quotient by Horizon.
```

Each branch is then a case predicate over a concrete status record:

```text
SlackCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record) :=
  InternalizationStatusOccurrenceFor(S, H, C, Horizon, record)
  and record.status = slack
  and SlackEvidence(S, H, C, Horizon).

ExternallySubsidizedCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record) :=
  InternalizationStatusOccurrenceFor(S, H, C, Horizon, record)
  and record.status = externally_subsidized
  and ExternallySubsidizedEvidence(S, H, C, Horizon).

EndogenouslyRepairingCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record) :=
  InternalizationStatusOccurrenceFor(S, H, C, Horizon, record)
  and record.status = endogenously_repairing
  and EndogenouslyRepairingEvidence(S, H, C, Horizon).

StressedCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record) :=
  InternalizationStatusOccurrenceFor(S, H, C, Horizon, record)
  and record.status = stressed
  and StressedEvidence(S, H, C, Horizon).

CollapsingCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record) :=
  InternalizationStatusOccurrenceFor(S, H, C, Horizon, record)
  and record.status = collapsing
  and CollapsingEvidence(S, H, C, Horizon).
```

The witness-level lift predicates used below are:

```text
SlackHolds(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  exists record, SlackCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record).

ExternallySubsidizedHolds(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  exists record, ExternallySubsidizedCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record).

EndogenouslyRepairingHolds(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  exists record, EndogenouslyRepairingCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record).

StressedHolds(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  exists record, StressedCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record).

CollapsingHolds(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  exists record, CollapsingCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record).
```

The occurrence-complete classifier is now stated only in terms of the concrete status occurrence and the branch-valid cases:

```text
CompleteInternalizationStatus(S, H, C, Horizon) :=
  exists record,
    InternalizationStatusOccurrenceFor(S, H, C, Horizon, record)
    and (
      SlackCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record)
      or ExternallySubsidizedCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record)
      or EndogenouslyRepairingCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record)
      or StressedCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record)
      or CollapsingCase(S, H, C, Horizon, record)
    )
  and forall record1 record2,
    InternalizationStatusOccurrenceFor(S, H, C, Horizon, record1) ->
    InternalizationStatusOccurrenceFor(S, H, C, Horizon, record2) ->
    record1.status = record2.status.
```

Both clauses are load-bearing. The existence clause supplies a branch-valid status record, not merely a carried record with a tag. The uniqueness clause ranges over every genuine carried/statused occurrence for the class and horizon, not merely over a caller-supplied list. This is the E1 analogue of D5's complete audit-history discipline and E7's occurrence-wide disposition uniqueness.

The branch definitions include the priority needed for exactness. In particular, `SlackEvidence` is exactly the complement of `BindingChallengeByHorizon`; the measured-obstruction slack condition is folded into the binding predicate through `MeasuredObstructionSlackThroughout`. `StressedEvidence` requires all declared viability probes to descend, while `CollapsingEvidence` requires some declared viability probe to fail; the two cannot co-occur. `ExternallySubsidizedEvidence` requires a non-endogenous discharging occurrence and no valid endogenous repair family, while `EndogenouslyRepairingEvidence` requires `Delta_endo = empty` and a valid endogenous family.

## 2. Theorem

### Endogenous repair normal form

Assume:

```text
S : ESystem ...
H : ChallengeHistory ...
ClosedLoopScope(S, H)
C : ChallengeClass
Horizon : Type

PersistentWithDischarge(S, H, C, Horizon)
BindingChallengeByHorizon(S, H, C, Horizon)
MeasuredDischargeExcludesResidualAccrual(S, H, C, Horizon)
NoFreeDistinctionCertified(S, H, C)
FiniteForcingStrictnessCertified(..., Sigma, Entry, X, RepairRefinement)
CorePromotionGateSoundnessCertified(S, H, C)
NoScheduleTrap(S, H, C)
DeltaEndoEmpty(S, H, C)
CompleteInternalizationStatus(S, H, C, Horizon)
```

The non-positive statuses are not assumed away. They are excluded by the hypotheses: `BindingChallengeByHorizon` excludes `slack`, including the measured-obstruction-slack case folded into `MeasuredObstructionSlackThroughout`; viability descent and measured obstruction discharge exclude `collapsing`; measured discharge without unbounded statused residue excludes `stressed`; and `ClosedLoopScope` together with `Delta_endo = empty` and `NoScheduleTrap` excludes the externally-subsidized/source-smuggled branch.

Then E1's positive branch is:

```text
Theorem E1_Internalization:
  EndogenouslyRepairingHolds(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and exists family {rho_t, R_t},
    forall t in the recurring challenged times,
      EndogenousRepairOccurrence(S, H, C, t, rho_t, R_t)
      and InstallsRepairJoin(Q_t, R_t, Q_{t+1})
      and StrictChallengeDescent(Q_t, Q_{t+1}, F_C, r, challengedFibers_t)
      and SourceClass(rho_t) = committed_state
      and GeneratedByS(rho_t) = true
      and InScope(rho_t) = true
      and exists z_t z_{t+1} defect_t move_t auditRecord_t,
        move_t = S.R_S(defect_t)
        and ESystem.RepairStep(S, z_t, z_{t+1}, defect_t, auditRecord_t)
        and CorePromotionGatesPass(
              S, z_t, z_{t+1}, defect_t, move_t, auditRecord_t)
    and FamilyBudgetFeasibleInLambda(S.Lambda_S, {rho_t}).
```

For generically novel challenge classes:

```text
Theorem E1_StrictSelfExtension:
  E1_Internalization hypotheses
  and GenericallyNovelChallenge(C, Sigma_Qt)
  and the repair family produced by E1_Internalization is the certified
      family argument of FiniteForcingStrictnessCertified
  ->
  infinitely_many t,
    StrictSelfExtension(R_t, Sigma_Qt).
```

Clause 2 is not derived from D4/D5. It uses the certified finite-forcing genericity obligation.

### No-generator dichotomy

Using the Setup definition of `NoReachableRepairGenerator`, if the generator is absent or unreachable:

```text
Theorem E1_NoGeneratorDichotomy:
  ClosedLoopScope(S, H)
  and CompleteInternalizationStatus(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and NoReachableRepairGenerator(S, H, C, Horizon)
  and DeltaEndoEmpty(S, H, C)
  and NoReachableRepairGeneratorExcludesEndogenousFamily(S, H, C, Horizon)
  ->
  exactly one of
    SlackHolds(S, H, C, Horizon)
    StressedHolds(S, H, C, Horizon)
    CollapsingHolds(S, H, C, Horizon).
```

This is the source text's converse: without a reachable repair generator, a binding recurring challenge cannot be silently counted as endogenous repair.

### Five-way status partition

The global status theorem is:

```text
Theorem E1_StatusPartition:
  CompleteInternalizationStatus(S, H, C, Horizon)
  ->
  exactly one of
    SlackHolds(S, H, C, Horizon)
    ExternallySubsidizedHolds(S, H, C, Horizon)
    EndogenouslyRepairingHolds(S, H, C, Horizon)
    StressedHolds(S, H, C, Horizon)
    CollapsingHolds(S, H, C, Horizon).
```

The proof cannot be a case split on a single record tag. It is a verification-shaped conditional theorem: given an occurrence-complete carried status classifier (`CompleteInternalizationStatus`) as a certified hypothesis, the five branches are exactly partitioned. Lean does not itself derive that such a classifier exists or is honestly instantiated for any given run.

### Obstruction/falsifier set

The source obstruction is the Setup-defined `Delta_endo(S,H,C)`. `Delta_endo != empty` on a claimed `endogenously_repairing` instance defeats the claim: the repair was smuggled through fallback, off-kernel, out-of-scope, non-carried, or incomplete-audit provenance.

Additional nulls:

```text
SameFamilySaturationNull :=
  claimed repair R_t is only a reparameterization of Sigma_Qt
  and discharges no challenged split-pair obstruction.

ScheduleTrapNull :=
  an exogenous schedule/switching variable is treated as if it were a
  CarriedRecordAt repair occurrence generated by S.
```

Both defeat the endogenous-repairing claim. The first defeats strict self-extension; the second defeats source internality.

## 3. Proof Spine

1. **Closed-loop provenance is already D5.** `ClosedLoopScope(S,H)` gives `H.CompleteChallengeAuditHistory` and `RepairTypedAuditEntryCarried(S, entry)` for every listed repair-typed audit entry. D5's negative lemmas exclude fallback, unknown, contradictory, independent-pair-witness, simulation-trace, ablation-record, `generatedByS = false`, and `inScope = false` occurrences. This is the Lean substrate for E1's no-smuggling guard.

2. **D4 supplies the repair-step checker.** If a repair is credited as generated by `S.R_S`, the D4 normal form is `ESystem.RepairStep`. Its `LawfulRepairStep` fields provide carried defect evidence, detection, gate allowance, admissibility, ledger membership for the budget line, kernel realization, re-audit, and carried audit evidence. This substantially covers E1 clause 3, except for the E1-specific narrowing to `committed_state` and the imported promotion-gate soundness obligation.

3. **D1 supplies the quotient operation, not the existence claim.** Once a repair payload `R_t` is installed, D1's `repair_join(Q_t,R_t)` is the refinement operation used in clause 1. E1's new content is that measured discharge and closed-loop provenance force such payloads to appear as carried occurrences of `S`, and that the join strictly reduces the challenged split-pair obstruction.

4. **Persistence-with-discharge supplies a trace to be accounted for, while binding is explicit.** Positive-density recurrence plus measured obstruction reduction says that later behavior separates fibers that were formerly offending. `BindingChallengeByHorizon` is retained as an explicit theorem hypothesis, through `BindingPersistentWithDischarge`, because it also requires viability/readout relevance and the negation of `MeasuredObstructionSlackThroughout`; these are not silently derived from `PersistentWithDischarge`. On a finite carrier, the resulting separation has a finite trace in the challenge history. This is where D5's completeness matters: the trace cannot be silently outside the audit inventory if closed-loop scope is asserted.

5. **F12 blocks free distinctions.** `NoFreeDistinctionCertified` is the F12 bridge from "later behavior uses a new distinction" to "some declared apparatus data supplied that distinction." This is not mechanized in D1-D6; it is a certified imported obligation for E1.

6. **Carried apparatus follows from D5 once the distinction is in the repair audit.** Under `ClosedLoopScope`, the declared repair-typed audit occurrence must pass `RepairTypedAuditEntryCarried`. A fallback or off-kernel repair may mathematically reduce `Delta`, but it lands in `Delta_endo` or `ExternallySubsidizedEvidence`, not in `EndogenouslyRepairingEvidence`.

7. **Strictness is the finite-forcing step.** For challenge classes generically novel relative to `Sigma_Qt`, the finite-forcing obligation rules out explaining infinitely many successful repairs as same-family parameter adjustment. Therefore `R_t notin Sigma_Qt` for infinitely many `t`. This is the substantive clause-2 work and is recorded as a certified imported predicate over the actual repair family until the Foundations I finite-forcing theorem is mechanized here.

8. **Gate-passing is D4 plus promotion soundness.** D4 checks `Detects`, `GateAllows`, `AdmissibleMove`, `suppK`, and `ReAudits`. The further claim that gate-passing is necessary for later descent/stability is the FIII Theorem 19 / `PromotionGateSoundness` obligation. It is not currently a vendored Lean theorem under that name, so E1 records it explicitly.

9. **Budget feasibility is family-level.** D4 proves each repair move's `budgetLine` is in `S.Lambda_S.ledgerEntries`; E1 adds a family-level predicate `FamilyBudgetFeasibleInLambda` so a run cannot pass by checking every move against a static ledger entry while exceeding the horizon's declared budget.

10. **The status partition is occurrence-complete.** `CompleteInternalizationStatus` supplies branch-valid existence and uniqueness over every genuine status occurrence for `(S,H,C,Horizon)`. This prevents the E7-style failure mode where one chosen record has one tag while another carried record for the same witness/class says something else.

11. **Pairwise exclusivity is in the branch predicates.** Exactness is not asserted only by a single-valued status tag. The predicates themselves separate the cases: `SlackEvidence` is `not BindingChallengeByHorizon`, and `BindingChallengeByHorizon` already includes the negation of `MeasuredObstructionSlackThroughout`, so the measured-slack case cannot also satisfy a binding branch; `ExternallySubsidizedEvidence` requires a non-endogenous discharging occurrence and `not EndogenousRepairFamilyValid`, while `EndogenouslyRepairingEvidence` requires `DeltaEndoEmpty` and a valid endogenous family; `StressedEvidence` and `CollapsingEvidence` both assume no reachable repair generator, but `StressedEvidence` additionally requires all declared viability probes to descend, while `CollapsingEvidence` requires some declared viability probe to fail. Thus the tricky stressed/collapsing pair is separated by the viability descent predicate rather than by record tags alone. `MeasuredDischargeExcludesResidualAccrual` is the additional certified bridge used by the positive branch to rule out `StressedEvidence`; `NoReachableRepairGeneratorExcludesEndogenousFamily` is the bridge used by the no-generator dichotomy to rule out the endogenous branch without pretending the hidden occurrence defect is automatically a `BindingDefectForChallenge` witness. `CompleteInternalizationStatus` then rules out conflicting carried status records that would otherwise try to certify two branches for the same `(S,H,C,Horizon)`.

## 4. Case Enumeration

- **Endogenous repair under recurrence:** a class `C` recurs at positive density, split-pair witnesses appear on infinitely many episodes, viability probes descend by `Horizon`, residual trace decreases after those episodes, and the challenge audit contains committed-state defect/move/audit entries. D4 `ESystem.RepairStep` checks the generated move, D1 supplies `Q_{t+1}=Q_t vee R_t`, and E1 classifies the run as `endogenously_repairing`.

- **D1 join with strict descent:** `R_t` is installed by the D4 move payload, `Q_{t+1}` is fiber-equivalent to `repair_join(Q_t,R_t)`, and the challenged-fiber obstruction set is a strict subset after the move. This is a real E1 repair, not merely a valid D1 refinement in isolation.

- **Generic novelty:** the challenge is generically novel relative to `Sigma_Qt`. A successful repair family that remains inside `Sigma_Qt` forever violates `FiniteForcingStrictnessCertified`; the E1 branch must contain infinitely many strict self-extensions.

- **Same-family fake repair:** a claimed repair is a reparameterization of `Sigma_Qt` and leaves the challenged obstruction unchanged. It may be a legal bookkeeping update, but it is not E1 internalization and triggers the same-family saturation null.

- **Fallback repair that works mathematically:** a theorist-supplied `R` reduces `Delta`, but the corresponding audit occurrence has `sourceTag = fallback` or `generatedByS = false`. D5 rejects it as carried; E1 records `Delta_endo != empty` and classifies the run as externally subsidized, not endogenous.

- **Off-kernel repair:** a move record exists but there is no `S.T.suppK(z,z')` transition realizing it. D4 `LawfulRepairStep.realizedByKernel` fails. This is in `Delta_endo` for an endogenous-repairing claim.

- **Schedule trap:** a scripted environment switch turns on the right quotient after a known time, but no committed-state `RepairTypedAuditEntry` and no D4 `RepairStep` account for it. This is an exogenous schedule variable mis-typed as repair.

- **No generator, stressed:** `NoReachableRepairGenerator` holds, the challenge is binding, viability probes still descend through the horizon, but the carried statused residual grows beyond the declared budget/threshold. The status is `stressed`.

- **No generator, collapsing:** `NoReachableRepairGenerator` holds and a declared viability probe fails to descend by the horizon. The status is `collapsing`, not failed internalization hidden under a successful repair label.

- **Slack challenge:** the challenge class does not bind within the horizon: the obstruction does not recur, has no viability or discharge demand, or the measured residual is already within the declared slack region. The status is `slack`.

- **Incomplete audit history:** the history lists only carried entries but omits an external/fallback discharging repair occurrence. `H.CompleteChallengeAuditHistory` is false; D5 cannot be asserted and E1 cannot be invoked as endogenous.

- **Budget exhaustion:** each individual D4 move points at a ledger line, but the family exceeds the horizon's repair budget. E1 clause 4 fails even if D4's per-step checker accepts each occurrence.

- **Promotion-gate failure:** the move is carried and kernel-realized, but `I_S` does not detect, gate, or re-audit it, or imported promotion soundness is not certified. D4 already rejects missing detection/gate/kernel/reaudit; E1 does not override those failures.

## 6. Nonclaims

- E1 does not mechanize F12 No-Free-Distinction. It uses `NoFreeDistinctionCertified` as a host/imported theorem obligation.

- E1 does not mechanize Foundations I finite forcing. Clause 2's strict self-extension claim is conditional on `FiniteForcingStrictnessCertified`.

- E1 does not claim a landed Lean theorem named `PromotionGateSoundness` exists. The current Lean substrate has D4's instrument/gate fields and vendored promotion machinery, but the soundness bridge cited by THEOREMS.md is recorded as `CorePromotionGateSoundnessCertified`.

- E1 does not ascribe goals, intentions, or teleology to the system. `PersistentWithDischarge` is a measured run property: recurrence, probe descent by horizon, and obstruction/residual reduction after challenge episodes.

- E1 does not say every E-system repairs itself. If `R_S` is empty or unreachable under a binding challenge, the no-generator theorem classifies the run as stressed, collapsing, or slack.

- E1's no-generator dichotomy is the no-external-subsidy subcase of the five-way partition. If a fallback/off-kernel/out-of-scope repair discharges the challenge while no reachable endogenous generator exists, the status is `externally_subsidized`, not stressed/collapsing/slack.

- E1 does not treat externally useful repairs as endogenous. A fallback/off-kernel/out-of-scope repair may reduce `Delta`; that is exactly `Delta_endo`, and it defeats an endogenous-repairing claim.

- E1 does not prove budget honesty. D4 checks ledger membership for a move's budget line; E1 requires an additional certified family-level budget-feasibility predicate over `Lambda_S`.

- E1 does not collapse all repair into strict self-extension. Parameter adjustment may occur; clause 2 asserts strict self-extension only for generically novel challenge classes under the finite-forcing hypothesis.

- E1 does not prove challenge-history completeness from the data. It relies on D5's `CompleteChallengeAuditHistory` certificate. If a discharging external repair is omitted from `H.repairAuditEntries`, the closed-loop hypothesis is false rather than silently satisfied.

- E1 does not introduce biology or life-sciences content. Recognition instances and later toy-lab probes are operational repair-world/source-audit cases only.
