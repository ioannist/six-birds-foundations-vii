# E5 - Reclosure Collapse Law (Repair-Economy Collapse)

**Source text:** THEOREMS.md §2, E5 (lines 181-201).
**Target Lean module:** `SixBirdsFoundationsV.Laws.E5ReclosureCollapse`.
**Grounding:** D3 supplies carried records and source classes; D4 supplies `ESystem`, `S.R_S`,
`ESystem.RepairStep`, `LawfulRepairStep`, `S.AdmissibleMove`, `S.Lambda_S`, and `S.T.suppK`.
E1 (landed, this repo) supplies `Delta`, `ViabilityProbesDescendAtH`, and the
`Delta_endo`/`DeltaEndoEmpty` emptiness-wrapper convention for obstruction predicates. E3 (landed)
supplies the closure-apparatus/reclosure surface (`ClosureApparatus`, `ClosureMaintenanceOperator`,
`MaintenanceReinstatementRecord`, `MaintenanceReinstatementFor`) directly reused for the source text's
"apparatus reclosure E'" move type. D6/E6+E9 (landed) supply the budgeted acquisition surface:
`ProbeEconomy`, `LawfulAcquisition`, `ExposureBudgetWitness`, `BudgetFeasible`, and
`CandidateAcquisition`. F19 dissolution/viability-kernel content and the Agents-paper
greatest-fixed-point viability-kernel construction are not mechanized in Foundations V; E5 treats them
as certified host input, following E3/E7/E12's established F19/Agents-paper precedent.

The Agents paper is a recognized-instance anchor, not a Lean dependency. Its finite ring-world
exhibits state that when maintenance/repair is removed under costs, the viability kernel collapses
(`|K| = 0`) and that the noise-maintenance sweep exhibits a phase boundary where the robust kernel
collapses as repair becomes too expensive. E5 lifts that observed collapse boundary into a carried
repair-economy normal form with reversibility grades, subsidy, suspension, and revival statuses.

## 1. Setup

Let

```text
S : ESystem FData RuleFamily ResidualFamily AuditAccessData
      InstrumentRecord LedgerEntry DefectRecord MovePayload MoveRecord AuditRecord
H : ChallengeHistory ChallengeClass SourceQuotient DeclaredFamily
      TargetReadout ObstructionWitness DefectRecord MoveRecord AuditRecord
C : ChallengeClass
horizon : Horizon
```

be a D4 E-system, an E1/D5 challenge-history surface, and a declared challenge class and horizon.
E5 does not introduce a parallel carrier or a second repair ledger. All repair, reclosure, boundary,
and acquisition candidates are credited only if they are carried in the D3/D4 sense and their budget
entries live in `S.Lambda_S`.

The concrete branch records below own the link from their native D4/E1/E3/D6 record to the common

them from raw transitions in the six-field normal form.

### Declared viability probes and descent

E1 already provides the abstract certified predicate:

```text
ViabilityProbesDescendAtH(System, History, ChallengeClass, Horizon)
```

with field

```text
holds : System -> History -> ChallengeClass -> Horizon -> Prop.
```

This is a direct vocabulary match for the source text's "declared viability probes descend." It is
not, by itself, a move-specific causal witness. E5 therefore reuses E1's predicate as the declared
descent surface and adds a move-owned bridge tying descent to the same rescue candidate being credited.
This avoids the E4 v24/v25 quotient-linking bug class: descent evidence cannot be supplied for one
move while classifying an unrelated move.

```text
structure DeclaredViabilityProbeFamily where
  probeFamilyRecord : ViabilityProbeFamilyRecord
  carried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt viabilityProbeFamilyPolicy probeFamilyRecord
        n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true

DescentReadoutForProbeFamily(descentReadoutRecord, probeFamilyRecord) : Prop

RescueMoveCreditedForDescent(moveRecord, descentReadoutRecord) : Prop
  -- certified, data-indexed by CollapseMoveRecord and ViabilityDescentReadoutRecord;
  -- this is the field that actually discriminates between candidates (see below)

structure RescueDescentCertificate
    (moveRecord : CollapseMoveRecord) where
  probeFamily : DeclaredViabilityProbeFamily
  descentForMove : CollapseMoveRecord
  moveRecordLinked : descentForMove = moveRecord
  descends : ViabilityProbesDescendAtH.holds S H C horizon
  descentReadoutRecord : ViabilityDescentReadoutRecord
  readoutForDeclaredFamily :
    DescentReadoutForProbeFamily descentReadoutRecord probeFamily.probeFamilyRecord
  moveCreditedForDescent :
    RescueMoveCreditedForDescent moveRecord descentReadoutRecord
  readoutCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt viabilityDescentPolicy descentReadoutRecord
        n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
```

`RescueDescentCertificate` is certified measured-run content: it says that this concrete rescue move
made the declared probes descend within the horizon. It is now explicitly tied to the carried
`DeclaredViabilityProbeFamily` through `readoutForDeclaredFamily`, so the descent certificate cannot
quietly use a different probe family than the one declared for the E5 scope. The future toy-lab must
compute this from exact rational probe curves and move application, not from an ignored boolean flag.

**Lean-mechanization addendum (candidate-to-descent linkage fix, caught during Setup mechanization).**
`descends : ViabilityProbesDescendAtH.holds S H C horizon` is E1's certified predicate — genuinely
global over `(S, H, C, horizon)`, with no move parameter of its own. A first Lean attempt at
`CollapseRescueDescends` used only this global field, discovered to be logically vacuous once written
out precisely: Lean's proof irrelevance for `Prop` makes any two proofs of the same global fact
definitionally equal, so no `Prop`-typed projection off a per-move certificate can discriminate between
candidates — the exact same class of bug as E4's v24 candidate-quotient-linkage finding, just
resurfacing through a subtler mechanism (proof irrelevance rather than an unlinked free parameter). The
fix adds `RescueMoveCreditedForDescent`, a certified fact genuinely indexed by *data* (a concrete
`CollapseMoveRecord` and `ViabilityDescentReadoutRecord`, not a bare `Prop`), and `moveCreditedForDescent`
is what `CollapseRescueDescends` actually projects — see below.

### Exact budget surface

The source text's "or the move exceeds Lambda_S" is represented by a concrete budget witness owned by
each move candidate:

```text
structure CollapseBudgetWitness (moveRecord : CollapseMoveRecord) where
  spend : Rat
  budget : Rat
  budgetEntry : LedgerEntry
  spendEntry : LedgerEntry
  budgetEntryInLedger : budgetEntry in S.Lambda_S.ledgerEntries
  spendEntryInLedger : spendEntry in S.Lambda_S.ledgerEntries
  budgetedMoveRecord : CollapseMoveRecord
  moveRecordLinked : budgetedMoveRecord = moveRecord

def CollapseBudgetFeasible (w : CollapseBudgetWitness moveRecord) : Prop :=
  w.spend <= w.budget
```

Every cost, budget, and residual quantity is exact rational data (`Rat` in Lean; `fractions.Fraction`
in the eventual sweep). A move that has a positive descent certificate but fails `CollapseBudgetFeasible`
does not defeat collapse: the source text explicitly allows failure by exceeding `Lambda_S`.

### Four rescue move families

E5's collapse core quantifies over four move types: repair `R`, boundary update `B`, apparatus
reclosure `E'`, and budget-feasible acquisition (E9). Each type has a dedicated carried witness. The
common wrapper below is only for status records and falsifiers; it does not erase the branch-specific
lawfulness checks.

#### Repair rescue

A repair rescue reuses E1's occurrence-level repair vocabulary directly. The repair occurrence must
be the source of the same `moveRecord` being credited, and the budget/descent witnesses must point to
that same record.

```text
structure RepairRescueCandidate where
  moveRecord : CollapseMoveRecord
  kindOk : moveRecord.kind = CollapseMoveKind.repair
  t : Nat
  rho_t : RepairTypedAuditEntry DefectRecord MoveRecord AuditRecord
  R_t : RepairRefinement
  occurrence :
    EndogenousRepairOccurrence S H installs postState C t rho_t R_t
  moveRecordLinked : repairMoveRef rho_t = moveRecord
  budget : CollapseBudgetWitness moveRecord
  descent : RescueDescentCertificate moveRecord

def RepairRescueAdmissibleInBudget (r : RepairRescueCandidate) : Prop :=
  CollapseBudgetFeasible r.budget
```

This is narrower than arbitrary D4 repair lawfulness because E5's statement is challenge-relative:
the repair must be a carried E1-style occurrence for the declared `C`, not merely any `S.R_S` step.

#### Boundary update rescue

No landed law yet owns E5's boundary-update operation. E7 has a forward reference to E5's boundary
nonclosure branch, but E7 does not supply a generic E5 boundary-repair move. E5 therefore introduces a
certified boundary-update interface, with concrete carried record and budget/descent links:

```text
structure BoundaryUpdateCandidate where
  moveRecord : CollapseMoveRecord
  kindOk : moveRecord.kind = CollapseMoveKind.boundary_update
  preBoundary : CollapseBoundaryRecord
  postBoundary : CollapseBoundaryRecord
  updateRecord : BoundaryUpdateRecord

    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt boundaryUpdatePolicy updateRecord
        n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
  linksPrePost :
    updateRecord.preBoundary = preBoundary
      and updateRecord.postBoundary = postBoundary
      and updateRecord.moveRecord = moveRecord
  kernelRealized : S.T.suppK preBoundary.sourceState postBoundary.targetState

  descent : RescueDescentCertificate moveRecord

  CollapseBudgetFeasible b.budget
```

`BoundaryUpdateCandidate` is the place where future E7/E5 correspondence may later connect
`BoundaryNonclosureCase` to an explicit boundary repair. For now it is certified host input with
D3/D4 carriedness and kernel realization.

#### Apparatus reclosure rescue

This is a substantive direct dependency on E3, not a naming coincidence. E3's "reclosure" means a
maintenance operator reinstates a `ClosureApparatus`; E5's apparatus reclosure move is exactly that
kind of candidate when it is offered as a rescue from collapse.


The load-bearing anti-unlinked-evidence guard is inherited from E3:

#### Budget-feasible acquisition rescue

E5's E9 acquisition move reuses the already-landed D6/E6+E9 acquisition surface. A rescue acquisition
must be strict, budget-feasible, and linked to the same `CollapseMoveRecord`:

```text
structure AcquisitionRescueCandidate
```

carried active-family occurrences.

### Unified constructive falsifier

The obstruction/falsifier set is the source text's key negative test: a collapse verdict is defeated
by a named admissible in-budget carried move that makes the declared viability probes descend.
`CollapseRescueCandidate` is the self-scope candidate type; subsidizer-scope moves use the separate
`SubsidizerRescueCandidate` type introduced below.

```text
inductive CollapseRescueCandidate
  | repair (r : RepairRescueCandidate)
  | boundary (b : BoundaryUpdateCandidate)
  | reclosure (e : ApparatusReclosureCandidate)
  | acquisition (a : AcquisitionRescueCandidate economy)

def CollapseRescueAdmissibleInBudget : CollapseRescueCandidate -> Prop
  | repair r => RepairRescueAdmissibleInBudget r
  | boundary b => BoundaryUpdateAdmissibleInBudget b
  | reclosure e => ApparatusReclosureAdmissibleInBudget e
  | acquisition a => AcquisitionRescueAdmissibleInBudget a

def CollapseRescueDescends : CollapseRescueCandidate -> Prop
  | repair r => RescueMoveCreditedForDescent r.moveRecord r.descent.descentReadoutRecord
  | boundary b => RescueMoveCreditedForDescent b.moveRecord b.descent.descentReadoutRecord
  | reclosure e => RescueMoveCreditedForDescent e.moveRecord e.descent.descentReadoutRecord
  | acquisition a => RescueMoveCreditedForDescent a.moveRecord a.descent.descentReadoutRecord
  -- projects moveCreditedForDescent, not the global `descends` field directly (see the
  -- Lean-mechanization addendum above) -- this is what makes the check genuinely
  -- candidate-specific rather than a global fact restated per branch

def CollapseRescueMoveRecord : CollapseRescueCandidate -> CollapseMoveRecord
  | repair r => r.moveRecord
  | boundary b => b.moveRecord
  | reclosure e => e.moveRecord
  | acquisition a => a.moveRecord

structure CompleteCollapseRescueInventory where
  declaredCandidates : List CollapseRescueCandidate
  complete :
    forall move : CollapseRescueCandidate,
      move in declaredCandidates

def CollapseFalsifier (S H C horizon)
    (inventory : CompleteCollapseRescueInventory) : Prop :=
  exists move : CollapseRescueCandidate,
    move in inventory.declaredCandidates
      and CollapseRescueAdmissibleInBudget move
      and CollapseRescueDescends move

def NoCollapseFalsifier (S H C horizon)
    (inventory : CompleteCollapseRescueInventory) : Prop :=
  not exists move : CollapseRescueCandidate,
    move in inventory.declaredCandidates
      and CollapseRescueAdmissibleInBudget move
      and CollapseRescueDescends move
```

This mirrors the Needles/D3 record-level discipline: failed dissolution/collapse must fail by named
missing or defective records, not by an anonymous absence claim. The inventory completeness field is
load-bearing: without it, collapse could be manufactured by leaving a real rescue candidate out of the
model. `NoCollapseFalsifier` is therefore only evaluated through an `inventory :
CompleteCollapseRescueInventory`, so completeness is a type-level input to the collapsed core. Because
`CollapseRescueCandidate` is already the self-scope candidate type, completeness is unconditional over
that type; there is no separate certified ownership gate that could be left false to make the
inventory vacuously complete.

### Collapse core and irreversibility inputs

Collapse is about the repair economy, not activity level. A system may be active and collapsed if it
keeps transitioning while no admissible in-budget carried rescue move makes the probes descend. A
dormant or suspended system need not be collapsed if its apparatus and generator remain intact.

```text
structure StatusedResidualAccrual (S H C horizon) where
  challengeClass : ChallengeClass
  residualHorizon : Horizon
  scopeLinked :
    challengeClass = C and residualHorizon = horizon
  residualRecord : ResidualStatusRecord
  residualSpend : Rat
  residualBudget : Rat
  residualCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt residualPolicy residualRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
  accrualWithinBudget : residualSpend <= residualBudget

def CollapsedCore (S H C horizon)
    (inventory : CompleteCollapseRescueInventory) : Prop :=
  NoCollapseFalsifier S H C horizon inventory
```

F19's viability kernel and the Agents-paper greatest-fixed-point operator are certified host input:

```text
structure RepairGeneratorReachabilityCertified where
  reachabilityRecord : RepairGeneratorReachabilityRecord
  selfReachable : Prop
  externallyReachableOnly : Prop
  unreachable : Prop
  exclusive :
    exactly one of selfReachable, externallyReachableOnly, unreachable

structure ApparatusViabilityKernelCertified where
  kernelRecord : ApparatusViabilityKernelRecord
  kernelNonempty : Prop
  kernelEmpty : Prop
  complement : kernelNonempty <-> not kernelEmpty

structure IrreversibilityCoherence
    (C : ChallengeClass) (horizon : Horizon)
    (reach : RepairGeneratorReachabilityCertified)
    (kernel : ApparatusViabilityKernelCertified) where
  currentScopeLinked :
    reach.reachabilityRecord.scope.challengeClass = C
      and reach.reachabilityRecord.scope.horizon = horizon
      and kernel.kernelRecord.scope.challengeClass = C
      and kernel.kernelRecord.scope.horizon = horizon
  sameDeclaredScope : reach.reachabilityRecord.scope = kernel.kernelRecord.scope
  unreachableIffKernelEmpty : reach.unreachable <-> kernel.kernelEmpty

def IrreversibleCollapseInput (C : ChallengeClass) (horizon : Horizon)
    (reach : RepairGeneratorReachabilityCertified)
    (kernel : ApparatusViabilityKernelCertified) : Prop :=
  exists coherence : IrreversibilityCoherence C horizon reach kernel,
    reach.unreachable and kernel.kernelEmpty

def RecoverableCollapseInput (C : ChallengeClass) (horizon : Horizon)
    (reach : RepairGeneratorReachabilityCertified)
    (kernel : ApparatusViabilityKernelCertified) : Prop :=
  exists coherence : IrreversibilityCoherence C horizon reach kernel,
    (reach.selfReachable or reach.externallyReachableOnly or kernel.kernelNonempty)
      and not IrreversibleCollapseInput C horizon reach kernel
```

`externallyReachableOnly` is the source text's "repair generator reachable from outside states only."
`IrreversibilityCoherence` encodes the source text's equivalence between repair-generator
unreachability and empty apparatus-level viability kernel for the same declared scope; inconsistent
host certificates are rejected rather than read as merely "not irreversible."

**External-review-gate addendum (v26 CHANGE-REQUEST fix): current-scope linkage.** The first Lean
mechanization tied `reach`/`kernel`'s own declared scopes only to EACH OTHER
(`sameDeclaredScope`), never to the theorem's own ambient `(C, horizon)` being classified — a
wrong-scope-but-mutually-consistent certificate pair could certify irreversibility for an unrelated
scope. `IrreversibilityCoherence` now takes `(C, horizon)` explicitly and requires
`currentScopeLinked`, matching the same scoping discipline already used for
`StatusedResidualAccrual`. A parallel finding in the Python toy-lab mirror — `collapse_budget_feasible`
compared a budget witness's `move_record` to itself (a tautology) instead of to the classified
candidate's own move record — was fixed the same review round; Lean's own dependently-typed
`CollapseBudgetWitness` was never vulnerable to this, only the Python mirror.
`RecoverableCollapseInput` is the default collapsed grade once irreversibility is not certified and a
declared reachability/kernel inventory still records some recovery surface. It is certified because
this file does not mechanize the forward-reachable-set computation. The toy lab must compute it
concretely from the finite repair-world graph.

### Subsidy, suspension, and revival

Subsidized systems are not collapsed, but they are not self-maintaining. The status is inherited from
the subsidizer's scope, so subsidy withdrawal can reclassify the system discontinuously.
E5 uses an external-only reading of subsidy: a subsidized branch is available only when the complete
self-scope rescue inventory contains no admissible in-budget descending move, while a separate
subsidizer-scope move supplies descent.

```text
SubsidizerScopeOwns(subsidizer, moveRecord) : Prop
SubsidizerBudgetFeasible(subsidizer, moveRecord) : Prop
SubsidizerMoveDescends(subsidizer, moveRecord, H, C, horizon) : Prop

structure SubsidizerRescueCandidate where
  subsidizer : ExternalCarrier
  moveRecord : CollapseMoveRecord
  carriedInSubsidizerScope : SubsidizerScopeOwns subsidizer moveRecord
  budgetFeasibleInSubsidizerScope :
    SubsidizerBudgetFeasible subsidizer moveRecord
  descends : SubsidizerMoveDescends subsidizer moveRecord H C horizon

structure ExternalSubsidyWitness where
  subsidyRecord : SubsidyRecord
  subsidyRecordCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt subsidyPolicy subsidyRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
  subsidizer : ExternalCarrier
  suppliedMove : SubsidizerRescueCandidate
  suppliedMoveOwnedBySubsidizer : suppliedMove.subsidizer = subsidizer
  suppliedMoveDescends : suppliedMove.descends
  suppliedMoveInSubsidizerScope :
    SubsidizerScopeOwns subsidizer suppliedMove.moveRecord
  notSelfCarried :
    not exists selfMove : CollapseRescueCandidate,
      CollapseRescueMoveRecord selfMove = suppliedMove.moveRecord

def ActiveExternalSubsidyAt(S,H,C,horizon) : Prop :=
  exists subsidy, ExternalSubsidyWitness(subsidy)

def NoActiveExternalSubsidyAt(S,H,C,horizon) : Prop :=
  not ActiveExternalSubsidyAt(S,H,C,horizon)

structure SubsidyWithdrawalEvent where
  priorSubsidyRecord : SubsidyRecord
  withdrawalRecord : SubsidyWithdrawalRecord
  carried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt subsidyWithdrawalPolicy withdrawalRecord
        n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
  recordLinks :
    withdrawalRecord.priorSubsidyRecord = priorSubsidyRecord
```

Suspension is not collapse. It is an apparatus-intact state with operations gated off:

```text
structure SuspensionWitness where
  suspensionRecord : SuspensionRecord
  suspensionRecordCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt suspensionPolicy suspensionRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
  apparatusIntact : ApparatusIntactCertified S H C horizon
  operationsGatedOff : OperationsGatedOffCertified S H C horizon
  noCurrentBindingOperation :
    not BindingOperationalChallengeActive S H C horizon
```

Revival is a post-subsidy or post-collapse reclosure with a new apparatus lineage. It must be tied to
both the prior collapsed/subsidized status record and the new E3-style reclosure witness:

```text
structure ApparatusLineageRecord where
  lineageId : Nat

structure RevivalWitness where
  priorStatusRef : ReclosureCollapseStatusRecordRef
  priorWasSubsidizedOrCollapsed :
    PriorStatusIsSubsidizedOrCollapsed priorStatusRef
  newLineage : ApparatusLineageRecord
  oldLineage : ApparatusLineageRecord
  newLineageCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt apparatusLineagePolicy newLineage
        n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
  oldLineageCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt apparatusLineagePolicy oldLineage
        n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
  lineageChanged : newLineage != oldLineage
  reclosure : ApparatusReclosureCandidate
  selfCarriedNow :
    ApparatusReclosureAdmissibleInBudget reclosure
```

The lineage change is load-bearing. Without it, "revived" would overlap ordinary viable reclosure.

## 2. Theorem

E5's theorem surface is verification-shaped. It does not derive viability-kernel emptiness,
reachability, subsidy, suspension, or rescue-move descent from weaker premises. Those are certified or
computed facts about a declared finite repair economy.

### Status type and carried status record

```text
inductive ReclosureCollapseStatus
  | revived
  | subsidized
  | suspended
  | viable
  | stressed
  | collapsed_recoverable
  | collapsed_irreversible

structure ReclosureCollapseStatusRecord where
  challengeClass : ChallengeClass
  horizon : Horizon
  status : ReclosureCollapseStatus
  residualRecord : Option ResidualStatusRecord
  rescueMoveRecord : Option CollapseMoveRecord
  subsidyRecord : Option SubsidyRecord
  suspensionRecord : Option SuspensionRecord
  priorStatusRecord : Option ReclosureCollapseStatusRecordRef
  lineageRecord : Option ApparatusLineageRecord
  reachabilityRecord : Option RepairGeneratorReachabilityRecord
  kernelRecord : Option ApparatusViabilityKernelRecord
  supportingLedgerEntries : List LedgerEntry
  supportingAuditRecords : List AuditRecord

def ReclosureCollapseStatusOccurrenceFor
    (statusPolicy : CarriedRecordPolicy S.T ReclosureCollapseStatusRecord)
    (record : ReclosureCollapseStatusRecord) : Prop :=
  record.challengeClass = C
    and record.horizon = horizon
    and exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt statusPolicy record n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
    and forall entry in record.supportingLedgerEntries,
      entry in S.Lambda_S.ledgerEntries
    and forall auditRecord in record.supportingAuditRecords,
      HasCarriedRecordEvidence S.auditRecordPolicy auditRecord
```

As in E3/E4/E11/E12, occurrence only credits the carried record. Branch cases below tie optional
fields to the concrete witnesses. The theorem statements must take a carried status-record witness
explicitly; it cannot be derived from collapse facts alone.

### Priority-normalized seven-way status cases

The priority order is:

```text
revived > subsidized > suspended > viable > stressed >
  collapsed_irreversible > collapsed_recoverable
```

Rationale:

1. `revived` is a transition fact: a new apparatus lineage after subsidy/collapse. It overrides the
   current viability reading, because it says this is not the same self-maintaining lineage.
2. `subsidized` is an active external-carrier fact. It overrides `viable`: probes may descend, but not
   by the system's own repair economy.
3. `suspended` is an activity-gating fact. It prevents mistaking "no current operation" for collapse.
4. `viable` and `stressed` share a non-collapsed core. `viable` has no statused residual accrual;
   `stressed` has statused residual accruing within budget.
5. `collapsed_recoverable` and `collapsed_irreversible` share the collapsed core
   (`NoCollapseFalsifier` over a `CompleteCollapseRescueInventory`) and split only on the
   repair-generator/apparatus-kernel reachability grade.
   Irreversible has priority over recoverable because a certified empty apparatus viability kernel
   is terminal for the declared scope.
6. `collapsed_recoverable` is the lowest-priority catch-all inside the collapsed core once
   irreversibility is not certified. It still requires `CollapsedCore` and a declared
   reachability/kernel inventory witnessing that some recovery surface remains; if neither
   recoverability nor irreversibility is certified, the occurrence-complete classifier should not be
   asserted for the scope.

```text
-- Every case predicate below is implicitly parameterized by S, H, C, horizon,
-- statusPolicy, and inventory : CompleteCollapseRescueInventory.

def RevivedEvidenceFor : Prop :=
  Nonempty (RevivalWitness S H C horizon descentAttribution)

def SubsidizedEvidenceFor : Prop :=
  exists subsidy : ExternalSubsidyWitness S H C horizon,
    not exists selfMove,
      selfMove in inventory.declaredCandidates
        and CollapseRescueAdmissibleInBudget selfMove
        and CollapseRescueDescends selfMove

def SuspendedEvidenceFor : Prop :=
  Nonempty (SuspensionWitness S H C horizon)

def ViableEvidenceFor : Prop :=
  exists move,
    move in inventory.declaredCandidates
      and CollapseRescueAdmissibleInBudget move
      and CollapseRescueDescends move
      and not exists residual,
        StatusedResidualAccrual S H C horizon residual

def ReclosureStressedEvidenceFor : Prop :=
  exists residual move,
    StatusedResidualAccrual S H C horizon residual
      and move in inventory.declaredCandidates
      and CollapseRescueAdmissibleInBudget move
      and CollapseRescueDescends move

def CollapsedIrreversibleEvidenceFor : Prop :=
  exists reach kernel,
    CollapsedCore S H C horizon inventory
      and IrreversibleCollapseInput C horizon reach kernel

RevivedEvidence(record, revival) :=
  RevivalWitness(revival)
    and record.status = revived
    and record.priorStatusRecord = some revival.priorStatusRef
    and record.lineageRecord = some revival.newLineage
    and record.rescueMoveRecord = some revival.reclosure.moveRecord

RevivedCase(record) :=
  ReclosureCollapseStatusOccurrenceFor statusPolicy record
    and exists revival, RevivedEvidence(record, revival)

SubsidizedEvidence(record, subsidy) :=
  ExternalSubsidyWitness(subsidy)
    and not exists selfMove,
      selfMove in inventory.declaredCandidates
        and CollapseRescueAdmissibleInBudget selfMove
        and CollapseRescueDescends selfMove
    and record.status = subsidized
    and record.subsidyRecord = some subsidy.subsidyRecord

SubsidizedCase(record) :=
  not RevivedEvidenceFor
    and ReclosureCollapseStatusOccurrenceFor statusPolicy record
    and exists subsidy, SubsidizedEvidence(record, subsidy)

SuspendedEvidence(record, suspension) :=
  SuspensionWitness(suspension)
    and record.status = suspended
    and record.suspensionRecord = some suspension.suspensionRecord

SuspendedCase(record) :=
  not RevivedEvidenceFor
    and not SubsidizedEvidenceFor
    and ReclosureCollapseStatusOccurrenceFor statusPolicy record
    and exists suspension, SuspendedEvidence(record, suspension)

ViableEvidence(record, move) :=
  move in inventory.declaredCandidates
    and CollapseRescueAdmissibleInBudget move
    and CollapseRescueDescends move
    and not exists residual, StatusedResidualAccrual S H C horizon residual
    and record.status = viable
    and record.rescueMoveRecord = some (CollapseRescueMoveRecord move)

ViableCase(record) :=
  not RevivedEvidenceFor
    and not SubsidizedEvidenceFor
    and not SuspendedEvidenceFor
    and ReclosureCollapseStatusOccurrenceFor statusPolicy record
    and exists move, ViableEvidence(record, move)

-- Lean-mechanization note: E1Internalization.lean already defines top-level
-- StressedEvidence/StressedCase/StressedHolds in the same flat SixBirdsFoundationsV
-- namespace, so the Lean mechanization renames E5's own versions to
-- ReclosureStressedEvidence/ReclosureStressedCase/ReclosureStressedHolds. The
-- `stressed` status constructor name is unaffected.
ReclosureStressedEvidence(record, residual, move) :=
  StatusedResidualAccrual S H C horizon residual
    and move in inventory.declaredCandidates
    and CollapseRescueAdmissibleInBudget move
    and CollapseRescueDescends move
    and record.status = stressed
    and record.residualRecord = some residual.residualRecord
    and record.rescueMoveRecord = some (CollapseRescueMoveRecord move)

ReclosureStressedCase(record) :=
  not RevivedEvidenceFor
    and not SubsidizedEvidenceFor
    and not SuspendedEvidenceFor
    and not ViableEvidenceFor
    and ReclosureCollapseStatusOccurrenceFor statusPolicy record
    and exists residual move,
      ReclosureStressedEvidence(record, residual, move)

CollapsedIrreversibleEvidence(record, reach, kernel) :=
  CollapsedCore S H C horizon inventory
    and IrreversibleCollapseInput C horizon reach kernel
    and record.status = collapsed_irreversible
    and record.reachabilityRecord = some reach.reachabilityRecord
    and record.kernelRecord = some kernel.kernelRecord
    and record.rescueMoveRecord = none

CollapsedIrreversibleCase(record) :=
  not RevivedEvidenceFor
    and not SubsidizedEvidenceFor
    and not SuspendedEvidenceFor
    and not ViableEvidenceFor
    and not ReclosureStressedEvidenceFor
    and ReclosureCollapseStatusOccurrenceFor statusPolicy record
    and exists reach kernel,
      CollapsedIrreversibleEvidence(record, reach, kernel)

CollapsedRecoverableEvidence(record, reach, kernel) :=
  CollapsedCore S H C horizon inventory
    and RecoverableCollapseInput C horizon reach kernel
    and record.status = collapsed_recoverable
    and record.reachabilityRecord = some reach.reachabilityRecord
    and record.kernelRecord = some kernel.kernelRecord
    and record.rescueMoveRecord = none

CollapsedRecoverableCase(record) :=
  not RevivedEvidenceFor
    and not SubsidizedEvidenceFor
    and not SuspendedEvidenceFor
    and not ViableEvidenceFor
    and not ReclosureStressedEvidenceFor
    and not CollapsedIrreversibleEvidenceFor
    and ReclosureCollapseStatusOccurrenceFor statusPolicy record
    and exists reach kernel,
      CollapsedRecoverableEvidence(record, reach, kernel)
```

**Design note:** `stressed` is the lowest non-collapsed branch, not a collapse grade. It shares the
positive rescue core with `viable` and splits on statused residual accrual. The two collapsed branches
share `CollapsedCore` and split on certified reachability/kernel evidence. If the host cannot provide
either recoverability or irreversibility evidence, the classifier is incomplete; E5 does not silently
invent a status.

Every priority negation is claim-scoped and record-independent: it tests the
corresponding `...EvidenceFor` predicate, never a sibling case on the same
status-tagged record. Some exclusions are defense-in-depth rather than
load-bearing. `SubsidizedEvidence` already
excludes a self-scope in-budget descending move from the complete inventory, so subsidized cannot
substantively overlap viable/stressed; the explicit priority order keeps the status record shape
readable. `ViableCase` and `ReclosureStressedCase` are substantively disjoint
because `ViableEvidence` negates scoped residual accrual and
`ReclosureStressedEvidence` requires it. The load-bearing priority exclusions are:
`revived` over every ordinary current-state reading, `suspended` over activity-based readings, and
`collapsed_irreversible` over `collapsed_recoverable`. The last one is essential because
`collapsed_recoverable` is the lower-priority collapsed grade once
`CollapsedIrreversibleEvidenceFor` is not certified.

This records the source text's discontinuity claim under the intended "the subsidy relationship is

`subsidized`. Withdrawal does not imply irreversible collapse by itself; it forces reclassification
under the post-withdrawal complete classifier.

## 3. Proof Spine

1. **Declare the repair-economy scope.** Fix `S`, `H`, `C`, `horizon`, declared viability probes,
   carried status policy, a `CompleteCollapseRescueInventory`, and complete status records.
   Rescue-inventory completeness is a host/classifier hypothesis; E5 does not discover all possible
   moves by logic alone, and it never treats omission from the modeled list as collapse evidence.

2. **Classify admissible in-budget rescue moves.** Each candidate is one of four concrete types:
   E1 repair occurrence, certified boundary update, E3 apparatus reclosure, or D6/E6+E9 acquisition.
   In every case, the candidate owns its budget witness and its move-specific descent certificate.

3. **Apply the constructive falsifier.** If any named candidate in the complete self-scope inventory is
   carried, admissible, in-budget, and descends the declared probes, `CollapsedCore` is false. This is
   the main anti-smuggling guard: collapse is not a vibe about failure; it is the nonexistence of a
   named positive witness in a declared complete inventory.

4. **Separate non-collapsed statuses.**
   - `revived` fires first when a prior subsidized/collapsed status record is linked to a new
     apparatus lineage and a self-carried E3 reclosure witness.
   - `subsidized` fires when no self-scope rescue in the complete inventory descends and descent comes
     from a separate subsidizer-scope move.
   - `suspended` fires when the apparatus is intact but operations are gated off.
   - `viable` fires when a self-carried in-budget descent move exists and no statused residual accrues.
   - `stressed` fires when a self-carried in-budget descent move exists but statused residual accrues
     within budget.

5. **Grade collapsed statuses.** Both collapsed branches require `CollapsedCore`. They split on
   certified repair-generator/apparatus-kernel evidence: `collapsed_irreversible` requires generator
   unreachable and apparatus-level kernel empty; `collapsed_recoverable` is the lower-priority
   collapsed grade when irreversibility is not certified and the declared inventory still records a
   self-reachable, externally reachable, or nonempty-kernel recovery surface. The two share the same
   collapse core and differ only in the reachability grade.

6. **Prove exactly-one from the carried classifier.** `CompleteReclosureCollapseStatus` supplies one
   branch-valid carried status record and uniqueness for all genuine status occurrences at the same
   `(S,H,C,horizon)`. The record-independent evidence exclusions prevent
   higher-priority overlaps; status uniqueness excludes two records
   with different tags.

7. **Treat kernel emptiness as certified input.** The Agents/F19 viability-kernel fixed point is not
   locally mechanized. The toy-lab may compute a concrete finite kernel, but Lean should not claim a
   universal kernel construction until the F19/Agents machinery is vendored or mechanized.

## 4. Case Enumeration

1. **Viable self-repair.** A carried E1 repair occurrence exists, is in budget, and owns a descent
   certificate for the declared viability probes. No statused residual accrues. Status: `viable`.

2. **Viable apparatus reclosure.** A carried E3 `MaintenanceReinstatementFor` witness reinstates the
   closure apparatus, is in budget, and descends the probes. Status: `viable` unless there is statused
   residual accrual, in which case `stressed`.

3. **Viable acquisition.** A D6/E6+E9 `LawfulAcquisition`/`CandidateAcquisition` with
   `BudgetFeasible` adds a strict probe/resource and descends the viability probes. Status: `viable`
   or `stressed` depending on residual accrual.

4. **Stressed but not collapsed.** Statused residual accrues within budget, and at least one
   self-carried in-budget rescue move still descends the probes. Status: `stressed`, not collapsed.

5. **Subsidized repair.** No self-scope rescue in the complete inventory descends, but an external
   carrier supplies a separate subsidizer-scope repair/reclosure/acquisition. Status: `subsidized`;
   collapse is not credited, but self-maintenance is also not credited.

6. **Subsidy withdrawal.** A prior `subsidized` record is withdrawn. If no active external subsidy
   exists at the post-withdrawal horizon, the post-withdrawal status is recomputed from the same
   evidence-priority classifier over the non-subsidized branches. The result may be `viable`, `stressed`,
   `collapsed_recoverable`, `collapsed_irreversible`, `suspended`, or `revived`; it is not
   automatically irreversible. If a new or renewed external subsidy is active, the theorem does not
   rule out `subsidized`.

7. **Suspension.** The apparatus is intact and operations are gated off. No active repair-economy
   failure is inferred from inactivity. Status: `suspended`.

8. **Collapsed recoverable.** No self-carried in-budget move descends the probes, irreversibility is
   not certified, and the declared reachability/kernel inventory still records a recovery surface
   (self-reachable, externally reachable, or nonempty apparatus-level kernel). Status:
   `collapsed_recoverable`.

9. **Collapsed irreversible.** No self-carried in-budget move descends the probes, the repair
   generator is unreachable, and the apparatus-level viability kernel is certified empty. Status:
   `collapsed_irreversible`.

10. **Revived lineage.** A prior collapsed/subsidized record is linked to a new apparatus lineage, and
    a self-carried E3 reclosure witness now holds. Status: `revived`, priority above ordinary viability.

11. **Over-budget descent.** A carried move would descend the probes but its spend exceeds the declared
    budget. It does not falsify collapse; it is a named failed rescue candidate.

12. **Uncarried or fallback rescue.** A repair/reclosure/acquisition-shaped object exists, but its
    source tag is fallback/unknown/out-of-scope/generated outside `S`, or it is missing from the
    carried ledger/audit inventory. It does not falsify collapse and may support `subsidized` if an
    external carrier owns it.

13. **Off-kernel boundary or reclosure.** The record names plausible pre/post apparatus or boundary
    data, but `S.T.suppK sourceState targetState` fails. The rescue candidate is rejected.

14. **Unlinked descent certificate.** A descent certificate names a different `CollapseMoveRecord` than
    the carried rescue candidate. The case predicate rejects it; this is the E5 analogue of the E4 v24
    candidate-quotient bug.

15. **Active collapsed system.** The system continues ordinary transitions or low-level activity, but
    no admissible in-budget carried rescue move descends the probes. Collapse can hold despite activity.

16. **Dormant non-collapsed system.** No operation is currently active, but suspension evidence shows
    apparatus intact and operations gated. Status: `suspended`, not collapsed.

17. **Future sweep guard.** The toy-lab must compute rescue descent, budget feasibility, complete
    rescue-inventory membership, generator reachability, viability-kernel emptiness, subsidy/revival
    lineage, and status classification from carried records and finite transitions. It must not
    hardcode the seven statuses or pass/fail rows.

## 5. Interaction Status

- **D3 carried records.** Every rescue candidate, descent readout, budget witness, subsidy record,
  reachability/kernel record, lineage record, and status record is credited only through
  `CarriedRecordAt`/`HasCarriedRecordEvidence`-style occurrence data with admissible source tags,
  `generatedByS=true`, and `inScope=true`.

- **D4 E-system and ledger.** E5 is a law over `S.T`, `S.R_S`, `S.AdmissibleMove`, `S.Lambda_S`, and
  `S.T.suppK`. It does not introduce a parallel repair kernel or budget ledger.

- **E1 internalization.** E5 reuses `Delta` and `ViabilityProbesDescendAtH` directly. E1's
  `EndogenousRepairOccurrence` is the repair-rescue branch. E5 is broader than E1 because collapse can
  be defeated by boundary updates, E3 apparatus reclosure, or E9 acquisition, not only by a repair join.

- **E3 self-maintaining reclosure.** E3's `ClosureApparatus`/`ClosureMaintenanceOperator`/
  `MaintenanceReinstatementRecord`/`MaintenanceReinstatementFor` are directly reusable for E5's
  apparatus reclosure `E'` move. E3's "reclosure" is a component of E5's rescue/falsifier surface; E5's
  "reclosure collapse" is the higher-level status law about failure or recovery of the repair economy.

- **D6 and E6+E9 priced access.** E5's acquisition branch should reuse D6 `LawfulAcquisition` and
  E6+E9 `ExposureBudgetWitness`/`BudgetFeasible`/`CandidateAcquisition`. A same-family or over-budget
  acquisition cannot defeat collapse.

- **E4 repair compilation.** E4 is not a direct dependency of E5's core statement. A compiled repair may
  be one way a future instance supplies a repair rescue candidate, but E5 does not need E4 to define
  collapse. The E4 v24 lesson is imported methodologically: descent/collapse evidence must be owned by
  the candidate record, not passed as a free parameter.

- **E7 alarm law.** E7's boundary-nonclosure branch is a forward reference to E5 onset. E5 supplies the
  collapse/reclosure status vocabulary that should later discharge `ForwardObligation(E5_boundary_nonclosure_correspondence)`.
  E7's alarm currentization is not itself an E5 boundary update unless the boundary-update witness is
  carried and tied to E5's descent certificate.

- **E12 individuation.** E12 directly reused E3 for self-maintained boundaries and treated Agents-paper
  viability-kernel data as certified input. E5 follows the same certified-kernel discipline but asks a
  different question: whether the repair economy for a declared challenge class has collapsed.

- **F19 and Agents-paper viability kernels.** F19 dissolution/identity and the Agents-paper
  `K = gfp V` viability kernel are certified host input. The toy-lab may compute finite kernels, but
  Lean will not mechanize F19/Agents fixed-point theory locally in this law.

- **Closure-Reality Boundary F53.** E5 is adjacent to F53 and F19 but does not reduce to them.
  It defines collapse as self-repair failure, with subsidy and reversibility grades.

- **Certified-input / governance boundary guard.** E5 proves the normal form under honest declared
  probe families, complete rescue-candidate inventories, honest budget/readout/reachability/kernel
  certificates, and honest carried status records. It does not prove global bankruptcy, institutional
  receivership, or cognitive-collapse governance facts without those inputs.

## 6. Nonclaims

- E5 does not mechanize F19's viability-kernel fixed point, dissolution obstruction, or identity-rewrite
  statuses. `RepairGeneratorReachabilityCertified` and `ApparatusViabilityKernelCertified` are certified
  host inputs until that machinery is imported or developed locally.

- E5 does not derive collapse from activity level. Active systems can be collapsed; dormant systems can
  be suspended rather than collapsed.

- E5 does not say every failed viability descent is irreversible. Irreversibility additionally requires
  repair-generator unreachability and apparatus-level kernel emptiness.

- E5 does not say subsidized systems are collapsed. They are not self-maintaining, but their status is
  inherited from the subsidizer's scope until subsidy withdrawal.

- E5 does not say subsidy withdrawal itself proves collapse. It triggers reclassification under the
  post-withdrawal complete classifier.

- E5 does not treat an over-budget move as a falsifier. The source text explicitly allows failure by
  exceeding `Lambda_S`.

- E5 does not credit boundary updates through a vague boundary label. A boundary-update rescue must own
  carried pre/post boundary records, kernel realization, budget feasibility, and a linked descent
  certificate.

- E5 does not conflate E3's maintained closure status with E5 revival. Revival requires a prior
  subsidized/collapsed status record and a new apparatus lineage; ordinary E3 reclosure without lineage
  change is a rescue candidate, not automatically `revived`.

- E5 does not import E4's compilation theorem. Compiled repairs may instantiate repair candidates in a
  future bridge, but E5's collapse normal form only needs carried rescue moves and descent certificates.

- E5 does not allow a caller-provided list of "missing moves" to establish collapse. Collapse requires
  a complete declared rescue-candidate inventory and the absence of a named admissible in-budget descent
  witness across all four move families.

- E5 does not claim the future toy-lab phase diagram can use floats, random thresholds, or disconnected
  flags. Noise, repair cost, budget, residual accrual, and kernel curves must be exact-rational and
  computed from the finite repair-world fixture.
