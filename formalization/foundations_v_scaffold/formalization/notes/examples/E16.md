# E16. Adaptability Law

This is the six-field normal-form draft for E16, the Adaptability Law.  The
summary below is an explicit paraphrase of the source entry at
`THEOREMS.md:430-445`, in Cluster D ("Laws of Carried Memory"; heading at
`THEOREMS.md:390`).  The intended Lean target is
`lean/SixBirdsFoundationsV/Laws/E16Adaptability.lean`.

E16 is a single-`ESystem` law about route-dependent repair capacity hidden
below a fixed current practice.  Two admissible routes start from one declared
comparison base and finish at histories in the same current quotient, yet a
declared held-out challenge can distinguish their future discharge
distributions.  That raw predictive residue is adaptability only after it
survives the full control battery: it is not a support/protocol artifact, is
not removed by an admissible completion, is not made current-visible by an
admissible same-support refinement, and is not erased by the system's own
admissible continuations.  Currentizable residue is classified as legible
slack, not adaptability.

The abstract quotient spine is not new E16 mathematics.  The canonical Lean
core from *Holonomy with Memory* is vendored wholesale, line-preserving,
from `six-birds-transport/lean/HolonomyMemory/` to
`lean/vendor/foundations/holonomy-memory/HolonomyMemory/`.  The `lean/lakefile.toml`
registers a `HolonomyMemory` `lean_lib` whose `srcDir` is
`vendor/foundations/holonomy-memory`, matching the direct-library convention
used for `Xi` at `lean/lakefile.toml:12-18`.  No upstream file is to be edited.
The nine upstream modules are `Basic`, `Interfaces`, `Equivalences`,
`Transport`, `Sufficiency`, `Witnesses`, `Loops`, `Asymmetry`, and
`Instances`; the root aggregator imports all nine
(`HolonomyMemory.lean:1-9`).  E16 imports `HolonomyMemory.Asymmetry` and
`HolonomyMemory.Witnesses` directly.  Mirroring the Xi convention means
copying the module tree, not nesting an independent Lake project: the upstream
manifest is empty and its `lakefile.toml` has no `[[require]]`, so this repo's
root `lean/lakefile.toml` owns the new library registration and the existing
root `lean/lake-manifest.json` gains no package dependency.

References below to `Holonomy paper:<line>` mean
`six-birds-papers/Tsiokos_2026_Holonomy_with_Memory_in_Six_Birds_Theory_Predictive_Quotients_Witnesses_and_Fixed_Support_Route_Transport.tex:<line>`.

One API difference must remain visible.  The paper's route-transport package
includes a fixed support object (`Holonomy paper:139-164`), but the canonical
Lean `RouteTransportCore` begins with `Interface` and contains no `Support`
field (`HolonomyMemory/Interfaces.lean:8-28`).  E16 therefore wraps the
unchanged core in a carried `DeclaredRouteTransportPackage` with one explicit
comparison base.  This is not a replacement quotient theory.  It is the
minimum local data needed to express the paper's support-fixation control over
the actual vendored core.

`LANDING_PLAN.md:472` places E16 in free chain E with D1-D6 plus F3 and
Holonomy as grounding.  F3's inventory entry is `verified_correct` and cites
the same current/predictive quotient source (`imported_foundations.yml:42-55`).
Unlike E14 and E15, E16 can use the source's actual Lean quotient, predictive
witness, strict-refinement, and loop-asymmetry theorems.  The benchmark
taxonomy is not present in that Lean core; E16 owns the repair-ledger-specific
status and control vocabulary below.

The direct Holonomy reuse is exact:

- `RouteTransportCore` supplies typed interfaces, histories, continuations,
  events, identity/composition, push, observation, and their laws
  (`HolonomyMemory/Interfaces.lean:8-28`).
- `CurrentEventEquiv` and `FuturePredictiveEquiv` are respectively "same now"
  and "same after every admissible continuation/event"
  (`HolonomyMemory/Equivalences.lean:5-9,35-41`).
- `FlatAt` characterizes the interfaces where current equivalence already
  determines every future observation, equivalently where current equivalence
  is stable under every outgoing push
  (`HolonomyMemory/Equivalences.lean:76-94`).  The general core does not expose
  a current-quotient transport outside this flatness condition.
- `CurrentQuotient`, `PredictiveQuotient`, `predictiveTransport`, and
  `predictiveToCurrent` are the actual quotient constructions; cross-interface
  current observation is provided by `predictiveToCurrentTransport`, with the
  commutation triangle
  `predictiveToCurrent_after_predictiveTransport`
  (`HolonomyMemory/Transport.lean:5-23,25-69,71-91`).
- `PredictiveWitness` directly packages current equivalence plus failure of
  future equivalence, and is equivalent to strict refinement
  (`HolonomyMemory/Witnesses.lean:5-15,31-66`).
- `Loop` and `predictiveLoopAction` are the source's predictive
  endo-continuation apparatus (`HolonomyMemory/Loops.lean:5-20`).
  `CurrentLoopTrivial` is stated at representative level as current equivalence
  between each pushed history and its source; `LoopAsymmetry` and
  `loopAsymmetry_exhibits_movedPredictive_fixedCurrent` expose the exact
  current-fixed/predictive-moved shape (`HolonomyMemory/Asymmetry.lean:5-15,
  17-39`).
- `HolonomyMemory.Wheel` supplies the finite memory-wheel core, its genuine
  `PredictiveWitness`, strict refinement, loop asymmetry, and moved-predictive/
  fixed-current theorem (`HolonomyMemory/Instances.lean:6-124`).  This is the
  canonical Lean precedent for E16's flagship coherent-candidate fixture.  A
  Round A/B fixture may mirror its finite construction, but must still compute
  E16's own carried route, ledger, challenge, and control data natively rather
  than treating the vendored instance as an oracle.

The paper's controls are computational, not part of those Lean files.  The
paper defines support fixation, flattening, currentization, and bounded
perturbation survival at `Holonomy paper:520-529`; its dissipative benchmark
adds loss under an endogenous continuation (`:594-609`).  E16 therefore
mechanizes local carried evidence for those controls rather than citing an
unavailable theorem.

```lean
def E16Carried
    {Record : Type u} (policy : CarriedRecordPolicy S.T Record)
    (record : Record) : Prop :=
```

`CarriedRecordAt` receives the policy, record, trajectory endpoint, source tag,
and two source booleans (`ESystem.lean:40-50`).  `CarriedSource` receives only

The protocol and challenge family are shared by full-record equality.  The
trial inventory supplies actual D4 `S.RepairStep` observations.  There is no
stored discharge Boolean: a trial names a concrete before/after ledger effect,
and the shared comparator below decides whether that effect records discharge.
The eligible population is not a free predicate stored by each distribution:
every route and continuation-endpoint distribution specializes the one shared
`trialEligibleForRouteEndpoint` comparator at its own `routeEndpoint`
(`E16Adaptability.lean:163-205,223-290`).
The probability comparator receives the entire distribution, route endpoint,
protocol, complete trial inventory, and selected probability record; it checks
the exact successful/eligible ratio.  A
difference on an undeclared class cannot enter either complete distribution.
All probability and ledger-support records are carried under policies below.
The distribution is not a free map from route IDs to desired answers.

theorem can derive. `AdaptabilityCoreContext` owns route/trial/ledger/budget

that negative calls E15's actual context.  The E16 context does not contain a
status selector or a `coherent : Bool`.  Round B must implement every field
from one shared finite context and mutate every comparator against concrete
data.

### Shared carried-record policies

E16 declares `CarriedRecordPolicy` values for every package, comparison base,
route pair, challenge family, protocol, discharge trial, probability,
distribution, control counterpart, perturbation bound/trial, reconciliation
bridge, and status record.
Every positive evidence structure uses the existential `E16Carried S policy
record` helper defined above.  Ledger entries use the ledger's actual policy
and `ledgerCarried` proof (`ESystem.lean:72-83`), rather than a nonexistent
field on `S.Lambda_S`.

List membership and IDs never replace `E16Carried`.  Complete inventories
below require soundness, coverage, record `Nodup`, and ID `Nodup` separately.

This is the operational interpretation of

no measured repair-capacity difference is not yet an E16 candidate; a measured
difference without the vendored predictive witness is likewise insufficient.

This directly resolves E15's order-20 forward note
(`interaction_matrix.md:253`).  E16 imports E15's actual
`RouteResidueDebtRecord` (`E15OfflineReclosure.lean:60-66`) rather than
inventing an E15-like label.  The same full residue cannot be both "awaiting
reconciliation" and coherent adaptability at the same observation time.
Eligibility may begin only after a carried reconciliation bridge records the
transition.  E16 does not rewrite an earlier E15 debt classification or infer
reconciliation from elapsed time.  The negative is E15's actual context field
(`E15OfflineReclosure.lean:255-271`), not an E16 comparator with the same
English gloss; the reused residue also satisfies E15's own carried policy, as
required by `F3RouteResidueDebtItem.residueCarried` (`:458-475`).
`sourceClaimLinked` additionally requires the residue's E15 reconciliation claim to equal the E16 candidate claim.
The candidate ID remains an inventory key only: package, dependent route pair,
and full capacity-difference records are independently linked and are consumed
by the bridge-acceptance comparator.

An eligible in-bound trial satisfies the computed inequality
`trial.perturbationMagnitude <= bound.maximumMagnitude`.  Bound membership is
not a proposition stored on each trial.  The classifier-level
`perturbationTrialEligible` predicate declares the authoritative trial
universe, so completeness ranges over declared records rather than every
well-typed record.  The axiom-footprint-checked constructor
`emptyCompletePerturbationTrialInventory` builds the empty inventory when that
declared universe is empty, demonstrating non-vacuity without weakening
coverage or soundness (`E16Adaptability.lean:771-776,912-986`).

Freedom from a control is never proved by omitting its clearing counterpart.
The eligible universe is declared in the shared context, then completely
enumerated.  `singleValuedPerDeclaredKey` prevents contradictory duplicate
counterparts with different outcomes.

blocks coherent certification, but it is not itself dissipation
(`Holonomy paper:529`).

### Adjudicated design choices

1. **The support wrapper is local but the quotients are not.**  The canonical
   Lean core omits the paper's support field.  E16 adds only the carried base
   and package wrapper needed for support fixation; all equivalence, quotient,
   witness, transport, and loop definitions remain the vendored ones.

   The formal support-confound claim is deliberately limited to disagreement
   in declared visible support between an eligible carried comparison and the
   claim package.  The paper's support check compares declared labels
   (`Holonomy paper:520-524`); it cannot prove that no genuinely undeclared
   state variable exists.  Such a proxy is an empirical governance failure
   outside the positive theorem, not a mechanically detectable E16 status.

2. **Predictive witness and measured capacity are separate.**  The vendored
   witness proves a future-observable difference.  E16 additionally requires
   that a declared repair-discharge probability is the differing observable,
   computed from carried repair-ledger support under one protocol.

3. **The four controls are local theorem content.**  The paper's Lean appendix
   does not formalize its benchmark taxonomy.  Treating the controls as one
   certified host flag would evade E16's main burden, so E16 exposes complete
   counterpart inventories and one structure per control.

4. **Robustness is bounded but not dissipation.**  The fourth freedom record includes the system's own
   complete continuation inventory and the preregistered deterministic
   perturbation bundle.  It does not quantify over arbitrary possible model
   changes, matching the paper's caveat (`Holonomy paper:653-656`).  A failed
   carried in-bound perturbation blocks coherence but does not establish the
   separately typed continuation required by `dissipative`.

5. **E15 debt and E16 adaptability are time-scoped alternatives.**  The exact
   E15 residue type is reused.  A carried reconciliation bridge plus the
   negative check against E15's actual classifier context is necessary before
   E16 classification.
   This is a certified boundary because E15 does not expose a global theorem
   deciding future reconciliation, but the bridge data and time links are
   local and auditable.

6. **No probability fitting occurs inside the status classifier.**  The
   finite laboratory computes exact rational probabilities from declared
   challenge trials and ledger support.  Lean verifies the linked records and
   shared comparator boundary; it does not infer empirical probabilities from
   quotient inequality alone.

7. **Rejection is evidence-backed administration, not a seventh source regime.**  The six
   Holonomy regimes remain flat, artifact, flattenable, currentizable,
   dissipative, and coherent candidate.  `support_confound` is the declared-
   base guard and `adaptability_rejected` is the complete classifier's
   lowest administrative branch.  It is not reachable by bare elimination:
   it requires either a claim-linked bounded-perturbation failure or a carried
   claim attempt with a concrete verified declaration/admissibility/budget
   failure.  The draft deliberately rejects a pure "none of the above" arm.

### Claim-scoped carried status apparatus

### Record-independent priority evidence

The paper supplies the taxonomy content but does not prescribe E16's global
priority order.  E16 adjudicates the order as a house design choice: support
confound, artifact, flat, flattenable, currentizable, dissipative, coherent,
then the evidence-backed administrative `adaptability_rejected` branch.  That
branch is not a Holonomy regime and is not reachable by elimination alone.
This preserves the paper's control semantics while making collisions
deterministic.  Every `...EvidenceFor`, including rejection, is independent of
a status record.

### Holds wrappers and completeness

Define eight wrappers in the established form:

```lean
def CoherentAdaptabilityHolds ... claimRef : Prop :=
  exists record : AdaptabilityStatusRecord ...,
    AdaptabilityStatusOccurrenceFor statusPolicy claimRef record and
    CoherentAdaptabilityCase ... claimRef record
```

`SupportConfoundHolds`, `ArtifactHolds`, `FlatHolds`, `FlattenableHolds`,
`CurrentizableSlackHolds`, `DissipativeHolds`, and
`AdaptabilityRejectedHolds` have the same carried, occurrence-matching shape.

```lean
structure CompleteAdaptabilityStatus ... (claimRef : AdaptabilityClaimRef ...)
    where
  branchValid :
    SupportConfoundHolds ... claimRef or ArtifactHolds ... claimRef or
    FlatHolds ... claimRef or FlattenableHolds ... claimRef or
    CurrentizableSlackHolds ... claimRef or DissipativeHolds ... claimRef or
    CoherentAdaptabilityHolds ... claimRef or
    AdaptabilityRejectedHolds ... claimRef
  statusUnique :
    forall record1 record2,
      AdaptabilityStatusOccurrenceFor statusPolicy claimRef record1 ->
      AdaptabilityStatusOccurrenceFor statusPolicy claimRef record2 ->
      record1.status = record2.status
```

Completeness is per full claim.  It does not force two held-out challenge
classes or observation times to share status.  Its rejection disjunct expands
through `AdaptabilityRejectedCase` to
`AdaptabilityRejectedEvidenceFor`; completeness cannot be discharged by seven
negations plus a status tag.

## 3. Proof Spine

1. **Fix one carried comparison base.**  Construct one
   `DeclaredRouteTransportPackage`, carry its base/package/pair under D3, and
   verify `routePackageDeclared` and `routePairAdmissible`.  Both routes live
   in this one package; support equality is structural, not ID equality.

2. **Push both routes with the vendored core.**  Use the package's `push` and
   each route's linkage fields.  The source history and typed target interface
   are shared, so the endpoint comparison is well formed.

3. **Establish same current practice directly.**  Supply
   `CurrentEventEquiv` for the two endpoints and use `Quotient.sound` to obtain
   equality in `CurrentQuotient`.  No E16-specific current quotient is
   introduced.

4. **Establish genuine predictive residue.**  Link a real vendored
   `PredictiveWitness` to those exact endpoints.  Its
   `distinct_predictive_classes_same_current_class` theorem yields distinct
   predictive classes in one current fiber
   (`HolonomyMemory/Witnesses.lean:17-29`).  If the
   routes are future equivalent, the `flat` branch fires instead.

5. **Link residue to repair capacity.**  Carry one held-out family and shared
   measurement protocol, one `ProbeEconomy`, one move, and one binding
   `ExposureBudgetWitness`.  Complete both route-conditioned D4 trial
   inventories over exactly that family.  For each class, derive trial success
   from the concrete lawful repair/ledger effect and compute the exact
   successful/eligible ratio.  Then locate the same declared class in each
   complete carried distribution and prove the rational difference.

6. **Close the E15 double-use boundary.**  Link the candidate to the exact E15
   `RouteResidueDebtRecord`, require it under E15's route-residue carried
   policy, carry a reconciliation bridge, and negate the actual
   `e15ctx.f3RouteResidueAwaitingReconciliation` predicate at the claim's
   observation time.  An outstanding residue cannot proceed to coherent
   evidence.

7. **Run support/protocol control.**  Complete the eligible honest-protocol
   inventory.  Every counterpart links its stored base to its package's actual
   base.  A mismatch in declared visible support is `support_confound`; a
   same-base honest counterpart clearing the difference is `artifact`.
   Freedom requires every eligible counterpart to be present and none to
   clear.  A truly undeclared variable is outside this mechanical guarantee.

8. **Run completion control.**  Complete the admissible completion inventory.
   If a same-base completion collapses witness/discrepancy, classify
   `flattenable`.  Otherwise retain `FlatteningFreedomEvidence`.

9. **Run currentization control.**  Complete the admissible same-support
   refinement inventory.  A refined counterpart with zero witnesses, zero
   discrepancy, and singleton predictive-to-current fibers is
   `currentizable_slack`.  This is a current-visible capacity, not coherent
   hidden adaptability.

10. **Run continuation and perturbation controls.**  Enumerate every declared
    own continuation and deterministic perturbation trial, with one carried
    bound declared before the claim.  A continuation erasing the route-
    conditioned capacity difference is `dissipative`.  Failure under a trial
    whose magnitude is computed to be within the bound is separate robustness
    evidence: it blocks coherence but supplies no continuation witness, so it
    reaches `adaptability_rejected` when no substantive regime applies.

11. **Conclude coherent adaptability only from the full conjunction.**  Build
    `CoherentAdaptabilityEvidence` from the raw candidate and four independent
    freedom records.  Current audit blindness projects the candidate's
    `CurrentEventEquiv`; route manufacture projects the declared distribution
    inequality.  Neither conclusion is reconstructed from a status name.

12. **Normalize by raw evidence.**  Apply the eight cases in order.  Every
    lower case negates record-independent `...EvidenceFor` predicates.
    `CompleteAdaptabilityStatus.statusUnique` rules out two carried statuses
    for the same full claim and proves the eight-way `ExactlyOne` theorem.

13. **Specialize the loop anchor without overgeneralizing it.**  For a
    memory-wheel-style route pair, apply the vendored loop-asymmetry theorem.
    This supplies the current-fixed/predictive-moved quotient statement; E16's
    local control evidence is still required for coherent classification.

## 4. Case Enumeration

1. **Coherent adaptability.**  One fixed-support route pair is current
   equivalent, has a linked predictive witness and a declared held-out repair
   distribution difference, is reconciled out of E15 debt, and survives all
   four controls.  Status: `coherent_adaptability`.

2. **Memory-wheel coherent candidate.**  The route pair is identity versus a
   loop with trivial current action and nontrivial predictive action; no
   designated completion/refinement/continuation clears the repair-capacity
   effect.  It may instantiate item 1, but the benchmark alone is not a
   universal existence theorem.

3. **Support confound.**  The compared route packages use different declared
   comparison-base records or a control counterpart changes visible support.
   Status: `support_confound`, regardless of an observed distribution gap.

4. **Undeclared-state proxy limitation.**  A route label correlates with an
   actually undeclared state variable while all declared visible support
   labels match.  The paper's fixed-support comparator cannot detect this
   condition, so E16 does not claim that `SupportConfoundEvidence` fires.
   Instead the instance lies outside positive certification until the variable
   is added to the declared comparison base and the controls are rerun.  The
   formal `support_confound` status is restricted to changed declared support.

5. **Protocol artifact trap.**  A same-base honest protocol counterpart clears
   the apparent predictive/discharge difference.  Status: `artifact`.

6. **Flat control.**  Endpoints are current equivalent and future equivalent;
   no predictive witness exists.  Status: `flat`.

7. **Same current quotient with no repair-capacity difference.**  A generic
   future witness may exist, but the complete held-out distributions agree on
   every declared repair challenge.  `NoDeclaredRepairCapacityDifference`
   is parameterized by the claim-matched distribution pair and supplies the
   second `FlatEvidenceFor` route, so this reaches `flat` without falsely
   claiming full future equivalence.  An unrelated equal-capacity pair cannot
   be borrowed to flatten a claim-linked unequal pair.  Round A must keep this
   branch separate from the paper's all-future-observables flat benchmark.

8. **Flattenable mismatch.**  A carried admissible same-base completion has
   zero witness/discrepancy and clears the repair-capacity split.  Status:
   `flattenable`.

9. **Completion label without collapse.**  A designated completion remains
   predictive-distinct or retains nonzero capacity discrepancy.  It cannot
   witness `FlattenableResidueEvidence`.

10. **Currentizable legible slack.**  A same-support admissible refinement
    makes the distinction current-visible with zero hidden witnesses,
    discrepancy zero, and max fiber one.  Status: `currentizable_slack`, never
    coherent adaptability.

11. **Refinement on changed support.**  A refinement appears to currentize the
    effect but changes the comparison base.  It fails support fixation before
    it can establish currentizable slack.

12. **Flattening/currentization separation.**  One fixture removes the effect
    by completion; another preserves the capacity distinction as ordinary
    current-visible state.  They must reach distinct statuses.

13. **Dissipative residue.**  A genuine earlier predictive/capacity difference
    is erased by one inventory-member continuation owned by the system.
    Status: `dissipative`.

14. **Unregistered dissipating continuation.**  An off-inventory continuation
    cannot establish dissipation or defeat freedom.  Completeness separately
    requires every context-eligible continuation to be in the inventory.

15. **Brittle perturbation failure.**  The residue survives nominal
    continuations but fails one preregistered in-bound deterministic
    perturbation, where in-bound status is computed from the carried declared
    bound.  `BoundedPerturbationFailureEvidence` blocks the perturbation clause
    of `DissipationFreedomEvidence`, but no actual
    `RouteTransportCore.Continuation` collapses the effect.  Therefore it is
    not `dissipative`; with all substantive branches absent it reaches the
    evidence-backed administrative status `adaptability_rejected`.  This separation follows
    the paper's distinct robustness (`Holonomy paper:529`) and dissipation
    (`:594-609`) vocabulary.

16. **Out-of-bound perturbation.**  A model change outside the declared bound
    is not an eligible trial and does not by itself falsify the bounded claim.
    Likewise, a trial satisfying the old candidate/time/magnitude conditions
    but absent from `ctx.perturbationTrialEligible` is outside the declared
    universe: omitting it does not break coverage, while including it breaks
    inventory soundness.

17. **Difference only on an undeclared challenge class.**  The class is absent
    from `HeldOutChallengeFamily.classes`; distribution completeness cannot
    turn it into `DeclaredRepairCapacityDifference`.

18. **Incomplete held-out distribution.**  One declared class lacks a record.
    `everyClassCovered` fails even if another class differs strongly.

19. **Duplicate class records.**  Two records for one class disagree or reuse
    an ID.  `recordsNodup`, `recordIdsNodup`, or `singleValuedPerClass` fails.

20. **Uncarried ledger support.**  A probability record names a ledger entry
    absent from `S.Lambda_S` or lacking its carried proof.  The capacity
    difference is rejected.

21. **Wrong route endpoint.**  A valid distribution is attached to a different
    history than `gammaEndpoint` or `etaEndpoint`.  Full endpoint linkage
    fails; route IDs cannot repair it.  Swapping left/right trial populations
    while retaining their probability records also fails
    `everyTrialEligible`, because the shared eligibility comparator is
    specialized to each owning distribution's endpoint.

22. **Different measurement protocols.**  Left and right distributions use
    different challenge families, budgets, or measurement times.  The pair
    fails `sameProtocol`; no route comparison is registered.

23. **Budget-unbound capacity claim.**  The protocol lacks the declared
    binding budget accepted by `protocolUsesBindingBudget`.  It cannot support
    an E16 substantive distribution branch, including `flat`; absent a higher
    obstruction it reaches `adaptability_rejected`.

24. **E15 residue still awaiting reconciliation.**  The exact F3 residue is an
    outstanding E15 debt item at the E16 observation time.  The adaptability
    candidate is ineligible, preventing debt/coherence double interpretation.

25. **E15 residue reconciled by carried bridge.**  The same residue has a
    carried accepted bridge predating observation and is no longer awaiting
    reconciliation.  It may proceed to the four controls; the bridge alone
    does not make it coherent.

26. **Disconnected E15 bridge.**  A bridge references a different full
    `RouteResidueDebtRecord` with the same numeric residue ID.  Full-record
    equality fails.

27. **Incomplete protocol counterpart inventory.**  An eligible honest
    counterpart is omitted.  Artifact freedom cannot be constructed.

28. **Incomplete completion/refinement/continuation inventory.**  Omission of
    any eligible control record independently blocks the corresponding freedom
    evidence.

29. **Duplicate control counterpart.**  Reusing a counterpart ID or declaring
    contradictory records for one control key fails the duplicate-safe
    inventory before status selection.

30. **Current-only audit.**  An audit reads only events in the current
    interface.  Coherent endpoints remain indistinguishable by
    `CurrentEventEquiv`, while the held-out future protocol distinguishes
    them.  This exercises consequence (a).

31. **Route-manufactured capacity.**  Two histories have identical current
    practice but different route structure and exact future discharge
    probabilities on the same declared class.  This exercises consequence
    (b), subject to all controls.

32. **Wrong-claim evidence.**  Valid artifact/currentization/dissipation
    evidence for another route pair, challenge class, protocol, or observation
    time cannot outrank the current claim.

33. **Priority collision.**  Raw artifact and flattening evidence coexist for
    one claim.  `artifact` wins because cases exclude raw higher evidence in
    the declared order; the result is not selected by status-tag mismatch.

34. **Carried-status uniqueness.**  Two carried status occurrences for the
    same full claim with distinct tags violate
    `CompleteAdaptabilityStatus.statusUnique`.

35. **Comparator mutation battery.**  Independently mutate route
    admissibility, ledger computation, ledger relevance, budget linkage,
    counterpart admissibility, each clearing comparator, perturbation
    survival, E15 bridge acceptance, and E15 outstanding status.  Each mutation
    must change the intended evidence result without a stored status lookup.

36. **Six-regime census.**  Round A must include flat, artifact trap,
    flattenable, explicit latent/currentizable, dissipative, and
    coherent-candidate fixtures.  Support confound is an additional E16 guard,
    not a seventh regime claimed by the Holonomy paper.

Round A must cover all eight E16 statuses, every item above, and the four
controls separately.  It must compute current/future equivalence, quotient
classes, exact rational discharge distributions, inventory completeness,
control collapse/currentization/dissipation, and bounded perturbation survival
from finite data.  Expected statuses may not be fixture booleans.  The existing
`lab/sixbirds_foundations_v/_holonomy_support/` code is a cross-check precedent,
not an oracle: E16 Round B must compute the same logic natively over its own
`ESystem` and carried records.

## 6. Nonclaims

1. E16 does not call currentizable residue adaptability.  A same-support
   admissible refinement that makes the distinction current-visible is
   `currentizable_slack`: legible capacity, not hidden coherent residue.

2. E16 does not derive a new route-transport, quotient, witness, strict-
   refinement, or loop-asymmetry theory.  It reuses the canonical vendored
   Holonomy Lean modules unchanged.

3. E16 does not claim that the upstream Lean core already formalizes fixed
   support or the six benchmark regimes.  The core lacks a support field and
   taxonomy declarations; E16's carried support wrapper and controls are new
   local vocabulary whose limits are explicit.

4. E16 does not claim every pair of routes with equal current quotients is an
   adaptability candidate.  The routes must share one declared support,
   possess a genuine predictive witness, and differ on a declared held-out
   repair-capacity distribution.

5. E16 does not claim that a predictive witness of any kind is repair
   adaptability.  The witness must be linked to carried repair-ledger evidence
   and the exact challenge protocol named by the claim.

6. E16 does not claim arbitrary robustness.  Dissipation freedom ranges over
   the complete declared continuation inventory and preregistered bounded
   perturbation bundle, not every imaginable continuation or model change.  A
   failed in-bound perturbation is not dissipation; it blocks coherent evidence
   and routes to evidence-backed `adaptability_rejected` if no substantive
   regime applies.  That administrative status is not a new Holonomy taxonomy
   label and cannot be obtained by bare elimination.

7. E16 does not claim the memory-wheel benchmark proves adaptability exists
   generally or in any real institution.  It is a coherent-candidate
   realization of the abstract asymmetry theorem, exactly as the paper cautions
   (`Holonomy paper:612-615`).

8. E16 does not guarantee a positive coherent instance in the toy laboratory.
   The paper reports that most candidate effects can be flat, artifactual,
   flattenable, currentizable, or dissipative; finding no survivor is compatible
   with the law.  A survivor is a strong result because the controls are
   demanding.

9. E16 does not let an outstanding E15 F3 debt item count simultaneously as
   coherent adaptability.  A carried reconciliation bridge and a time-scoped
   negative outstanding check are mandatory.  The bridge does not erase the
   historical E15 debt record.

10. E16 does not claim that route-conditioned probability differences are
    causal merely because they are numerically unequal.  Fixed support, shared
    protocol/budget, complete distributions, and all control inventories are
    required to rule out the declared confounds.

11. E16 does not identify protocol artifact, flattenability,
