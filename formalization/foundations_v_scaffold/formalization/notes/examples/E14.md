# E14. Reconsolidation Law

This is the six-field normal-form draft for E14, the Reconsolidation Law.
The source entry is `THEOREMS.md:392-408`, in Cluster D ("Laws of Carried
Memory"; heading at `THEOREMS.md:390`).  The intended Lean target is
`lean/SixBirdsFoundationsV/Laws/E14Reconsolidation.lean`.

E14 is a single-`ESystem` law about retrieval-time record dynamics.  A
retrieval counts as reconsolidation only when a carried record is transported
through the current quotient in a genuinely context-dependent way and the
transported record conflicts with the demands attached to that record's own
claim.  Under those conditions the lawful local outcomes are exactly record
repair, strict coarsening, or a carried unresolved status charged to the
system's existing ledger.  Silent mutation and laundered provenance are
auditable defect classes; context-invariant retrieval is an ordinary read,
not reconsolidation.

`LANDING_PLAN.md:470` places E14 in independent chain E with D1-D6 as its
only local hard prerequisite and F9/F20/Holonomy as external grounding.  The
design below preserves that scope: E1/D1 vocabulary is reused directly, while
F9, F20, and Holonomy are certified or recognition inputs rather than hidden
new law prerequisites.

## 1. Setup

### Imports and grounding

Lean should import D3/D4, D1's quotient order, and E1's concrete split-pair
obstruction:

```lean
import SixBirdsFoundationsV.Definitional.ESystem
import SixBirdsFoundationsV.Definitional.RepairJoin
import SixBirdsFoundationsV.Laws.E1Internalization
```

Each import is load-bearing:

- `ESystem` supplies D3 carried records, D4 repair steps, and the existing
  carried ledger `S.Lambda_S` (`ESystem.lean:32-83`, `:123-150`,
  `:177-209`).  E14 does not introduce a parallel reconsolidation ledger.
- `RepairJoin` supplies both `repairJoin` and `Refines`
  (`RepairJoin.lean:13-21`).  The conflict test uses `repairJoin Q_t T_c(m)`.
  The coarsening branch uses `Refines qBefore qAfter` in the direction
  established by the actual definition: equality under `qBefore` implies
  equality under `qAfter`, so `qBefore` is at least as fine as `qAfter`.
- `E1Internalization` supplies the actual `Delta` predicate
  (`E1Internalization.lean:18-21`).  E14 can state the source conflict
  literally as an existential `Delta (repairJoin Q_t T_c(m)) F r` witness;
  it does not need E13's two-carrier bridge or E10's abstract Delta
  comparators.

F9 and F20 are not vendored Lean theorem modules in this repository.  They
are inventory-verified certified host inputs: F9 No Unstatused Residual is
recorded at `formalization/inventory/imported_foundations.yml:171-177`, and
F20 Fact / Record Formation at `:211-217`.  E7 established the house pattern
for consuming F9 through an explicit host contract rather than pretending to
reprove it (`E7Alarm.lean:159-165`).  E14 follows that boundary, but does not
make the whole trichotomy opaque: F9 certifies coverage and single-valuedness
of the typed disposition for a native retrieval conflict, while E14 locally
defines the three disposition tags and requires concrete carried evidence
realizing whichever tag is used.

The Holonomy paper is recognition and control grounding, not a Lean
dependency.  Its current/predictive quotient source is inventory-backed at
`imported_foundations.yml:42-50`; its exact finite benchmark taxonomy
distinguishes artifact, flattenable, explicit-latent, dissipative, and
coherent-candidate regimes.  E14 does not import that whole taxonomy as its
status partition.  In particular, the Holonomy paper's dissipative benchmark
is precedent for endogenous record change, while E14's repair/coarsen/status
trichotomy is new local vocabulary.

The function-valued fields are deliberate.  They allow E14's conflict and
coarsening obligations to use the real `Delta`, `repairJoin`, and `Refines`
definitions.  Stable numeric IDs remain available for audit links and toy-lab
serialization; IDs alone never establish semantic equality, descent,
coarsening, or disposition support.  A disposition directly owns the full
mutation or residual record it cites.

All host comparisons are supplied once.  No outcome case, obstruction, or
falsifier may choose a fresh comparator.

```lean
  coarseningMoveInstallsRecord :
    RepairMove S.T MovePayload LedgerEntry MoveRecord S.moveRecordPolicy ->

      CarriedMemoryRecord X RecordValue FOut Readout ->
      RecordQuotientRecord X Q -> RecordQuotientRecord X Q -> Prop

      MemoryClaimRecord X FOut Readout -> Prop

    RetrievalConflictRecord X RecordValue Q TransportedValue FOut Readout ->
      ReconsolidationDispositionRecord X RecordValue Q TransportedValue
        FOut Readout -> Prop

    MutationProvenanceRecord X RecordValue FOut Readout -> Prop
```

These are certified interfaces, not conclusion fields.  For example,
`repairMoveInstallsJoin` must be computed from the actual before/repair/after
record values in the toy lab; a disconnected `repaired = true` flag is not an
acceptable instance.  Coarsening itself is not delegated to the context:
the `Refines` direction and strict merged-fiber witness remain explicit below.

Absence and context invariance are scoped to a complete inventory for one
source record and one declared context family:

```lean
structure CompleteRetrievalTransportInventory
    (ctx : ReconsolidationClassifierContext S X RecordValue Q
      TransportedValue FOut Readout)
    (policies : ReconsolidationEvidencePolicies S X RecordValue Q
      TransportedValue FOut Readout)
    (sourceRecord : CarriedMemoryRecord X RecordValue FOut Readout)
    (family : DeclaredRetrievalContextFamily) where
  declaredTransports :
    List (RetrievalTransportRecord X RecordValue Q TransportedValue
      FOut Readout)
  completeForClaim :
    forall transport :
      DeclaredRetrievalTransport ctx policies sourceRecord family,
      transport.transportRecord in declaredTransports
  soundForClaim :
    forall transportRecord, transportRecord in declaredTransports ->
      exists transport :
        DeclaredRetrievalTransport ctx policies sourceRecord family,
        transport.transportRecord = transportRecord
  everyContextCovered :
    forall context, context in family.contexts ->
      exists transportRecord, transportRecord in declaredTransports and
        transportRecord.contextRecord = context
  transportSingleValuedPerContext :
    forall transport1, transport1 in declaredTransports ->
      forall transport2, transport2 in declaredTransports ->
        transport1.contextRecord = transport2.contextRecord ->
        forall x : X,
          transport1.transportedRecord x = transport2.transportedRecord x
```

Genuine transport requires an observed context contrast using the same
source and the complete declared inventory:

```lean
structure ContextDependentRetrieval
    (inventory : CompleteRetrievalTransportInventory ctx policies
      sourceRecord family) where
  transport1 transport2 :
    RetrievalTransportRecord X RecordValue Q TransportedValue FOut Readout
  transport1Declared : transport1 in inventory.declaredTransports
  transport2Declared : transport2 in inventory.declaredTransports
  distinctContexts :
    transport1.contextRecord != transport2.contextRecord
  contextChangesTransport :
    exists x : X,
      transport1.transportedRecord x != transport2.transportedRecord x
```

The named retrieval-without-transport control is the closed-world opposite:

```lean
structure RetrievalWithoutTransportEvidence
    (inventory : CompleteRetrievalTransportInventory ctx policies
      sourceRecord family) where
  contextInvariant :
    forall transport1, transport1 in inventory.declaredTransports ->
      forall transport2, transport2 in inventory.declaredTransports ->
        forall x : X,
          transport1.transportedRecord x = transport2.transportedRecord x
```

This is not a host-supplied `contextDependent : Bool`.  Round B must compute
the function values across the complete finite context family.  The family is
pairwise distinct, and `transportSingleValuedPerContext` makes each declared
`T_c(m)` functional: two carried declarations for the same context cannot
disagree pointwise.

### Retrieval conflict against the record's own claim

```lean
structure RetrievalConflictEvidence
    (ctx : ReconsolidationClassifierContext S X RecordValue Q
      TransportedValue FOut Readout)
    (policies : ReconsolidationEvidencePolicies S X RecordValue Q
      TransportedValue FOut Readout)
    (sourceRecord : CarriedMemoryRecord X RecordValue FOut Readout)
    (family : DeclaredRetrievalContextFamily) where
  transport : DeclaredRetrievalTransport ctx policies sourceRecord family
  conflictRecord :
    RetrievalConflictRecord X RecordValue Q TransportedValue FOut Readout
  conflictLinked :
    conflictRecord.sourceRecord = sourceRecord and
    conflictRecord.transportRecord = transport.transportRecord and
    conflictRecord.claimRecord = sourceRecord.claimRecord
  conflictCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt policies.conflictPolicy conflictRecord n0 sourceTag
        generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  deltaConflict :
    exists x x' : X,
      Delta
        (repairJoin transport.transportRecord.currentQuotient
          transport.transportRecord.transportedRecord)
        sourceRecord.claimRecord.demand sourceRecord.claimRecord.readout x x'
```

`deltaConflict` is exactly the nonempty split-pair condition from the source:
the quotient is the current quotient joined with the transported retrieval,
and `F`/`r` come from the source record's own claim.  An improvement or
conflict for another record or another claim cannot be copied into this
witness.

### F9 typed-disposition coverage

E14's F9 bridge is scoped to one concrete conflict.  It requires a complete
declared inventory of carried disposition records and single-valued status
within that inventory.

```lean
structure CompleteReconsolidationDispositionInventory
    (ctx : ReconsolidationClassifierContext S X RecordValue Q
      TransportedValue FOut Readout)
    (policies : ReconsolidationEvidencePolicies S X RecordValue Q
      TransportedValue FOut Readout)
    (conflictRecord :
      RetrievalConflictRecord X RecordValue Q TransportedValue FOut Readout) where
  declaredDispositions :
    List (ReconsolidationDispositionRecord X RecordValue Q TransportedValue
      FOut Readout)
  completeForConflict :
    forall dispositionRecord,
      dispositionRecord.conflictRecord = conflictRecord ->
      ctx.f9ClassifiesNativeConflict conflictRecord dispositionRecord ->
      (exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
        CarriedRecordAt policies.dispositionPolicy dispositionRecord n0
          sourceTag generatedByS inScope
          and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope) ->
      dispositionRecord in declaredDispositions
  soundForConflict :
    forall dispositionRecord,
      dispositionRecord in declaredDispositions ->
      dispositionRecord.conflictRecord = conflictRecord and
      ctx.f9ClassifiesNativeConflict conflictRecord dispositionRecord and
      exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
        CarriedRecordAt policies.dispositionPolicy dispositionRecord n0
          sourceTag generatedByS inScope
          and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  statusSingleValued :
    forall disposition1, disposition1 in declaredDispositions ->
      forall disposition2, disposition2 in declaredDispositions ->
        disposition1.disposition = disposition2.disposition
```

```lean
structure F9ReconsolidationCoverageCertified
    (inventory : CompleteReconsolidationDispositionInventory ctx policies
      conflictRecord) where
  dispositionRecord :
    ReconsolidationDispositionRecord X RecordValue Q TransportedValue
      FOut Readout
  dispositionDeclared : dispositionRecord in inventory.declaredDispositions

    (inventory : CompleteReconsolidationDispositionInventory ctx policies

```

The F9 contract supplies typed coverage and uniqueness, but does not assert
that a repair, coarsening, or residual record actually exists.  Each outcome
below must realize the disposition with its own carried records.  This split
avoids smuggling E14's conclusion into the certified F9 input.

### Lawful record repair

```lean

  auditRecord : AuditRecord
  repairPackageCarried :

      CarriedRecordAt policies.repairPackagePolicy repairPackage n0 sourceTag
        generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  afterRecordCarried : F20CarriedMemoryRecord ctx policies afterRecord
  mutationCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt policies.mutationPolicy mutationRecord n0 sourceTag
        generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope

    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt policies.provenancePolicy provenanceRecord n0 sourceTag
        generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope

    mutationRecord.beforeRecord = sourceRecord and
    mutationRecord.afterRecord = afterRecord and
    mutationRecord.retrievalTransportId =
      trigger.conflict.transport.transportRecord.transportId and
    mutationRecord.contextRecord =
      trigger.conflict.transport.transportRecord.contextRecord and
    mutationRecord.mutationKind = RecordMutationKind.repair
  provenanceLinked :
    provenanceRecord.mutationRecord = mutationRecord and
    provenanceRecord.declaredParent = sourceRecord and
    provenanceRecord.declaredDerivedRecord = afterRecord
  provenanceHonest : ctx.provenanceMatchesMutation provenanceRecord
  recordIdentityPreserved : afterRecord.recordId = sourceRecord.recordId
  claimPreserved : afterRecord.claimRecord = sourceRecord.claimRecord
  versionAdvances : afterRecord.version = sourceRecord.version + 1
  lawfulRepairStep : ESystem.RepairStep S zBefore zAfter defect auditRecord
  installsRecordJoin :
    ctx.repairMoveInstallsJoin (S.R_S defect) repairPackage sourceRecord

    repairPackage.contextRecord =
      trigger.conflict.transport.transportRecord.contextRecord
```

The theorem will not derive `installsRecordJoin`, provenance honesty, or D4
lawfulness from the fact that the record changed.  They are independently
verified fields of the repair witness.

### Adjudicated design choices

1. **Retrieval transport is intra-system data, not an E13 bridge.**  A carried
   `RetrievalTransportRecord` owns the actual context-indexed transported map.
   Context dependence is computed across a complete carried family whose
   contexts are pairwise distinct and whose transport is pointwise
   single-valued for each context.

2. **Conflict uses direct E1 vocabulary.**  Unlike E10's sanctioned abstract
   Delta comparator, E14 has all arguments needed for the literal
   `Delta (repairJoin Q_t T_c(m)) F r` witness.  The direct spelling removes an
   unnecessary certified-input boundary.

3. **Record repair uses one shared installation comparator.**  D4 deliberately
   leaves `MovePayload` opaque, and `m_t vee R_c` may change the value codomain.
   `repairMoveInstallsJoin` is therefore the E1-style host bridge from the
   actual `S.R_S defect`, repair package, and before/after records.  It is
   shared through `ctx` and must be computed from those arguments; it is not a
   per-witness assertion.

4. **Coarsening uses D1 directly and strictly.**  `Refines before after` has
   the right order direction, but equality also satisfies it.  The additional
   merged-pair witness is stored on the carried supportability audit itself, so
   the comparator certifies the exact pair that `strictMerge` proves was
   collapsed.

5. **F9 coverage and E14 realization are separate.**  F9 certifies a carried,
   single-valued typed disposition.  E14 separately requires the concrete
   repair/coarsening/residual record directly named by that disposition.  A
   missing or mismatched direct support reference receives
   `unrealized_disposition`; a completely empty F9 inventory receives
   `unstatused_conflict`.

6. **The source trichotomy remains the lawful core.**  The six additional
   statuses are audit/control outcomes needed to make the two named
   falsifiers, exclusivity, unsupported status labels, provenance laundering,
   and F9 absence mechanically visible.  They do not add alternative lawful
   memory fates.

### Case predicates

The priority order is:

```text
provenance_defect >
silent_rewrite >
ordinary_read >
outcome_collision >
unrealized_disposition >
record_repaired >
record_coarsened >
statused_unresolved >
```

Every priority negation below is against a record-independent `...EvidenceFor`

## 3. Proof Spine

1. **Establish F20/D3 record formation.**  The source memory and every
   credited post-retrieval memory record must have both a carried memory
   occurrence and a carried formation record accepted by the shared F20 host
   contract.  Context, transport, conflict, mutation, quotient, support audit,
   provenance, residual, disposition, and status records each retain their own
   D3 occurrence evidence.

2. **Establish genuine retrieval transport.**  Use the complete context-family
   inventory.  The family is duplicate-free, and transport is pointwise
   single-valued within each context.  `ContextDependentRetrieval` therefore
   requires two genuinely distinct declared contexts and an actual input at
   which their transported records differ.
   `RetrievalWithoutTransportEvidence` computes pointwise invariance across all
   declared transports and therefore fires `ordinary_read` before any
   reconsolidation outcome.

3. **Detect conflict literally with E1 `Delta`.**  Join the current quotient
   and the transported retrieval with `repairJoin`.  Produce `x,x'` for which
   the joined quotient identifies the inputs while the source record's own
   `r (F x)` readout differs.  No conflict about another record or challenge
   can satisfy `conflictLinked`.

4. **Apply F9 only at its honest boundary.**  For the native conflict, the
   complete disposition inventory is carried and single-valued.  F9 supplies
   a typed disposition record, not a repair/coarsening/residual witness.  E14's
   concrete evidence must realize that disposition with linked carried data.

5. **Verify record repair.**  Require a D4 `ESystem.RepairStep`, a carried
   repair package, a shared-comparator check that `S.R_S defect` installs the
   record join, a carried next-version record, and honest mutation provenance.

6. **Verify strict coarsening.**  Require a D4 repair step and move-to-record
   link, direct `Refines before after`, and a carried supportability audit that
   owns the explicit pair merged only after the step.  The same audit record is
   passed to `distinctionNoLongerSupportable`, tying the certified unsupported
   distinction to `strictMerge`.  This is the precise reading of
   `m_(t+1) = c_F(m_t)` available from D1; plain quotient equality does not
   count as coarsening.

7. **Verify statused unresolved.**  Keep the conflict record explicit, carry a
   positive exact-rational residual, name a concrete entry already in
   `S.Lambda_S.ledgerEntries`, obtain that ledger's carried evidence, and
   compute the charge through `ledgerEntryChargesResidual`.  The current
   record must preserve identity, claim, version, value, and provenance root.

8. **Reject overlap, unsupported labels, and absence.**  Two concrete outcomes
   for the same trigger produce `outcome_collision`, not a priority-selected
   lawful fate.  A carried F9 label whose directly referenced supporting record
   is not realized by the complete-and-sound outcome inventory produces
   `unrealized_disposition`.
   An empty complete F9 disposition inventory produces
   `unstatused_conflict`.  These positive defect branches make the source's
   "exactly one, and silently none" reviewable.

9. **Audit mutation provenance.**  A changed value with no authorized outcome
   and no complete-inventory provenance produces `silent_rewrite`.  A carried
   provenance record that fails the shared lineage check produces
   `provenance_defect`.  Both outrank lawful outcomes for the same retrieval
   claim.

10. **Partition per retrieval claim.**  Extract one carried status record from
    `CompleteReconsolidationStatus`, split on its branch-valid case, and use
    record-independent evidence exclusions plus same-claim status uniqueness
    to establish the
    nine-way result.  Excluding the five defect/control branches and the F9
    failure branch leaves exactly the three lawful source outcomes.

## 4. Case Enumeration

1. **Context-dependent conflict repaired.**  Two carried contexts produce
   genuinely different retrieval transports for the same carried memory.  The
   selected retrieval has a nonempty direct `Delta` conflict against that
   memory's own claim.  A D4 repair step installs a linked record join, advances
   the version, and carries honest provenance.  Status: `record_repaired`.

2. **Context-dependent conflict strictly coarsened.**  The same trigger holds,
   but the next record uses a quotient coarser under `Refines`; a concrete pair
   is distinct before and merged after, and a carried audit says that exact
   distinction is no longer supportable.  Status: `record_coarsened`.

3. **Context-dependent conflict statused unresolved.**  No mutation is
   credited.  Identity, claim, version, value, and provenance root all remain
   unchanged.  A positive rational residual is carried, assigned the local F9
   unresolved disposition, and linked to a real carried entry in `S.Lambda_S`.
   Status: `statused_unresolved`.

4. **Two outcomes claimed for one retrieval.**  For example, carried repair
   and carried coarsening witnesses both target the same conflict.  Status:
   `outcome_collision`; priority must not hide the inconsistent ledger.

5. **Typed disposition with no realizing record.**  F9 returns a carried
   repair/coarsening/unresolved tag, but its direct supporting record is absent
   from the corresponding complete-and-sound raw outcome inventory.  Status:
   `unrealized_disposition`; a status label alone is not a lawful outcome.

6. **Silent rewrite.**  The carried before/after values differ after the
   retrieval, while the complete outcome inventory contains no repair,
   coarsening, or unresolved record and the complete provenance inventory is
   empty.  Status: `silent_rewrite`; the instance fails E14 lawfulness.

7. **Laundered provenance ("false memory" defect).**  The value changes and a
   provenance record purports to cover the mutation, but the shared comparator
   finds that it names the wrong parent, derived record, retrieval, or lineage.
   Status: `provenance_defect`, not an anomalous untyped event.

8. **Retrieval without transport.**  At least two declared contexts are
   covered by a duplicate-free family, each context has a single-valued
   transport, and every declared `T_c(m)` is pointwise equal across the
   complete inventory.  Status: `ordinary_read`, even if a host informally
   labels the operation "reconsolidation."

9. **Native conflict with silently no outcome.**  Genuine context-dependent
   retrieval and direct `Delta` conflict are carried, but the complete F9
   disposition inventory is empty.  Status: `unstatused_conflict`; this is the
   constructive F9 falsifier.

10. **No conflict after genuine transport.**  Context changes the transported
   record, but exhaustive finite evaluation finds no `Delta` witness against
   the record's own claim.  E14's conditional trichotomy is not triggered and
   no reconsolidation-outcome claim should be registered for this episode.

11. **Conflict for another claim.**  A retrieved record conflicts under some
    other demand/readout, but not under `sourceRecord.claimRecord`.  It cannot
    construct `RetrievalConflictEvidence` for this claim and therefore cannot
    receive one of the three lawful E14 outcomes.

12. **Non-strict quotient change.**  `Refines before after` holds but no pair is
    merged, so `strictMerge` fails.  This is not coarsening; absent another
    concrete outcome it reaches `unrealized_disposition` when a coarsening tag
    was carried without support, or `unstatused_conflict` when no F9 tag was
    carried.  It never reaches `record_coarsened`.

13. **Wrong-direction refinement.**  The proposed after quotient is finer than
    the before quotient.  `Refines before after` fails in D1's actual direction,
    so the witness is not E14 coarsening.

14. **Uncarried post-retrieval record.**  Raw values look repaired or coarsened,
    but the next record or its F20 formation record lacks D3 evidence.  No
    positive repair/coarsening case can fire.

15. **Ledger charge by label only.**  A residual record stores an entry name or
    a disconnected `charged = true` flag, but the entry is absent from
    `S.Lambda_S` or `ledgerEntryChargesResidual` fails on the actual rational
    amount.  `statused_unresolved` does not fire.

16. **Per-retrieval scoping control.**  The same source memory is repaired in
    context `c1` and statused unresolved in context `c2`.  Distinct full
    `ReconsolidationClaimRef`s are independently complete; uniqueness does not
    force the two statuses to agree.  This does not permit competing outputs
    for one context: each claim's complete transport inventory still enforces
    pointwise single-valued `T_c(m)`.

17. **Unrelated mutation evidence.**  A lawful repair or provenance record for
    another source record, transport, context, or claim cannot satisfy the
    current status record's links.  The current claim remains uncredited.

18. **Loose exposure budget stratum.**  In the toy lab, a pre-registered loose
    E6/E8-style exposure-budget regime should be associated with more realized
    `record_repaired` outcomes than the matched tight regime.  This is a
    measured prediction, not a premise or theorem conclusion.

19. **Tight residual budget stratum.**  In the toy lab, a pre-registered tight
    residual-budget regime should be associated with more strict
    `record_coarsened` outcomes than the matched loose regime.  Exact counts
    and rational thresholds belong in Round A; E14 does not hardcode the
    dominance relation into a case predicate.

The later toy-lab pre-registration must cover all nine statuses plus items
10-19 as negative or stratification controls.  In particular, it must compute
transport context dependence, `Delta`, strict merged fibers, provenance
lineage, and `Lambda_S` membership from fixture data rather than status flags.

  `S.Lambda_S : CarriedLedger S.T LedgerEntry` directly
  (`ESystem.lean:177-209`).

## 6. Nonclaims

1. E14 does not claim that every retrieval mutates a record.  Genuine
   context-dependent transport with no `Delta` conflict lies outside the
   conditional trichotomy, and context-invariant retrieval is `ordinary_read`.

2. E14 does not claim that every record value change is lawful.  Lawful repair
   and coarsening require D4 steps, exact claim/transport links, carried next
   records, and honest provenance.  Silent and laundered changes are defect
   statuses.

3. E14 does not prove F9 or F20 locally.  It specializes their certified usage
   contracts and keeps all E14-specific outcomes and D3 evidence explicit.

4. E14 does not identify coarsening with arbitrary forgetting or cardinality
   reduction.  The formal branch requires D1 `Refines` in the correct direction,
   an explicit merged distinction, a carried supportability audit, and a lawful
   move installing the next record.

5. E14 does not claim that a status label alone charges `Lambda_S`.  The
   unresolved witness must name a real ledger member, obtain the ledger's
   carried evidence, and link the exact rational residual amount through the
   shared charge comparator.

6. E14 does not use "false memory" as a moral, clinical, or anomalous category.
   `provenance_defect` means only that a detected mutation's carried provenance
   fails the declared lineage comparator.

7. E14 does not import E13 communication/teaching/symbol transport.  Retrieval
   transport is intra-system and context-indexed, with no second carrier or
   paid inter-carrier bridge.

8. E14 does not prove the budget-dominance prediction.  Loose-exposure repair
   and tight-residual coarsening are pre-registered toy-lab strata and a future
   E6/E8 interaction question, not theorem hypotheses disguised as outcomes.

9. E14 does not classify all Holonomy regimes or claim every dissipative
   benchmark is reconsolidation.  Holonomy supplies recognized transport and
   endogenous-change instances; E14's own status criteria remain controlling.

10. E14 makes no general claim about biological or human memory beyond the
    operational recognized-instance list in `THEOREMS.md:402-408`.  Its direct
    objects are carried records in auditable agents, institutions, and software
    systems.
