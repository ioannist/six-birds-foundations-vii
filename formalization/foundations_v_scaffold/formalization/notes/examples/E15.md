# E15. Offline Reclosure Law

This is the six-field normal-form draft for E15, the Offline Reclosure Law.
The source entry is `THEOREMS.md:412-426`, in Cluster D ("Laws of Carried
Memory"; heading at `THEOREMS.md:390`).  The intended Lean target is
`lean/SixBirdsFoundationsV/Laws/E15OfflineReclosure.lean`.

E15 is a single-`ESystem` law about carried closure debt and operating-mode
alternation.  It aggregates three kinds of outstanding, ledger-backed debt:
E14 statused-unresolved retrieval residuals, F3 route residues awaiting
transport reconciliation, and Xi adequacy residuals awaiting discharge.  Its
necessity claim is deliberately conditional.  When the carried accrual rate
persistently exceeds the online discharge capacity computed from a declared
shared budget, lawful persistence requires recurring offline discharge.  When
online capacity is sufficient, remaining online is lawful.  Offline operation
outside the deficit regime is permitted but is not required by E15.

The principal anti-smuggling rule is structural: neither `kappaOn` nor
`kappaOff` is a free theorem parameter.  Both are projections of one carried
budget-allocation record tied to an actual `ExposureBudgetWitness`, and the
offline capacity increase is the exact online exchange allocation released by
gating exchange.  Likewise, closure debt and its accrual rate are finite sums
and quotients computed from complete carried inventories, not host-selected
numbers.

`LANDING_PLAN.md:471` places E15 after E14, E7, and E5, with Xi ladders and
Spend geometry as external grounding.  E15 directly discharges E14's recorded
forward obligation at `formalization/notes/interaction_matrix.md:231`: it
includes E14's actual statused-unresolved residual records in `D_t` and connects
the resulting debt to an online/offline budget schedule.  The E7/E5 cascade is
mechanized only to the honest depth supported by their current APIs: E15 carries
and links observations using their actual status vocabularies, while shared
certificates attest that the observations satisfy those laws' own apparatus.
E15 does not derive E7 or E5 from debt alone.

Each import is load-bearing, but at a different depth:

- E14 supplies `ReconsolidationResidualRecord` and
  `StatusedUnresolvedEvidence`.  The former exposes the exact rational
  `residualAmount` and its retrieval-conflict link
  (`E14Reconsolidation.lean:127-133`); the latter proves positive charge to a
  real carried entry in `S.Lambda_S` (`:624-666`).  E15 reuses those records
  directly rather than recreating an E14-shaped residual.
- E7 supplies the actual `AlarmDispositionKind` and
  `AlarmDiscountReason` vocabulary (`E7Alarm.lean:140-149`) and the carried
  alarm-disposition discipline (`:167-270`).  A cross-law acceptance
  comparator remains necessary because E7's full `AlarmDispositionFor`
  judgment depends on its own probe economy, constraints, and horizon.
- E5 supplies `ReclosureCollapseStatus`, including `stressed`
  (`E5ReclosureCollapse.lean:1265-1273`).  Its `SuspensionWitness`
  (`:1140-1177`) is a close operational cousin, but it is not E15 offline
  mode: E5 certifies apparatus intact and operations gated off, whereas E15
  gates external exchange specifically and additionally requires exact budget
  reallocation plus measured debt discharge.  E15 therefore does not force an
  offline phase into `SuspensionWitness`.
- E6/E9 supplies `ExposureBudgetWitness`, whose budget and spend values are
  linked to concrete entries already in `S.Lambda_S`, and
  `BindingExposureBudget` (`E6E9PricedAccess.lean:46-93`).  E15 adds only the
  online/offline split of that shared budget; it does not fit capacities after
  observing the schedule.
- FIII `Basic` supplies the actual primitive constructor
  `SixBirdsIII.Primitive.P2` (`SixBirdsIII/Basic.lean:3-10`).  E4 already
  demonstrates the usable house pattern: a D4 `RepairMove` whose
  `move.sort.val` is `P1` or `P2`, backed by `LawfulRepairStep`
  (`E4RepairCompilation.lean:137-154`).  E15 uses the strict P2 branch for the
  exchange gate.
- Xi supplies the literal finite-rational matrix function
  `adequacyResidual C L D KLLdagger : Mat z z`
  (`Xi/AdequacyResidual.lean:33-44`).  The matrix-to-ledger scalarization is not
  supplied by Xi and is therefore an explicit, shared, predeclared valuation
  policy below.  This is a certified-host boundary, not an implicit trace or
  norm chosen per residual.

F3 has no local Lean record type suitable for direct reuse.  The inventory's
"Current vs predictive quotient" entry is `verified_correct` and cites
Foundations IV `thm:F3` together with the Holonomy source
(`formalization/inventory/imported_foundations.yml:42-55`).  E15 represents an
F3 route residue with a local carried record and requires one shared
`f3RouteResidueAwaitingReconciliation` certificate.  This follows the existing
F19/F25/F31 certified-input discipline: the host certifies the imported source
claim, while all E15-specific amounts, ledger entries, scope links, and sums are
local and inspectable.

`xiResidualAmount` is a function, not a per-item proposition.  The valuation
policy is itself carried and must have been declared no later than the debt
horizon's start.  Thus two Xi records evaluated under one claim cannot receive
conveniently different scalarization rules.  The three time-indexed
"outstanding/awaiting" predicates must be computed from the host's complete
discharge/reconciliation registry as of the supplied time; they are shared
classifier policies, never Boolean fields stored on an individual debt item.

```lean
def ClosureDebtEntryRecord.amount : ClosureDebtEntryRecord ... -> Rat
```

```lean
structure CompleteClosureDebtInventory
    (scope : ClosureDebtScopeRecord)
    (asOf : Rat)
    (entries : List (ClosureDebtEntryRecord ...)) where
  keysNodup : (entries.map ClosureDebtEntryRecord.key).Nodup
  ledgerChargesNodup :
    (entries.map ClosureDebtEntryRecord.ledgerEntry).Nodup
  everyE14Sound :
    forall residualRecord ledgerEntry,
      ClosureDebtEntryRecord.e14 residualRecord ledgerEntry in entries ->
      exists item :
        E14ResidualDebtItem e14ctx e14policies ctx policies scope asOf,
        item.unresolved.residualRecord = residualRecord and
        item.ledgerEntry = ledgerEntry
  everyF3Sound :
    forall residueRecord ledgerEntry,
      ClosureDebtEntryRecord.f3 residueRecord ledgerEntry in entries ->
      exists item : F3RouteResidueDebtItem S ctx policies scope asOf,
        item.residueRecord = residueRecord and item.ledgerEntry = ledgerEntry
  everyXiSound :
    forall residualRecord ledgerEntry,
      ClosureDebtEntryRecord.xi residualRecord ledgerEntry in entries ->
      exists item : XiAdequacyResidualDebtItem S ctx policies scope asOf,
        item.residualRecord = residualRecord and item.ledgerEntry = ledgerEntry
  everyE14Covered :
    forall item :
      E14ResidualDebtItem e14ctx e14policies ctx policies scope asOf,
      ClosureDebtEntryRecord.e14 item.unresolved.residualRecord
        item.ledgerEntry in entries
  everyF3Covered :
    forall item : F3RouteResidueDebtItem S ctx policies scope asOf,
      ClosureDebtEntryRecord.f3 item.residueRecord item.ledgerEntry in entries
  everyXiCovered :
    forall item : XiAdequacyResidualDebtItem S ctx policies scope asOf,
      ClosureDebtEntryRecord.xi item.residualRecord item.ledgerEntry in entries
```

Soundness resolves each raw entry to a full witness; coverage requires every
in-scope witness to occur in the raw list.  The witnesses are claim-scoped
because each is indexed by the same `scope` and contains its full D3, time,
claim, amount, and ledger evidence.  `ledgerChargesNodup` prevents one physical
ledger charge from being counted once as F3 and again as Xi.  Component-tagged
numeric IDs alone do not establish distinct charges.

```lean
  (flow.accruedEntries.map ClosureDebtEntryRecord.amount).sum

def DischargedDebt (flow : ClosureDebtFlowRecord ...) : Rat :=
  (flow.dischargeRecords.map fun d => d.amount).sum

def PhaseDuration (phase : OperatingPhaseRecord) : Rat :=
  phase.endTime - phase.startTime

def ClosureDebtAccrualRate (flow : ClosureDebtFlowRecord ...) : Rat :=
  AccruedDebt flow / PhaseDuration flow.phaseRecord

def ClosureDebtDischargeRate (flow : ClosureDebtFlowRecord ...) : Rat :=
  DischargedDebt flow / PhaseDuration flow.phaseRecord
```

```lean
structure CompleteClosureDebtFlow
    (flow : ClosureDebtFlowRecord ...) where
  flowCarried : carried policies.debtFlowPolicy flow
  beforeComplete :
    Nonempty (CompleteClosureDebtSnapshot flow.phaseRecord.scope
      flow.beforeSnapshot)
  afterComplete :
    Nonempty (CompleteClosureDebtSnapshot flow.phaseRecord.scope
      flow.afterSnapshot)
  phaseLinked :
    flow.beforeSnapshot.observedAt = flow.phaseRecord.startTime and
    flow.afterSnapshot.observedAt = flow.phaseRecord.endTime
  accruedEntriesNodup :
    (flow.accruedEntries.map ClosureDebtEntryRecord.key).Nodup
  dischargesNodup : (flow.dischargeRecords.map fun d => d.dischargeId).Nodup
  dischargedEntriesNodup :
    (flow.dischargeRecords.map fun d => d.debtEntry).Nodup
  everyAccrualNew :
    forall entry, entry in flow.accruedEntries ->
      entry ∉ flow.beforeSnapshot.entries and
      entry in flow.afterSnapshot.entries
  everyNewEntryCovered :
    forall entry,
      entry in flow.afterSnapshot.entries ->
      entry ∉ flow.beforeSnapshot.entries ->
      entry in flow.accruedEntries
  everyDischargeCarried :
    forall discharge, discharge in flow.dischargeRecords ->
      carried policies.dischargePolicy discharge
  everyDischargeLinked :
    forall discharge, discharge in flow.dischargeRecords ->
      discharge.phaseRecord = flow.phaseRecord and
      discharge.phaseRecord.startTime <= discharge.dischargedAt and
      discharge.dischargedAt <= discharge.phaseRecord.endTime and
      discharge.debtEntry in flow.beforeSnapshot.entries and
      0 < discharge.amount and
      discharge.amount = ClosureDebtEntryRecord.amount discharge.debtEntry and
      discharge.debtEntry ∉ flow.afterSnapshot.entries
  balanceEquation :
    flow.afterSnapshot.totalDebt =
      flow.beforeSnapshot.totalDebt + AccruedDebt flow - DischargedDebt flow
```

The source's `dot D` is operationalized as the exact gross arrival rate of new
carried debt entries over a positive-duration phase.  Concurrent discharge is
separately measured and the balance equation reconciles both with snapshots.
This avoids inferring gross accrual from net debt change.

### Budget geometry derives `kappaOn` and `kappaOff`

Let `economy : ProbeEconomy S Probe XiFamily`, `move : ProbeMove Probe
XiFamily`, and `budgetData : ExposureBudgetWitness economy move` be declared
once for the claim.

```lean

      allocation.onlineInternalDischargeAllocation = budgetData.budget

      allocation.onlineInternalDischargeAllocation +

      allocation.offlineInternalDischargeAllocation = budgetData.budget
```

```lean
def kappaOn (geometry : DerivedOfflineBudgetGeometry ...) : Rat :=
  geometry.allocation.onlineInternalDischargeAllocation

def freedAllocation (geometry : DerivedOfflineBudgetGeometry ...) : Rat :=
  geometry.allocation.onlineExternalAllocation

def kappaOff (geometry : DerivedOfflineBudgetGeometry ...) : Rat :=
  geometry.allocation.offlineInternalDischargeAllocation
```

The geometry directly proves:

```lean
kappaOff geometry = kappaOn geometry + freedAllocation geometry
kappaOn geometry < kappaOff geometry
```

The first equation is not a theorem hypothesis supplied independently of the
allocation; it is the `offlineInternalIsReallocation` field unfolded through
the three definitions.  Capacity and allocation use the same declared debt-
discharge currency.  E15 does not infer physical discharge efficiency from
budget units: each offline phase must separately show measured discharge, and
measured discharge rate may not exceed `kappaOff`.

### Declared schedule, exchange census, and duty cycle

`DeclaredOperatingSchedule` was defined with the raw records before the policy
bundle, so the schedule itself can be carried without a recursive type.  Its
`scope`, duplicate-free nonempty phase list, horizon coverage, and adjacency
conditions are all data-linked.  Alternation and recurrence are evidence, not
fields of every schedule, because lawful `online_sufficient` schedules contain
no offline phase.


The eligible-exchange structure defines the census universe independently of
the list.  `completeForSchedule` prevents omission of a positive carried
offline exchange, `soundForSchedule` prevents foreign or uncarried additions,
and the two `Nodup` fields prevent exact-record and identity duplication.  The
per-phase coverage field requires an explicit zero observation when no
exchange occurred; an empty phase slice is not silently interpreted as zero.

```lean
def ExchangeInPhase inventory phase : Rat :=
  ((inventory.records.filter fun r => r.phaseRecord = phase).map
    fun r => r.exchangedAmount).sum

def TotalDuration schedule : Rat :=
  (schedule.phases.map PhaseDuration).sum

def OfflineDuration schedule : Rat :=
  ((schedule.phases.filter fun p => p.mode = .offline).map PhaseDuration).sum

def ObservedOfflineDuty schedule : Rat :=
  OfflineDuration schedule / TotalDuration schedule
```

For a persistent, phase-constant gross accrual rate `a`, online net debt grows
at `a - kappaOn`; offline external accrual is gated to zero and debt is
discharged at capacity `kappaOff`.  The boundary duty fraction is therefore:

```lean
def PredictedOfflineDuty (a : Rat)
    (geometry : DerivedOfflineBudgetGeometry ...) : Rat :=
  (a - kappaOn geometry) /
    ((a - kappaOn geometry) + kappaOff geometry)
```

This formula is a declared finite operating model, not a universal claim about
systems with exogenous offline accrual.  If debt can accrue while external
exchange is gated, that accrual must be represented in the flow inventory and
a different predeclared geometry is required; E15 does not silently fit the
formula afterward.

### Adjudicated design choices

1. **E14 aggregation is direct and discharges the recorded core obligation.**
   `E14ResidualDebtItem` contains the actual E14 trigger and concrete
   `StatusedUnresolvedEvidence`, plus a claim-linked carried E14 status record,
   `canonicalStatus`, `statusCase`, and `classifiedResidualLinked`.  Thus the
   exact residual summed by `ClosureDebt` is also the one E14 canonically
   classified `statused_unresolved` after F9 and priority adjudication.  E15
   supplies the missing `D_t` aggregation and connects it to the operating-mode
   theorem.  Nothing remains open on that E14-to-debt link.

2. **F3 is certified input, not invented Lean provenance.**  The repository
   has an inventory-verified F3 source but no carried route-residue structure.
   E15's local record and shared certificate expose this boundary.  A future
   vendored F3 theorem can replace the certificate without changing the debt
   inventory's ledger discipline.

3. **Xi is direct at the matrix level and certified at scalarization.**  The
   matrix must definitionally equal `adequacyResidual`; one shared predeclared
   valuation policy turns it into the rational ledger currency.  Choosing a
   per-instance trace, norm, or price after observing the result is forbidden.

4. **Capacities are derived, not supplied.**  `kappaOn`, `kappaOff`, and
   `freedAllocation` are definitions over one carried allocation record and one
   actual binding `ExposureBudgetWitness`.  The exact capacity-gain equation is
   structural.  Measured discharge remains a separate empirical check.

5. **Finite persistence is the formal scope.**  "Persistently" means every
   online phase in a carried declared horizon has the same computed gross
   accrual rate and lies in the deficit regime.  The law does not smuggle an
   infinite-time theorem into finite records.

6. **E5 suspension is not E15 offline mode.**  An E5 suspension can gate
   operations while keeping the apparatus intact without establishing budget
   reallocation or debt reduction.  E15 therefore defines a stricter
   `GenuineOfflinePhaseEvidence`; an application may prove that one phase is
   both an E5 suspension and an E15 offline phase, but neither type aliases the
   other.

7. **Cascade depth is deliberately limited.**  E15 uses E7/E5's actual status
   vocabularies and requires law-level acceptance certificates on carried
   observations.  It does not derive E7 alarms or E5 stress from a scalar debt
   threshold, because their existing laws require additional probe,
   viability, and closure evidence.

8. **Priority is evidence-level from the first draft.**  Every lower-priority
   case below negates record-independent, claim-scoped `...EvidenceFor`
   predicates.  No case negates a sibling case on the same status record.  A
   status-tag mismatch can never discharge a substantive priority exclusion.

9. **The exchange census is closed-world per schedule.**  Eligibility is the
   full carried, phase-member, nonnegative, in-phase occurrence predicate.
   `CompleteExternalExchangeInventory` is both complete and sound for that
   universe and rejects duplicate records and duplicate record IDs.  Therefore
   zero exchange is computed from an explicit complete census, not omission.

10. **P2 is a lawful move and channel closure is separately linked.**  The
    vendored `Primitive.P2` is usable through `RepairMove.sort.val`, so E15
    requires a carried gate record, an actual `LawfulRepairStep`, and repair-
    generator linkage.  Because P2 names the gating primitive but not the
    external-exchange channel, the one shared `gateClosesExternalExchange`
    comparator supplies only that semantic link.  Zero observed traffic alone
    never proves gating.

11. **Online capacity bounds measured discharge.**  Every deficit-regime
    online flow, including a bounded-online falsifier, satisfies measured
    discharge rate `<= kappaOn`.  The all-online `online_sufficient` branch is
    stronger: actual discharge must offset accrual while remaining within that
    bound.  This prevents a witness from staying bounded by spending capacity
    it does not possess.

12. **Recurrence bounds online extent, not the zero contiguous gap.**
    `ConsecutiveIn` gives an explicit list relation for adjacency.  Each online
    phase must itself satisfy `PhaseDuration phase <= maximumOnlineRun`, and a
    later offline phase must exist.  Optional-offline classification is
    schedule-wide: every offline phase must be genuine, not merely one selected
    favorable phase.

```lean
  record.allocationRecord = claimRef.allocationRecord and
  record.schedule = claimRef.schedule and
  record.toleranceRecord = claimRef.toleranceRecord and
  record.recurrenceBound = claimRef.recurrenceBound

def OfflineReclosureStatusOccurrenceFor statusPolicy claimRef record : Prop :=
  OfflineReclosureStatusRecordMatchesClaim claimRef record and
  exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
    CarriedRecordAt statusPolicy record n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope and
    CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
```

The full key includes debt scope, allocation, schedule, preregistered duty
tolerance, and preregistered recurrence bound.  Two budget geometries,
schedules, tolerances, or recurrence bounds for one debt claim are different
classification claims; their status records cannot satisfy each other's
occurrence predicate.

Every wrapper uses the three-conjunct house discipline:

```lean
structure CompleteOfflineReclosureStatus ... (claimRef : OfflineReclosureClaimRef)
    where
  branchValid :
    DeficitOnlineCounterexampleHolds ... claimRef or
    DecorativeOfflineHolds ... claimRef or
    DutyCycleMismatchHolds ... claimRef or
    SkippedOfflineCascadeHolds ... claimRef or
    AlternationRequiredHolds ... claimRef or
```

Completeness is per full claim.  It does not force unrelated debt horizons,
budgets, schedules, tolerances, or recurrence bounds to share a status.

All theorem targets are verification-shaped.  The main law takes the complete
budget, debt, schedule, and case evidence as independently verified inputs.  It
does not derive F3, Xi valuation honesty, E7 alarm acceptance, E5 stress
acceptance, or empirical discharge from weaker premises.

## 3. Proof Spine

1. **Carry and scope every debt source.**  For one `ClosureDebtScopeRecord`,
   construct E14, F3, and Xi debt items with full D3 source evidence, positive
   exact amounts, time-indexed outstanding/awaiting evidence, and actual
   carried ledger entries.

2. **Reuse E14 without weakening it.**  Extract the amount and ledger entry
   from `StatusedUnresolvedEvidence`.  Preserve its conflict link and exact
   `ledgerEntryChargesResidual` witness, and require the linked carried E14
   status occurrence, canonical `statused_unresolved` tag, full
   `StatusedUnresolvedCase`, and exact full-record residual equality.  A generic
   residual with an E14-like label, or a concrete residual canonically assigned
   another E14 status, cannot enter this component.

3. **Audit F3 and Xi at their explicit boundaries.**  F3 requires the shared
   route-residue certificate.  Xi requires literal `adequacyResidual`, a
   predeclared carried valuation policy, one shared matrix-to-rational
   function, and a real ledger charge.  No per-record comparator is permitted.

4. **Compute closure debt from a complete inventory.**  Prove component
   soundness and coverage, component-key uniqueness, and physical ledger-charge
   uniqueness.  `ClosureDebt` is the sum of entry amounts; a snapshot's stored
   total must equal that sum.

5. **Compute flows and gross accrual.**  Enumerate every newly arrived debt
   entry and every discharge over each positive-duration phase.  Use the exact
   balance equation to link before/after snapshots, and require every discharge
   timestamp to lie inside its linked phase.  Divide the computed arrival sum
   by duration to obtain `dot D`.

6. **Derive capacities from the shared budget.**  Carry the allocation record,
   tie it to a binding `ExposureBudgetWitness`, and establish the online and
   offline budget equations.  Unfolding definitions yields
   `kappaOff = kappaOn + freedAllocation` and, from positive external
   allocation, strict capacity gain.

7. **Recognize the deficit regime.**  Use a complete phase-flow inventory to
   show every online phase has the same computed gross accrual rate and that
   this rate is strictly greater than derived `kappaOn`; independently check
   that each measured online discharge rate is at most `kappaOn`.  Equality
   belongs to the sufficient-capacity regime, not the deficit regime.

8. **Verify genuine offline operation.**  Require carried phase/flow records,
   membership of that exact flow in the claim's complete phase-flow inventory,
   a carried phase-linked lawful P2 repair move whose shared comparator closes
   external exchange, zero exchange from a complete/sound duplicate-free
   census, the exact offline allocation, positive measured discharge, strict
   debt reduction, and a discharge rate no greater than derived `kappaOff`.
   An offline enum tag or an inactive channel proves none of these.

9. **Verify recurrence and duty cycle.**  The carried schedule has at least two
   offline phases, explicitly consecutive contiguous alternating phases, and a
   later offline phase for every online phase.  The recurrence bound applies to
   each online phase's own duration, not the automatically-zero gap to its
   contiguous successor.  For each offline phase, one inventory-member witness
   jointly establishes genuineness, exact `kappaOff` discharge, and zero new
   debt.  For the exact duty model, online and those same offline flows realize
   `kappaOn` and `kappaOff`.
   Compute observed duty from durations and predicted duty from accrual and
   geometry, then compare them under a tolerance declared before the horizon.

10. **Keep the non-deficit boundary lawful and nonexclusive.**  An all-online
    schedule whose measured discharge offsets accrual without exceeding
    `kappaOn` satisfies `online_sufficient`.  A genuine offline schedule under
    the same capacity inequality may satisfy `offline_optional`, but every
    offline phase in that schedule must pass the full P2/census/allocation/
    discharge evidence.  E15 does not forbid such optional phases.

11. **Measure, do not infer, the failure cascade.**  When offline phases are
    skipped and debt grows, require carried chronological alarm and stress
    observations accepted by E7/E5-specific shared comparators.  Each timestamp
    must lie inside the phase named by its observation before their ordering is
    compared.  This proves the E15 cascade branch under supplied cross-law
    evidence, not a new theorem that scalar debt entails those laws.

12. **Normalize priorities by raw evidence.**  For the same full claim,
    exclude every strictly higher `...EvidenceFor` predicate before accepting a
    lower case.  `CompleteOfflineReclosureStatus.statusUnique` then gives the
    eight-way exactly-one result.  The top-priority regression theorem checks
    that genuine counterexample evidence excludes every lower Holds branch.

## 4. Case Enumeration

1. **Deficit-regime lawful alternation.**  All three debt-component inventories
   are complete, online `dot D > kappaOn`, at least two genuine offline phases
   recur, each offline phase owns a carried lawful P2 gate, has zero exchange
   under the complete census, and reduces debt, and the observed duty lies
   within the preregistered tolerance.  Status:
   `alternation_required`.

2. **Online capacity sufficient.**  Every phase remains online, exchange is
   positive, and measured discharge actually offsets each computed accrual rate
   without exceeding `kappaOn`.  Status: `online_sufficient`; no offline phase
   is required.

3. **Optional offline outside deficit.**  Online capacity is sufficient but a
   carried schedule still contains offline phases.  Every one of those phases
   must own a lawful P2 gate, reallocate budget, respect `kappaOff`, and
   discharge debt.  Status: `offline_optional`, demonstrating non-exclusivity.

4. **Deficit online bounded counterexample.**  Every phase is online,
   `dot D > kappaOn` throughout the preregistered necessity-test horizon, and
   every carried snapshot remains below a bound declared before the horizon;
   the horizon is longer than the preregistered maximum online run, and every
   measured online discharge rate remains at or below `kappaOn`.
   Status: `deficit_online_counterexample`; this is the direct necessity
   falsifier and outranks every claimed lawful consequence.

5. **Decorative offline with exchange still active.**  A phase is labelled
   offline but the complete, sound, duplicate-free exchange census has positive
   exchange.  Status: `decorative_offline`.

6. **Decorative offline without discharge.**  Exchange may be zero, but debt
   does not strictly fall or measured discharge is nonpositive.  Status:
   `decorative_offline`, not `alternation_required`.

7. **Decorative offline without exact reallocation.**  The offline allocation
   fails `offlineInternal = onlineInternal + onlineExternal`, or offline
   external allocation is nonzero.  The proposed geometry cannot construct a
   lawful offline witness.  Status: `decorative_offline` if the malformed phase
   is carried and observed; otherwise the claim is rejected at construction.

8. **Duty-cycle mismatch.**  Alternating phases are otherwise genuine, but the
   exact observed duty differs from `PredictedOfflineDuty` by more than the
   tolerance declared before the horizon.  Status: `duty_cycle_mismatch`.

9. **Skipped-offline alarm-to-stress cascade.**  A deficit schedule remains
   online, debt grows, and carried chronological observations are accepted by
   the E7 and E5 bridge comparators, with the E5 observation tagged `stressed`.
   Status: `skipped_offline_cascade`.

10. **All three debt sources aggregate.**  One E14 unresolved residual, one F3
    route residue, and one Xi residual are distinct carried ledger charges.
    `ClosureDebt` equals the exact sum of all three rational amounts.  Dropping
    any sound in-scope item fails inventory completeness.

11. **E14 residual by direct witness only.**  An E14 residual ID and rational
    amount are copied into a local record without an actual
    `StatusedUnresolvedEvidence`.  It cannot construct
    `E14ResidualDebtItem` and contributes zero credited E14 debt.

12. **F3 label without carried reconciliation debt.**  A route residue is
    decodable but lacks the shared F3 certificate, claim link, or ledger charge.
    It cannot enter the complete debt inventory.

13. **Xi matrix with post-hoc scalarization.**  The matrix is a genuine
    `adequacyResidual`, but its valuation policy was declared after the horizon
    or its amount differs from the shared valuation function.  It cannot enter
    Xi debt.

14. **Xi amount without Xi matrix.**  A positive ledger amount has no source
    matrices or `residualComputed` equality.  It is not an Xi adequacy residual.

15. **Duplicate physical ledger charge.**  One ledger entry is presented as
    both an F3 and Xi debt item.  Component keys differ, but
    `ledgerChargesNodup` fails, preventing double counting.

16. **Free-capacity smuggling attempt.**  A host supplies convenient
    `kappaOn`/`kappaOff` rationals without a carried allocation and binding
    `ExposureBudgetWitness`.  No `DerivedOfflineBudgetGeometry` can be built;
    no positive E15 status can credit those capacities.

17. **Capacity-gain equation control.**  With online external allocation `f >
    0`, `kappaOff` must equal `kappaOn + f` exactly.  A larger or smaller fitted
    gain fails geometry even if it predicts the observed duty better.

18. **Gross accrual versus net change.**  New debt of 5 and concurrent
    discharge of 3 over duration 1 gives `dot D = 5`, discharge rate 3, and net
    debt change 2.  Classifying the regime from 2 instead of 5 fails the flow
    definition.

19. **Deficit-boundary equality.**  `dot D = kappaOn` is not strict deficit.
    An all-online trace may be `online_sufficient`; E15 does not force
    alternation at equality.

20. **Incomplete phase-flow inventory.**  One declared online phase has no
    unique complete flow.  Persistent deficit and any duty-cycle theorem fail;
    a label claiming alternation reaches `offline_reclosure_rejected` unless a
    higher positive obstruction applies.

21. **Single offline episode, not recurrence.**  A long schedule contains one
    valid offline phase but fails `atLeastTwoOfflinePhases`, lacks a later
    offline phase, or has an online phase whose own duration exceeds
    `maximumOnlineRun`.  It is not the recurring offline mode claimed by E15.

22. **Duty tolerance declared post hoc.**  The observed duty can be made to fit
    only by widening tolerance after the horizon starts.  Both positive duty
    evidence and mismatch evidence reject that tolerance record.

23. **Claim-scoping control.**  The same debt scope under two different budget
    allocations, schedules, tolerances, or recurrence bounds has two different
    full `OfflineReclosureClaimRef`s.  Status uniqueness is internal to each
    claim; evidence from one schedule cannot classify the other.

24. **Status-tag priority control.**  Genuine
    `DeficitOnlineCounterexampleEvidenceFor` exists, while a second carried
    status record is tagged `alternation_required`.  The latter cannot satisfy
    `AlternationRequiredCase` because it must negate the raw counterexample
    evidence.  A different status tag does not make that negation true.

25. **Alarm label without E7 acceptance.**  A carried observation stores an
    `AlarmDispositionKind` but `e7AlarmObservationAccepted` fails.  The cascade
    branch cannot fire.

26. **Stress label without E5 acceptance.**  A carried status tag says
    `stressed`, but the E5 comparator does not accept the observation under its
    own apparatus.  The cascade branch cannot fire.

27. **Wrong cascade chronology or lineage.**  Stress precedes alarm, either
    timestamp lies outside the phase named by its observation, or either
    observation belongs to another debt scope/schedule.  It cannot witness the
    E15 cascade.

28. **E5 suspension without E15 discharge.**  An apparatus is intact and
    operations are gated under E5, but no complete E15 exchange census,
    reallocation, or debt reduction exists.  It may be an E5 suspension but is
    not an E15 offline phase.

29. **Offline discharge beyond derived capacity.**  A fixture claims a
    discharge rate greater than `kappaOff`.  It positively satisfies the
    over-capacity disjunct of `DecorativeOfflineEvidence`.  Because
    `OptionalOfflineOutsideDeficitEvidence` requires every offline phase to be
    genuine, one separate valid phase cannot hide this malformed phase.  Status:
    `decorative_offline`, not `offline_optional`.

30. **No substantive branch.**  A carried status claim has incomplete debt,
    budget, or schedule evidence and no positive obstruction witness.  Status:
    `offline_reclosure_rejected`, explicitly a catch-all rather than a causal
    diagnosis.

31. **Exchange omission/duplication control.**  Omitting a carried eligible
    positive exchange from an offline phase violates `completeForSchedule`;
    repeating an exchange record or ID violates the corresponding `Nodup`
    field.  Neither malformed census can establish zero exchange or distort a
    phase's exchange total.

32. **Zero exchange without P2 gating.**  The complete census reports zero,
    but no carried phase-linked lawful P2 move accepted by
    `gateClosesExternalExchange` exists.  `GenuineOfflinePhaseEvidence` cannot
    be constructed: inactivity is not a gate.  Absent another positive
    obstruction, the claim reaches `offline_reclosure_rejected`.

Round A must cover all eight statuses and the controls in items 10-32.  It must
compute exact sums, phase durations, rates, capacity projections, exchange
censuses, and duty fractions from finite fixture data.  Expected statuses may
not be stored as fixture booleans or selected from a lookup table.

## 6. Nonclaims

1. E15 does not claim that offline modes occur only in deficit systems.
   `offline_optional` explicitly permits genuine offline operation when online
   capacity is sufficient.

2. E15 does not claim that every system must go offline.  Necessity is scoped
   to a carried declared horizon with persistent strict
   `dot D > kappaOn`.  Equality and surplus capacity belong to the lawful
   online branch.

3. E15 does not claim an infinite-time theorem from finite data.  The direct
   counterexample and persistence tests use preregistered finite horizons and
   must be rerun for any extension.

4. E15 does not identify an offline label with offline reclosure.  Exchange
   gating requires a carried lawful P2 move linked to the external channel;
   zero traffic from the complete exchange census is only the separate observed
   effect.  Exact freed-budget reallocation, positive measured discharge,
   strict debt reduction, and the capacity bound are also independently
   required.

5. E15 does not allow `kappaOn`, `kappaOff`, debt, accrual rate, observed duty,
   predicted duty, or tolerance to be fit post hoc.  Capacities are projections
   of a carried allocation, debt and rates are finite computations, and the
   valuation/tolerance/recurrence/allocation records must predate the horizon.

6. E15 does not supply a canonical scalar norm for Xi residual matrices.  It
   requires a shared, carried, predeclared valuation policy and records the
   exact matrix-to-ledger link.

7. E15 does not reprove F3, Xi strict-extension results, E7, or E5.  It consumes
   their actual or certified interfaces and keeps the limits of each bridge
   explicit.

8. E15 does not prove that growing closure debt alone causes an E7 alarm or E5
   stress.  The cascade theorem requires carried observations independently
   accepted by those laws and proves only their E15-scoped chronology.

9. E15 does not prove collapse after stress, even though the toy-lab probe may
   count stress-to-collapse outcomes.  A collapse-rate theorem would require
   E5's full collapse apparatus and an additional bridge.

10. E15 makes no claim about sleep, animal or human biology, subjective
    experience, or clinical memory consolidation.  Its scope is operational:
    societies, economics, institutions, and artificial cognitive agents with
    explicit carried ledgers and measurable budget schedules.

11. E15 does not claim one universal duty-cycle formula outside the declared
    budget model.  The stated formula assumes external debt accrual is gated
    offline and uses the exact rational geometry recorded for the claim.

12. E15 does not treat duplicate labels or IDs as distinct debt.  Physical
    ledger-charge uniqueness and direct record links are required before an
    amount enters `D_t`.
