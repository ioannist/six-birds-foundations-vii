# E13. Repair Transport Law

This is the six-field normal-form draft for `THEOREMS.md` E13, "Repair
Transport Law (communication, teaching, and symbols as transported repair)"
(`THEOREMS.md:367-390`).  The eventual Lean target is
`SixBirdsFoundationsV.Laws.E13RepairTransport`.

E13 is the first law in this landing sequence that is explicitly two-carrier:
it classifies repair transported from an emitting E-system `A` into a receiving
E-system `B`, rather than activity wholly inside one `ESystem`.  The directly
grounded reuse is D3/D4 carried records and repair moves, E1-style `Delta`
split-pair reduction for the receiver-side repair, and the already-landed
source-tag discipline around `FineSourceTag.committed_state`.  FIV F17/F29/F30
are recorded in the imported-foundations inventory
(`formalization/inventory/imported_foundations.yml:203-249`) but are not
vendored Lean modules, so they enter here as certified host interfaces.
F22 is named by the source text (`THEOREMS.md:378`) but is not present in the
vendored Lean tree or imported-foundations inventory found by grep in this
round; it is also treated as certified host input.  The Institutions paper's
coercion-null/probe-memory/material-forcing content is inventory-backed
(`formalization/inventory/imported_foundations.yml:305-314`,
`:449-457`) but not vendored Lean, so E13 makes it concrete through carried
forcing/free-response records rather than deriving it locally.  Xi has a
vendored data-processing/transfer substrate (`Xi.DataProcessing`, especially
`lean/vendor/foundations/xi/Xi/DataProcessing.lean:9-16` and `:327-395`), but
no landed law yet supplies a bridge between two distinct `ESystem` instances;
E13 therefore introduces a certified inter-carrier bridge with paid defects as
new first-of-kind vocabulary.

## 1. Setup

### Shared carriers and records

E13 is parameterized by two independent E-systems, not one:

```lean
variable
  {FDataA RuleFamilyA ResidualFamilyA AuditAccessDataA : Type}
  {InstrumentRecordA LedgerEntryA DefectRecordA MovePayloadA MoveRecordA AuditRecordA : Type}
  {FDataB RuleFamilyB ResidualFamilyB AuditAccessDataB : Type}
  {InstrumentRecordB LedgerEntryB DefectRecordB MovePayloadB MoveRecordB AuditRecordB : Type}
  (S_A :
    ESystem FDataA RuleFamilyA ResidualFamilyA AuditAccessDataA
      InstrumentRecordA LedgerEntryA DefectRecordA MovePayloadA MoveRecordA AuditRecordA)
  (S_B :
    ESystem FDataB RuleFamilyB ResidualFamilyB AuditAccessDataB
      InstrumentRecordB LedgerEntryB DefectRecordB MovePayloadB MoveRecordB AuditRecordB)
```

The record surface is deliberately data-heavy.  Every certifying witness is
owned by the object being classified or by the bridge between the objects, not
supplied as an unrelated theorem parameter.

```lean
inductive TransportClaimKind where
  | communication
  | teaching
  | symbol

structure ChallengeClassRecord where
  challengeId : ChallengeClassId
  taxonomyId : TaxonomyId

structure RepairRoleRecord where
  roleId : RepairRoleId
  challengeClass : ChallengeClassRecord
  packageKind : RepairPackageKind

structure TransportTokenRecord where
  tokenId : TokenId
  sourceCarrier : CarrierName
  targetCarrier : CarrierName
  emittedFor : ChallengeClassRecord
  sourcePackageRef : SourcePackageId

structure SourceRepairPackageRecord where
  packageId : PackageId
  sourceChallenge : ChallengeClassRecord
  roleRecord : RepairRoleRecord
  emittedToken : TransportTokenRecord

structure TargetRepairRecord where
  targetRepairId : TargetRepairId
  receiptToken : TransportTokenRecord
  targetChallenge : ChallengeClassRecord
  inducedMove :
    RepairMove S_B.T MovePayloadB LedgerEntryB MoveRecordB S_B.moveRecordPolicy
  roleRecord : RepairRoleRecord

structure BridgeDefectRecord where
  defectId : BridgeDefectId
  bridgeRef : BridgeId
  sourceLedgerEntry : LedgerEntryA
  targetLedgerEntry : LedgerEntryB

  bridgeId : BridgeId
  sourceCarrier : CarrierName
  targetCarrier : CarrierName
  channelRecord : BridgeChannelRecord
```

`InterCarrierBridge` is the first E-series bridge between two distinct
`ESystem` instances.  Its "defects paid" clause is not a naked boolean: it is a
pair of carried ledger memberships plus a bridge-defect record whose own
bridge reference is linked to this bridge.  The discharge check is computed
through a shared comparator over the defect and the two ledger entries, rather
than a `discharged = true` flag stored on the defect record.  This mirrors the
same hardcoded-stand-in fix class that E8's Round B sweep needed for proxy,
lineage, and residual-explanation witnesses.

```lean
structure BridgeDischargeComparator where
  discharges :
    BridgeDefectRecord -> LedgerEntryA -> LedgerEntryB -> Prop

structure BridgeDischargeCertificate
    (comparator : BridgeDischargeComparator)
    (defectRecord : BridgeDefectRecord) where
  sourceEntry : LedgerEntryA
  targetEntry : LedgerEntryB
  sourceEntryLinked : defectRecord.sourceLedgerEntry = sourceEntry
  targetEntryLinked : defectRecord.targetLedgerEntry = targetEntry
  paidByLedgerData :
    comparator.discharges defectRecord sourceEntry targetEntry

structure BridgeDefectsPaid
    (comparator : BridgeDischargeComparator)
    (bridge : InterCarrierBridgeRecord) where
  defectRecord : BridgeDefectRecord
  defectLinked : defectRecord.bridgeRef = bridge.bridgeId
  sourceEntryInLedger : defectRecord.sourceLedgerEntry in S_A.Lambda_S.ledgerEntries
  targetEntryInLedger : defectRecord.targetLedgerEntry in S_B.Lambda_S.ledgerEntries
  sourceEntryCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt sourceLedgerPolicy defectRecord.sourceLedgerEntry
        n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  targetEntryCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt targetLedgerPolicy defectRecord.targetLedgerEntry
        n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  dischargeCertificate :
    BridgeDischargeCertificate comparator defectRecord

structure InterCarrierBridge
    (dischargeComparator : BridgeDischargeComparator)
    (S_A : ESystem ...)
    (S_B : ESystem ...) where
  bridgeRecord : InterCarrierBridgeRecord
  sourceMatches : bridgeRecord.sourceCarrier = CarrierNameOf S_A
  targetMatches : bridgeRecord.targetCarrier = CarrierNameOf S_B
  bridgeCarriedByA :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt bridgePolicyA bridgeRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  bridgeCarriedByB :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt bridgePolicyB bridgeRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  defectsPaid : BridgeDefectsPaid dischargeComparator bridgeRecord
```

This certified bridge is intentionally not the same object as FIII's
single-stack `TopDownChannelRecord` and not the same object as Xi's matrix
bridge theorem.  Xi `exactReducingBridge`/`defectiveBridge` can justify a
matrix substrate when a transport proof instantiates those matrices, but E13's
classified object is an audited repair transported from `S_A` to `S_B`.

### Source taxonomy and same-class alignment

The source text warns that "same challenge class" needs a declared taxonomy
(`THEOREMS.md:386`).  E13 therefore does not compare challenge-class names by
string equality alone.

```lean
structure DeclaredTransportTaxonomy where
  taxonomyRecord : TaxonomyRecord
  classRecords : List ChallengeClassRecord
  nonempty : classRecords.length > 0
  carried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt taxonomyPolicy taxonomyRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope

structure SameTransportChallengeClass
    (taxonomy : DeclaredTransportTaxonomy)
    (sourceClass targetClass : ChallengeClassRecord) where
  sourceDeclared : sourceClass in taxonomy.classRecords
  targetDeclared : targetClass in taxonomy.classRecords
  sameTaxonomy : sourceClass.taxonomyId = targetClass.taxonomyId
  sameClassId : sourceClass.challengeId = targetClass.challengeId
```

Behavioral change in a different declared class is still transport/influence
data, but it is not communication about the claimed class.

The F22/F17 "plumbing" named by the source text is represented by a certified
interface record over the same bridge and taxonomy.  This avoids treating the
transport channel as an untyped parameter pipe.

```lean
structure InterfaceMediationCertified
    (dischargeComparator : BridgeDischargeComparator)
    (bridge : InterCarrierBridge dischargeComparator S_A S_B)
    (taxonomy : DeclaredTransportTaxonomy) where
  sourceAccessRecord : InterfaceAccessRecordA
  targetAccessRecord : InterfaceAccessRecordB
  sourceAccessCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt sourceAccessPolicy sourceAccessRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  targetAccessCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt targetAccessPolicy targetAccessRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  bridgeLinked :
    sourceAccessRecord.bridgeRef = bridge.bridgeRecord and
    targetAccessRecord.bridgeRef = bridge.bridgeRecord
  taxonomyLinked :
    sourceAccessRecord.taxonomyRef = taxonomy.taxonomyRecord and
    targetAccessRecord.taxonomyRef = taxonomy.taxonomyRecord
```

### B-side induced repair and strict Delta reduction

E13 reuses E1's `Delta` shape (`E1Internalization.lean:18`) but applies it to
the receiver-side quotient before and after the transported repair.

```lean
structure ReceiverQuotientInstallCertified where
  installs :
    forall {X Q Q' : Type},
      RepairMove S_B.T MovePayloadB LedgerEntryB MoveRecordB S_B.moveRecordPolicy ->
      (X -> Q) -> (X -> Q') -> Prop

structure TransportDeltaReduction
    (targetRepair : TargetRepairRecord)
    (install : ReceiverQuotientInstallCertified)
    {X Q Q' FOut Readout : Type}
    (qBefore : X -> Q)
    (qAfter : X -> Q')
    (F_C : X -> FOut)
    (r_B : FOut -> Readout) where
  afterQuotientInstalledByMove :
    install.installs targetRepair.inducedMove qBefore qAfter
  subset :
    forall x x',
      Delta qAfter F_C r_B x x' -> Delta qBefore F_C r_B x x'
  strict :
    exists x x',
      Delta qBefore F_C r_B x x' and not Delta qAfter F_C r_B x x'
```

The strict witness is now data-linked to the target repair record: `qAfter`
must be certified as the quotient installed by `targetRepair.inducedMove`.
This is the E13 version of the E4 v24 quotient-linkage fix; a host cannot pick
an unrelated easy quotient merely to make the `Delta` inequality true.

```lean
structure InducedBRepair
    (dischargeComparator : BridgeDischargeComparator)
    (bridge : InterCarrierBridge dischargeComparator S_A S_B)
    (taxonomy : DeclaredTransportTaxonomy)
    (sourcePackage : SourceRepairPackageRecord)
    (token : TransportTokenRecord)
    (targetRepair : TargetRepairRecord)
    (install : ReceiverQuotientInstallCertified)
    {X Q Q' FOut Readout : Type}
    (qBefore : X -> Q)
    (qAfter : X -> Q')
    (F_C : X -> FOut)
    (r_B : FOut -> Readout) where
  tokenLinked : sourcePackage.emittedToken = token
  repairReceiptLinked : targetRepair.receiptToken = token
  bridgeLinked :
    bridge.bridgeRecord.sourceCarrier = token.sourceCarrier and
    bridge.bridgeRecord.targetCarrier = token.targetCarrier
  sameClass :
    SameTransportChallengeClass taxonomy
      sourcePackage.sourceChallenge targetRepair.targetChallenge
  targetMoveCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt targetRepairPolicy targetRepair n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  targetMoveLawful :
    exists z z' defect auditRecord,
      LawfulRepairStep S_B.Lambda_S S_B.defectRecordPolicy S_B.moveRecordPolicy
        S_B.auditRecordPolicy S_B.I_S S_B.AdmissibleMove
        z z' defect targetRepair.inducedMove auditRecord
  deltaReduction :
    TransportDeltaReduction targetRepair install qBefore qAfter F_C r_B
```

### Role-preserving transport

F29 presentation invariance is applied to repair-package role, not token
shape.  Since F29 is inventory-backed but unvendored, this is a certified
interface whose predicate is tied to concrete source and target role records.

```lean
structure RoleTransportComparator where
  preservesRole :
    SourceRepairPackageRecord -> TargetRepairRecord -> RepairRoleRecord -> Prop

structure RolePreservingTransportCertified
    (comparator : RoleTransportComparator)
    (sourcePackage : SourceRepairPackageRecord)
    (targetRepair : TargetRepairRecord) where
  sourceRoleLinked : sourcePackage.roleRecord.challengeClass = sourcePackage.sourceChallenge
  targetRoleLinked : targetRepair.roleRecord.challengeClass = targetRepair.targetChallenge
  sameRoleRecord : sourcePackage.roleRecord.roleId = targetRepair.roleRecord.roleId
  certified :
    comparator.preservesRole sourcePackage targetRepair sourcePackage.roleRecord
```

This is the anti-token-semantics guard: an isomorphic or translated signal
form is irrelevant unless the B-side repair has the same structural role under
B's audit.

```lean
structure BRoleStateRecord where
  stateId : RoleStateId
  activeRoles : List RepairRoleRecord
  contextFamilyRef : ContextFamilyRecord

structure RoleActivationComparator where
  roleActiveBefore :
    BRoleStateRecord -> RepairRoleRecord -> ContextFamilyRecord -> Prop
  roleActiveAfter :
    BRoleStateRecord -> RepairRoleRecord -> ContextFamilyRecord -> Prop

inductive ResponseMode where
  | observed_free_response
  | fully_forced_response
  | scaffolded_response
  | primed_response

structure ForceOpportunityComparator where
  classifies :
    ForceScheduleRecord -> List FreeResponseOpportunityRecord -> ResponseMode -> Prop
```

The classifier uses one shared context for a given transport-classification
run.  The taxonomy, role comparator, bridge-discharge comparator,
quotient-install certificate, and force/opportunity comparator are not chosen
freshly by individual case witnesses; this applies the same fix pattern as
E8's `SummaryLegitimacyComparators` review fix.

```lean
structure RepairTransportClassifierContext where
  taxonomy : DeclaredTransportTaxonomy
  roleComparator : RoleTransportComparator
  activationComparator : RoleActivationComparator
  bridgeDischargeComparator : BridgeDischargeComparator
  quotientInstall : ReceiverQuotientInstallCertified
  forceComparator : ForceOpportunityComparator
```

### A-absence capacity and teaching

Teaching is communication plus a later B-owned capacity test.  The later repair
must be carried by `B` as `committed_state`, generated by `B`, in `A`'s absence.
The absence condition is represented by a concrete probe schedule, not by a
theorist-declared flag.

```lean
structure AAbsenceProbeSchedule where
  scheduleRecord : ProbeScheduleRecord
  challengeClass : ChallengeClassRecord
  probeTimes : List Nat
  nonempty : probeTimes.length > 0
  aCarrierPresentAt : Nat -> Bool
  bridgeOpenAt : Nat -> Bool
  absenceAtEveryProbe :
    forall t, t in probeTimes -> aCarrierPresentAt t = false and bridgeOpenAt t = false
  carried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt schedulePolicy scheduleRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope

structure BCommittedRepairInAAbsence
    (taxonomy : DeclaredTransportTaxonomy)
    (schedule : AAbsenceProbeSchedule)
    (targetRepair : TargetRepairRecord)
    (install : ReceiverQuotientInstallCertified)
    {X Q Q' FOut Readout : Type}
    (qBefore : X -> Q)
    (qAfter : X -> Q')
    (F_C : X -> FOut)
    (r_B : FOut -> Readout) where
  repairInSameClass :
    SameTransportChallengeClass taxonomy
      schedule.challengeClass targetRepair.targetChallenge
  repairTime : Nat
  repairTimeInSchedule : repairTime in schedule.probeTimes
  sourceIsBCommitted :
    CarriedRecordAt targetRepairPolicy targetRepair repairTime
      FineSourceTag.committed_state true true
  lawfulRepair :
    exists z z' defect auditRecord,
      LawfulRepairStep S_B.Lambda_S S_B.defectRecordPolicy S_B.moveRecordPolicy
        S_B.auditRecordPolicy S_B.I_S S_B.AdmissibleMove
        z z' defect targetRepair.inducedMove auditRecord
  descendsAgain :
    TransportDeltaReduction targetRepair install qBefore qAfter F_C r_B

structure TeachingCapacityEvidence
    (ctx : RepairTransportClassifierContext)
    (bridge : InterCarrierBridge ctx.bridgeDischargeComparator S_A S_B)
    (sourcePackage : SourceRepairPackageRecord)
    (token : TransportTokenRecord)
    (initialRepair : TargetRepairRecord)
    {X0 Q0 Q0' FOut0 Readout0 : Type}
    (qBeforeInitial : X0 -> Q0)
    (qAfterInitial : X0 -> Q0')
    (F_initial : X0 -> FOut0)
    (r_initial : FOut0 -> Readout0)
    (communication :
      CommunicationTransportEvidence ctx bridge sourcePackage token initialRepair
        qBeforeInitial qAfterInitial F_initial r_initial)
    (schedule : AAbsenceProbeSchedule)
    {X Q Q' FOut Readout : Type}
    (qBeforeAAbsent : X -> Q)
    (qAfterAAbsent : X -> Q')
    (F_C : X -> FOut)
    (r_B : FOut -> Readout) where
  subsequentRepair : TargetRepairRecord
  tokenLinked : sourcePackage.emittedToken = token
  communicationSameClass :
    SameTransportChallengeClass ctx.taxonomy
      sourcePackage.sourceChallenge initialRepair.targetChallenge
  communicationBridgeLinked :
    bridge.bridgeRecord.sourceCarrier = token.sourceCarrier and
    bridge.bridgeRecord.targetCarrier = token.targetCarrier
  bCommittedCapacity :
    BCommittedRepairInAAbsence ctx.taxonomy schedule subsequentRepair
      ctx.quotientInstall qBeforeAAbsent qAfterAAbsent F_C r_B
  sameChallengeAsSourcePackage :
    SameTransportChallengeClass ctx.taxonomy
      sourcePackage.sourceChallenge subsequentRepair.targetChallenge
  capacityLinkedToPackage :
    subsequentRepair.roleRecord.roleId =
      sourcePackage.roleRecord.roleId
```

Scaffolding and priming are failures of this capacity criterion: they may
improve behavior while `A` or an environmental arrangement is present, but no
later `B`-committed, A-absent repair exists in the same class.

### Forced/free response and coercion-null

The coercion-null clause is load-bearing for E13 and is expected to be the
first law where the null battery's coercion-null item becomes applicable.  The
forced/free distinction is not a bare boolean.  It is a source-classified
interaction record with a carried free-response opportunity census.

```lean
structure InteractionForcingRecord where
  interactionId : InteractionId
  token : TransportTokenRecord
  targetCarrier : CarrierName
  responseMode : ResponseMode
  forceSchedule : ForceScheduleRecord

structure FreeResponseOpportunityCensus where
  censusRecord : FreeResponseCensusRecord
  interaction : InteractionForcingRecord
  forceSchedule : ForceScheduleRecord
  opportunities : List FreeResponseOpportunityRecord
  scheduleLinked : forceSchedule = interaction.forceSchedule
  carried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt censusPolicy censusRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope

structure FullyForcedInteraction
    (forceComparator : ForceOpportunityComparator) where
  forcingRecord : InteractionForcingRecord
  opportunityCensus : FreeResponseOpportunityCensus
  censusLinked : opportunityCensus.interaction = forcingRecord
  forcingCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt forcingPolicy forcingRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  responseModeComputed :
    forceComparator.classifies forcingRecord.forceSchedule
      opportunityCensus.opportunities forcingRecord.responseMode
  responseModeLinked : forcingRecord.responseMode = ResponseMode.fully_forced_response
  noFreeOpportunity : opportunityCensus.opportunities = []
```

The Institutions-paper coercion-null/probe-memory content is then encoded as a
certified interface tied to the interaction, token, and target repair surface.

```lean
structure CoercionNullCertified
    (forceComparator : ForceOpportunityComparator)
    (forced : FullyForcedInteraction forceComparator)
    (token : TransportTokenRecord)
    {X Q Q' FOut Readout : Type}
    (qBeforeForce : X -> Q)
    (qAfterForce : X -> Q')
    (F_C : X -> FOut)
    (r_B : FOut -> Readout) where
  tokenLinked : forced.forcingRecord.token = token
  carriedCensusWitness : forced.opportunityCensus.carried
  forcedClassificationComputed : forced.responseModeComputed
  noBCommittedTransportedRepair :
    not exists targetRepair : TargetRepairRecord,
      targetRepair.receiptToken = token and
      exists t, CarriedRecordAt targetRepairPolicy targetRepair t
        FineSourceTag.committed_state true true
  targetQuotientUnrefined :
    forall x x',
      Delta qAfterForce F_C r_B x x' <-> Delta qBeforeForce F_C r_B x x'
```

This is the planned toy-lab guard: free observed response can write
transported repair, but pure force cannot.  A protocol that merely observes
compliance under force must classify as `coercion_null`, not `taught`.

### Scaffolding and priming controls

```lean
structure ScaffoldedOrPrimedInteraction
    (forceComparator : ForceOpportunityComparator) where
  interaction : InteractionForcingRecord
  opportunityCensus : FreeResponseOpportunityCensus
  censusLinked : opportunityCensus.interaction = interaction
  interactionCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt forcingPolicy interaction n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
        and CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS inScope
  responseModeComputed :
    forceComparator.classifies interaction.forceSchedule
      opportunityCensus.opportunities interaction.responseMode
  modeIsScaffoldOrPrime :
    interaction.responseMode = ResponseMode.scaffolded_response or
    interaction.responseMode = ResponseMode.primed_response

structure ScaffoldingOrPrimingWitness
    (forceComparator : ForceOpportunityComparator)
    (token : TransportTokenRecord)
    (sourcePackage : SourceRepairPackageRecord)
    (claimClass : ChallengeClassRecord)
    (taxonomy : DeclaredTransportTaxonomy)
    (install : ReceiverQuotientInstallCertified)
    {X Q Q' FOut Readout : Type}
    (qBeforeWithAid : X -> Q)
    (qAfterWithAid : X -> Q')
    (F_C : X -> FOut)
    (r_B : FOut -> Readout) where
  scaffoldedOrPrimed :
    ScaffoldedOrPrimedInteraction forceComparator
  tokenLinked : scaffoldedOrPrimed.interaction.token = token
  claimClassLinked :
    SameTransportChallengeClass taxonomy sourcePackage.sourceChallenge claimClass
  responseModeComputed : scaffoldedOrPrimed.responseModeComputed
  behaviorImprovesWhilePresent :
    exists targetRepair : TargetRepairRecord,
      targetRepair.receiptToken = token and
      SameTransportChallengeClass taxonomy claimClass targetRepair.targetChallenge and
      TransportDeltaReduction targetRepair install qBeforeWithAid qAfterWithAid F_C r_B
  noAAbsentBCommittedCapacity :
    not exists schedule targetRepair,
      SameTransportChallengeClass taxonomy
        sourcePackage.sourceChallenge targetRepair.targetChallenge and
      targetRepair.roleRecord.roleId = sourcePackage.roleRecord.roleId and
      BCommittedRepairInAAbsence taxonomy schedule targetRepair install
        qBeforeWithAid qAfterWithAid F_C r_B
```

The negative result is source-classified: the setup aided B, but did not
transfer repair capacity to B's own committed state in A's absence.  The
`modeIsScaffoldOrPrime` equality is intentionally downstream of
`responseModeComputed`; the fixture may not classify an interaction as
scaffolded or primed without the shared comparator computing that response
mode from the carried schedule and opportunity census.

### Symbols and context-family reactivation

Symbols are compressed repair handles, not mere labels.  The declared context
family has a structural non-vacuity guard.

```lean
structure TransportContextRecord where
  contextId : ContextId
  challengeClass : ChallengeClassRecord
  contextReadout : ContextReadoutRecord

structure DeclaredTransportContextFamily where
  familyRecord : ContextFamilyRecord
  contexts : List TransportContextRecord
```

## 2. Theorem

### Case predicates

Priority order is:

```text
coercion_null > symbolic > taught > communicated >
  scaffolded_or_primed > influence_only > transport_rejected
```

`coercion_null` is a priority override because a fully forced interaction
cannot write transported repair even if compliance-like behavior is observed.
`symbolic` is checked before ordinary teaching/communication because it is a
cross-context reactivation claim, not a one-context receipt claim.  `taught`
is checked before `communicated` because teaching is communication plus
A-absent B capacity.  `transport_rejected` is the unconditional catch-all.

The snippets below suppress long binder lists in the same way the E4/E5 first
drafts did.  Any quotient/readout/taxonomy/comparator name appearing in a case
body (`qBefore`, `F_C`, `taxonomy`, `roleComparator`, and so on) is intended as
an explicit parameter of that case predicate in the Lean mechanization, not a
free global fact.

```lean
      record.targetRepairRecord = some targetRepair

          record.tokenRecord record.sourcePackageRecord bridgeRecord targetRepair.targetChallenge
        and exists bridge : InterCarrierBridge ctx.bridgeDischargeComparator S_A S_B,
          bridge.bridgeRecord = bridgeRecord
            and exists communication :
              CommunicationTransportEvidence ctx bridge record.sourcePackageRecord
                record.tokenRecord targetRepair qBefore qAfter F_C r_B,
              exists schedule,
                exists teaching :
                  TeachingCapacityEvidence ctx bridge record.sourcePackageRecord
                    record.tokenRecord targetRepair qBefore qAfter F_C r_B

    and not SymbolicEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and not TaughtEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef

          and record.bridgeRecord = some bridge.bridgeRecord

    and not SymbolicEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and not TaughtEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and not CommunicatedEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and record.status = RepairTransportStatus.scaffolded_or_primed
    and TransportClaimAllowsScaffoldOrPrime claimRef

    and not CoercionNullEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and not SymbolicEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and not TaughtEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and not CommunicatedEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and not ScaffoldedOrPrimedEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef

    and not SymbolicEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and not TaughtEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and not CommunicatedEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and not ScaffoldedOrPrimedEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and not InfluenceOnlyEvidenceFor S_A S_B policies ctx claimRef
    and record.status = RepairTransportStatus.transport_rejected
```

The apparent redundancy in the priority exclusions is intentional.  Every
exclusion is a claim-scoped, record-independent `...EvidenceFor` predicate,
so a lower status tag cannot make it vacuously true. Some exclusions are
load-bearing (`coercion_null` vs. communication/teaching,
`taught` vs. `communicated`, `symbolic` vs. one-context communication).  Others
are defensive clarity: once same-class alignment and A-absence capacity are
properly scoped, several lower branches are already mutually exclusive by
their positive evidence, but the explicit evidence exclusions make the
partition proof mechanical.

## 3. Proof Spine

1. Establish two-carrier carriedness.  Every credited token, source package,
   bridge, bridge-defect ledger entry, B-side induced repair, A-absence probe
   schedule, forcing record, context family, and status record must have
   `CarriedRecordAt` evidence with `CarriedSource sourceTag generatedByS
   inScope`.  This is the D3/D4 spine; no uncarried bridge or token can enter a
   positive status.

2. Use bridge paidness before repair credit.  `CommunicationTransportEvidence`
   can be constructed only through `InterCarrierBridge`, whose `defectsPaid`
   field ties both A and B ledgers to a bridge defect record and a
   comparator-backed `BridgeDischargeCertificate`.  This is where the
   "Xi/Needles transfer discipline" is represented at E-system level.  The
   vendored Xi transfer theorems are matrix-level support, not a replacement for
   the E-system carried bridge.

3. Prove strict receiver-side improvement by `Delta` subset plus witness.
   `TransportDeltaReduction` mirrors the E1 convention: a strict reduction is
   not a bare cardinality decrease and not a global "B improved" proposition.
   It is tied to the concrete `TargetRepairRecord` by requiring `qAfter` to be
   installed by `targetRepair.inducedMove`.

4. Apply same-class alignment.  A token that changes B in another challenge
   class is classified as `influence_only`, not `communicated`, even if the
   behavioral effect is real.

5. Apply F29 role preservation as a certified comparator over repair-package
   roles, and require F22/F17 interface mediation as a field of
   `CommunicationTransportEvidence`.  Presentation changes in signal form do
   not matter; role drift in the induced repair does.

6. For teaching, add the A-absence capacity test.  The later B repair must be
   carried by B as `committed_state` at a probe time where the probe schedule
   records A absent and the bridge closed.  This is the source-class upgrade
   named by `THEOREMS.md:496`.

7. For coercion-null, force wins early.  A fully forced interaction whose
   response mode is computed from the carried force schedule/opportunity census,
   with no free-response opportunity and no B-committed transported repair, has
   `coercion_null` priority.  This is descriptive status logic, not a normative
   claim.

8. For symbols, quantify across a declared context family.  Non-vacuity is on
   the family type (`atLeastTwoContexts`).  Every context needs reactivation,
   roles must be stable, and saturation-strictness rules out relabeling current
   structure.

9. Partition by carried status record.  `CompleteRepairTransportStatus` is
   scoped to one `TransportClaimRef`; status uniqueness is per claim, not
   global over all tokens or bridges.

## 4. Case Enumeration

1. **Clean communication.**  A emits token `s` for class `C`; B receives it
   through a carried paid bridge; the induced B repair is lawful, carried,
   same-class, strict in `Delta`, and role-preserving.  Status:
   `communicated`.

2. **Different-class influence.**  Token receipt causes a carried, lawful,
   bridge-linked strict B-side repair, but the target repair is in `C' != C`
   under the declared taxonomy.  Status: `influence_only`, not
   `communicated`.  Raw uncarried quotient improvement is not enough for this
   branch.

3. **Teaching by A-absent capacity.**  Communication holds for the same token,
   source package, paid bridge, and source challenge named by the teaching
   claim, and a later B repair in the same class is carried as
   `committed_state` during an A-absent, bridge-closed probe.  Status:
   `taught`.

4. **Scaffolding.**  B improves while A remains present or an environmental aid
   remains active; the shared force/opportunity comparator computes a
   scaffolded response from the carried schedule/census, but no later
   B-committed A-absent repair exists.  Status: `scaffolded_or_primed`, not
   `taught`.

5. **Priming.**  A token changes the immediate response distribution but the
   shared force/opportunity comparator computes a primed response, and the
   later A-absent probe has no B-committed repair.  Status:
   `scaffolded_or_primed`.

6. **Fully forced compliance.**  The force/opportunity comparator computes
   mode `fully_forced_response` from the carried force schedule and empty
   free-response opportunity census, and the target quotient is unrefined.
   Status: `coercion_null`.

7. **Free observed response.**  A free response is observed and later carried as
   B's committed-state repair.  This is the positive contrast to coercion-null
   and can support `taught` if communication also holds.

8. **Stable symbol.**  Token `s` reactivates the same role-preserving package
   through the same carried paid bridge across at least two declared contexts,
   with strict descent in each context and no current-structure relabel.
   Status: `symbolic`.

9. **Symbol context instability.**  At least one declared context lacks
   reactivation.  Status: `transport_rejected` for a symbol claim unless a
   lower-priority non-symbol communication claim is separately made for a
   different `TransportClaimRef`.

10. **Symbol role drift.**  Reactivation occurs in every context but the
    package role changes between contexts.  Status: `transport_rejected` for
    the symbol claim.

11. **Saturation-only relabel.**  The token names a role already carried by B's
    current structure, and no genuine package is reactivated.  Status:
    `transport_rejected`.

12. **Unpaid bridge.**  Token and repair records exist, but bridge defects lack
    carried ledger entries and a discharge certificate on both sides.  Status:
    `transport_rejected`.

13. **Uncarried target repair.**  The target move improves the quotient in a
    raw calculation, but the repair record lacks D3 carried occurrence evidence.
    Status: `transport_rejected`, including when the raw improvement is in a
    different challenge class.

14. **Role-preservation failure.**  Same-class strict reduction occurs, but the
    induced B repair plays a different structural role under B's audit.  Status:
    `transport_rejected`.

15. **Parameter-channel-only interaction.**  A channel changes B's parameters
    without a carried B repair record and without role-preserving package
    transport.  Status: `transport_rejected`; this is deliberately not an E11
    `stack_active` claim.

16. **E12-style flow crossing without E13 bridge.**  A record crosses a
    boundary-like interface but lacks an E13 paid inter-carrier bridge.  It may
    be relevant to E12's `EntryClosedOn`/flow-crossing analysis, but E13
    classifies the transport claim as `transport_rejected`.

## 6. Nonclaims

- E13 does not define meaning as token-object reference.  A token communicates
  only through a same-class, role-preserving, carried B-side repair.

- E13 does not say every influence is communication.  Different-class
  behavioral change is `influence_only`.

- E13 does not say every communication is teaching.  Teaching requires B's
  later `committed_state` repair in A's absence.

- E13 does not count scaffolding, priming, or environmental arrangement as
  teaching unless the A-absent capacity criterion is met.

- E13 does not count fully forced compliance as transported repair.  The
  coercion-null branch is deliberately load-bearing and is expected to be the
  first applicable coercion-null item in the null battery.

- E13 does not say every repeatable token is a symbol.  Symbols require a
  declared non-vacuous context family, context-stable role preservation, and
  saturation-strict reactivation.

- E13 does not mechanize F22/F17/F29/F30 or the Institutions paper locally.
  Those enter as certified host interfaces until vendored Lean support exists.

- E13 does not fully discharge E11's institutional-label/matched-comparison
  forward obligation or E12's `EntryClosedOn` flow-crossing forward obligation.
  It supplies the transport vocabulary those bridges should use.

- E13 does not transport gates, norms, or constraints as such.  THEOREMS.md's
  deferred "Norm as transported constraint" candidate is a likely sibling law,
  not part of this repair-transport normal form.

- E13 makes no empirical, normative, welfare, or consciousness claim.  It is a
  finite carrier-level classification of audited repair transport.
