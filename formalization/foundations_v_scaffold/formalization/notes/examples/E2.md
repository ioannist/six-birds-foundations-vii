# E2 - Bounded Reflexivity Law (Carried Audit Towers)

**Source text:** THEOREMS.md §3, E2.  
**Target Lean module:** `SixBirdsFoundationsV.Laws.E2BoundedReflexivity`.  
**Grounding:** D3 supplies carried records; D4 supplies `ESystem`, `ActiveCarriedInstrument`, `CarriedLedger`, `Lambda_S`, and the repair-step instrument hooks; E1 supplies the carried audited-repair trigger through `EndogenousRepairOccurrence` and `CorePromotionGatesPass`. ### Same-level soundness claims on a carried instrument

FIII's `SameLevelSelfAuditClaim` only records the claim-space booleans. E2's carrier bridge ties those booleans to a concrete carried instrument occurrence:

```text
SameLevelSelfAuditClaimFor(S, level, claim) :=
  CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level)
  and claim targets Sound(level.instrument) under level.instrument
  and claim.inClaimTypes records whether Sound(level.instrument)
      is in the level's declared claim types
  and claim.selfDependent records whether the claim depends on the same
      level's own audit judgment
  and claim.hasLevelShiftBridge records whether a strictly higher-level
      bridge is supplied.
```

The target relation is certified bridge data at the D4/FIII boundary. It is not a new proof of FIII's claim classifier; it is the statement that this particular carried claim is the one being classified by the imported FIII theorem.

### E1 audited-repair trigger

E2's stratification trigger is not "some audit exists" in isolation. It is the E1 occurrence in which a repair generated by `S.R_S` is detected, gated, kernel-realized, and reaudited by `S.I_S`:

```text
AuditedRepairTrigger(S, H, C, horizon) :=
  exists t rho_t R_t,
    ChallengedEpisodeTime(H, C, t)
    and EndogenousRepairOccurrence(S, H, C, t, rho_t, R_t)
    and rho_t in H.repairAuditEntries
    and exists z z' defect move auditRecord,
      move = S.R_S(defect)
      and ESystem.RepairStep(S, z, z', defect, auditRecord)
      and CorePromotionGatesPass(S, z, z', defect, move, auditRecord).
```

This uses E1's already-landed occurrence discipline. The same occurrence supplies the repair entry, generated move, audit record, and `CorePromotionGatesPass`; E2 must not accept a free-floating carried repair record and an unrelated lawful repair step as evidence of audited repair.

### Carried instrument tower

D4 has one running instrument `S.I_S`. E2 generalizes this to a finite tower of carried instruments, each level being a genuine D4-style carried instrument occurrence:

```text
structure CarriedInstrumentLevel where
  levelIndex : Nat
  levelTag : Nat
  instrument : ActiveCarriedInstrument ...
  levelRecord : InstrumentRecord
  auditsLowerStack : List Nat -> Prop
  lowerStackTarget : List Nat
```

```text
CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level) :=
  level.instrument.instrumentRecordCarried(level.levelRecord)
  and level.instrument.carried
  and the level record is exposed on S's carried audit surface
  and every lower index named by level.lowerStackTarget is < level.levelIndex.
```

For a tower

```text
tower : Nat -> Option CarriedInstrumentLevel
n : Nat
```

write:

```text
TowerLevel(S, tower, n, k, level) :=
  k < n
  and tower(k) = some(level)
  and CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level)
  and level.levelIndex = k.

StrictlyIncreasingLevelTags(S, tower, n) :=
  forall i j level_i level_j,
    TowerLevel(S,tower,n,i,level_i) ->
    TowerLevel(S,tower,n,j,level_j) ->
    i < j ->
    level_i.levelTag < level_j.levelTag.

EachLevelAuditsLowerStack(S, tower, n) :=
  forall k level,
    TowerLevel(S,tower,n,k,level) ->
    k > 0 ->
      level.auditsLowerStack([0,...,k-1])
      and level.lowerStackTarget = [0,...,k-1].
```

The tower is complete over the carried audit levels of `S`:

```text
CompleteCarriedInstrumentTower(S, tower, n) :=
  forall level,
    CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level) ->
      level.levelIndex < n ->
        exists k, TowerLevel(S,tower,n,k,level).
```

This chooses an occurrence-complete reading scoped to the tower's current declared index range: every genuine carried instrument level for the running E-system with `level.levelIndex < n` must appear in the actual tower. A fresh rotation candidate at index `n` is not yet a member of the current tower and must not be forced into `tower` before `E2_ForcedStratification` constructs the extension. E2 does not introduce a vague "participates in this tower" gate; the numeric index bound is the independent, non-circular restriction.

### Bridge to FIII rotating-audit stacks

E2 uses a certified bridge from the D4 tower to the FIII stack record:

```text
FIIIInstrumentStackBridge(S, tower, n, stack) :=
  CompleteCarriedInstrumentTower(S, tower, n)
  and stack.length = n
  and stack.finiteRecords = true
  and stack.admissibleLowerStack = true
  and stack.strictlyIncreasingLevels = true
  and stack.stackDefectEmpty records whether the carried lower-stack
      defect census is empty.
```

The bridge is deliberately one-way: it says the carried tower gives a valid FIII `InstrumentStack`. It does not claim FIII's abstract stack structure itself creates D4 carried records.

To realize an imported rotating extension as a carried level, E2 uses:

```text
CarriedRotatingExtensionRealized(S, tower, n, stack, ext, nextLevel) :=
  FIIIInstrumentStackBridge(S, tower, n, stack)
  and ext.lower = stack
  and ext.extensionInstrumentPresent = true
  and ext.extensionLevelHigher = true
  and ext.bridgeAdmissible = true
  and ext.reportAccepted = true
  and ext.selfSoundnessAccepted = false
  and CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, nextLevel)
  and nextLevel.levelIndex = n
  and nextLevel.levelTag is greater than every carried lower level tag
  and nextLevel.auditsLowerStack([0,...,n-1]).
```

This prevents unlinked-evidence proofs: the extension, the new carried instrument record, and the lower-stack audit relation are tied to the same `nextLevel`.

Capacity openness alone can derive a positive-footprint carried candidate at index `n`; it cannot derive that the candidate's level tag exceeds every lower carried level tag or that its lower-stack audit fields are filled correctly. E2 therefore names that residual D4 bridge explicitly:

```text
FreshRotatingLevelBridge(S, tower, n) :=
  forall nextLevel,
    CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, nextLevel) ->
    nextLevel.levelIndex = n ->
      nextLevel.levelTag is greater than every carried lower level tag
      and nextLevel.auditsLowerStack([0,...,n-1])
      and nextLevel.lowerStackTarget = [0,...,n-1].
```

This is certified host data about the fresh carried level's tag/lower-stack fields. It is not a capacity premise and it does not assert the existence of `nextLevel`; existence is derived from non-saturation of a currently realizable, currently admissible tower.

### Capacity and footprint

Clause 3 is new Foundations-V content. The repo has no existing carried-record capacity or per-level footprint apparatus, so E2 introduces a local certified counting interface:

```text
structure AuditTowerCapacityMeasure where
  RecordId : Type
  carrierRecordUniverse : S.T.Z -> FiniteSet RecordId
  cap : S.T.Z -> Nat
  capIsUniverseCard :
    forall z,
      card(carrierRecordUniverse(z)) = cap(z)
  levelRecordSet : CarriedInstrumentLevel -> FiniteSet RecordId
  footprint : CarriedInstrumentLevel -> Nat
  footprintIsCard :
    forall level,
      CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level) ->
      footprint(level) = card(levelRecordSet(level))
  countsOnlyCarriedRecords :
    forall level,
      CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level) ->
      levelRecordSet(level) is exactly the level's carried instrument record,
      visibility records, threshold records, check-rule records, and
      carried ledger/audit entries used by that level
  levelRecordsWithinCarrier :
    forall z level,
      CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level) ->
      levelRecordSet(level) subset carrierRecordUniverse(z)
  disjointAcrossLevels :
    forall level1 level2,
      CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level1) ->
      CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level2) ->
      level1.levelIndex != level2.levelIndex ->
      disjoint(levelRecordSet(level1), levelRecordSet(level2))
  positiveFootprint :
    forall level,
      CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level) ->
      footprint(level) > 0.
```

`AuditTowerCapacityMeasure` is certified host data, but it is not the theorem conclusion in disguise. It exposes a finite carried-record universe at carrier state `z`, certifies that `cap(z)` is the cardinality of that universe, ties each level's footprint to a concrete finite subset of that universe, and requires pairwise disjointness across distinct tower levels. These are structural counting premises; none is equivalent to `TowerFootprint <= cap(z)`.

Fix an ambient certified capacity interface:

```text
measure : AuditTowerCapacityMeasure.
```

All capacity predicates below use this named `measure`, following E1's convention of introducing shared certified data once in Setup rather than repeating it in every predicate signature.

For a finite carried tower:

```text
TowerFootprint(S, measure, tower, n) :=
  sum over k < n of measure.footprint(level_k)
  where TowerLevel(S,tower,n,k,level_k).

CapacityAdmissible(S, measure, tower, n, z) :=
  TowerFootprint(S, measure, tower, n) <= measure.cap(z).

CapacityRealizableTower(S, tower, n) :=
  CompleteCarriedInstrumentTower(S, tower, n)
  and StrictlyIncreasingLevelTags(S, tower, n)
  and EachLevelAuditsLowerStack(S, tower, n).
```

A tower is saturated when every positive-footprint next level would exceed the declared capacity:

```text
CapacitySaturated(S, measure, tower, n, z) :=
  CapacityRealizableTower(S, tower, n)
  and CapacityAdmissible(S, measure, tower, n, z)
  and forall nextLevel,
    CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, nextLevel) ->
    nextLevel.levelIndex = n ->
    measure.footprint(nextLevel) > 0 ->
      not CapacityAdmissible(
        S, measure, tower extended by nextLevel, n+1, z).
```

The source text says this capacity is a `Lambda_S` line item "priced per E8." Since E8 has not landed, E2 records:

```text
ForwardObligation(E8_capacity_pricing_correspondence):
  when E8 lands, verify that the capacity counted by AuditTowerCapacityMeasure
  is carried/priced on Lambda_S in E8's access-pricing vocabulary.
```

This is not a blocking dependency for E2's capacity theorem. E2 needs a declared capacity bound; E8 later supplies the pricing/accounting law for that bound.

As in E1 and E7, the exact-one claim is stated over carried status occurrences:

The D3/D4 policy used to carry E2 status records is fixed once:

  and every entry in record.supportingLedgerEntries is in S.Lambda_S.ledgerEntries.

The branch evidence predicates are:

```text
CircularBlockAtLevel(S, tower, n, record) :=
  exists level claim,
    TowerLevel(S,tower,n,record.levelIndex,level)
    and SameLevelSelfAuditClaimFor(S, level, claim)
    and claim.hasLevelShiftBridge = false
    and SameLevelSelfAuditClassify(claim) != ClaimStatus.accepted.

NoCircularBlockAtLevel(S, tower, n, record) :=
  not CircularBlockAtLevel(S, tower, n, record).

ActiveScopedEvidence(
    S, H, C, tower, n, z, horizon, targetLevel, record) :=
  BoundedReflexivityStatusOccurrenceFor(
    S, tower, n, z, targetLevel, record)
  and record.status = active_scoped
  and NoCircularBlockAtLevel(S, tower, n, record)
  and the cited level's active claim is in declared scope
  and the cited claim is not SameLevelSelfAuditClaimFor a self-dependent
      Sound(I) claim at the same level
  and CapacityAdmissible(S, measure, tower, n, z)
  and not CapacitySaturated(S, measure, tower, n, z)
  and not AuditedRepairTrigger(S, H, C, horizon).

RotatingEvidence(
    S, H, C, tower, n, z, horizon, targetLevel, record) :=
  BoundedReflexivityStatusOccurrenceFor(
    S, tower, n, z, targetLevel, record)
  and record.status = rotating
  and NoCircularBlockAtLevel(S, tower, n, record)
  and AuditedRepairTrigger(S, H, C, horizon)
  and not CapacitySaturated(S, measure, tower, n, z)
  and exists stack ext nextLevel,

    and CarriedRotatingExtensionRealized(S, tower, n, stack, ext, nextLevel).

`RotatingEvidence` is a status for `targetLevel`, the current carried level/lower-stack position whose audit demand forces rotation. The existential `nextLevel` is the higher extension realized by the rotation; it is not the status record's own level. Once `nextLevel` is installed into an extended tower, it receives its own per-level status record.

SaturatedEvidence(S, tower, n, z, targetLevel, record) :=
  BoundedReflexivityStatusOccurrenceFor(
    S, tower, n, z, targetLevel, record)
  and record.status = saturated
  and NoCircularBlockAtLevel(S, tower, n, record)
  and CapacitySaturated(S, measure, tower, n, z).

CircularBlockedEvidence(S, tower, n, z, targetLevel, record) :=
  BoundedReflexivityStatusOccurrenceFor(
    S, tower, n, z, targetLevel, record)
  and record.status = circular_blocked
  and CircularBlockAtLevel(S, tower, n, record).
```

The witness-level predicates are:

```text
ActiveScopedHolds(S, H, C, tower, n, z, horizon, targetLevel) :=
  exists record,
    ActiveScopedEvidence(

```

The complete status classifier is:

```text
CompleteBoundedReflexivityStatus(
    S, H, C, tower, n, z, horizon, targetLevel) :=
  exists record,
    BoundedReflexivityStatusOccurrenceFor(
      S, tower, n, z, targetLevel, record)
    and (
      ActiveScopedEvidence(
        S, H, C, tower, n, z, horizon, targetLevel, record)
      or RotatingEvidence(
        S, H, C, tower, n, z, horizon, targetLevel, record)
      or SaturatedEvidence(S, tower, n, z, targetLevel, record)
      or CircularBlockedEvidence(S, tower, n, z, targetLevel, record)

```

This is verification-shaped governance data, not a claim that arbitrary hosts expose an honest status classifier. The existence clause is branch-valid; the uniqueness clause ranges over every genuine carried status occurrence for the level/tower, not a caller-provided list.

## 2. Theorem

### Self-soundness obstruction

Assume:

```text
CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level)
SameLevelSelfAuditClaimFor(S, level, claim)
claim.hasLevelShiftBridge = false
```

Then:

```text
Theorem E2_SelfSoundnessObstruction:
  SameLevelSelfAuditClassify(claim) != ClaimStatus.accepted.
```

For same-level `Sound(I)` claims, the outcome is therefore `outsideScope` or `undefinedCircular` in the FIII classifier. A carried instrument can audit scoped object claims, but it cannot carry an accepted complete soundness certificate for itself at the same level.

### Forced stratification

Assume:

```text
AuditedRepairTrigger(S, H, C, horizon)
FIIIInstrumentStackBridge(S, tower, n, stack)
stack.finiteRecords = true
stack.admissibleLowerStack = true
stack.strictlyIncreasingLevels = true
CapacityRealizableTower(S, tower, n)
CapacityAdmissible(S, measure, tower, n, z)
not CapacitySaturated(S, measure, tower, n, z)
FreshRotatingLevelBridge(S, tower, n)
```

Then:

```text
Theorem E2_ForcedStratification:
  exists ext nextLevel,
    CarriedRotatingExtensionRealized(S, tower, n, stack, ext, nextLevel)
    and ext.extensionLevelHigher = true
    and ext.bridgeAdmissible = true
    and ext.reportAccepted = true
    and (ext.complianceAccepted = true <-> ext.stackDefectEmpty = true)
    and ext.selfSoundnessAccepted = false.
```

The system may rotate to a higher audit level, but the new level still does not accept its own complete self-soundness. The tower is stratified rather than final.

### Capacity bound

Assume:

```text
CapacityRealizableTower(S, tower, n)
AuditTowerCapacityMeasure.disjointAcrossLevels applies to every pair
  of distinct carried levels in tower
AuditTowerCapacityMeasure.levelRecordsWithinCarrier places every level's
  record set inside carrierRecordUniverse(z)
AuditTowerCapacityMeasure.capIsUniverseCard(z)
AuditTowerCapacityMeasure.footprintIsCard for every carried level
```

Then:

```text
Theorem E2_CapacityBound:
  TowerFootprint(S, measure, tower, n) <= measure.cap(z).
```

This is the capacity theorem in its counting form. E2 does not assume that a carried tower is already admissible. It derives the bound from the fact that the tower's level footprints are pairwise-disjoint finite subsets of the same finite carrier record universe, whose cardinality is `measure.cap(z)`.

Moreover, if `CapacitySaturated(S, measure, tower, n, z)` holds, every positive-footprint rotating extension is non-admissible unless the host first retires, compresses, or reprices carried audit records through a lawful ledger mechanism. This use of `CapacityAdmissible` inside `CapacitySaturated` is not circular: saturation is a status of a currently admissible tower together with a claim about every next positive-footprint extension failing admissibility.

### Status partition

Assume:

```text
targetLevel < n
CompleteBoundedReflexivityStatus(
  S, H, C, tower, n, z, horizon, targetLevel)
```

Then:

```text
Theorem E2_StatusPartition:
  exactly one of
    ActiveScopedHolds(S, H, C, tower, n, z, horizon, targetLevel)
    RotatingHolds(S, H, C, tower, n, z, horizon, targetLevel)
    SaturatedHolds(S, tower, n, z, targetLevel)
    CircularBlockedHolds(S, tower, n, z, targetLevel)
  holds.
```

This is the fixed-level form of THEOREMS.md's "exactly-one status per instrument level" claim. As in E1 and E7, exactness is not a case split on a single arbitrary record tag. It follows from an occurrence-complete carried status classifier whose branch-valid existence clause supplies one full case for `targetLevel` and whose uniqueness clause rules out conflicting carried status records for that same level/tower/horizon.

### Obstruction/falsifier set

```text
Delta_refl :=
  { (level, claim) |
      CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence(S, level)
      and SameLevelSelfAuditClaimFor(S, level, claim)
      and claim.hasLevelShiftBridge = false
      and SameLevelSelfAuditClassify(claim) = ClaimStatus.accepted }.
```

Any nonempty `Delta_refl` falsifies the self-soundness obstruction by exhibiting a carried same-level `Sound(I)` acceptance that survives the FIII circularity check.

```text
Delta_cap :=
  { tower |
      CapacityRealizableTower(S, tower, n)
      and the measure's level record sets for tower are pairwise disjoint
      and every level record set is a subset of carrierRecordUniverse(z)
      and cap(z) is the cardinality of carrierRecordUniverse(z)
      and every level footprint is the cardinality of its level record set
      and TowerFootprint(S, measure, tower, n) > measure.cap(z) }.
```

Any nonempty `Delta_cap` falsifies the counting theorem by exhibiting a carried tower whose certified disjoint record footprints outgrow the certified finite carrier record universe.

## 3. Proof Spine

### Clause 1: inherited limit, sharpened to carriers

1. Use `SameLevelSelfAuditClaimFor` to identify the D4 carried claim as a FIII `SameLevelSelfAuditClaim`.
2. The no-shift hypothesis is exactly `claim.hasLevelShiftBridge = false`.
3. Apply `same_level_self_audit_failure` to obtain `SameLevelSelfAuditClassify(claim) != ClaimStatus.accepted`.
4. Because the claim is tied to a carried `CarriedInstrumentLevelOccurrence`, the imported FIII limit is specialized to the carrier's instrument rather than to an external theorist's claim-space policy.

No claim-classifier theorem is re-proved in Foundations V.

### Clause 2: forced stratification

1. E1 supplies the trigger through `AuditedRepairTrigger`, which is built from `EndogenousRepairOccurrence`, `H.repairAuditEntries`, `ESystem.RepairStep`, and `CorePromotionGatesPass`.
2. `FIIIInstrumentStackBridge` converts the existing carried tower into a FIII `InstrumentStack` with `finiteRecords = true`, `admissibleLowerStack = true`, and `strictlyIncreasingLevels = true`.
3. Apply `finite_rotating_audit` to obtain a `RotatingAuditExtension`.
4. The extension has `extensionLevelHigher = true`, `bridgeAdmissible = true`, `reportAccepted = true`, and `selfSoundnessAccepted = false`.
5. Since the tower is currently `CapacityRealizableTower` and `CapacityAdmissible`, `not CapacitySaturated` implies there exists a positive-footprint carried `nextLevel` at index `n` whose extension remains capacity-admissible.
6. `FreshRotatingLevelBridge` supplies the residual tag-ordering and lower-stack-audit fields for that derived `nextLevel`.
7. `CarriedRotatingExtensionRealized` ties the FIII extension to the same D4 carried `nextLevel` and to the lower stack it audits, preventing the unlinked-evidence error where an abstract extension and an unrelated carried record are paired.

If capacity is unavailable, the status is not "final self-certification"; it is `saturated`.

### Clause 3: finite capacity

1. `AuditTowerCapacityMeasure` supplies a numeric carrier capacity `cap(z)` and a positive footprint for each genuinely carried instrument level.
2. Each level's `footprint(level)` is the cardinality of a concrete finite set `levelRecordSet(level)`.
3. Every `levelRecordSet(level)` is a subset of the same finite carrier record universe `carrierRecordUniverse(z)`, and `cap(z)` is the cardinality of that universe.
4. Distinct tower levels have disjoint `levelRecordSet` values by `disjointAcrossLevels`.
5. Therefore the finite sum of the cardinalities of the level sets is bounded by the cardinality of the common universe. This is the elementary finite-set counting step: pairwise-disjoint subsets of a finite set cannot have total cardinality exceeding the ambient set.
6. `TowerFootprint` is exactly that finite sum, so `TowerFootprint(S, measure, tower, n) <= measure.cap(z)`.
7. Since every added carried level has positive footprint, a fixed-capacity tower cannot keep adding positive-footprint levels without eventually becoming saturated or requiring a carried retirement/compression/repricing/out-of-scope disposition.
8. The future E8 pricing theorem is not needed for the counting inequality. E8 is needed later to verify that retirement, compression, and repricing actions are priced and repaired honestly on `Lambda_S`.

Lean note for the later mechanization step: if the Mathlib-free environment lacks a convenient finite-cardinality lemma for pairwise-disjoint finite sets, E2 may need a small certified `FiniteDisjointCardBound` lemma/input. That would be a counting bridge, not a replacement for the theorem conclusion.

The proof keeps two notions separate:

```text
ExistentialFeasibleTower:
  some capacity-admissible tower could be formed by a host.

ActualCarriedTower:
  the tower records actually carried by S and captured by
  CompleteCarriedInstrumentTower.
```

E2's theorem is about the actual carried tower when the host supplies the occurrence-complete tower evidence; it is not a policy-independent feasibility statement about all possible towers.

### Status exactness

1. Fix `targetLevel`. `CompleteBoundedReflexivityStatus` supplies a carried status record for that level satisfying one full branch predicate.
2. Lift that branch-valid record to one of `ActiveScopedHolds`, `RotatingHolds`, `SaturatedHolds`, or `CircularBlockedHolds` at `targetLevel`.
3. Any competing witness-level branch for the same `targetLevel` supplies another carried status record for the same level/tower/horizon.
4. The uniqueness clause forces the two status tags equal.
5. The constructors of `BoundedReflexivityStatus` are pairwise distinct, so two different branches cannot both hold.

The branch predicates are deliberately comparable in rigor: every branch uses `BoundedReflexivityStatusOccurrenceFor`, and every branch adds a substantive payload rather than relying on a status tag alone.
They also include explicit priority guards. Unshifted same-level self-soundness claims are classified as `circular_blocked`; non-circular levels that need higher audit and have capacity rotate; non-circular levels without rotation pressure remain `active_scoped`; and non-circular levels blocked by the capacity bound are `saturated`.

## 4. Case Enumeration

1. **Active-scoped.** A carried instrument level audits a scoped object claim inside its declared authority, has no unshifted same-level self-soundness block, has no E1 audited-repair trigger at this horizon, remains within the tower capacity bound, and is not saturated. This is the lawful ordinary case for a bounded audit level.

2. **Circular-blocked.** A carried level asserts `Sound(I)` for itself without a level-shift bridge. `same_level_self_audit_failure` forces the FIII classifier away from `ClaimStatus.accepted`; the status record must mark the claim `outsideScope` or `undefinedCircular`, not accepted.

3. **Rotating.** An E1 audited-repair trigger requires the system to audit the lower stack. If the level is not circular-blocked and capacity admits a positive-footprint extension, `finite_rotating_audit` supplies a higher-level rotating extension; the carried bridge realizes it as `nextLevel`, and `selfSoundnessAccepted = false`.

4. **Saturated.** The carried tower is otherwise well-formed, non-circular, and occurrence-complete, but any further positive-footprint audit level would exceed `cap(z)`. Continued repair requires retirement, compression, outsourcing, or repricing; it is not a hidden final self-audit.

5. **Over-capacity obstruction.** A claimed carried tower whose level record sets are certified as pairwise disjoint subsets of the same finite carrier record universe, but whose `TowerFootprint` still exceeds `cap(z)`, belongs to `Delta_cap`. This is a genuine counting falsifier, not merely an unpriced ledger gap.

6. **Unlinked bridge obstruction.** A proof that combines an abstract FIII extension for one stack with an unrelated D4 carried record for another level does not satisfy `CarriedRotatingExtensionRealized`.

7. **Incomplete tower inventory.** A tower that omits a genuine carried level occurrence with `level.levelIndex < n` cannot satisfy `CompleteCarriedInstrumentTower`; exactness cannot be claimed from a caller-supplied list. A fresh candidate at index `n` is outside this current-tower completeness check until the tower is extended.

8. **Future sweep guard.** The toy-laboratory step must compute tower footprint, saturation, rotating extension, and status classification from carried records and capacities. It must not hardcode status tables or pass/fail rows from the pre-registration.

## 5. Interaction Status

- **D3 carried records.** E2 status records, tower-level records, and capacity/pricing records are credited only through `CarriedRecordAt`/`HasCarriedRecordEvidence`-style evidence. Completeness is occurrence-wide, not list-scoped.

- **D4 E-system and ledger.** E2 is explicitly about D4's `ActiveCarriedInstrument` and `S.I_S`. Capacity is a bound on the carried records and ledger entries exposed through `S.Lambda_S`, not an external audit channel.

- **D5 closed-loop history.** E2 does not independently define challenge histories. Its repair-audit trigger passes through E1's `EndogenousRepairOccurrence`, which already requires `rho_t in H.repairAuditEntries` and D5/D4 carried audit evidence.

- **E1 internalization.** E2 is downstream of E1's clause 3. `AuditedRepairTrigger` reuses `EndogenousRepairOccurrence` and `CorePromotionGatesPass`; it does not re-derive endogenous repair or repair-family validity.

- **FIII InstrumentClaims / F21-T24.** `SameLevelSelfAuditClaim`, `SameLevelSelfAuditClassify`, and `same_level_self_audit_failure` are imported recognition-source apparatus. E2 specializes them to carried instruments.

- **FIII RotatingAudit / T25.** `InstrumentStack`, `RotatingAuditExtension`, `oneLevelRotatingAuditExtension`, and `finite_rotating_audit` are imported recognition-source apparatus. E2 supplies the D4 carried-tower bridge and the capacity guard.

- **E7 alarm-fatigue forward reference.** E7.1 names a finite preemption/discharge capacity claim and recorded `ForwardObligation(E2_capacity_bound_correspondence)`. E2 supplies the general carried audit-capacity vocabulary, but it does not by itself prove E7's preemption-channel saturation dynamics.

- **E8 forward reference.** `ForwardObligation(E8_capacity_pricing_correspondence)` remains open. E2 can prove that an actual carried tower must respect a declared capacity; E8 must later verify that such capacity is priced and statused on `Lambda_S`.

- **Certified-input / governance boundary guard.** E2 relies on honest host bridges from D4 carried instruments to FIII claims/stacks, honest `AuditTowerCapacityMeasure` footprint counting, complete tower/status occurrence inventories, and honest capacity declarations. These are certified inputs, not consequences of the FIII theorems alone.

## 6. Nonclaims

- E2 does not re-prove F21, T24, or T25. It imports `same_level_self_audit_failure` and `finite_rotating_audit` and adds the carrier bridge/capacity layer.

- E2 does not claim a final self-certifying instrument exists. The rotating extension supplied by FIII has `selfSoundnessAccepted = false`.

- E2 does not claim the `AuditTowerCapacityMeasure` is unique, optimal, or the same object as Reflexive-SBT's `Cap` functional. Reflexive-SBT's capacity functionals are a quantitative template; E2's measure counts carried record footprints.

- E2 does not prove the toy-lab prediction that an optimal nonzero, non-maximal tower depth exists. That is a step-4 empirical/simulation claim about an error process and cost curve, not part of the Lean theorem target.

- E2 does not discharge E8's pricing law. Capacity is represented as a declared carried bound here; E8 must later account for pricing, budget repair, and ledger honesty.

- E2 does not claim every real-world audit hierarchy fits this footprint convention. It states the law for hosts that expose a certified carried-record capacity measure and complete tower/status occurrences.

- E2 does not turn a merely feasible tower into the system's actual tower. Actual carried-tower claims require `CompleteCarriedInstrumentTower`; feasibility-only claims must be labeled separately.

- E2 does not weaken E1. The audited-repair trigger is the already-landed E1 occurrence shape and does not license fallback, off-kernel, or uncarried repair records as endogenous audit evidence.
