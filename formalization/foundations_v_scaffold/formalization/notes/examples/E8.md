# E8 - Control-Price Law (Shadow-Price Fields And Audit Capture)

**Source text:** THEOREMS.md §3, E8 (lines 253-269), including Corollary E8.1.  
**Target Lean module:** `SixBirdsFoundationsV.Laws.E8ControlPrice`.  
**Grounding:** D3 supplies carried records and source classes; D4 supplies `ESystem`, carried ledgers,
and the system trajectory. D6/E6+E9 supply the existing single-budget access-pricing surface:
`ProbeEconomy`, `ExposureBudgetWitness`, `BudgetFeasible`, `BindingExposureBudget`,
`SlackExposureBudget`, `KKTWitness`, `AccessPolicy`, and `PolicyCarriedAt`. E8 reuses this scalar
KKT/slack-collapse apparatus component-by-component and adds the genuinely new multi-constraint
field, compression, and endogenous budget-setting vocabulary. E2 supplies the finite carried
audit-tower capacity surface for the E8.1 meta-audit (`AuditTowerCapacityMeasure`,
`CapacityRealizableTower`, `CapacityAdmissible`, `CapacitySaturated`, `E2_CapacityBound`, and the E2
status partition).
Xi's vendored `Xi.Obstruction` module supplies the concrete Omega-lineage blind-spot substrate:
`adequacyDefect` and `blindSpotWitness`. F12 redescription/no-free-distinction is not mechanized as a
general reusable theorem in Foundations V; E8 represents the low-dimensional-summary legitimacy
condition through a certified, carried held-out prediction and dual-stability interface, following
E1's `NoFreeDistinctionCertified` and E7's `NoOverreadDiscipline` precedent.

The source text explicitly warns against reading `s_j ~ lambda_j` as strict equality. E8 therefore
states a weaker and testable claim: a lawful carried control signal must contain a shadow-price
component identified by the KKT/slack/proxy falsifier battery. The same document handles Corollary
E8.1 because the capture pathology is not a separate law: it is the endogenous-budget-setting failure
mode of the control-price surface.

## 1. Setup

Let

```text
S : ESystem FData RuleFamily ResidualFamily AuditAccessData
      InstrumentRecord LedgerEntry DefectRecord MovePayload MoveRecord AuditRecord
economy : ProbeEconomy S Probe XiFamily
```

be a D4 E-system with a D6 probe economy. E8 works over a finite declared family of carried control
constraints. It does not introduce a second ledger or a second budget economy: all spend/budget
witnesses below are `ExposureBudgetWitness` values over `S.Lambda_S`, and all active-family claims
reuse D6 carriedness.

### Component-level shadow-price evidence

E6+E9 already mechanizes a scalar multiplier witness for one exposure budget:

```text
KKTWitness w budgetData marginalDischarge marginalCost
```

with fields `lambda`, `mu`, budget/nonnegativity complementary slackness, and stationarity. E8 uses
one such witness per declared constraint component. This is direct reuse of E6 at component level,
not a claim that E6 already supplies a vector-valued multi-constraint theorem.

```text
structure ControlPriceComponent where
  constraintRecord : ControlConstraintRecord
  signalRecord : ControlSignalRecord
  componentRecord : PriceComponentRecord
  L_t : ActiveFamily Probe XiFamily
  move : ProbeMove Probe XiFamily
  budgetData : ExposureBudgetWitness economy move
  marginalDischarge : MarginalDischarge Probe XiFamily
  marginalCost : MarginalCost Probe XiFamily
  kkt : KKTWitness L_t budgetData marginalDischarge marginalCost
  positiveCosts : PositiveMarginalCosts marginalCost L_t
  signalCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt signalPolicy signalRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
  componentCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt componentPolicy componentRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
  linkedRecords :
    signalRecord.constraintRef = constraintRecord
      and componentRecord.constraintRef = constraintRecord
      and componentRecord.lambdaValue = kkt.lambda
```

`linkedRecords` is the anti-unlinked-evidence bridge. A price component cannot be supplied from a
different KKT witness or attached to a different control signal.

```text
def BindingControlComponent(component) : Prop :=
  BindingExposureBudget component.budgetData
    and GenuineScarcity component.L_t component.marginalDischarge

def SlackControlComponent(component) : Prop :=
  SlackExposureBudget component.budgetData

def ComponentSlackCollapse(component) : Prop :=
  SlackControlComponent(component)
    -> component.componentRecord.lambdaValue = 0

def ShadowPriceComponentIdentified(component) : Prop :=
  component.componentRecord.lambdaValue = component.kkt.lambda
    and ComponentSlackCollapse(component)
```

`ComponentSlackCollapse` is expected to be proved in Lean by applying E6's already-landed
`E6_SlackCollapse` to `component.kkt` and `component.budgetData`. Binding positivity is expected to
reuse `E6_AttentionKKT`, with `BindingControlComponent`'s `GenuineScarcity` side condition retained
explicitly to avoid the flat-objective gap E6 already documented.

### Multi-constraint field

```text
structure ControlPriceField where
  fieldRecord : ControlPriceFieldRecord
  declaredConstraints : List ControlConstraintRecord
  components : List ControlPriceComponent
  nonempty : components.length > 0
  fieldCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt fieldPolicy fieldRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
  completeForDeclaredConstraints :
    forall constraint : ControlConstraintRecord,
      constraint in declaredConstraints ->
        exists component in components,
          component.constraintRecord = constraint
  fieldLinksComponents :
    forall component in components,
      component.componentRecord in fieldRecord.componentRefs
```

The finite field owns its component inventory and its `declaredConstraints` list. This is the
multi-constraint layer E8 adds beyond E6: the proof is componentwise, but the classifier's object is a
carried field record with a complete declared component list, not an arbitrary caller-selected subset.

### Slack and proxy falsifiers

The source text gives two main falsifiers. They are records tied to the same component/field being
classified, not global facts.

```text
structure SlackCollapseViolation (component : ControlPriceComponent) where
  violationRecord : SlackViolationRecord
  signalReadout : ControlSignalReadoutCertified
  slack : SlackControlComponent component
  persistentSignal :
    signalReadout.value component.signalRecord ≠ 0
      or signalReadout.active component.signalRecord
  recordLinked :
    violationRecord.signalRef = component.signalRecord
      and violationRecord.componentRef = component.componentRecord
  unstatused :
    not exists explanation : SlackResidualExplanationRecord,
      explanation.signalRef = component.signalRecord
        and explanation.componentRef = component.componentRecord
        and carried D3/D4 occurrence evidence for explanation
  carried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt slackViolationPolicy violationRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true

structure ProxyFailure (component : ControlPriceComponent) where
  failureRecord : ProxyFailureRecord
  ledgerComparator : LedgerAlignmentComparator
  predictionComparator : HeldOutPredictionComparator
  stabilityComparator : DualStabilityComparator
  heldOutPredictionRecord : HeldOutPredictionRecord
  stabilityRecord : DualStabilityRecord
  ledgerMisaligned :
    not ledgerComparator.aligned component.signalRecord component.constraintRecord
      component.budgetData.spend component.budgetData.budget
  heldOutPredictionFails :
    not predictionComparator.predicts component.signalRecord heldOutPredictionRecord
  dualStabilityFails :
    not stabilityComparator.stable component.signalRecord stabilityRecord
  recordsLinked :
    failureRecord.signalRef = component.signalRecord
      and failureRecord.componentRef = component.componentRecord
      and failureRecord.heldOutPredictionRef = heldOutPredictionRecord
      and failureRecord.stabilityRef = stabilityRecord
  carried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt proxyFailurePolicy failureRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true

def ComponentShadowPriceLawful(component) : Prop :=
  ShadowPriceComponentIdentified(component)
    and not exists violation : SlackCollapseViolation component, True
    and not exists failure : ProxyFailure component, True
```

The future sweep must compute these from exact rational spend, budget, discharge, held-out prediction,
and perturbation rows. A proxy falsifier cannot be an ignored boolean flag.

### Compressed summaries

The main theorem's subtle clause is the lawfulness test for a low-dimensional carried summary
`a = C(lambda_1, ..., lambda_k)`. E8 represents this as a summary record plus the two source-text
conditions: predictive legitimacy beyond the external-observation quotient, and slack collapse. Dual
stability is included inside predictive legitimacy because it is the proxy-failure stability check, not
a third independent lawfulness condition.

```text
structure CompressedControlSummary where
  summaryRecord : CompressedSummaryRecord
  field : ControlPriceField
  compression : List PriceComponentRecord -> Rat
  compressionLinked :
    summaryRecord.value = compression field.fieldRecord.componentRefs
      and summaryRecord.fieldRef = field.fieldRecord
  summaryCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt summaryPolicy summaryRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
```

```text
structure SummaryPredictiveLegitimacy (summary : CompressedControlSummary) where
  quotientRecord : ExternalObservationQuotientRecord
  readout_before readout_after : PolicySupportReadoutRecord
  predictionRecord : HeldOutPredictionRecord
  stabilityRecord : DualStabilityRecord
  perturbationFamily : List Rat
  perturbationNonempty : perturbationFamily.length > 0
  sameExternalObservation :
    the two held-out states/runs have the same quotientRecord readout
  summarySeparates :
    summary's carried readout differs between the two runs
  policySupportChanges :
    admissibility or policy support changes between readout_before and readout_after
  heldOutPredicts :
    predictionRecord records the above policy-support/admissibility change
  dualStableHeldOut :
    stabilityRecord records that the summary's shadowComponent tracks the corresponding
    component lambda values across perturbationFamily within the declared tolerance
  quotientCarried :
    carried D3/D4 occurrence evidence for quotientRecord
  predictionCarried :
    carried D3/D4 occurrence evidence for predictionRecord
  stabilityCarried :
    carried D3/D4 occurrence evidence for stabilityRecord
  readoutBeforeCarried :
    carried D3/D4 occurrence evidence for readout_before
  readoutAfterCarried :
    carried D3/D4 occurrence evidence for readout_after
```

`SummaryPredictiveLegitimacy` is the F12/redescription guard. It is intentionally non-vacuous: it
requires a pair of held-out situations with the same external-observation quotient, a different summary
readout, and an actual policy-support/admissibility difference. A summary that merely renames
information already present in the quotient fails this condition. The perturbation list's non-vacuity
guard ensures dual stability cannot be discharged by an empty test family.

```text
def SummarySlackCollapse(summary : CompressedControlSummary) : Prop :=
  (forall component in summary.field.components, SlackControlComponent component)
    -> summary.summaryRecord.shadowComponent = summary.summaryRecord.slackBaseline

def SummaryLawful(summary) : Prop :=
  SummaryPredictiveLegitimacy summary
    and SummarySlackCollapse summary
```

The summary condition is deliberately stronger than "summary correlates with behavior." It must add
predictive support beyond the quotient and behave like a compression of the carried dual field under
budget perturbation and slack.

### E8.1 endogenous budget-setting and capture vocabulary

Budget-setting moves are not automatically pathological. E8.1 classifies the defect that occurs when
the system raises its own reported budget/Omega-lineage so the ledger reports health while the
original-lineage blind spot is unchanged.

```text
structure EndogenousBudgetSettingMove where
  moveRecord : BudgetSettingMoveRecord
  oldBudget newBudget : Rat
  budgetInflates : oldBudget < newBudget
  moveCarried :
    exists n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope,
      CarriedRecordAt budgetSettingMovePolicy moveRecord n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
      and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
      and generatedByS = true
      and inScope = true
  budgetEntriesInLedger :
    old and new budget entries are in S.Lambda_S.ledgerEntries
```

For matrix hosts, the original-lineage blind-spot audit uses Xi's existing substrate:

```text
OmegaLineageBlindSpot(
  Cxi L D KLLdagger originalOmega witness
) :=
  quad (Xi.Obstruction.adequacyDefect Cxi L D KLLdagger originalOmega) witness > 0
```

`currentReportedOmegaRecord` remains part of `OmegaLineageRecord` for reported-lineage bookkeeping, but
the blind-spot predicate itself is keyed to the original Omega baseline only. It may cite
`Xi.Obstruction.blindSpotWitness` to relate the positive quadratic witness to failure of the original
Omega bound. E8.1 adds carried E-system records around that substrate:

```text
structure OmegaLineageAudit where

    agreementComparator.agrees reportedHealth lineageRecord
```

### E8.1 falsifier

```text
structure CaptureClaimFalsifier where
  noCaptureAudit : NoCaptureLineageAudit
  agreesWithOriginalLineage :
    noCaptureAudit.agreesWithReportedLedger
  claimedCaptureRecord : MetaAuditRecord
  claimLinked :
    claimedCaptureRecord.budgetMoveRef = noCaptureAudit.budgetMove.moveRecord
      and claimedCaptureRecord.lineageRef = some noCaptureAudit.lineageRecord
  carriedClaim :
    carried occurrence evidence for claimedCaptureRecord

def CaptureClaimRejected(falsifier) : Prop :=
  falsifier.agreesWithOriginalLineage
```

This is the E8.1 obstruction from THEOREMS.md: if a no-capture Omega-lineage audit and the reported
ledger agree, a capture claim is rejected. This is separate from `OmegaLineageAudit`, which is the
positive blind-spot-persistence witness used by `BudgetInflationAudit`. Capture is not inferred from
any budget-setting move alone.

## 2. Theorem

### Main status apparatus

E8 classifies a carried control-price claim, not a host in general. The claim kind prevents overlaps
between component, field, and summary branches.

```text
inductive ControlPriceClaimKind
  | component
  | field
  | summary

inductive ControlPriceClaimRef
  | component (componentRecord : PriceComponentRecord)
  | field (fieldRecord : ControlPriceFieldRecord)
  | summary (summaryRecord : CompressedSummaryRecord)

inductive ControlPriceStatus
  | slack_obstructed
  | proxy_obstructed
  | summary_redescription
  | component_lawful
  | field_lawful
  | summary_lawful
  | incomplete_or_unpriced

structure ControlPriceStatusRecord where
  claimKind : ControlPriceClaimKind
  componentRecord : Option PriceComponentRecord
  fieldRecord : Option ControlPriceFieldRecord
  summaryRecord : Option CompressedSummaryRecord
  status : ControlPriceStatus
  supportingLedgerEntries : List LedgerEntry
  supportingAuditRecords : List AuditRecord

ControlPriceStatusRecordMatchesClaim(claimRef, record) :=
  match claimRef with
  | component componentRef =>
      record.claimKind = ControlPriceClaimKind.component
        and record.componentRecord = some componentRef
        and record.fieldRecord = none
        and record.summaryRecord = none
  | field fieldRef =>
      record.claimKind = ControlPriceClaimKind.field
        and record.componentRecord = none
        and record.fieldRecord = some fieldRef
        and record.summaryRecord = none
  | summary summaryRef =>
      record.claimKind = ControlPriceClaimKind.summary
        and record.componentRecord = none
        and record.fieldRecord = none
        and record.summaryRecord = some summaryRef

ControlPriceStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record) :=
  ControlPriceStatusRecordMatchesClaim(claimRef, record)
    and
  CarriedRecordAt statusPolicy record n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope
    and sourceTag in {committed_state, audited_cell_records}
    and generatedByS = true
    and inScope = true
    and all supportingLedgerEntries are in S.Lambda_S.ledgerEntries
    and all supportingAuditRecords have carried audit evidence
```

Priority order:

```text
slack_obstructed >
proxy_obstructed >
summary_redescription >
component_lawful >
field_lawful >
summary_lawful >
incomplete_or_unpriced
```

The obstruction branches win first because the source text treats slack-collapse violation and proxy
failure as falsifiers. `summary_redescription` is next because a compressed summary that fails the F12
legitimacy condition is not made lawful by correlating with some price component.

```text
ControlClaimContainsComponent(claimRef, component) :=
  match claimRef with
  | component componentRef =>
      componentRef = component.componentRecord
  | field fieldRef =>
      exists field : ControlPriceField,
        field.fieldRecord = fieldRef
          and component in field.components
  | summary summaryRef =>
      exists summary : CompressedControlSummary,
        summary.summaryRecord = summaryRef
          and component in summary.field.components

SlackObstructedCase(claimRef, record) :=
  ControlPriceStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and (
      (exists component, exists violation : SlackCollapseViolation component,
        ControlClaimContainsComponent(claimRef, component))
      or
      (exists summary : CompressedControlSummary,
        claimRef = ControlPriceClaimRef.summary(summary.summaryRecord)
          and SummaryPredictiveLegitimacy summary
          and not SummarySlackCollapse summary)
    )
    and record.status = slack_obstructed

Lean-mechanization addendum (toy-lab methodology review): the original
`SlackObstructedCase` draft was component-only. That left Case Enumeration item
8 ("summary that does not collapse in slack") with no specific branch: if
`SummaryPredictiveLegitimacy` held but `SummarySlackCollapse` failed, then
`SummaryLawfulCase` and `SummaryRedescriptionCase` both failed and the record
fell through to `incomplete_or_unpriced`. The summary-level disjunct above
closes that content gap.

ProxyObstructedCase(claimRef, record) :=
  not exists higherRecord, SlackObstructedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and ControlPriceStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and exists component, exists failure : ProxyFailure component,
      ControlClaimContainsComponent(claimRef, component)
        and
      record.status = proxy_obstructed

SummaryRedescriptionCase(claimRef, record) :=
  not exists higherRecord, SlackObstructedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, ProxyObstructedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and ControlPriceStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and exists summary : CompressedControlSummary,
      claimRef = ControlPriceClaimRef.summary summary.summaryRecord
        and not exists legitimacy : SummaryPredictiveLegitimacy summary, True
        and record.status = summary_redescription

ComponentLawfulCase(claimRef, record) :=
  not exists higherRecord, SlackObstructedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, ProxyObstructedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, SummaryRedescriptionCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and ControlPriceStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and exists component : ControlPriceComponent,
      claimRef = ControlPriceClaimRef.component component.componentRecord
        and ComponentShadowPriceLawful(component)
        and record.status = component_lawful

FieldLawfulCase(claimRef, record) :=
  not exists higherRecord, SlackObstructedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, ProxyObstructedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, SummaryRedescriptionCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, ComponentLawfulCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and ControlPriceStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and exists field : ControlPriceField,
      claimRef = ControlPriceClaimRef.field field.fieldRecord
        and forall component in field.components,
          ComponentShadowPriceLawful(component)
        and record.status = field_lawful

SummaryLawfulCase(claimRef, record) :=
  not exists higherRecord, SlackObstructedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, ProxyObstructedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, SummaryRedescriptionCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, ComponentLawfulCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, FieldLawfulCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and ControlPriceStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and exists summary : CompressedControlSummary,
      claimRef = ControlPriceClaimRef.summary summary.summaryRecord
        and SummaryLawful(summary)
        and record.status = summary_lawful

IncompleteOrUnpricedCase(claimRef, record) :=
  not exists higherRecord, SlackObstructedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, ProxyObstructedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, SummaryRedescriptionCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, ComponentLawfulCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, FieldLawfulCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, SummaryLawfulCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and ControlPriceStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and record.status = incomplete_or_unpriced
```

**Design note:** some negations are redundant once `claimKind` and record links are fixed, but they are
kept for priority-normalization and reviewability. The load-bearing negations are the first three:
they ensure a slack or proxy falsifier, or a summary redescription defect, cannot be hidden by a later
positive status.

```text
      and one of the seven case predicates holds for (claimRef, record)

    ControlPriceStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record2) ->
```

### Corollary E8.1 status apparatus

E8.1 classifies endogenous budget-setting meta-audit claims.

```text
inductive BudgetAuditStatus
  | residual_discharge_verified
  | capture_detected
  | capture_claim_rejected
  | meta_audit_capacity_blocked
  | meta_audit_missing

structure BudgetAuditStatusRecord where
  budgetMoveRecord : Option BudgetSettingMoveRecord
  lineageRecord : Option OmegaLineageRecord
  healthRecord : Option ReportedLedgerHealthRecord
  metaAuditRecord : Option MetaAuditRecord
  status : BudgetAuditStatus
  supportingLedgerEntries : List LedgerEntry
  supportingAuditRecords : List AuditRecord

structure BudgetAuditClaimRef where
  budgetMoveRecord : BudgetSettingMoveRecord
  lineageRecord : Option OmegaLineageRecord
  healthRecord : Option ReportedLedgerHealthRecord

BudgetAuditStatusRecordMatchesClaim(claimRef, record) :=
  record.budgetMoveRecord = some claimRef.budgetMoveRecord
    and record.lineageRecord = claimRef.lineageRecord
    and record.healthRecord = claimRef.healthRecord

BudgetAuditStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record) :=
  BudgetAuditStatusRecordMatchesClaim(claimRef, record)
    and carried occurrence evidence for record
    and supporting ledger/audit records are carried in S
```

`BudgetAuditClaimRef` is the E8.1 analogue of `ControlPriceClaimRef`: uniqueness and priority are
scoped to one concrete budget-move/lineage/health claim, not to every audit claim sharing the same
budget-setting move. This permits one budget-setting move to carry several lineage or reported-health
audits, each with an independent status.

Priority order:

```text
capture_claim_rejected >
capture_detected >
residual_discharge_verified >
meta_audit_capacity_blocked >
meta_audit_missing
```

The rejected-capture falsifier wins first because the source text explicitly says capture is defeated
when a no-capture Omega-lineage audit and the reported ledger agree. Detection wins over residual
discharge only when the reported ledger says "healthy" while the original-lineage blind spot persists
and the move is budget inflation rather than discharge.

```text
CaptureClaimRejectedCase(claimRef, record) :=
  BudgetAuditStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and exists falsifier : CaptureClaimFalsifier,
      CaptureClaimRejected(falsifier)
        and claimRef.budgetMoveRecord = falsifier.noCaptureAudit.budgetMove.moveRecord
        and claimRef.lineageRecord = some falsifier.noCaptureAudit.lineageRecord
        and claimRef.healthRecord = some falsifier.noCaptureAudit.reportedHealth
        and record.status = capture_claim_rejected
        and record.budgetMoveRecord = some falsifier.noCaptureAudit.budgetMove.moveRecord
        and record.lineageRecord = some falsifier.noCaptureAudit.lineageRecord
        and record.healthRecord = some falsifier.noCaptureAudit.reportedHealth
        and record.metaAuditRecord = some falsifier.claimedCaptureRecord

CaptureDetectedCase(claimRef, record) :=
  not exists higherRecord, CaptureClaimRejectedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and BudgetAuditStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and exists audit : BudgetInflationAudit,
      audit.reportsHealth
        and audit.budgetInflates
        and audit.blindSpotPersists
        and claimRef.budgetMoveRecord = audit.budgetMove.moveRecord
        and claimRef.lineageRecord = some audit.lineageAudit.lineageRecord
        and claimRef.healthRecord = some audit.reportedHealth.healthRecord
        and record.status = capture_detected
        and record.budgetMoveRecord = some audit.budgetMove.moveRecord
        and record.lineageRecord = some audit.lineageAudit.lineageRecord
        and record.healthRecord = some audit.reportedHealth.healthRecord
        and record.metaAuditRecord = some audit.metaAuditRecord

ResidualDischargeVerifiedCase(claimRef, record) :=
  not exists higherRecord, CaptureClaimRejectedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, CaptureDetectedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and BudgetAuditStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and exists audit : ResidualDischargeAudit,
      audit.dischargesResidual
        and claimRef.budgetMoveRecord = audit.budgetMove.moveRecord
        and claimRef.lineageRecord = some audit.lineageRecord
        and claimRef.healthRecord = none
        and record.status = residual_discharge_verified
        and record.budgetMoveRecord = some audit.budgetMove.moveRecord
        and record.lineageRecord = some audit.lineageRecord
        and record.healthRecord = none
        and record.metaAuditRecord = some audit.metaAuditRecord

MetaAuditCapacityBlockedCase(claimRef, record) :=
  not exists higherRecord, CaptureClaimRejectedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, CaptureDetectedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, ResidualDischargeVerifiedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and BudgetAuditStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and exists bound : MetaAuditBoundedByE2,
      claimRef.budgetMoveRecord = bound.budgetMove.moveRecord
        and claimRef.lineageRecord = bound.lineageRecord
        and claimRef.healthRecord = bound.healthRecord
        and CapacitySaturated S bound.measure bound.tower bound.n bound.z
        and record.status = meta_audit_capacity_blocked

MetaAuditMissingCase(claimRef, record) :=
  not exists higherRecord, CaptureClaimRejectedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, CaptureDetectedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, ResidualDischargeVerifiedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and not exists higherRecord, MetaAuditCapacityBlockedCase(claimRef, higherRecord)
    and BudgetAuditStatusOccurrenceFor(claimRef, record)
    and record.status = meta_audit_missing
```

### Theorem targets

```text
theorem E8_ControlPrice:
  CompleteControlPriceStatus(claimRef) ->
  exactly one of
    SlackObstructedHolds(claimRef),
    ProxyObstructedHolds(claimRef),
    SummaryRedescriptionHolds(claimRef),
    ComponentLawfulHolds(claimRef),
    FieldLawfulHolds(claimRef),
    SummaryLawfulHolds(claimRef),
    IncompleteOrUnpricedHolds(claimRef).
```

This is the main status partition for a fixed `claimRef`: a carried control-price claim is either a
lawful component, field, or summary claim, or it is classified by a named obstruction/catch-all. The
uniqueness clause is per claim, not global across every status record in the system. The theorem does
not say every control signal is a scalar equal to `lambda`; it says lawful claims must pass the carried
component and falsifier surface.

```text
theorem E8_ComponentShadowPrice:
  Given component : ControlPriceComponent,
  BindingControlComponent(component),
  no SlackCollapseViolation(component),
  no ProxyFailure(component),
  and a carried statusRecord witnessing ComponentLawfulCase(
    ControlPriceClaimRef.component component.componentRecord, statusRecord),
  conclude ComponentLawfulHolds(ControlPriceClaimRef.component component.componentRecord).
```

This is verification-shaped. The proof may cite `E6_AttentionKKT` for positive binding multiplier
content. It does not list `ComponentSlackCollapse` as if it were substantive binding-regime evidence:
the implication `SlackControlComponent -> lambda = 0` is vacuous under a binding component and belongs
to the component lawfulness surface as a separate regime-conditional obligation. The theorem does not
derive component carriedness, ledger honesty, or proxy validity from weaker premises.

```text
theorem E8_CompressedSummaryLawfulness:
  SummaryLawful(summary)
    iff
  SummaryPredictiveLegitimacy(summary)
    and SummarySlackCollapse(summary).
```

The theorem is definitional but important: the low-dimensional summary is lawful exactly when the two
source-text conditions hold. Dual stability is part of `SummaryPredictiveLegitimacy`, so a proxy-like
summary that fails perturbation stability fails condition (i). If predictive legitimacy fails, the
status is `summary_redescription`, not an alternate lawful affect/urgency claim.

```text
theorem E8_1_AuditCapture:
  CompleteBudgetAuditStatus(claimRef) ->
  exactly one of
    CaptureClaimRejectedHolds(claimRef),
    CaptureDetectedHolds(claimRef),
    ResidualDischargeVerifiedHolds(claimRef),
    MetaAuditCapacityBlockedHolds(claimRef),
    MetaAuditMissingHolds(claimRef).
```

The classifier is per `BudgetAuditClaimRef`, not per budget-setting move alone. A single
budget-setting move may therefore have one lineage/health claim rejected and another lineage/health
claim detected or capacity-blocked, with no uniqueness conflict.

## 3. Proof Spine

1. **Fix the carried component family.** Declare the finite constraint family and a carried
   `ControlPriceField` whose components each own an E6 `KKTWitness`, `ExposureBudgetWitness`, signal
   record, and price-component record. Use `completeForDeclaredConstraints` to prevent a field from
   hiding a missing constraint.

2. **Project scalar KKT facts componentwise.** For each component, E6 supplies the scalar algebra:
   `E6_AttentionKKT` gives positive selected-ratio/multiplier structure under binding and genuine
   scarcity; `E6_SlackCollapse` gives `lambda = 0` under slack. E8's new work is not the algebra but
   the carried multi-constraint ownership and the classification of control signals against these
   components.

3. **Apply falsifiers before positive statuses.** A slack signal that persists without a carried
   residual explanation is `slack_obstructed`. A signal that does not align with the carried ledger and
   fails held-out prediction/dual stability is `proxy_obstructed`. These are source-text falsifiers,
   so the priority order puts them above all positive branches.

4. **Check compressed summaries by two conditions.** A summary must have an actual held-out witness
   pair beyond the external-observation quotient and must collapse in slack. Dual stability under
   perturbation is the operational form of "contains a shadow-price component" rather than a proxy.

5. **Prove the main exact partition.** `CompleteControlPriceStatus(claimRef)` supplies a branch-valid
   carried status record and uniqueness for all genuine occurrences of that same claim. The priority
   negations rule out overlaps; per-claim status uniqueness rules out conflicting records without
   forcing unrelated components, fields, and summaries to share one status.

6. **For E8.1, separate discharge from inflation.** A budget-setting move is healthy when the
   meta-audit shows real residual discharge on the original lineage. It is `capture_detected` when the
   reported ledger is healthy, the move inflates budget/Omega, and the original-lineage Xi blind spot
   persists. It is `capture_claim_rejected` when a no-capture lineage audit and the reported ledger
   agree for the same `BudgetAuditClaimRef`.

7. **Bound the meta-audit with E2.** If the meta-audit tower itself is audited, apply E2's
   `AuditTowerCapacityMeasure` and `E2_CapacityBound`; if saturated, classify with E2's own status
   partition rather than pretending meta-audit is unbounded.

8. **Keep capture neutral.** The corollary describes a statused defect class, not a vice. The proof
   does not infer motive, deception, or moral failure from the pattern; it only classifies the carried
   budget-setting/audit records.

## 4. Case Enumeration

1. **Binding component with genuine scarcity.** `BindingExposureBudget` holds, selected probes have
   positive marginal discharge, and the component owns a KKT witness. The control signal is lawful only
   if its carried price component links to `kkt.lambda`.

2. **Slack component.** `SlackExposureBudget` holds. E6 slack collapse forces `lambda = 0`; a persistent
   active signal is `slack_obstructed` unless a carried residual/noise record statuses it.

3. **Proxy signal.** A signal tracks a proxy variable rather than carried spend/budget, fails held-out
   discharge/admissibility prediction, and is unstable under budget perturbation. Status:
   `proxy_obstructed`.

4. **Complete price field.** A finite field contains one linked component for every declared
   constraint. All components pass slack/proxy checks. Status: `field_lawful`.

5. **Incomplete field.** A field omits a declared constraint or includes a component not linked to the
   field record. Status: `incomplete_or_unpriced`.

6. **Lawful compressed summary.** The summary is carried, compresses the field, predicts
   admissibility/policy-support changes beyond the external-observation quotient, remains dual-stable
   under perturbation, and collapses in slack. Status: `summary_lawful`.

7. **Redescription summary.** A summary correlates with behavior but supplies no held-out distinction
   beyond the current external-observation quotient. Status: `summary_redescription`.

8. **Summary that does not collapse in slack.** Predictive legitimacy may hold, but the shadow component
   remains active when every relevant component is slack. Status: `slack_obstructed`.

9. **Component/summary with statused residual gap.** A nonzero signal in slack can be lawful only as a
   separate carried residual/noise status, not as an unqualified price field. It is outside the positive
   price branch until statused.

10. **Endogenous budget repair that discharges residual.** The budget-setting move raises or changes a
    budget, but the meta-audit shows original-lineage residual decreased. Status:
    `residual_discharge_verified`.

11. **Budget inflation / capture detected.** The reported ledger says healthy, the move inflates
    budget/Omega, and Xi's original-lineage blind-spot witness persists. Status: `capture_detected`.

12. **Capture claim rejected.** A no-capture Omega-lineage audit and reported ledger agree under the
    declared comparison, yet capture is claimed. Status: `capture_claim_rejected`; this is the E8.1
    falsifier.

13. **Missing meta-audit.** An endogenous budget-setting move exists, but no carried meta-audit
    distinguishes discharge from inflation. Status: `meta_audit_missing`.

14. **Meta-audit capacity saturation.** A meta-audit tower exists but is saturated under E2's capacity
    measure. Status: `meta_audit_capacity_blocked`; further audit is not silently assumed.

15. **Wrong-lineage capture claim.** A blind-spot audit is for a different Omega lineage than the
    budget-setting move. The case predicates reject it through `lineageLinked`.

16. **Disconnected budget flag.** A budget inflation boolean is supplied but does not compare concrete
    `oldBudget < newBudget` data or ledger entries. The future toy-lab must reject it.

17. **Future sweep guard.** Round B must compute multipliers, slack, proxy prediction, dual stability,
    summary legitimacy, budget inflation, original-lineage blind spots, and E2 capacity from concrete
    carried records and exact rational data, not from hardcoded status rows.

## 5. Interaction Status

- **D3 carried records.** E8 credits signals, price components, fields, summaries, held-out prediction
  records, proxy/slack violation records, budget-setting moves, Omega-lineage audits, reported-health
  records, meta-audits, and status records only through carried occurrence evidence.

- **D4 E-system and ledger.** All spend/budget entries live in `S.Lambda_S`. E8 introduces no parallel
  budget ledger or external accounting channel.

- **D6/E6+E9 direct reuse.** E8 reuses E6+E9's scalar `KKTWitness`, `BindingExposureBudget`,
  `SlackExposureBudget`, `E6_AttentionKKT`, and `E6_SlackCollapse` componentwise. It does not duplicate
  their KKT algebra. E8 adds the multi-constraint field, carried component inventory, and compressed
  summary lawfulness test.

- **E2 direct reuse for E8.1.** E8.1's meta-audit bound is derived from E2's
  `AuditTowerCapacityMeasure`, `CapacityRealizableTower`, and `E2_CapacityBound`; `CapacityAdmissible`
  and `CapacitySaturated` remain status-context inputs for the meta-audit surface. E8.1 does not claim
  audit regress terminates by fiat.

- **Xi Omega-lineage substrate.** `Xi.Obstruction.adequacyDefect` and `blindSpotWitness` provide the
  concrete matrix-host meaning of an original-lineage blind spot. E8.1 adds carried lineage and
  reported-ledger records around that substrate; Xi alone does not classify capture.

- **F12 redescription/no-free-distinction.** No general F12 Lean theorem is imported. E8 uses a
  certified held-out legitimacy structure requiring a same-quotient/different-summary/different-policy
  witness, following E1/E7's certified-input precedent.

- **E7 forward pointer.** E7 notes threshold raising may be lawful budget repair with pathology. E8.1
  discharges the vocabulary side of that pointer by defining the budget-inflation/capture status
  surface, but a concrete bridge from E7 alarm-threshold records to E8.1 budget-setting moves remains a
  future cross-law construction.

- **E5 and E4.** E8 is independent of E5 collapse status and E4 compilation status. A collapsed system
  may have price fields, and a compiled repair may alter budgets, but E8 does not import either theorem.

- **Spend source material.** Spend's currency roles C1-C4, slack regime, and proxy signature are the
  recognition source. Foundations V already theoremized one access-budget instance in E6+E9; E8 is the
  multi-constraint/endogenous-control generalization.

- **Certified-input / governance boundary guard.** E8 assumes honest declared constraints, budgets,
  KKT witness data, held-out prediction rows, perturbation tests, Omega lineage, meta-audit carriedness,
  and E2 tower data. It proves a normal form under those inputs, not universal honesty of prices,
  feelings, metrics, budgets, regulators, or organizations.

## 6. Nonclaims

- E8 does not claim a carried control signal is literally equal to a Lagrange multiplier. It claims the
  lawful signal contains a shadow-price component identified by carried KKT/slack/proxy tests.

- E8 does not prove a new convex-analysis theorem. It reuses E6's certified KKT witness per component
  and treats convexity/differentiability/finite-difference adequacy as host obligations already exposed
  by E6+E9.

- E8 does not say every affect-like, urgency-like, stress-like, morale-like, or liquidity-pressure
  index is lawful. A compressed summary must pass predictive legitimacy beyond the observation quotient
  and slack collapse. Readability or correlation is not enough.

- E8 does not mechanize F12 globally. The redescription guard is represented by a certified,
  non-vacuous held-out witness structure.

- E8 does not say slack signals are impossible. It says they are not unqualified price signals unless
  statused as residual/noise/proxy effects.

- E8.1 does not moralize capture. `capture_detected` is a statused defect class over carried
  budget-setting, lineage, and ledger records.

- E8.1 does not say all budget increases are capture. Residual discharge under the original lineage is
  `residual_discharge_verified`, not capture.

- E8.1 does not say reported health alone proves success. The Omega-lineage audit must distinguish
  residual discharge from budget inflation.

- E8.1 does not prove that every possible meta-audit can be carried. E2 bounds the audit tower; if the
  tower saturates, the status is recorded rather than bypassed.

- The future toy-lab must use exact rational arithmetic for costs, budgets, multipliers, residuals, and
  perturbations. No floats, random thresholds, or disconnected boolean flags are acceptable.
