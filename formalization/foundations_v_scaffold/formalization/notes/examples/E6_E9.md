### Exposure budget

D6 gives ledger hooks, but it does not define binding or slack. E6 introduces:

```text
B_exp : carried exposure-budget value in S.Lambda_S
Spend_exp(w) : carried exposure spend of allocation w
Cost_exp(p, delta) : carried marginal exposure cost for increasing p by delta

BudgetFeasible(w) :=
  Spend_exp(w) <= B_exp
  and all cost/spend/budget entries are present in S.Lambda_S
  and economy.BudgetAdmissible(allocation move for w)

BindingExposureBudget(w) :=
  BudgetFeasible(w)
  and Spend_exp(w) = B_exp

SlackExposureBudget(w) :=
  BudgetFeasible(w)
  and Spend_exp(w) < B_exp
```

For a differentiable finite host, define the marginal discharge of owned probe `p` at allocation `w` by

```text
MarginalDischarge(p, w) :=
  - d/d alpha [Residual(w + alpha e_p)] at alpha = 0+
```

For a discrete finite host, replace this with the certified one-step finite difference:

```text
FiniteMarginalDischarge(p, w, delta) :=
  Residual(w) - Residual(w[p := w(p)+delta])
```

The marginal ratio is:

```text
AllocationRatio(p, w) :=
  MarginalDischarge(p, w) / MarginalCost(p, w)
```

where `MarginalCost(p,w) > 0` is a carried exposure cost unless the null-mode legality clause explicitly marks the direction as zero-cost and therefore non-creditable.

The acquisition/access ratio theorems use the same positivity discipline:

```text
PositiveAcquisitionCosts(candidates) :=
  every listed acquisition candidate has AcquisitionCost(M) > 0

PositiveAccessMoveCosts(candidates) :=
  every listed access move has AccessMoveCost(move) > 0.
```

Zero-cost or non-positive-cost candidates are not creditable ratio candidates unless separately statused by the null-mode legality clause.

The strict multiplier conclusion needs a nondegenerate scarcity hypothesis, not merely equality of spend and budget:

```text
GenuineScarcity(w) :=
  exists p,
    p in support(L_t)
    and w(p) > 0
    and MarginalDischarge(p,w) > 0.
```

This rules out the flat-objective case where the budget happens to be fully spent but every selected probe has zero marginal discharge.

### Acquisition discharge

For a candidate new probe/family `M` not in `support(L_t)`, D6 supplies:

```text
AcquisitionStrict(L_t, M) := not SameFamilySaturated(L_t, M)
```

In the Xi host, the expected residual discharge of acquiring `M` is the chain-rule contraction:

```text
AcqDischarge(L_t, M) :=
  tr (K_DM|L * K_MM|L^dagger * K_MD|L)
```

with the conditional-currency blocks imported from the Xi critical-pair/strict-extension apparatus. The acquisition ratio is:

```text
AcquisitionRatio(L_t, M) :=
  AcqDischarge(L_t, M) / AcquisitionCost(M)
```

under the side conditions:

```text
M in economy.catalog
M notin support(L_t)
AcquisitionStrict(L_t, M)
BudgetFeasible(acquisition M)
ViabilityRiskAdmissible(S, L_t, M)
```

The risk admissibility predicate is a carried/certified guard; E9 does not claim lawful curiosity outside that bound.

## 2. Theorem

### E6 attention normal form

Assume:

```text
PersistentViable(S)
LawfulActiveFamilyAt(economy, L_t, ...)
BudgetFeasible(w)
FiniteConvexAccessHost(S, economy, Residual, Cost_exp)
NoOverreadDiscipline(S, Q_w)
NullModeLegality(S, economy)
```

`FiniteConvexAccessHost` packages the finite-dimensional hypotheses needed for KKT: a finite active support, a convex feasible set, differentiable or subgradient discharge objective, and carried positive costs on credited directions.

The lawful attention policy is the following normal form.

```text
Theorem E6_AttentionKKT:
  If BindingExposureBudget(w*) and GenuineScarcity(w*) and w* is a lawful viable optimum for

      maximize Discharge(w) := Residual(w_0) - Residual(w)
      subject to Spend_exp(w) <= B_exp, w >= 0, support(w) subset support(L_t),

  then there exists a carried lambda_exp > 0 such that

      for selected p with w*(p) > 0:
        AllocationRatio(p, w*) = lambda_exp

      for unselected p with w*(p) = 0:
        AllocationRatio(p, w*) <= lambda_exp.
```

Obstruction:

```text
Delta_att(eps) :=
  { (p,p') |
      ViabilityOptimalAllocationClaimed(w*)
      and (p is selected in w*)
      and (p' is a feasible alternative probe at w*)
      and AllocationRatio(p',w*) > AllocationRatio(p,w*) + eps
      and no carried EpsilonKKTGap accounts for the difference }
```

An element of `Delta_att(eps)` defeats the claimed normal form at tolerance `eps`.

Clause 2, slack collapse:

```text
Theorem E6_SlackCollapse:
  If SlackExposureBudget(w*) and w* solves the same finite convex program,
  then lambda_exp = 0.
```

In this slack regime, attention signals that claim positive scarcity price are unlawful unless carried as proxy/noise/statused residuals.

Clause 3, salience:

```text
Delta_Xi(w) := Xi_C(D | L_w) - Omega

SalienceField(w) :=
  spectral top direction(s) of Delta_Xi(w)
  with magnitude lambda_max(Delta_Xi(w)).
```

If a carried attention/salience claim identifies what the system is pulled toward, it must factor through this spectral excess readout. A claimed salience direction not supported by `Delta_Xi` is an obstruction.

Clause 4, inherited discipline:

```text
NoOverread:
  claimed attention distinctions must descend through Q_w / purchased exposure.

NullModeLegality:
  no probe may receive discharge credit along a zero-cost or null residual direction.
```

Obstructions:

```text
OverreadNull :=
  claimed distinction not purchasable from current allocation
  or not descending through Q_w         -- F11/F12 violation

ZeroCostCreditNull :=
  credited discharge on a direction whose carried marginal cost is zero
  or whose Xi residual contraction is null.
```

Clause 5, bounded deviation:

```text
EpsilonKKT(w*, eps) :=
  all selected/unselected ratio violations are <= eps
  and EpsilonKKTGap(w*, eps) is a D3/D4 carried statused residual.
```

A satisficing allocation is lawful only when the deviation gap is carried and statused, not hidden as optimality.

Proxy-attention null:

```text
ProxyAttentionNull :=
  weights are uncorrelated with carried cost/spend ledger entries
  and fail held-out prediction of discharge
  and fail dual stability under budget perturbation.
```

This is inherited from the Spend proxy-currency failure signature.

### E9 curiosity normal form

For candidate strict extensions:

```text
CandidateAcquisition(L_t, M) :=
  M in economy.catalog

  and AcquisitionStrict(L_t, M)
  and BudgetFeasible(acquisition M)
```

```text
Theorem E9_CuriosityArgmax:
  Given a nonempty finite candidate list that is complete for
  CandidateAcquisition(L_t, M), and positive acquisition costs on that list,

      M* in argmax_M AcquisitionRatio(L_t, M)

  over every CandidateAcquisition(L_t, M), not merely over the supplied list.
```

Equivalently, for every admissible strict candidate `M`,

```text
AcquisitionRatio(L_t, M) <= AcquisitionRatio(L_t, M*) + eps
```

with any positive `eps` carried as a statused residual if satisficing rather than exact optimality is claimed.

### Joint arbitration theorem

Define a common access move type:

```text
AccessMove :=
  | allocate(p, delta)       -- D6 allocation within support(L_t)
  | acquire(M)               -- D6 acquisition strict extension
```

with:

```text
AccessMoveDischarge(move) :=
  allocation finite/marginal discharge        for allocate(p,delta)
  AcqDischarge(L_t,M)                         for acquire(M)

AccessMoveCost(move) :=
  carried exposure cost of delta              for allocate(p,delta)
  carried acquisition cost of M               for acquire(M)

AccessMoveRatio(move) :=
  AccessMoveDischarge(move) / AccessMoveCost(move)
```

The finite access-move list used by the theorem must be complete:

```text
CompleteAccessMoveCandidateList(candidates) :=
  every AccessMoveAdmissible(move) appears in candidates.
```

and admissibility:

```text
AccessMoveAdmissible(move) :=
  D6 lawfulness pre/post active-family guards
  and budget admissibility
  and, for acquisitions, AcquisitionStrict and ViabilityRiskAdmissible.
```

The bundled E6/E9 claim is:

```text
Theorem E6_E9_AccessArbitration:
  A single carried AccessPolicy over allocation and acquisition is lawful only if,
  whenever it selects move m under binding exposure/acquisition budget,

      AccessMoveRatio(m') <= AccessMoveRatio(m) + eps

  for every admissible alternative m',
with eps = 0 for exact arbitration and eps carried as EpsilonKKTGap otherwise.
```

The Lean surface separates the existence-of-best-move theorem from the policy theorem: `E6_E9_AccessArbitration` produces a maximum-ratio admissible move from a complete positive-cost list, while `selected_access_move_ratio_bound` and its epsilon variant state that a carried policy's certified selected argmax dominates every admissible alternative.

This is the precise meaning of "attention and curiosity are primal-dual siblings": E6 optimizes owned-probe weights, E9 optimizes purchase of strict extensions, and the boundary is decided by one carried comparison of marginal discharge per carried cost. An arbitration violation is:

The arbitration statement itself compares carried ratios and does not require `GenuineScarcity` unless it is also used to infer a strictly positive exposure multiplier from the selected allocation side.

```text
Delta_access(eps) :=
  { (m,m') |
      AccessPolicy selects m
      and AccessMoveAdmissible(m')
      and AccessMoveRatio(m') > AccessMoveRatio(m) + eps
      and no carried statused residual records the gap }
```

## 3. Proof Spine

### What is straightforward from D1-D6 and Xi

- D6 supplies the legal move shapes. Allocation cannot add support; acquisition must add a catalog probe and must include `AcquisitionStrict`; retirement is not part of E6/E9 optimization except as a possible background policy move.
- D6's pre/post `LawfulActiveFamilyAt` guards ensure E6/E9 never optimize from a malformed active-family state.
- D3/D4 supply carried policy, multiplier, salience, and residual-gap records by ordinary `CarriedRecordAt`/ledger hooks.
- D2 supplies the role of predictive surplus. In the Xi host, the residual trace `tr Xi_C(D|L_w)` is the concrete discharge functional.
- The Xi chain rule supplies E9's numerator:

  ```text
  Xi_C(D | [L;M]) =
    Xi_C(D | L) - K_DM|L K_MM|L^dagger K_MD|L
  ```

  so the trace of the contraction term is exactly expected residual discharge for a candidate acquisition.

- Same-family saturation is already the D6/Xi strictness theorem: if `M = B L` and the conditional currency term vanishes, the extension leaves the residual unchanged. Therefore E9's fake-novelty null reduces to D6's existing strictness gate plus D2's Xi readout.

### New proof work

The main new proof is finite-dimensional constrained optimization.

For E6, fix an admissible currency and finite support. The optimization problem is:

```text
maximize    D(w) := Residual(w_0) - Residual(w)
subject to  C(w) := sum_p c_p w_p <= B_exp
            w_p >= 0
            support(w) subset support(L_t).
```

Equivalently minimize `Residual(w)` over the same feasible polytope. The Lagrangian for minimization is:

```text
L(w, lambda, mu) :=
  Residual(w) + lambda (C(w) - B_exp) - sum_p mu_p w_p
```

with:

```text
lambda >= 0
mu_p >= 0
lambda (C(w*) - B_exp) = 0
mu_p w*_p = 0
grad_p Residual(w*) + lambda c_p - mu_p = 0.
```

Writing marginal discharge as `-grad_p Residual(w*)`, selected probes (`w*_p > 0`) satisfy:

```text
MarginalDischarge(p,w*) / c_p = lambda
```

and unselected probes satisfy the inequality form.

Binding alone only states `C(w*) = B_exp`; it does not force `lambda > 0`, because complementary slackness is then `lambda * 0 = 0`. In the degenerate flat-objective case, every selected probe may have `MarginalDischarge(p,w*) = 0`, and stationarity permits `lambda = 0` even though the budget is fully spent. The added `GenuineScarcity(w*)` hypothesis closes this gap: for some selected probe `p`, stationarity gives `lambda = MarginalDischarge(p,w*) / c_p`, and the finite host's positive credited cost condition gives `lambda > 0`.

If the budget is slack, complementary slackness gives `lambda = 0`. This proves E6 clause 1 under binding plus genuine scarcity, and clause 2 under slack, under the finite convex host hypotheses.

For E6 clause 3, the proof is spectral rather than KKT. On a finite operator laboratory, `Delta_Xi = Xi_C(D|L_w)-Omega` is a concrete square matrix. The carried salience field is the top eigendirection/eigenvalue of this residual excess. The proof obligation is to show the claimed salience record is exactly this spectral readout, or is statused as a bounded approximation.

For E9, the optimization is finite arg-max over admissible candidate acquisitions:

```text
maximize AcquisitionRatio(L_t,M)
over CandidateAcquisition(L_t,M).
```

If the catalog is finite and costs are positive on credited candidates, an arg-max exists. Ties are lawful only when carried as a tie/statused residual or resolved by a declared deterministic policy.

For joint arbitration, combine the finite owned-probe marginal move set and finite candidate-acquisition set into one finite access-move set and apply the same ratio arg-max condition. This does not require E8 to be landed; it is the single-budget access case. The statement that this "specializes E8" is a forward reference and must be recorded as:

```text
ForwardObligation(E8_access_specialization):
  When E8 lands, show lambda_exp is the exposure component of the general
  carried control-price field.
```

This obligation is not a blocker for E6/E9.

### What is not supplied by D6

D6 does not supply:

- convexity/concavity of the discharge objective;
- differentiability or a subgradient calculus;
- positivity/nondegeneracy of every cost functional;
- a proof that a host's abstract `SameFamilySaturated` predicate is honestly the Xi predicate;
- a completed bridge from closure-deficit scores to Xi traces.

E6/E9 must state these as theorem hypotheses or host obligations. This is a proof-effort gap, not a defect in D6's interface.

## 4. Case Enumeration

- **Binding budget, unique KKT solution:** `Spend_exp(w*) = B_exp`, costs are positive, the convex program has a unique optimum satisfying `GenuineScarcity(w*)` (some selected probe has strictly positive marginal discharge), and all selected probes share the same discharge-per-cost ratio `lambda_exp > 0`. E6 holds exactly with `eps = 0`.
- **Binding budget, non-unique/tied optimum:** multiple probes or access moves share the top ratio. The law allows this only with a carried tie-breaker or carried `EpsilonKKTGap`; otherwise a claimed unique attention target is over-specified.
- **Slack budget:** `Spend_exp(w*) < B_exp`. Complementary slackness gives `lambda_exp = 0`; a persistent positive scarcity/salience signal is a slack-collapse obstruction unless statused as noise/proxy.
- **Same-family-saturated candidate:** `SameFamilySaturated(L_t,M)` holds. D6's strictness gate rejects acquisition, and Xi gives zero residual contraction. A "curiosity" claim for this move is fake novelty.
- **Genuinely discharging strict extension:** `M` is catalog-declared, not in support, not same-family-saturated, budget-feasible, risk-admissible, and has positive `AcqDischarge/cost`. E9 ranks it against other strict candidates and allocation moves.
- **Allocation/acquisition tie boundary:** an owned-probe reweighting and a strict acquisition have equal top ratio. A single carried policy may select either only if the tie is declared/statused; otherwise the arbitration theorem is underspecified.
- **Viability-risk-violating candidate:** `M` has high discharge ratio but violates `ViabilityRiskAdmissible`. It is outside E9's admissible set; excluding it is not a failure of curiosity.
- **Proxy-attention case:** weights track a theorist proxy or raw surprise but not carried costs/spend and fail held-out discharge prediction or dual stability. This triggers the Spend proxy-failure null.
- **Overread case:** an attention claim distinguishes states not purchasable from `Q_w` or not descending through the current exposure quotient. This is an F11/F12 obstruction, not a better attention policy.
- **Zero-cost/null-mode credit:** a probe receives credited discharge on a zero-cost or null residual direction. E6 rejects the credit even if the host's raw optimizer would choose it.

- **D4 E-system and ledger:** all costs, budgets, multipliers, risk guards, and statused residuals live on the D4 carried ledger and audit surface. E6/E9 do not introduce a second accounting channel.
- **D5 closed-loop scope:** E6/E9 do not require closed-loop scope by default. If an exposure allocation or acquisition is later used as a repair-typed audit entry, D5 decides whether that occurrence is endogenous rather than theorist-supplied.
- **D6 probe economy:** this is the direct dependency. E6 discharges D6's deferred obligations for KKT allocation, slack collapse, and salience. E9 discharges D6's deferred obligation to combine acquisition strictness with D2/Xi discharge and budget binding.
- **FIV F9 statused residual:** clause 5 uses F9 discipline: bounded deviation is lawful only as a carried/statused residual, not as hidden optimality.
- **F11 no-overread and F12 null-mode/free-distinction legality:** E6's discipline clause inherits these. Attention cannot read distinctions not purchased by the current quotient, and zero-cost/null residual directions cannot be credited as exposure.

- **Spend currency duality:** Spend supplies currency roles C1-C4, shadow price, slack collapse, and proxy-currency failure. E6 theorem-izes the exposure-budget instance of that duality.
- **E8 forward reference:** E6's wording that it "specializes E8" is a forward obligation, not a proved dependency. Record `ForwardObligation(E8_access_specialization)` for the later E8 landing.

Anti-reductionism check: E6/E9 are not merely D6 plus an optimizer and not merely D2/Xi restated. D6 defines lawful exposure moves; D2 defines surplus/residual readouts. E6/E9 define the carried governance policy that decides how scarce exposure budget is spent and how owned allocation competes with strict acquisition under audit.

## 6. Nonclaims

- E6/E9 do not assert a unique universal cost functional. The law is a family over admissible exposure currencies; falsifiers are currency-relative.
- E6 does not claim all empirical or psychological attention is exhausted by this structural form. Recognition instances such as rational inattention, selective attention, or monitoring design are cited as instances, not as priority or phenomenology claims.
- E9's statement that novelty-seeking is not a preference is structural only: strict acquisition must not be same-family-saturated if it is to discharge residual. No motivational or psychological claim is made.
- E6/E9 do not prove the general E8 control-price law. The access-budget specialization is recorded as a forward obligation for E8.
- E6/E9 inherit D6's certified-input limits: `CompleteProbeCatalog`, `BudgetAdmissible`, cost-ledger honesty, risk-admissibility honesty, and the host's abstract `SameFamilySaturated` predicate are not derived internally.
- E6/E9 do not prove that every abstract probe economy has an Xi matrix representation. The Xi route is the concrete operator-lab instantiation.
- E6/E9 do not allow allocation to smuggle acquisition. D6 support membership remains the definition of active exposure; zero-to-positive activation is acquisition and must pass E9's strictness/risk/budget gates.
- E6/E9 do not claim that violating viability risk is "curiosity." Such candidates are outside the theorem's admissible set.
- E6/E9 do not claim the salience field is phenomenal salience. It is the carried spectral readout of residual excess.
- E6/E9 do not introduce biological or life-sciences content.
- `selected_access_move_ratio_bound` does not derive `hselected_argmax` from `policy.selects`; it takes the selected move's argmax certificate as separately supplied, certified data. External review v12 confirmed this is acceptable for the current `AccessPolicy` (a bare `selects` predicate with no internal decision procedure to derive optimality from) but noted a bundled `PolicySelectedArgmax(policy, selected, candidates, pricing)` predicate, and a future wrapper theorem additionally requiring `PolicyCarriedAt`, would make the policy machinery load-bearing rather than decorative. Recorded as a deferred, non-blocking refinement, not a gap in the current theorem.
