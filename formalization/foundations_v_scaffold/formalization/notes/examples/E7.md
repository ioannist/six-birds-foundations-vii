# E7 - Alarm Law

**Source text:** THEOREMS.md §3, E7.  
**Target Lean module:** `SixBirdsFoundationsV.Laws.E7Alarm`.  
**Grounding:** E7 sits after E6+E9. It reuses the D4 E-system, the D6 probe economy, E6's carried attention/access policy and budget apparatus, and the Xi residual machinery. F19 viability coupling is not mechanized in this repo; E7 therefore treats the dissolution-obstruction/viability-kernel side as certified host predicates, following D6's precedent for `ViabilityRiskAdmissible`.

## 1. Setup

Let

```text
S : ESystem ...
T := S.T
economy : ProbeEconomy S Probe XiFamily LedgerEntry
policy : AccessPolicy Probe XiFamily
```

be a D4 E-system with a D6 probe economy and an E6/E9 access policy. The active family at the alarm read point is

```text
L_t : ActiveFamily Probe XiFamily
```

with D6 well-formedness:

```text
LawfulActiveFamilyAt(economy, L_t, n_t, sourceTag_t, generatedByS_t, inScope_t)
```

E7 does not weaken D6's catalog/support/carriedness invariant.

The Xi side uses the same matrix/readout family as D2, D6, and E6/E9:

```text
C           carried exposure currency / precision matrix
D           declared dissolving/readout family
KLLdagger   supplied block inverse for the active family
Omega       carried residual budget / alarm threshold matrix

ResidualMatrix(L_t) := Xi_C(D | L_t)
Delta_Xi(L_t, Omega) := ResidualMatrix(L_t) - Omega
```

In Lean-facing terms, the concrete Xi host should use the already-vendored names:

```text
ResidualMatrix(L_t) := adequacyResidual C L D KLLdagger
Delta_Xi := matSub (adequacyResidual C L D KLLdagger) Omega
BlindSpotWitness(z) := quad Delta_Xi z > 0.
```

The source text's `z*(Xi_C(D|L_t)-Omega)z > 0` is therefore represented by the existing `quad` form over `matSub`.

### Viability coupling

F19's viability kernel and dissolution obstruction are not mechanized here. E7 introduces certified predicates:

```text
Horizon : Type

DissolutionObstructionNonempty(S, L_t, z, horizon) : Prop
ViabilityProbeDescends(S, L_t, z, horizon) : Prop

ViabilityCoupled(S, L_t, z, horizon) :=
  DissolutionObstructionNonempty(S, L_t, z, horizon).
```

`ViabilityCoupled` says the positive Xi residual is not merely unread; it bears on whether the declared viability probe will descend within the horizon. This is the abstract E7 placeholder for F19's `Delta_diss` content, not a reimplementation of F19.

### Attention constraint set and preemption

E6/E9 choose allocation/acquisition moves inside a standing access-policy feasible set. E7's differentia is that alarm rewrites that feasible set before comparison:

```text
AttentionConstraintSet :=
  carried exposure budget entries
  + admissible access moves
  + active priority/threshold records
  + policy-level constraints used by AccessPolicy.

AlarmConstraintRewrite(before, after, z) :=
  before governs the standing E6 AccessPolicy
  and after is carried in S.Lambda_S
  and after differs from before
  and after makes a z-directed exposure/currentization move admissible
  and after marks the standing E6 policy as preempted for this witness.
```

Thus alarm is not an `AccessMove` that wins a high `AccessMoveRatio` inside the old E6 policy. It is a carried rewrite of the policy's constraint set. The later exposure move may be a D6 allocation/acquisition under the rewritten constraints, but the alarm signature is the rewrite itself.

The source phrase "P6<-P6 move" is read as a P6/accounting constraint rewrite on the ledger/audit surface, not as a D4 `RepairSort`: D4's repair generator intentionally excludes P6. E7's preemption channel lives in the ledger/audit accounting layer that can rewrite allocator constraints.

### Alarm disposition records

To make the "exactly one of" statement non-vacuous, E7 uses a carried disposition record with a shared branch discriminant and a witness-level uniqueness certificate. The discriminant alone only says one record has one tag; E7 needs the stronger claim that the system cannot carry conflicting disposition records for the same witness and horizon.

```text
inductive AlarmDispositionKind
  | currentize
  | boundary_nonclosure
  | lawful_discount

structure AlarmDispositionRecord where
  witnessVector : WitnessVector
  horizon : Horizon
  kind : AlarmDispositionKind
  sourcePolicy : AccessPolicy Probe XiFamily
  preConstraintSet : AttentionConstraintSet
  postConstraintSet : AttentionConstraintSet
  statusRecord : AlarmStatusRecord
```

The record is credited only at a concrete occurrence:

```text
AlarmDispositionCarriedAt(record, n0, sourceTag, generatedByS, inScope) :=
  CarriedRecordAt alarmDispositionPolicy record n0 sourceTag generatedByS inScope.

AlarmDispositionFor(S, L_t, z, horizon, record) :=
  record.witnessVector = z
  and record.horizon = horizon
  and AlarmDispositionCarriedAt(record, n0, sourceTag, generatedByS, inScope)
  and record belongs to the carried alarm-response surface of S at L_t
      via responseForActiveFamily.
```

Each branch must use the same carried disposition record and the same witness/horizon data:

```text
CurrentizationCase(record) :=
  record.kind = currentize
  and AlarmDispositionFor(S, L_t, record.witnessVector, record.horizon, record)
  and AlarmConstraintRewrite(record.preConstraintSet, record.postConstraintSet, record.witnessVector)
  and ForcedExposureMoveRecorded(
        record.postConstraintSet.exposureBudgetEntry,
        record.witnessVector)
  and PreemptionOverrides(policy, record.witnessVector)
  and NoOverreadDiscipline(S, Q_w, record.postConstraintSet)
  and NullModeLegality(S, economy, record.postConstraintSet, record.witnessVector).

BoundaryNonclosureCase(record) :=
  record.kind = boundary_nonclosure
  and AlarmDispositionFor(S, L_t, record.witnessVector, record.horizon, record)
  and not ViabilityProbeDescends(S, L_t, record.witnessVector, record.horizon)
  and BoundaryNonclosureStatusRecorded(record.statusRecord).

LawfulDiscountCase(record) :=
  record.kind = lawful_discount
  and AlarmDispositionFor(S, L_t, record.witnessVector, record.horizon, record)
  and F9StatusedDiscount(record.statusRecord)
  and DiscountReason(record.statusRecord) in
      { protocol_artifact, currentizable_residue, out_of_horizon, coupling_dissolved }.

CurrentizationHolds(S, L_t, z, horizon) :=
  exists record, AlarmDispositionFor(S, L_t, z, horizon, record)
    and CurrentizationCase(record).

BoundaryNonclosureHolds(S, L_t, z, horizon) :=
  exists record, AlarmDispositionFor(S, L_t, z, horizon, record)
    and BoundaryNonclosureCase(record).

LawfulDiscountHolds(S, L_t, z, horizon) :=
  exists record, AlarmDispositionFor(S, L_t, z, horizon, record)
    and LawfulDiscountCase(record).
```

The three cases are comparably strict: each requires the same carried occurrence discipline, the same witness/horizon identity, and a branch-specific status payload.

Finally, E7 assumes a totality-and-uniqueness certificate:

```text
LawfulAlarmDispositionUniqueTotality(S, L_t, z, horizon) :=
  exists record,
    AlarmDispositionFor(S, L_t, z, horizon, record)
    and (
      CurrentizationCase(record)
      or BoundaryNonclosureCase(record)
      or LawfulDiscountCase(record)
    ),
  and forall record1 record2,
    AlarmDispositionFor(S, L_t, z, horizon, record1) ->
    AlarmDispositionFor(S, L_t, z, horizon, record2) ->
    record1.kind = record2.kind.
```

This is certified host governance data, analogous to D3/D4/D5 completeness certificates. It is not a theorem that arbitrary hosts respond honestly. The existence clause is load-bearing: it must supply a branch-valid record, not just a carried record with a tag, because a `currentize` tag without `AlarmConstraintRewrite`/ledger/audit/no-overread/null-mode payload is not an alarm disposition. The uniqueness clause is also load-bearing: without it, a host could carry both a currentization record and a boundary-nonclosure record for the same witness and still satisfy each record-local branch predicate separately.

Lean note: `AlarmDispositionFor` intentionally ranges over every genuinely
carried occurrence matching the witness/horizon and `responseForActiveFamily`;
it is not limited to a finite `inventory.records` list. The `records` field in
`AlarmDispositionInventory` is bookkeeping for hosts that want an enumerable
inventory, not the scope of the trichotomy's uniqueness claim.

## 2. Theorem

Assume:

```text
S : ESystem ...
economy : ProbeEconomy S Probe XiFamily LedgerEntry
policy : AccessPolicy Probe XiFamily
PolicyCarriedAt(policyRecord, policy, ...)
LawfulActiveFamilyAt(economy, L_t, ...)
BlindSpotWitness(z)                       -- quad(Delta_Xi,z) > 0
ViabilityCoupled(S, L_t, z, horizon)      -- abstract F19 Delta_diss nonempty
LawfulAlarmDispositionUniqueTotality(S, L_t, z, horizon)
```

`PolicyCarriedAt` and `LawfulActiveFamilyAt` are statement-faithful hypotheses:
the alarm law classifies a carried E6/E9 policy over a lawful D6 active family.
The Lean proof itself uses the already-certified
`LawfulAlarmDispositionUniqueTotality` package for the finite classification
step, but the theorem signature keeps these hypotheses to match this setup.

F11/F12 discipline is not an idle top-level hypothesis. It is load-bearing inside `CurrentizationCase` and `E7_PreemptionSignature`: a currentization record is lawful only when the rewritten constraint set preserves no-overread and null-mode legality.

E7 states the forced-currentization trichotomy:

```text
Theorem E7_AlarmTrichotomy:
  For any positive, viability-coupled blind-spot witness z,
  there exists a disposition kind k such that:

    every carried AlarmDispositionRecord for (S, L_t, z, horizon)
    has record.kind = k,

  and exactly one of the witness-level propositions holds:

    CurrentizationHolds(S, L_t, z, horizon)
    BoundaryNonclosureHolds(S, L_t, z, horizon)
    LawfulDiscountHolds(S, L_t, z, horizon).
```

"Exactly one" is not an assumed disjunction and is not merely the tautology that one chosen record has one tag. It follows from:

```text
1. branch-valid existence: LawfulAlarmDispositionUniqueTotality supplies a carried record
   satisfying one full branch predicate;
2. witness-level lift: that branch-valid record gives one of
   CurrentizationHolds/BoundaryNonclosureHolds/LawfulDiscountHolds;
3. global uniqueness: all carried records for the same witness/horizon share that kind;
4. exclusivity: the three constructors of AlarmDispositionKind are pairwise distinct;
5. branch discipline: any witness-level branch must cite the same witness/horizon and its
   branch-specific payload.
```

Thus the theorem rules out two simultaneously carried, differently tagged disposition records for the same witness, e.g. one `currentize` record and one `boundary_nonclosure` record for the same `(S, L_t, z, horizon)`.

### Preemption theorem

The currentization branch carries the alarm/attention distinction:

```text
Theorem E7_PreemptionSignature:
  CurrentizationCase(record) ->
    AlarmConstraintRewrite(record.preConstraintSet, record.postConstraintSet, z)
    and record.preConstraintSet != record.postConstraintSet
    and the z-directed exposure move is admissible under postConstraintSet
    and NoOverreadDiscipline(S, Q_w, record.postConstraintSet)
    and NullModeLegality(S, economy, record.postConstraintSet, z)
    and not merely selected by AccessMoveRatio inside preConstraintSet.
```

Alarm therefore changes the constraint set of the attention policy itself while preserving the F11/F12 discipline inherited by E6/E9: preemption cannot smuggle an overread distinction or credit a zero-cost/null-mode direction. A high-ratio access move inside the old policy is attention/curiosity, not alarm.

### Obstruction/falsifier set

```text
FalseAlarm :=
  CurrentizationHolds(S, L_t, z, horizon)
  and not BlindSpotWitness(z)
```

or, operationally, currentization caused by a protocol artifact that is not caught and statused by the carried audit.

```text
MissedAlarm :=
  BlindSpotWitness(z)
  and ViabilityCoupled(S,L_t,z,horizon)
  and witness persists through the declared horizon
  and not CurrentizationHolds(S,L_t,z,horizon)
  and not BoundaryNonclosureHolds(S,L_t,z,horizon)
  and not LawfulDiscountHolds(S,L_t,z,horizon).
```

A missed alarm either exhibits a hidden channel outside the declared carried monitoring or defeats the claimed E7 instance.

```text
AlarmFatigue(E7.1) :=
  sustained positive excess
  and finite preemption/discharge capacity
  and insufficient discharge capacity over the horizon
  -> preemption channel saturates, forcing lawful threshold repair
     or producing pathology.
```

E7.1 is recorded here as a corollary/falsifier family but is not part of the core E7 Lean obligation until E2's capacity bound lands.

## 3. Proof Spine

### What is straightforward from landed apparatus

- D6 and E6/E9 supply the access-policy and exposure-budget vocabulary being preempted: `AccessPolicy`, `PolicyCarriedAt`, `BudgetFeasible`, `ExposureBudgetWitness`, `AccessMoveRatio`, and D6 `LawfulAllocation`/`LawfulAcquisition`.
- D2/Xi supplies the positive-excess witness shape. In a matrix host, the witness is exactly `quad (matSub (adequacyResidual C L D KLLdagger) Omega) z > 0`.
- D3/D4 supply carried occurrence discipline for `AlarmDispositionRecord`, constraint-rewrite records, ledger entries, discount records, and audit statuses.
- F9 supplies the statused-residual discipline for lawful discount and bounded/shelved residues.

### New proof work

The core new construction is the alarm-disposition interface:

```text
AlarmDispositionKind
AlarmDispositionRecord
AlarmDispositionCarriedAt
AlarmDispositionFor
CurrentizationCase
BoundaryNonclosureCase
LawfulDiscountCase
CurrentizationHolds
BoundaryNonclosureHolds
LawfulDiscountHolds
LawfulAlarmDispositionUniqueTotality
AlarmConstraintRewrite
```

The trichotomy proof is finite record classification plus a branch-validity certificate, not viability-kernel analysis:

1. Use `LawfulAlarmDispositionUniqueTotality` to obtain a carried disposition record for the witness/horizon that already satisfies one full branch predicate.
2. Lift that branch-valid record to the corresponding witness-level predicate (`CurrentizationHolds`, `BoundaryNonclosureHolds`, or `LawfulDiscountHolds`).
3. Use the uniqueness clause to show any other carried disposition record for the same witness/horizon has the same `kind`.
4. Pairwise exclusivity follows globally: two witness-level cases with different constructors would produce two carried records whose kinds must be equal by uniqueness and unequal by constructor disjointness.

The preemption proof is structural:

1. `CurrentizationCase` includes a carried `AlarmConstraintRewrite`.
2. `AlarmConstraintRewrite` includes `preConstraintSet != postConstraintSet`.
3. The z-directed exposure move is made admissible by the post-rewrite constraints.
4. The forced-exposure ledger evidence is tied to this disposition's own
   `record.postConstraintSet.exposureBudgetEntry`, preventing stale
   witness-shared entries from being borrowed across currentization records.
5. `CurrentizationCase` also requires `NoOverreadDiscipline` and `NullModeLegality` for the rewritten constraint set, so preemption cannot bypass the F11/F12 guards ordinary E6/E9 access moves obey.
6. Therefore the alarm did not merely compete under the old `AccessMoveRatio` ordering.

## 4. Case Enumeration

- **Positive witness, viability-coupled, currentized:** `quad(Delta_Xi,z)>0`, `ViabilityCoupled` holds, unique totality supplies a branch-valid `currentize` disposition, every carried disposition record for the same witness/horizon has that same kind, and the system records a carried `AlarmConstraintRewrite` satisfying no-overread and null-mode legality. This is the normal alarm case.
- **Boundary nonclosure:** the witness is positive and viability-coupled, but the carried disposition is `boundary_nonclosure`; the viability probe fails to descend within the declared horizon. This uses E5-compatible status vocabulary but does not require E5 to be landed yet.
- **Lawful protocol-artifact discount:** the witness is audited as a protocol artifact and shelved by an F9 statused record. There is no alarm, and the discount is lawful only because the discount record is carried.
- **Lawful out-of-horizon discount:** the witness has positive excess but the viability coupling is statused as outside the declared horizon. The system shelves it without currentization; this is not a missed alarm.
- **Lawful currentizable-residue discount:** the residual can be currentized by ordinary audit/currentization without preemption. It is discounted as not an alarm because it lacks the viability-coupled forced-preemption condition.
- **False alarm:** currentization occurs without a positive-excess witness, or after audit identifies the witness as artifact/out-of-scope but the system still preempts. This violates E7 and predicts chronic exposure misallocation.
- **Missed alarm:** a positive viability-coupled witness persists unpriced through the horizon with no currentization, no boundary nonclosure status, and no lawful discount. This exposes a hidden channel or defeats the claimed E7 instance.
- **In-policy competition only:** the witness direction receives more exposure because it wins under the standing E6/E9 `AccessMoveRatio`, but the constraint set is unchanged. This may be attention or curiosity, not alarm.
- **Non-viability-coupled residual:** `BlindSpotWitness(z)` holds but `ViabilityCoupled` fails. The system may attend to the residual under E6/E9, but E7's alarm trichotomy is not triggered.
- **Alarm fatigue / E7.1:** sustained excess persists while finite discharge/preemption capacity is insufficient. The preemption channel saturates and lawful threshold repair or pathology appears. This case is recorded as a deferred E7.1/E8 interaction, not as core E7 proof content.

## 5. Interaction Status

- **D2 / Xi adequacy residual:** E7's blind-spot witness is the Xi quadratic excess `quad (Xi_C(D|L_t)-Omega) z > 0`. It reuses `adequacyResidual`, `matSub`, and `quad`; it does not invent a new residual calculus.
- **D3 carried records:** alarm dispositions, constraint rewrites, threshold/status records, and lawful discounts are credited only as carried records at concrete occurrences via D3/D4 `CarriedRecordAt`.
- **D4 E-system and ledger:** preemption is a ledger/audit-surface constraint rewrite in `S.Lambda_S`, not a free policy override. E7 uses D4's carried ledger and does not create a second alarm ledger.
- **D5 closed-loop scope:** E7 does not require closed-loop scope by default. If an alarm record later enters a repair-typed audit history, D5 decides whether that occurrence is endogenous.
- **D6 probe economy:** the forced exposure move ultimately acts on D6 active-family/budget infrastructure, but E7's signature is the pre-policy constraint rewrite, not an in-policy D6 allocation victory.
- **E6/E9 attention and curiosity:** E7 presupposes E6/E9 standing access policy. Alarm preempts that policy by changing the constraint set; without preemption, the same exposure shift remains attention/curiosity.
- **FIV F6 No-Needles:** F6 says formed packages cannot host unpriced needles; E7 supplies the system-side detection/pricing mechanism for a viability-coupled Xi excess.
- **FIV F9 statused residual:** lawful discount and alarm-fatigue status records use F9 discipline. A discounted alarm is lawful only if statused, carried, and auditable.
- **FIV F19 viability:** E7 cites F19's dissolution obstruction and viability descent vocabulary through abstract predicates. The actual F19 viability-kernel/greatest-fixed-point machinery is not mechanized here.
- **E5 forward reference:** `ForwardObligation(E5_boundary_nonclosure_correspondence)` records that the boundary-nonclosure branch should match E5 onset once E5 lands.
- **E2 forward reference:** `ForwardObligation(E2_capacity_bound_correspondence)` records that E7.1's finite-capacity/alarm-fatigue claim must be rechecked against E2 when E2 lands.
- **E8.1 forward pointer:** threshold raising as budget repair may be lawful but pathological; E8.1 must later distinguish residual discharge from budget inflation and audit capture.
- **Spend currency duality:** E7 uses Spend-style budget/constraint rewriting: the alarm branch rewrites the allocator's constraint set and budget priority rather than merely adding information.
- **Certified-input / governance boundary guard:** a well-typed alarm record does not prove the Xi matrices, `Omega`, viability-coupling predicate, horizon, audit discount, or totality certificate are honestly instantiated. E7 proves the carried disposition form under these certified inputs, not universal alarm governance honesty.

Anti-reductionism check: E7 is not "just attention with a high weight" and not "just a salience field." E6/E9 select within the existing access constraints. E7 is the lawful form of viability-coupled excess that rewrites those constraints and preempts the standing policy.

## 6. Nonclaims

- E7 does not formalize F19's viability kernel, greatest fixed point, or dissolution obstruction. `DissolutionObstructionNonempty`, `ViabilityCoupled`, and `ViabilityProbeDescends` are certified host predicates until F19 is mechanized locally or imported.
- E7 does not prove E5. Boundary nonclosure is stated with E5-compatible status vocabulary and recorded as `ForwardObligation(E5_boundary_nonclosure_correspondence)`.
- E7 does not prove E2's capacity bound. Alarm fatigue/E7.1 is deferred to the E8/E2 stage and recorded as `ForwardObligation(E2_capacity_bound_correspondence)`.
- E7 does not prove E8.1's audit-capture or budget-inflation theorem. Threshold raising is recorded as a forward pointer, not discharged here.
- E7 does not claim subjective urgency, phenomenology, panic, pain, or biological alarm. It is an operational currentization/preemption law.
- E7 does not say every positive Xi residual is an alarm. The residual must be viability-coupled within the declared horizon, or it remains an E6/E9 salience/attention object or a lawful discount.
- E7 does not say every viability-coupled hazard is successfully repaired. Currentization can still fail to save viability; boundary nonclosure is one lawful branch.
- E7 does not allow in-policy competition to masquerade as alarm. Without a carried `AlarmConstraintRewrite`, the event is not E7 currentization.
- E7 does not certify the honesty of `Omega`, the horizon, the carried monitoring apparatus, `responseForActiveFamily`, or the totality certificate. Those are governance/certified-input obligations.
- `E7_PreemptionSignature`'s conclusion does not separately restate the forced-exposure ledger conjunct (`ForcedExposureMoveRecorded`), even though `CurrentizationCase` already requires it. External review v14 noted this as a non-blocking traceability nicety, not a soundness gap -- recorded as a deferred, optional refinement, not implemented here.
- E7 does not introduce biological or life-sciences content.
