E4 directly reuses E1's `EndogenousRepairOccurrence`/`Delta`/`Delta_endo`/`DeltaEndoEmpty` for the
"repeatedly invoked, gate-passing, obstruction reduction" clause, FIII's `TopDownChannelAcceptedBool`/

F2/F9 and the Strict-Tests paper's memory-only comparator are not vendored in this repo; E4 treats them
as certified host input, following E1/E3's own precedent for unvendored content.

Scope note: E4 proves the compilation normal form under certified memory-only-comparator and F2 host
input, honest carried window/candidate inventories, and direct FIII/Xi/E1 reuse. Critically,
`E4_Compilation` does **not** derive `CompiledDescent`/`HigherPackageGoesSilent` from
`CompilationLawful` plus the window's own obstruction-reducing/idempotence history — nothing in
D4/E1/FIII/Xi supports that derivation, and the six-field review's own round-5 finding caught an
earlier draft claiming otherwise. Descent and silence are independently-verified hypotheses: descent is candidate-scoped through
`candidate.inducedQuotient` (post-v25), and silence is window-scoped through the shared audit trail
(`CompiledOperatorRecordAttributedTo` already ties that window to the specific candidate), matching
this project's established verification-shaped house style. The Round B
sweep grounds one deterministic instance: all 16 registered comparisons in
`E4_repair_compilation_results.md` PASS, including all five status branches (with both P1 and P2
compilation forms), the shared lawful/descent/silent core split by out-of-class-witness existence, the
priority-2 decompilation override, and two independent window-scope-failure controls.
