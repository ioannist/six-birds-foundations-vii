The vendored `SixBirdsIII.Promotion` module supplies the nine gate fields and

the panel is scored manually against the landed theorem and sweep artifacts.

Scope note: the E2 sweep exercises same-level circularity, audited-repair
triggered rotation, finite capacity arithmetic, per-level status exactness,
the over-capacity control, the unlinked-bridge control, incomplete tower
inventory, and the diminishing-returns depth tradeoff. All 13 registered
comparisons in `E2_bounded_reflexivity_results.md` PASS. E2 does not reprove
F21/T24/T25; it imports the FIII theorems and bridges them into D4/E1 carried
apparatus. The capacity measure remains a certified interface
(`AuditTowerCapacityMeasure`) whose record universe, embeddings, footprint
sets, subset, and disjointness certificates must be honest. E8 capacity
pricing remains a forward obligation rather than an E2 theorem.
