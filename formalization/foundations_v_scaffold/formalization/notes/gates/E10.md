# E10 Gate And Null-Battery Record

**Unit:** E10 - Cognitive Demarcation Law, with Corollaries E10.1 Intention and E10.2 Goal
**Kind:** Theorem / carried internal-package cognition classifier, intention classifier, goal classifier, and mandatory falsifier corollaries
**Recorded:** 2026-07-08

E10 sits over D3 carried records, D4 `ESystem` grounding, E1-style `Delta`
descent vocabulary, direct vendored FIII `SixBirdsIII.TopDownChannel`
acceptance, and certified host input for the Agents-paper
enablement/causation nulls, F25/F31 counterfactual discipline, and F19-style
route-substitution persistence. It classifies carried internal packages by
repair descent, top-down access/action kernel gating, and carried provenance;
then classifies intention as audited advance support restriction and goal as
persistent target-quotient selection under route substitution. Its Lean file
proves three partition theorems, four mandatory-falsifier corollaries, and
three lower-priority-exclusion theorems (one per chain):

```text
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E10_CognitiveDemarcation' depends on axioms: [propext]
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E10_DecodableCorrelate' does not depend on any axioms
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E10_ScheduleTrapNull' does not depend on any axioms
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E10_1_Intention' depends on axioms: [propext]
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E10_1_PostHocIntentionFalsifier' does not depend on any axioms
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E10_2_Goal' depends on axioms: [propext]
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E10_2_RewardProxyNotGoal' does not depend on any axioms
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E10_ScheduleTrapExcludesLowerPriority' depends on axioms: [propext]
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E10_1_PostHocIntentionExcludesLowerPriority' depends on axioms: [propext]
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E10_2_RewardProxyOnlyExcludesLowerPriority' depends on axioms: [propext]
```

This is a narrow footprint: the three exactly-one partition theorems and the
three lower-priority-exclusion theorems use only `propext`, while the four
named falsifier corollaries are axiom-free. This is
not unique across the project -- E1/E5/E12 have axiom-free theorem sets and
E11/E13 also have axiom-free wrapper theorems -- but it is still materially
lighter than dependency-heavy laws such as E8.

E10 proves the cognitive, intention, and goal normal forms under shared
top-down-channel comparators, claim-scoped complete inventories, carried
provenance/repair/support/probe/route records, and certified persistence host
input. The Round B sweep grounds one deterministic fixture: `26/26`
registered comparisons PASS, covering all eight cognitive statuses, all five
intention statuses, all four goal statuses, intervention/support linkage,
per-claim scoping, the four mandatory-falsifier priorities, universal route
evaluation, and uncarried intention/goal rejection.
