# D5 Gate And Null-Battery Record

**Unit:** D5 - Closed-loop scope
**Kind:** Definition / challenge-history source-admissibility predicate
**Recorded:** 2026-07-04

D5 is a predicate over a declared challenge/audit history for an already-built
D4 E-system. It does not operate the repair loop or gate live moves. Its
load-bearing contribution is provenance discipline: every listed repair-typed
audit occurrence must be carried under that occurrence's own source fields, and
the history must be complete under the declared scope.

## Promotion Gates

| gate | status | rationale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| suff | notRequired | D5 declares no future-probe family and makes no sufficiency or factorization claim; it only checks repair-entry source admissibility in a declared history. |
| desc | notRequired | D5 is not a descent-through-quotient theorem and does not prove any F2 obstruction is reduced. |
| stab | notRequired | D5 asserts no recurrence, persistence, convergence, repair capacity, or stability; a zero-repair-entry complete history can satisfy it vacuously. |
| ctrl | notRequired | D5 does not define an operational control channel or gate live moves; D4's `GateAllows` controls repairs, while D5 classifies whether already-declared repair audit entries have admissible carried sources. |
| nosmuggle | pass | D5 is a direct no-smuggling guard: closed-loop scope requires a complete repair-audit history and checks each listed occurrence with `CarriedRecordAt` against that occurrence's own `n0`, `sourceTag`, `generatedByS`, and `inScope` fields, not a borrowed value-level witness. |
| vis | pass | The Lean surface exposes the source-labeled entry class, tagged repair-entry cases, episode attachment, completeness certificate, per-entry carriedness dispatch, and exclusion lemmas. |
| audit | pass | D5 is centrally an audit-history classifier: it does not perform fresh re-audit actions, but it audits the declared history by requiring every repair-typed audit entry to pass D3/D4 carried-source discipline. |
| strict | notRequired | D5 is not a strict-promotion, nonfactorization, or strict-extension theorem. |
| locglob | notRequired | D5 asserts no local/global obstruction, globalization, or locality-to-globality result. |
