# E13 Gate And Null-Battery Record

**Unit:** E13 - Repair Transport Law
**Kind:** Theorem / audited inter-carrier repair transport, teaching capacity, coercion-null, symbols, and seven-way status partition
**Recorded:** 2026-07-08

E13 sits over D3 carried records, D4 `ESystem`/repair moves/lawful repair
steps, E1's `Delta` convention for receiver-side strict quotient reduction,
and certified host input for F22/F17 interface mediation, F29 role
preservation, F30 saturation-strict symbol reactivation, the Institutions
paper's coercion-null/probe-memory/material-forcing substrate, and the
Xi/Needles paid-defect bridge discipline. It is the first landed E-series law
with two simultaneous `ESystem` instances (`S_A`, `S_B`) and an audited
inter-carrier bridge. Its Lean file proves the four affirmative subclaim
wrappers, the seven-way partition, and the coercion-null lower-priority
exclusion theorem:

```text
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E13_Communication' does not depend on any axioms
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E13_TeachingCapacity' does not depend on any axioms
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E13_CoercionNull' does not depend on any axioms
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E13_SymbolicRepair' does not depend on any axioms
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E13_StatusPartition' depends on axioms: [propext]
'SixBirdsFoundationsV.E13_CoercionNullExcludesLowerPriority' depends on axioms: [propext]
```

This is a narrow footprint: the four affirmative E13 subclaim wrappers are
axiom-free, and the two axiom-bearing E13 theorems -- the partition theorem
and the coercion-null lower-priority-exclusion theorem -- each use only
`propext`. This is narrower than dependency-heavy laws such
as E3/E4/E8, but it is not unique across the project: E1, E5, and E12 have
axiom-free theorem sets, and E11 also has axiom-free affirmative wrapper
theorems.

E13 proves the transport normal form under declared two-carrier bridge,
taxonomy, role, force/opportunity, quotient-install, A-absence, and
context-family inventories. The Round B sweep grounds one deterministic
fixture: `19/19` registered comparisons PASS, covering all seven statuses,
same-class communication, A-absent teaching capacity, coercion-null, stable
symbols, scaffolding/priming, different-class influence, eight rejection
paths, shared comparator nontriviality, claim-kind scoping, per-claim
independence, and falsifier claim-linkage.
