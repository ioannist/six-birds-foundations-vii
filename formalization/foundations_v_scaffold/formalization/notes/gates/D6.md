# D6 Gate And Null-Battery Record

**Unit:** D6 - Exposure cost and probe economy
**Kind:** Definition / carried, priced probe-economy move interface
**Recorded:** 2026-07-04

D6 is a controlled probe-economy definition over a D4 E-system. It turns the
Hiddenness paper's binary, free, exogenous exposure predicate into a carried
active-family process with catalog, support, weight, strict-acquisition, budget,
and ledger hooks. Its load-bearing contribution is not an attention-optimality
law; it is the structural discipline that allocation cannot add probes, that
acquisition must pass same-family strictness and budget checks, and that probe
state is carried by the system.

## Promotion Gates

| gate | status | rationale |

| suff | notRequired | D6 declares catalog, active-family, and move infrastructure, but it makes no future-probe sufficiency, factorization, or F7-style closure claim; E6 and later laws own any sufficiency use of the weighted family. |
| desc | notRequired | D6 does not prove descent through a quotient or reduce an F2 split-pair obstruction; probe acquisition may later support a D1/F2 repair, but D6 itself is not a descent theorem. |

| nosmuggle | pass | D6 directly guards against probe smuggling: allocation must preserve support, `lawfulAllocation_no_new_active_probe` proves inactive probes cannot become active by allocation alone, acquisition requires catalog membership and strictness, and retirement must cite a probe-specific `RetirementRecordEntry`. | strict | notRequired | `AcquisitionStrict` is D6's local fake-extension guard, not a FIII strict-promotion or global nonfactorization theorem. |
| locglob | notRequired | D6 asserts no local/global obstruction, globalization, or local-to-global result. |
