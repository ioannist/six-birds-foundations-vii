## 0. Thesis: the endogenous turn

Every law in the existing corpus — the 52-row Foundations IV catalog (F2–F53), the BirdInt calculus of Foundations III, the residual/currency/hiddenness machinery — shares one silent asymmetry: **the theorist performs the operations**. The theorist declares the lens, detects the split pair, installs the repair, prices the residual, chooses which hidden structure to expose, sets the budgets, runs the audit. The modeled system only *undergoes* closure.

The corpus itself marks this as its open boundary, five separate times:

1. **Foundations II** defers the *endogenous-instrument meta-limit* (no internal argument simultaneously proves exact-six, certifies instrument soundness, and certifies completeness — Gödel–Löb lineage).
2. **Reflexive SBT** leaves as conjecture F3 that closure pressure contracts the capacity functional *of the analysis pipeline* — and never models a substrate that hosts its own audit loop.
3. **Emergence IS the Needle Killer** names "a quantitative theory of probe-family enlargement" — *which probes to add, under what budget* — as open.
4. **To Spend a Stone** names the cell **P6←P6** ("accounting regulates itself") and the reading of attention weights as prices, both undeveloped; every budget in the corpus is exogenous.
5. **Institutions Are Strict Extensions of the Game** ends by demanding "a within-agent learning/acquisition carrier in which the individual agent is treated as a carrier object" — its pre-registered human-data reversal (history-dependence *grows* with experience) is an unexplained fact waiting on exactly that carrier.

The thesis of Foundations V is that **society, economics, and cognition are the layers where the six birds move inside the carrier**: a social, economic, or cognitive system is a formed package that *carries, prices, audits, rewrites, and re-instates its own closure apparatus*, subject to a strict and productive limit on self-audit. Every law below is an instance of one movement — **internalization**: take a theorist-side operation `O` from the corpus (repair, audit, exposure, budget-setting, witness search, boundary declaration) and state the lawful conditions under which the carrier hosts `Carried(O)`, together with the obstructions, prices, and no-go limits this incurs.

This is not another quotient law, another hiddenness law, or another holonomy law. Those axes are occupied (F2–F53). What is provably unoccupied — verified against the full catalog, its deferred-candidate list, and term-level searches of the corpus — is: **attention as budgeted refinement selection; endogenous repair and self-maintaining closure; active viability maintenance; endogenized alarm/witness detection; shadow-price control fields with dynamics; probe-acquisition (curiosity) selection; institutional support-rewrite; repair transport (communication/teaching); retrieval-forced record repair; offline debt discharge; repair-space holonomy (adaptability).** The E-series below claims exactly this territory.

### 1.2 New definitional layer (Foundations V owns these)

**D1 (Repair join).** For quotients $Q, R$ on a common carrier, $Q\vee R$ is the common refinement (meet under the finer-to-coarser order): the coarsest quotient refining both. *Source refinement by a repair package $R$* is the move $Q\mapsto Q\vee R$. (The corpus performs source refinements but never defines the join symbol; the brainstorm used it informally. This definition makes the brainstorm's notation lawful.)

**D2 (Predictive surplus, quantified).** The brainstorm's scalar $S(Q,M)$ does not exist in the corpus (hiddenness is a predicate there). Define the surplus of $Q$ against $M$ relative to declared futures as
$$S_\tau(Q\,\|\,M) := \mathsf{CD}_\tau(Q) - \mathsf{CD}_\tau(M)\ \geq 0,$$
the closure-deficit gap; equivalently, in operator laboratories, use $\operatorname{tr}\,\Xi_C(D\mid L_Q)$ for the declared dissolving family. Both versions are monotone under the imported data-processing discipline. Every E-law that "collapses surplus" is stated against $S_\tau$ or $\Xi$, never against an undeclared scalar.

**D3 (Carried record; carried instrument).** A record $\rho$ is **carried** by system $S$ when (i) $\rho$ is part of the carrier state ($\rho$-fields are coordinates of $Z$, not annotations kept by the theorist), (ii) $\rho$ is updated by $S$'s own transition kernel (the updating move lies in $\operatorname{supp}K$), and (iii) $\rho$'s source record classifies as `committed_state` or `audited_cell_records` **generated by $S$'s own trajectory** under the declared scope — never `fallback`, never a theorist edit. A **carried instrument** $I_S$ is an instrument in the FIII sense all of whose visibility, threshold, and check-rule records are carried. *This single definition is the answer to the brainstorm's flagged hard problem ("distinguish observer-added from system-carried refinement"): the distinction is not philosophical, it is a source-of-truth classification, and the corpus already owns the classifier.*

**D4 (Endogenous closure system, E-system).** An **E-system** is a tuple
$$\mathbb S = (\mathcal T,\ I_S,\ \Lambda_S,\ \mathfrak R_S)$$
where $\mathcal T=(Z,f,\Sigma_f,E,\mathcal A)$ is a formed package; $I_S$ a carried instrument (D3); $\Lambda_S$ a **carried ledger** (budgets, prices, statused residuals as state coordinates); and $\mathfrak R_S$ a **carried repair generator**: a map from carried defect records to admissible moves in the P1–P5 sorts (rewrite, gate, reroute, refine, package) realized as transitions of the kernel and gated by $I_S$. The pair (defect detected by $I_S$) → (move emitted by $\mathfrak R_S$) → (re-audit by $I_S$) internalizes the Reflexive-SBT loop onto the carrier.

**D5 (Closed-loop scope).** A challenge history is **closed-loop** for $\mathbb S$ when the declared scope admits no external repair source: every repair-typed record entering $\mathcal T$'s audit must classify as carried (D3). (Theorist-supplied refinements are then inadmissible by source policy, not by rhetoric.)

**D6 (Exposure cost; probe economy).** A **probe catalog** $\mathcal P$ is a declared finite family of candidate observables/probes; the system's active family $L_t\subseteq\mathcal P$ is a carried record. Moves: **allocation** (re-weighting within $L_t$, cost per unit exposure), **acquisition** (strict extension $L_t\mapsto [L_t; M]$, $M\notin\operatorname{span}$ of $L_t$ — same-family saturation is the strictness test), **retirement**. Costs and the exposure budget $B_{\mathrm{exp}}$ live in $\Lambda_S$. This makes the Hiddenness paper's binary, free, exogenous $\mathrm{Exposure}$ predicate into a controlled, priced, carried variable — the declared missing coupling.

## 2. Cluster A — Laws of Endogeny

### E1. Internalization Law (Endogenous Source-Repair) — *the flagship*

**Statement.** Let $\mathbb S$ be an E-system on finite carrier under closed-loop scope (D5), with declared challenge class $\mathcal C$ recurring at positive density, declared viability probes $v_i$, budgets in $\Lambda_S$, and horizon $H$. Suppose the split-pair obstruction recurs, $\Delta(Q_t, F_{\mathcal C}, r)\neq\emptyset$ at infinitely many $t$, and suppose $\mathbb S$ **persists with discharge**: viability probes descend through the running quotient at $H$ and the measured obstruction (via $\mathsf{CD}_\tau$ or residual trace) is reduced after challenge episodes. Then there exists a family of **carried repair records** $\{\rho_t\}$ such that:

1. each $\rho_t$ installs a refinement $R_t$ with $Q_{t+1} = Q_t \vee R_t$ and $\Delta(Q_{t+1},F_{\mathcal C},r) \subsetneq \Delta(Q_t,F_{\mathcal C},r)$ on the challenged fibers;
2. for challenge classes that are generically novel relative to $\Sigma_{Q_t}$ (finite-forcing sense), $R_t\notin\Sigma_{Q_t}$ for infinitely many $t$ — the system performs **strict self-extension**, not parameter adjustment;
3. each $\rho_t$ carries source class `committed_state` from $\mathbb S$'s own trajectory and passes the core promotion gates evaluated by $I_S$;
4. the family is budget-feasible in $\Lambda_S$.

Conversely (dichotomy): if $\mathbb S$ carries no repair generator ($\mathfrak R_S$ empty or unreachable), then by horizon $H$ exactly one of: the ledger accrues unbounded statused residual (*stressed*), a viability probe fails to descend (*collapsing*), or the challenge class was never binding (*slack*) (the no-generator, no-external-subsidy subcase of the five-way partition; a discharging fallback/off-kernel repair under no reachable generator is `externally_subsidized`, not stressed/collapsing/slack).

**Exactly-one status per (system, class, horizon):** {slack, externally-subsidized, endogenously-repairing, stressed, collapsing}.

**Obstruction/falsifier set.** $\Delta_{\mathrm{endo}} := \{$repair-typed records discharging $\mathcal C$-obstructions whose source class is `fallback` or off-kernel$\}$. Nonempty $\Delta_{\mathrm{endo}}$ on a claimed endogenously-repairing instance defeats the claim (the repair was smuggled). Additional nulls: same-family saturation (a "repair" that is a reparameterization of $\Sigma_{Q_t}$ discharges nothing — fake repair); schedule trap (an exogenous switching variable mis-typed as a carried repair).

**Proof spine (sketch).** Persistence-with-discharge + finiteness + closed loop ⇒ the discharge has a trace on the carrier (provenance accounting: something separated the offending fibers, and only carried records are admissible sources). No-Free-Distinction (F12) ⇒ the new distinctions used by later behavior require declared apparatus data — which, under D5, is carried. Finite forcing (Foundations I) ⇒ genericity of strictness in clause 2. Gate-passing is required or the installed refinement fails descent/stability later, contradicting persistence (F2 + FIII Theorem 19, PromotionGateSoundness, §10.3).

**Position.** F2 (Descent-Repair) says what a repair is once the theorist acts. E1 states when the **carrier itself is the repair generator** — and makes "itself" a checkable source-of-truth condition rather than a philosophical one. No catalog row or deferred candidate covers this; Reflexive SBT's loop is analyst-side; FIII's `repair` update sort is a schema slot with no existence law.

**Recognition source.** Internal quotient apparatus (F2, F12, finite forcing) + FIII source-of-truth/audit calculus; substrate anchor: the within-agent acquisition carrier demanded by the Institutions paper (its crystallization-bridge failure is the standing empirical residual E1 must discharge).

**Instances.** Economics: regulatory learning, crisis-response playbook formation, institutional recovery without external administrators. Society: constitutional amendment mechanisms, common-law accretion. Cognition: belief revision, policy learning, route repair in artificial agents.

**Toy-laboratory probe.** Repair-world (§7): environment switches challenge class mid-run; compare matched systems with $\mathfrak R_S$ enabled/ablated; measure $\Delta$-discharge, source-class census of all installed refinements, budget spend. Registered prediction: ablated systems land in {stressed, collapsing} exactly when the challenge binds.

**Risk.** The persistence-with-discharge hypothesis must be operationalized so it cannot smuggle teleology: it is a measured property of the run (probes descend at $H$), not a goal ascription. Medium risk that clause 2's genericity needs a carefully declared novelty measure on challenge classes.

### E2. Bounded Reflexivity Law (carried audit towers are incomplete, stratified, and capacity-bounded)

**Statement.** Let $\mathbb S$ be an E-system whose repair records are gated by carried instruments. Then:

1. *(Inherited limit, sharpened to carriers.)* No carried instrument $I_S$ certifies its own complete soundness: the claim $\mathrm{Sound}(I_S)$ under $I_S$ is `outside_scope` or `undefined_circular` (F21/T24 specialized to carried instruments).
2. *(Forced stratification.)* If $\mathbb S$'s repairs are themselves audited (as E1 clause 3 requires), then $\mathbb S$ carries a rotating/stratified instrument tower $I_S^{(0)}, I_S^{(1)},\dots$ with strictly increasing level tags; each level audits the stack below (T25) and none is final.
3. *(Capacity bound — the new content.)* On a finite carrier, the tower height and per-level scope are bounded by carried capacity: writing $\mathrm{cap}(Z)$ for the carrier's record capacity and $c_k$ for the footprint of level $k$, only towers with $\sum_k c_k \le \mathrm{cap}(Z)$ are realizable. Hence every persistent E-system operates with a **strictly incomplete, finitely stratified self-model**, and allocates audit capacity as a scarce budgeted resource (a $\Lambda_S$ line item, priced per E8).

**Exactly-one status per instrument level:** {active-scoped, rotating, saturated (capacity-bound reached), circular-blocked}.

**Obstruction/falsifier set.** Any exhibited carried instrument whose acceptance of $\mathrm{Sound}(I_S)$ survives the circularity check; any tower exceeding declared capacity without ledger charge.

**Position.** F21 is theorist-facing (audit policies on claim spaces). E2 moves the tower **onto the carrier** and adds the finite-capacity tradeoff, which is what makes it a social/economic/cognitive law: self-audit is not just logically incomplete, it is *expensive*, so its depth is an economic variable. This also *positively* reframes the Foundations II endogenous-instrument meta-limit: the limit is not a defect of the framework, it is a structural law of the systems themselves.

**Recognition source.** F21 + T24/T25 (internal apparatus); ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation and Sarbanes–Oxley auditor-independence as recognized instances; Reflexive-SBT capacity functionals as the quantitative template for clause 3.

**Instances.** Economics/society: separation of powers, external-auditor requirements, regulator-of-regulators, audit-committee depth vs cost. Cognition: incompleteness of introspection; metacognitive levels as budgeted, non-final monitors; why self-monitoring in artificial agents must rotate rather than terminate.

**Toy-laboratory probe.** Repair-world with carried audit flags at 1–3 levels; sweep audit capacity against repair-error rates; registered prediction: an optimal nonzero, non-maximal tower depth exists for fixed $\mathrm{cap}(Z)$ and error process — audit depth behaves as a priced good with diminishing returns.

**Risk.** Low for clauses 1–2 (direct inheritance). Clause 3 needs a clean carried-capacity measure; Reflexive SBT's $\mathrm{Cap}$ functionals are the candidate but were built for subspace localization, not record footprints.

### E3. Self-Maintaining Reclosure Law (operator maintenance ≠ object persistence)

**Statement.** Let $\mathcal T_t=(Z_t,f_t,\Sigma_t,E_t,\mathcal A_t)$ be the running package of an E-system and let
$$\mathrm{App}_t := (E_t\text{-gates},\ \Theta_t\ \text{thresholds},\ B_t\ \text{boundary},\ \mathcal A_t\ \text{audit data})$$
be its **closure apparatus**. Call $\mathbb S$ **self-maintaining** when the apparatus is itself a carried record reinstated by the system's own transitions: there is a carried maintenance operator $\mathfrak m$ with
$$\mathrm{App}_{t+1} = \mathfrak m(z_t),\qquad \mathfrak m \text{ carried (D3)},$$
and $\mathrm{App}$ is an approximate fixed point of $\mathfrak m$ under challenge-free dynamics ($d(\mathrm{App}_{t+1},\mathrm{App}_t)\le\varepsilon$) while under challenge $\mathfrak m$ performs gated apparatus repair (E1 applied at the apparatus level). Then persistence claims split into a **two-level fixed-point condition**: object-level ($E_t^2 = E_t$ up to defect — F19/F20 territory) and apparatus-level ($\mathfrak m(\mathfrak m$-output$)$ stable — new). Under persistent perturbation exposure, a package whose apparatus is *not* carried decays at the apparatus level at a rate set by the perturbation process even while its object-level fixed points transiently persist.

**Exactly-one status:** {crystal-grade (object persists; apparatus not carried), **maintained closure** (apparatus carried and reinstated), subsidized closure (apparatus reinstated by an external carrier), decaying closure}.

**Obstruction/falsifier set.** $\Delta_{\mathrm{maint}} := \{$apparatus components whose reinstatement records are absent, off-kernel, or `fallback`$\}$. The **ablation falsifier** separates the statuses experimentally: cut $\mathfrak m$'s channel; in maintained closure the apparatus decays while object-level $E$ initially persists — the two decay curves must separate, or the instance was crystal-grade all along.

**Position.** F19 (Object Persistence, incl. ViablePersistence) tracks objects through declared transports; F20 tracks records. Neither asks whether the *closure-making machinery* is reproduced, nor by whom. E3 is the formal line — requested by the brainstorm and confirmed absent from the catalog — between a merely stable pattern and an institutional/economic/cognitive one: **a crystal persists; a firm re-instates the conditions of its own persistence.** The brainstorm's risk ("may collapse into object persistence") is discharged by the two-level fixed point plus the ablation falsifier: they are different measurable decay modes.

**Recognition source.** Internal apparatus (F19/F20 + D3/D4); substrate anchors: the Agents paper's repair-cost/viability collapse boundary (objecthood requires payment) and the Language paper's Phase-20/21 joint requirement (parents drift without cross-layer maintenance feedback — the corpus's own statement that un-maintained closure decays).

**Instances.** Economics: a firm re-hires, re-trains, re-capitalizes, renews compliance — reinstating admissibility conditions, not just states; market infrastructure (clearinghouses) maintaining the rules that make clearing meaningful. Society: institutions reproducing their enforcement and legitimacy apparatus. Cognition: an agent maintaining its own calibration, thresholds, and memory-integrity checks.

**Toy-laboratory probe.** Repair-world variant where gate/threshold/boundary parameters are state coordinates subject to drift; systems with/without carried $\mathfrak m$; measure apparatus-decay vs object-decay curves; sweep maintenance budget → status phase diagram {crystal, maintained, subsidized, decaying}.

**Risk.** Medium: "apparatus as state" must avoid infinite regress (the maintenance operator maintaining itself) — E2's capacity bound is the intended stop: maintenance towers are finite and their top is maintained only statistically (rotating), never certified. This interplay (E3×E2) is itself a publishable proposition.

### E4. Repair Compilation Law (habits and institutionalization are one law)

**Statement.** Let repair package $R$ be repeatedly invoked by $\mathbb S$ under challenge class $\mathcal C$, each invocation gate-passing with obstruction reduction, with stable audit and idempotence defect below threshold across invocations. Then there exists an admissible **compilation**
$$R \leadsto K_\ell^{\mathrm{compiled}}$$
— a lower-layer operator rewrite (P1) or support gate (P2) — such that formerly-repaired futures descend *without exposing the higher package*: $\Delta(Q_\ell^{\mathrm{compiled}}, F_{\mathcal C}, r)=\emptyset$ while the higher-layer invocation record goes silent. Compilation is **lawful only if**: (i) it is certified by a top-down channel record (≥2 interventions on the compiled gate, matched controls, effect threshold — FIII §4.7); (ii) it survives the **memory-only comparator** (a control carrier supplied with history but not the repair structure does not reproduce the descent — Strict-Tests panel); (iii) same-family saturation is respected (renaming the repair is not compiling it); (iv) the compiled operator carries a decompilation/annotation record (else later challenge-class drift produces unstatused residuals, violating F9).

Conversely, compiled operators are **brittle out of class**: for challenges outside $\mathcal C$, the compiled gate contributes split-pair obstructions that the uncompiled (exposed) repair would have avoided — compilations trade audit cost for scope.

**Exactly-one status per (repair, class):** {exposed (invoked per-episode), compiling (mixed), compiled (silent descent), mis-compiled (out-of-class obstruction accruing), decompiled}.

**Obstruction/falsifier set.** $\Delta_{\mathrm{compile}} := \{$futures in $\mathcal C$ descending under the memory-only comparator$\}$ (compilation claim defeated: it was just memory) $\cup$ $\{$out-of-class obstructions left unstatused$\}$.

**Position.** F8 (Strict Extension) covers upward layer growth; nothing covers the **reverse flow** — a higher package becoming a lower affordance. The corpus's downward machinery (structural paths, NC-TD, top-down channel records) provides exactly the discipline that keeps this from being "downward causation" metaphysics: compilation is an audited operator install, full stop. Cognition (habit/skill) and society/economics (routinization, institutionalization, compliance templates, market conventions, crisis playbooks) are **the same law at two scales** — this unification is itself a claim of the paper: the brainstorm's #8 and #9 are one theorem.

**Recognition source.** FIII top-down channel + promotion gates (internal); substrate anchor: Institutions paper's formation→formed transition (the institution becomes autonomous and future-sufficient — $\mathsf{CD}=0$ on institutional rows — while stage lenses stay obstructed) read as the compilation of repeated coordination repairs into an autonomous macro-law.

**Instances.** Economics: standardized contracts and clearing conventions replacing case-by-case negotiation; compliance checklists compiling regulatory repair. Society: precedent compiling adjudication. Cognition: skill acquisition in artificial agents — policy distillation as gated compilation; brittleness out-of-distribution as the predicted out-of-class obstruction.

**Toy-laboratory probe.** Repair-world: fix a recurring challenge; let $\mathfrak R_S$ install per-episode repairs; enable a compiler move that writes the repair into the kernel's support at a cost; measure (a) audit-cost savings in class, (b) obstruction accrual out of class, (c) memory-only comparator, (d) top-down channel certification. Registered prediction: a compilation frontier in (challenge-recurrence × class-drift) space — compile when recurrence high and drift low.

**Risk.** Medium-low. The machinery exists end-to-end; the work is assembling the certificate battery and proving the brittleness converse (which should follow from F2 applied to the compiled gate under class drift).

### E5. Reclosure Collapse Law (collapse = loss of the repair generator, with irreversibility grades)

**Statement.** For E-system $\mathbb S$, challenge class $\mathcal C$, and declared budgets, say $\mathbb S$ is **collapsed relative to $\mathcal C$** when no admissible carried move — repair $R$, boundary update $B$, apparatus reclosure $E'$, or budget-feasible acquisition (E9) — makes the declared viability probes descend:
$$\forall\ \text{carried}\ R,B,E':\quad \Delta(Q\vee R,\ F_{\mathcal C},\ v)\neq\emptyset\ \ \text{or the move exceeds}\ \Lambda_S.$$
Collapse is **irreversible** when additionally the repair generator itself is unreachable: the states from which $\mathfrak R_S$ can fire lie outside the forward-reachable set (equivalently, the viability kernel of the *apparatus-level* dynamics is empty). Then: (1) collapse is a statement about the repair economy, not about activity level (an active system can be collapsed; a dormant one need not be); (2) the statuses below are mutually exclusive and exhaustive given declared scope; (3) *subsidized* systems (external carrier supplies repairs) are not collapsed but are not self-maintaining — their status is inherited from the subsidizer's scope, which is why subsidy withdrawal reclassifies them discontinuously.

**Exactly-one status:** {viable, stressed (statused residual accruing within budget), subsidized, suspended (apparatus intact, operations gated off), collapsed-recoverable (repair generator reachable from outside states only), collapsed-irreversible, revived (post-subsidy reclosure with new apparatus lineage)}.

**Obstruction/falsifier set.** Exhibiting an admissible in-budget carried move that makes $v$ descend defeats a collapse verdict (constructive falsifier, mirroring the Needles record-level discipline: a failed dissolution must fail by a *named* missing record).

**Position.** Closure–Reality Boundary (F53) and F19 dissolution are adjacent but neither defines collapse as **self-repair failure**; the Agents paper exhibits kernel collapse ($\mathcal K=\emptyset$ under `no_repair`) but does not grade reversibility or subsidy. The status partition answers the brainstorm's worry about borderline cases by making them first-class statuses.

**Recognition source.** Internal (F19 + E1 + viability-kernel machinery); recognized instances: insolvency vs illiquidity (stressed vs collapsed), receivership (subsidized), suspension of convertibility (suspended), corporate revival through acquisition (revived — with new apparatus lineage, connecting to F19's identity-rewrite status).

**Instances.** Economics: bankruptcy taxonomy, market shutdowns, zombie firms (subsidized), sovereign default and IMF programs. Society: state failure vs occupation vs reconstruction. Cognition: an agent whose learning channel is severed (collapsed-irreversible for novel classes) vs one whose exploration budget is exhausted (stressed).

**Toy-laboratory probe.** Repair-world phase diagram over (noise, repair cost, budget): reproduce the Agents paper's collapse boundary, then extend with the new axes — subsidy injection, apparatus reachability — to exhibit all seven statuses and hysteresis under subsidy withdrawal.

**Risk.** Low-medium. Main work: the apparatus-level viability kernel (viability of the repair economy itself) — a clean, new, computable object.

## 3. Cluster B — Laws of Priced Access (the cognition core)

### E6. Attention Law (attention = budgeted exposure allocation; attention weights are dual variables)

**Statement.** Let $\mathbb S$ carry a hiddenness interface (current $Q_t$ from active probes $L_t$, predictive $M$), a probe economy (D6) with exposure budget $B_{\mathrm{exp}}$ in $\Lambda_S$, and declared future/readout demands. Define the discharge of an allocation $w$ over $L_t$ as the reduction of surplus $S_\tau(Q_w\|M)$ (equivalently the residual contraction given by the Xi chain rule for the probes weighted in). Then, for a persistent viable E-system under **binding** exposure budget:

### E9. Curiosity Law (probe acquisition = residual-discharge-per-cost selection; the dual of attention)

**Obstruction/falsifier set.** Fake-novelty null (acquisitions failing saturation — repackaged probes claimed as exploration); discharge-free acquisition streaks under a claimed lawful policy (defeats instance); viability-risk violations (curiosity without the bound is not this law's object).

### E15. Offline Reclosure Law (debt-discharge alternation in the deficit regime)

**Statement.** Let $\mathbb S$ accumulate **closure debt** $D_t$ := statused-unresolved residuals (E14) + route residues awaiting transport reconciliation (F3) + adequacy residuals $\Xi$ awaiting discharge — all carried ledger quantities. Then persistence requires an **offline mode**: a recurring phase in which external exchange is gated off (P2) and the freed budget is reallocated to internal transport/repackaging, with $\kappa_{\mathrm{off}} > \kappa_{\mathrm{on}}$ by exactly the freed allocation. Consequences: (1) lawful E-systems in the deficit regime exhibit **alternation** (online accrual / offline discharge) with duty cycle set by the budget geometry — a computable, falsifiable schedule, not a design whim; (2) skipping offline phases converts debt into E7 alarms and then E5 stress — a lawful failure cascade; (3) the law claims **necessity only in the deficit regime**: systems with $\kappa_{\mathrm{on}}\ge\dot D$ lawfully never go offline (scoped, per the brainstorm's non-exclusivity caution).

**Obstruction/falsifier set.** Deficit-regime instance persisting indefinitely online with bounded debt (defeats necessity); offline phases without measurable debt discharge (the "offline" label was decorative — protocol artifact null); duty-cycle mispredictions beyond tolerance under declared budget geometry.

**Position.** Nothing in the catalog treats operating-mode alternation; the Xi ladder appendix (strict-collapse ladders discharging residual memory) supplies the discharge mathematics but stages are theorist-run. E15 is that ladder as a carried operating mode.

**Recognition source.** Xi predictive-adequacy ladders + budget geometry of Spend (internal); recognized instances: market closing hours and clearing/settlement cycles, maintenance windows and change freezes in operations, closed-book audit periods, batch reconciliation in payment systems — economics/operations own this pattern natively.

**Instances.** Economics: settlement cycles, end-of-day books, trading halts as forced offline reclosure (link E7). Society: legislative recesses/committee seasons as institutional reconciliation phases (interpretation-grade until measured). Cognition: offline replay/consolidation phases in artificial agents; model refresh windows; garbage-collection pauses as the computational anchor.

**Toy-laboratory probe.** Repair-world with shared exposure/repair budget and tunable challenge throughput: sweep accrual rate → observe the alternation onset at the deficit boundary; measure duty cycle vs predicted budget geometry; ablate offline phase → cascade census (alarm→stress→collapse rates).

**Risk.** Low-medium; the main care is defining $\kappa_{\mathrm{on}}/\kappa_{\mathrm{off}}$ without smuggling the conclusion (they must come from the declared budget geometry, not be fit post hoc).

### E16. Adaptability Law (adaptability = coherent holonomy in repair space)

**Statement.** For E-system $\mathbb S$, consider routes $\gamma,\eta$ through a declared perturbation space that return the *practice* to the same current quotient: $Q(\gamma h)=Q(\eta h)$. $\mathbb S$ exhibits **adaptability holonomy** when the routes nonetheless leave different **repair-capacity distributions**:
$$P\big(\text{discharge}(F_{\mathcal C'})\mid \gamma h\big) \neq P\big(\text{discharge}(F_{\mathcal C'})\mid \eta h\big)$$
for declared future challenge classes $\mathcal C'$ — same visible practice, different latent capacity. The law: adaptability is exactly the **coherent-class route residue of the repair ledger** — it must survive the full Holonomy control battery (not an artifact of protocol bookkeeping; not flattenable by admissible completion; not currentizable by any admissible same-support interface refinement; not dissipated by the system's own continuations). Residues that are currentizable are not adaptability but *legible slack* (they can be turned into ordinary reported state); only the coherent remainder is adaptability proper. Consequently adaptability is (a) invisible to any audit confined to current practice (it lives in $\pi$-fibers of $M\to Q$ over the repair ledger), and (b) route-manufacturable: histories with identical outcomes but different route structure produce measurably different future repair distributions — path dependence of capacity, not of state.

**Obstruction/falsifier set.** The Holonomy taxonomy as control battery (artifact/flattenable/currentizable/dissipative must all be excluded); unmeasured-context confound (route difference proxying an undeclared state variable — the brainstorm's flagged risk, handled by the fixed-support discipline: routes are compared on one declared comparison base).

**Position.** F3 forces memory from route residue; the Holonomy paper classifies residues; neither identifies the *coherent residue of the repair ledger* as a named capacity, nor gives it the society/economics/cognition reading (organizational slack, crisis experience, drill history as route-manufactured capacity). E16 is F3's social/cognitive recognition.

**Recognition source.** Holonomy-with-Memory (taxonomy + memory-wheel coherent benchmark as substrate anchor) + F3 (internal); recognized instances: hysteresis in labor markets and capital formation (route-dependent capacity), drill/exercise effects in operations.

**Instances.** Economics: two firms with identical books but different crisis histories carrying different restructuring capacity — priced by markets as intangibles; stress-test regimes as deliberate route manufacture. Society: institutional experience effects; post-crisis reform windows. Cognition: curriculum-order effects in artificial agents (same final performance, different transfer capacity).

**Toy-laboratory probe.** Repair-world with route pairs engineered to same-practice endpoints (memory-wheel style) but different repair-ledger residues; measure future discharge distributions under held-out challenge classes; run the full control battery so surviving residue is certified coherent.

**Risk.** Medium: coherent-residue certification is demanding (the Holonomy paper's own searches show most residues are artifacts or currentizable); the payoff is proportional — surviving instances are strong results.
