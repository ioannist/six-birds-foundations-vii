## 2. Theorem → Lean target

Two theorems, matching the prose's two-part Theorem exactly. **Part (a) (unconditional)**:
`part_a_reduction` proves `PointwiseLiveness T lt A ↔ EmptyBadTailIntersection T lt A`. **Part (b)
(conditional)**: `part_b_discharge` proves that `GhostConvergence T lt A emb U` and `NativeSeparation T lt
A emb U` together imply `PointwiseLiveness T lt A`, by deriving a contradiction from an assumed infinite
bad thread and then REUSING `part_a_reduction`'s reverse direction. This coupling — Part (b) depending on
Part (a) rather than standing beside it as an independent schema — is structurally different from every
prior two-schema law (G5, G7, G9), which had two logically independent conclusions. Both theorems depend
on `propext`, `Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound` — this is now the **second** law in the project (after
G7) depending on `Classical.choice`, via `Classical.byContradiction` used to recover an existential
liveness witness from a negated-universal bad-tail-membrane statement over a fully abstract, non-decidable
carrier.

## 6. Nonclaims → Lean/lab cross-check

G1 has **four** toy-lab probes, more than any prior law, each with a precisely different scope:

- **G1-L1** (`lab/foundations_vi_lab/g01_solvency/collatz_ledger.py`): exact affine-ledger and
  descent-certificate validation for odd `n < 10^6` (independently reproduced by the manager at
  `2 * 10^6` with zero failures across `~4.5M` checkpoints). Validates `THEOREMS.md`'s Setup FORMULAS
  only — does not touch `part_a_reduction`/`part_b_discharge` at all.
- **G1-L2** (`bad_tail.py`): the bad-tail membrane census (`n < 10^7`) and the `2^L-1` ghost-shadowing
  family (`L=2..80`). The real, unconditional prose claims (the `a_i=1` shadowing pattern and the
  closed-form formula) held exactly. A stronger, **manager-authored** extra prediction (`k*=L-1`) was
  falsified for every tested `L` — honestly reported via a dated correction note rather than hidden,
  exactly matching this project's falsification-first discipline. The manager independently re-derived
  algebraically why `k*=L-1` cannot hold (`n_{L-1} = 2·3^{L-1}-1 > n_0` for all `L≥2`, since
  `(3/2)^{L-1}≥1`), confirming this is a sound mathematical fact, not a bug.
- **G1-L3** (`ghost_toy.py`) — **the crucial lab**: a finite residue-ring toy (`Z/2^16`, the real
  accelerated map with a declared lift rule) that exhaustively classifies all `32768` odd residues and
  genuinely, non-vacuously discharges finite analogues of `GhostConvergence`/`NativeSeparation` (two
  nonterminal residues, `14563` and `21845`, provably reach the declared ghost before any descent; every
  other nonterminal residue has an explicit computed descent horizon). This went through a manager-caught
  fix round: the classifier's raw "bad cycle `(1,)`" is exactly `THEOREMS.md`'s own stated `T(1)=1`
  terminal fixed point (Case (a), `A={1}`), not a new obstruction — corrected to exclude it, leaving
  `Gamma_16 = {14563, 21845}` as the genuinely nonterminal ghost locus, with zero other nonterminal bad
  cycles found. This is the **only** G1 lab that touches the ghost/completion machinery at all, and it
  discharges it only for this finite toy — the real infinite Collatz instance's Part (b) hypotheses
  remain fully open.
- **G1-L4** (`aliquot.py`): illustrative-only aliquot-sequence tracking (`n <= 10^5`), showing a G1
  ledger need not be scalar. Independently verified by the manager (sigma cross-checked against
  brute-force divisor sums beyond the built-in spot-check range; all four emergent fixed points are
  exactly the classical perfect numbers `6, 28, 496, 8128`; the well-documented "Lehmer five" starters
