## 2. Theorem → Lean target

Two independent schemas, matching the prose's two-part Theorem. **Negative schema** (the substantive
theorem in this file): `census_excludes_unbounded_creation` proves `Census unresolved → ¬
UnboundedCreation unresolved` — an eventually-non-increasing unresolved count cannot also be unbounded on

## 3. Proof spine → Lean target

The negative schema's proof chains two helper lemmas: `census_bound_from_start` (ordinary induction:
eventual non-increase from `N` propagates to `unresolved(N+k) ≤ unresolved(N)` for every offset `k`) and
`census_tail_bound` (the same fact reindexed via `n = N+k`). The main theorem then derives a direct
contradiction: instantiating `UnboundedCreation` at `B := unresolved N`, `N' := N` produces some `n ≥ N`
with `unresolved n > unresolved N`, directly contradicting the tail bound at that same `n`. This mirrors
G7's `no_infinite_nat_descent` in spirit (an elementary, hand-rollable `Nat`-sequence incompatibility
argument) though the two are logically distinct facts. The positive schema's proof is a one-line
existential repackaging, hand-traced by the manager and confirmed to be an honest shallow proof, not
manufactured depth.
