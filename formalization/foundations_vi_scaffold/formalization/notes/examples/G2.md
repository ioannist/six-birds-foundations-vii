## 2. Theorem → Lean target

- **Part (a)** (unconditional termination): `no_infinite_nonterminal_run` (the direct contradiction form)
  and `not_nonterminal_of_stage_coherence` (the contrapositive wrapper, `¬ Nonterminal x A`). Extracting

## 3. Proof spine → Lean target

The abstract argument ("assume infinite nonterminal run, escrow values form an infinite strictly
descending chain, contradicting well-foundedness") is `no_descending_chain_from_acc` (a hand-rolled,
Mathlib-free "no infinite descending `Nat`-indexed chain from an accessible element" lemma — proved by
induction on the `Acc` witness, shifting the sequence by one index per recursive step — the standard
textbook argument, absent from Lean core's `WellFounded` API) plus `wellFounded_no_descending_chain`
(lifts it to any `WellFounded` relation). `no_infinite_nonterminal_run` builds the escrow sequence
`k ↦ e k (pi k (x k))` from `StageCoherence` and applies these.

The Goodstein worked example (hereditary base-2 representation of 4, rebasing to base 3, the ordinal
assignment `O_k`, and the concrete descent `omega^2*2+omega*2+2 < omega^omega`) is **not** part of the
abstract Lean law — it is calibration/instantiation content, mechanized instead in the lab (§4).

## 4. Case enumeration → lab target (no Lean witnesses)

(natively monotone / promoted scalar-lexicographic / transfinite escrow / no escrow at declared strength)
is a **classification taxonomy of which kind of well-founded order `W` happens to be**, not competing
properties needing separate mechanized proofs — the prose itself says the rows are "exhaustive... because
every proposed `W` either supplies a well-founded stage-coherent descent certificate at some declared
order strength or does not," which is definitionally true given what "declared order strength" means, not
a theorem requiring its own Lean witness construction. No Lean subsection was built for this; the decision
is recorded here rather than left silent.

## 6. Nonclaims → Lean/lab cross-check

`THEOREMS.md` §G2 "6. Nonclaims" item 4 ("Stage-coherence is load-bearing. A proof that decreases only
after an unrecorded re-presentation has not discharged `[H-G2-stage-coherence]`") is enforced
*structurally* by the Lean encoding: `StageCoherence`'s definition requires the composed inequality
`wlt (e (k+1) (pi (k+1) (x (k+1)))) (e k (pi k (x k)))` in one piece — a proof that only shows descent
before or after rebasing separately, without this composed form, simply does not typecheck as a
`StageCoherence` witness. This is the same "the type signature itself blocks the smuggle" pattern already
seen in G8's case (b) witness, now confirmed for a second law.

Nonclaim 3 (Part (b) imported, not reproved) is honestly reflected by Part (b)'s complete absence from the
Lean file — nothing was mechanized under that name, and the gate/interaction records say so explicitly
rather than silently.
