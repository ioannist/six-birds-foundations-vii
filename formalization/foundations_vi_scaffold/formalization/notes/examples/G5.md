## 2. Theorem → Lean target

Two independent theorems. **No-go**: `no_fixed_depth_future_sufficient`/`no_future_sufficient_depth`
prove, from `UnboundedHorizon q Probe`, that no fixed depth `m` is future-sufficient (pointwise and
existential-negation forms). **Confinement**: `closed_pattern_confinement` proves that given
`ClosedPattern R Target Pcal` and entry `Pcal (Iterate R N x)`, every later iterate `Iterate R (N+t) x`
stays in `Pcal` and misses `Target`. `iterate_add` and `closed_pattern_confinement` depend on `propext`;
the remaining three theorems are fully axiom-free (confirmed by fresh, independently re-run `#print
axioms`). The two schemas are deliberately **not merged** into one "escape" theorem — Nonclaim 3 forbids
reading the no-go half alone as an escape certificate.

## 3. Proof spine → Lean target

`no_fixed_depth_future_sufficient`'s proof is a direct contradiction: destructuring `hunbounded m` gives
`⟨j, y, z, hq, hneq⟩`; applying the assumed `hsufficient j y z hq` gives `Probe j y = Probe j z`,
contradicting `hneq` directly. `closed_pattern_confinement`'s proof combines `closed_pattern_persists`
(induction propagating `Pcal` membership through `t` applications of `R` from the entry point) with
`iterate_add` (the bridge lemma `Iterate R (N+t) x = Iterate R t (Iterate R N x)`, itself proved by
induction on `t`). Both hand-traced term-by-term by the manager during the Lean review round — confirmed
as genuine packaging steps, not restatements.

The concrete base-2 four-phase confinement calibration (`P0(r)→P1(r)→P2(r)→P3(r)→P0(r+1)`, seed
`22 = 10110₂`) is deliberately **not** part of this abstract Lean file — that's `G5-L1` lab content (§6).
