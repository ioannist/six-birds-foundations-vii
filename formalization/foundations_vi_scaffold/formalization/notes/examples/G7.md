# G7 — Adversarial Mobility Confinement — Deep-Dive Notes

**Status:** `landed_step8` (Lean mechanization, toy-lab probe, promotion-gate panel, and
interaction-status recording all complete; external review pending — this document is the key artifact
for that review).

This note is the six-part deep-dive split out from `THEOREMS.md`'s compressed G7 catalog entry, per
`design/06_FORMALIZATION.md` §6 and `formalization/notes/README.md`'s convention. G7 is the second law in
the "calibration-anchored schema" cluster (`design/06_FORMALIZATION.md` §9: G5, G7, G9). It is the first
law in this project to mechanize a genuine two-player adversarial game (angel vs. devil) rather than a
single-agent dynamical system. It is the first law to break the six-law `ctrl=notRequired` precedent
(every prior law scored `ctrl` the same way), and it gives a new kind of `desc=pass` rationale distinct
from the prior `desc=pass` calls (G2, G8 — the other four laws, G3/G5/G10/G11, scored `desc=notRequired`).

## 1. Setup → Lean target

`THEOREMS.md` §G7 "1. Setup" (board `B`, mobility `p`, devil budget `b`, visible history `H_n`,
`[H-G7-escape-strategy]`, `[H-G7-confinement-strategy]`) is mechanized in `lean/SixBirdsFoundationsVI/
Laws/G7AdversarialMobilityConfinement.lean` at a deliberately **abstract** level: `GameState (Pos Obs)`
bundles the agent position and the devil's obstruction record (standing in for `H_n`), `applyTurn`,
`Stuck`. `Z^2`, the Chebyshev metric, and finite boards are **not** constructed generically — that's
calibration/lab content (§4).

**No Lean imports.** Standalone, like every prior law's file — confirmed by direct grep.

**One design decision, corrected during review**: the first draft had the devil's strategy `sigmaD` and
legality predicate `devilLegal` depend only on the pre-turn state, not on the angel's just-chosen move.
Reviewer caught this as a MAJOR fidelity gap against the prose's actual turn order ("the angel chooses a
legal next position... then the devil deletes..."): a non-reactive devil is a strictly different,
stronger-for-the-devil game than the one `THEOREMS.md` describes. Fixed by threading the angel's chosen
`Pos` through both `devilLegal : GameState Pos Obs → Pos → Obs → Prop` and
`sigmaD : GameState Pos Obs → Pos → Obs` everywhere.

**Second devil-legality fix, corrected during external review**: the reactive-devil fix above made
`devilLegal` depend on the angel's chosen move, but `ConfinementStrategy` still failed to require the
devil's own prescribed response `sigmaD s p` to satisfy that legality predicate. External review caught
the resulting illegal-instant-trap countermodel: a strategy could return an illegal obstruction record
that decreases `rank` and thereby satisfy the old confinement predicate. Fixed by changing
`ConfinementStrategy` to take `devilLegal` explicitly and require both
`devilLegal s p (sigmaD s p)` and strict rank decrease for every legal angel move.

## 2. Theorem → Lean target

Two independent theorems, matching the prose's two-part Theorem (points 1 and 2; point 3, "open band," is
a documentation-only classification, not something to prove — see §4). **Escape**: `escape_never_stuck`
proves that a discharged `EscapeStrategy` (angel strategy `sigmaA` + invariant `I`, quantified over every
legal reactive devil response) keeps the play inside `I` and never stuck, at every finite turn, against
any legal devil strategy — fully axiom-free. **Confinement**: `confinement_forces_trap` proves that a
discharged `ConfinementStrategy` (legal devil strategy `sigmaD` + a `Nat`-valued `rank` strictly decreasing
against every legal angel move and legal devil response) traps any honest angel strategy (`AngelHonest`: proposes a legal move
whenever one exists) within `rank s0` turns — via a fuel-indexed induction, depending on `propext`,
`Classical.choice`, and `Quot.sound`. `Quot.sound` already appears in G3's and G8's axiom reports, but
`Classical.choice` is new: this is the **first law in this project depending on `Classical.choice`**,
because the proof case-splits on the abstract, generally-undecidable `Stuck` predicate over an arbitrary
`Pos` type. Reviewer confirmed this is a genuine, appropriately-scoped necessity of the abstraction level
(not a smuggled shortcut), and confirmed it stays confined to the confinement side — `escape_never_stuck`
and the mutual-exclusivity theorem below remain axiom-free apart from `propext`.

## 3. Proof spine → Lean target

The escape proof is a two-step application of the schema (once to advance the invariant, once more at the
successor state to extract a legal-move witness). The confinement proof's fuel induction needed a
`play_succ_from_initial` shift lemma (`Play s (n+1) = Play (applyTurn s (sigmaA s) (sigmaD s (sigmaA
s))) n`) — structurally the same "peel first step vs. peel last step" bridge as G5's `iterate_add`,
proved the same way by ordinary induction. The Case Enumeration's explicit contradiction argument ("play
the two strategies against each other; the resulting single play would have to be both infinite and
finitely trapped") is mechanized directly as `escape_confinement_mutually_exclusive`, via a hand-rolled
`no_infinite_nat_descent` lemma (no `Nat`-valued sequence can strictly decrease forever — proved
elementarily: `f n + n <= f 0` by induction, then instantiate at `n = f 0 + 1` for a direct contradiction).
All three proofs were hand-traced term-by-term by the manager, both before and after the reactive-devil
fix.

## 4. Case enumeration → left as documentation, plus one mechanized theorem

Unlike G10/G11 (partial Lean witnesses) or G5 (left entirely as documentation), G7's Case Enumeration gets
**one** piece of real Lean content beyond the two schemas: the mutual-exclusivity theorem above, which is
explicitly the mathematical content behind "the rows are mutually exclusive." The three-way classification
itself (Confined / Escaping / Open band) is an audit-record convention over submitted evidence — Coder's
independent assessment, confirmed by the manager, is that exhaustiveness needs no further Lean content
beyond mutual exclusivity, since "a declared game has an escape certificate, a confinement certificate, or
neither" is a statement about which evidence has been submitted, not an additional mathematical claim.

## 6. Nonclaims → Lean/lab cross-check

`THEOREMS.md` §G7 "6. Nonclaims" items 1 and 4 (no general threshold for arbitrary boards/metrics/budgets;
finite-board searches are exhibits, not proofs) are directly cross-checked by the lab:
`lab/foundations_vi_lab/g07_confinement/{finite_game,run}.py` (G7-L1) implements an exact (memoized, non-
heuristic) minimax solver over `N=3` (horizon 9) and `N=4` (horizon 8) torus boards, `p in {1,2}`, `b in
{1,2}`. The verdict is an explicit **QUALIFIED PASS**, not a clean one: of the four comparable `(N,b)`
pairs, only `N=4,b=2` shows a clean unconfounded `p=1`/`p=2` separation. The manager independently
computed the board geometry and confirmed the reason for the other three rows' lack of separation is not
uniform — `N=3`'s max torus-Chebyshev distance is exactly `1` (a verified geometric fact making `p=1` and
`p=2` literally identical move sets there, structurally incapable of showing separation), while
`N=4,b=1`'s lack of separation is a *different* phenomenon (inconclusive within the tested 8-turn horizon,
since `N=4`'s max distance is `2` and mobility genuinely differs there). This distinction, caught during
manager verification and fixed by Coder in a review round, keeps the verdict honest rather than
conflating a structural degeneracy with an inconclusive result. Nonclaim 2 (NC-18, load-bearing: the
adversary's strategy space must be fully declared and visible) is enforced directly by the corrected
reactive-devil Lean encoding and `AngelHonest`. Nonclaim 3 (no novelty for classical angel-problem
mathematics) is reflected in the lab's explicit refusal to attempt reproving Mathe/Kloster/Bowditch/Gacs.
