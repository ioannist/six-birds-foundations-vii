The vendored `SixBirdsIII.Promotion` module supplies the nine gate fields and

`GateStatus.outsideScope`, and the corresponding `StrictGateStatus` constructors

this panel is scored manually against the landed Lean declarations and lab
artifacts, following the same convention used by the G2, G3, G5, G8, G10, and
G11 gate notes.

Scope note: G7-L1 is a finite-torus bounded-horizon minimax calibration, not an
infinite-board angel-problem proof. Its verdict is a QUALIFIED PASS: among the
four comparable `(N,b)` pairs, only `N=4,b=2` gives a clean unconfounded
`p=1`/`p=2` separation; `N=3,b=1` and `N=3,b=2` are geometric degeneracies
because the max torus-Chebyshev distance is `1`, so `p=1` and `p=2` have
identical legal-move sets; and `N=4,b=1` is inconclusive within horizon `8`.
The lab also records a finite-board exhaustion/sweep-wall rank testbed for
`p=1,b=1`, where starts `0`, `5`, and `10` trap after `15`, `15`, and `14`
turns respectively with strict rank decrease at every tested devil move. The
aspirational `N <= 7` design target was reduced to `N=3,H=9` and `N=4,H=8`
because full unrestricted exact minimax grows combinatorially in the
deleted-cell set and devil response subsets.
