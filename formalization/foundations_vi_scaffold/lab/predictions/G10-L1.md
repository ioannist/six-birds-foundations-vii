# G10-L1 Prediction - Ducci Collapse

## Hypothesis
For seeded random Ducci tuples of lengths `k in {3..16}` with entries at most `10^6`, the power-of-two lengths `k in {4,8,16}` reach the all-zero tuple. Non-power-of-two lengths in the same range reach nonzero cycles for the seeded calibration tuples. The GF(2) linearization check verifies exactly that `(I+S)^{2^m}=0` for `k=2^m` with `k in {4,8,16}`.

## Predicted Outcome
All arithmetic is exact integer or GF(2) arithmetic. The seeded Ducci sweep collapses for `k=4,8,16`, records nonzero cycle periods for the other tested lengths, and the matrix nilpotency identity holds exactly for `m in {2,3,4}`.

## Falsification Condition
Any tested power-of-two length failing to reach zero within the declared step budget, or any failure of the exact GF(2) matrix identity `(I+S)^{2^m}=0`, falsifies the G10 collapse-side calibration. A seeded non-power-of-two tuple collapsing to zero would not falsify the theorem but would falsify this calibration prediction's chosen nonzero-cycle exhibit and would be reported as a surprise.
