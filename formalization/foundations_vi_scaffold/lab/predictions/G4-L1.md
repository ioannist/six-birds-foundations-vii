# G4-L1 Prediction - Greedy Egyptian Fractions

## Hypothesis

For every proper fraction `p/q` with `0 < p < q <= 500`, after reducing to
lowest terms, the Fibonacci-Sylvester greedy Egyptian-fraction algorithm
terminates in finitely many steps. At each step it chooses
`m = ceil(q/p)` exactly, subtracts `1/m`, and the reduced numerator of the
remaining proper fraction strictly decreases until it reaches `0`.

The resulting unit-fraction denominators are distinct, and the exact sum of
the recorded unit fractions equals the original reduced target fraction.

## Predicted Outcome

The sweep should pass with exact rational arithmetic only. Python's
`fractions.Fraction` and integer ceiling division are sufficient; no floating
point arithmetic or approximate comparison is needed.

The run should also expose the moving-cover shape by recording the certificate
length distribution and the largest certificate found among the tested
fractions.

## Falsification Condition

Any tested `p/q` that fails to terminate within the declared step cap, has a
non-strict numerator step, repeats a unit denominator, or whose final exact
unit-fraction sum differs from the target fraction falsifies this calibration.
Such a failure would indicate a real bug in the stated formula or in the
implementation.
