# G8-L1 Prediction - Sandpile Odometer Invariance

## Hypothesis
For each tested grid sandpile with sink, stabilization is order-independent:
for the same initial chip configuration, every legal firing order reaches the
same stable final configuration and the same odometer vector.

## Predicted Outcome
Using exact integer arithmetic only, seeded random legal firing orders on grids
10x10 through 50x50 will always produce exact equality of final configurations
and exact equality of odometer vectors across at least 100 legal firing orders
per initial configuration.

## Falsification Condition
For any fixed initial configuration, if two legal firing orders produce different
stable final configurations or different odometer vectors, then G8 Part A is
falsified for this sandpile instantiation.

## Registration
This prediction was written before implementing or running the G8-L1 experiment
in this packet.
