# Canonical primitive-role registry

Authority: Foundations II controls the preferred meanings. Foundations I’s older operational labels are retained as historical realizations, not competing definitions.

## P1 — Descent compatibility / obstruction

Compatibility of an update or transport with a quotient; when compatibility fails, the role records the obstruction to descent.

- **Behavioral:** Descent succeeds or fails for the declared update/readout.
- **Verification:** A descended-map proof or an explicit split-pair/no-map obstruction witness.
- **Structural-categorical:** Quotient data equipped with a descended map, or quotient/update/readout data carrying an obstruction record.
- **Activation:** Requires aligned quotient, update/transport, target readout, threshold data, witness schema, level and audit.
- **Non-collapse boundary:** Generic semantics, interpretation, or mere route data is not P1.
- **Source:** P027 `papers/Tsiokos_2026_Six_Birds_Foundations_II_Admissibility_Meta_Theory_and_the_Exact_Six_Program.tex` lines 268-284; 326-352 (`def:primitive-roles; rem:per-primitive-levels`).

## P2 — Macro-specification representability

Whether a declared macro specification, constraint, gate, or target is representable/realizable in the current typed setting.

- **Behavioral:** Representable or non-representable status.
- **Verification:** Representability or non-representability certificate.
- **Structural-categorical:** A macro-specification layer together with a realization relation.
- **Activation:** Requires a genuine specification/realization problem, threshold and witness—not a bare label or arbitrary constraint.
- **Non-collapse boundary:** P2 is not every predicate and not a substitute for P6 accounting.
- **Source:** P027 `papers/Tsiokos_2026_Six_Birds_Foundations_II_Admissibility_Meta_Theory_and_the_Exact_Six_Program.tex` lines 268-284; 326-352 (`def:primitive-roles; rem:per-primitive-levels`).

## P3 — Enriched route/coherence mismatch

Disagreement, non-reducibility, or holonomy among declared enriched routes or coherence data.

- **Behavioral:** Witnessed route disagreement or mismatch.
- **Verification:** Generated route terms and a non-reducibility/mismatch certificate.
- **Structural-categorical:** A categorical route grammar or reduced categorical universe carrying the routes.
- **Activation:** Requires two or more admissible routes and a typed comparison; generic transition data is insufficient.
- **Non-collapse boundary:** P3 is diagnostic route structure, not a directionality certificate; P6_drive is separately required.
- **Source:** P027 `papers/Tsiokos_2026_Six_Birds_Foundations_II_Admissibility_Meta_Theory_and_the_Exact_Six_Program.tex` lines 268-303; 326-352 (`def:primitive-roles; rem:repaired-alignments; rem:p6-drive`).

## P4 — Stage / order / transition / refinement structure

Typed architecture of stages, order, transitions, refinements, sectors, or gluing relations.

- **Behavioral:** Stage status, transition occurrence, or refinement behavior.
- **Verification:** Order/preorder, transition, or refinement certificate.
- **Structural-categorical:** A stage/refinement architecture with nontrivial laws.
- **Activation:** Requires non-identity ordered/refinement structure and its laws; arbitrary relations or labels do not activate P4.
- **Non-collapse boundary:** P4 records organization of change; it does not by itself certify novelty, closure, or directionality.
- **Source:** P027 `papers/Tsiokos_2026_Six_Birds_Foundations_II_Admissibility_Meta_Theory_and_the_Exact_Six_Program.tex` lines 268-284; 326-352; 1587-1608 (`def:primitive-roles; prop:p4-witness`).

## P5 — Quotient packaging / quotienting

Identification of distinctions into a quotient/package together with validity or universal-property data.

- **Behavioral:** Packaging or identification behavior.
- **Verification:** Equivalence and quotient-validity certificate.
- **Structural-categorical:** A quotient object, packaging map, and universal-property or tower/coarsening data.
- **Activation:** Requires distinct raw objects, a nontrivial equivalence/identification, a packaging map, and quotient-validity evidence.
- **Non-collapse boundary:** Generic paths or derivations are not P5; strictness and closure are separate predicates on a package/bridge.
- **Source:** P027 `papers/Tsiokos_2026_Six_Birds_Foundations_II_Admissibility_Meta_Theory_and_the_Exact_Six_Program.tex` lines 268-293; 326-352; 1610-1631 (`def:primitive-roles; prop:p5-witness`).

## P6 — Audit / bookkeeping / provenance / replay / checkability

Typed records that make claims, events, sources, dependencies, costs, replay, and checks interrogable.

- **Behavioral:** Audit-relevant event occurrence or record–event association.
- **Verification:** A checkable audit record.
- **Structural-categorical:** An audit schema carrying provenance, replay, dependency, and check relations.
- **Activation:** Requires event-reference, source/dependency, and replay/check structure; passive logs or partial records are insufficient.
- **Non-collapse boundary:** P6 is broader than directionality. P6_drive is a narrow affinity-carrying, detailed-balance-breaking face.
- **Source:** P027 `papers/Tsiokos_2026_Six_Birds_Foundations_II_Admissibility_Meta_Theory_and_the_Exact_Six_Program.tex` lines 268-303; 326-352; 1633-1653 (`def:primitive-roles; rem:p6-drive; prop:p6-witness`).
