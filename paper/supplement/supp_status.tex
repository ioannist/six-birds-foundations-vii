\label{supp:status}

\subsection{The certificate library}

The certificate library has \publicdecls{} public declarations: \publicthms{} theorems and
745 definitions, structures, inductive types and abbreviations. For every theorem an
executed \texttt{\#print axioms} report was recorded.

No theorem depends on \texttt{Classical.choice} or on \texttt{sorryAx}. The library is
built on \leantoolchain{} with no external library, together with byte-identical copies
of the Foundations~V and~VI libraries and of the earlier series' formal cores. The
structure used for commuting descent squares takes its four carriers in Lean's lowest
universe \texttt{Type}; every other general construction is universe-polymorphic.

\subsection{The semantic modules}

The three semantic modules have 45 theorems, including four private helpers. Their axiom
reports are listed in \Cref{tab:semantic-axioms}. Nine use no axioms, five use
\texttt{propext} only, four use \texttt{propext} and \texttt{Quot.sound}, and twenty-seven
use \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and \texttt{Quot.sound}. The modules
contain no \texttt{sorry}, no added axiom, no \texttt{admit} and no
\texttt{native\_decide}. The finite reachability search enumerates all sublists of a
covering list and is exponential in its length.

\subsection{What the formal development does not establish}

The kernel checks that each encoded statement follows from its encoded hypotheses. It does
not check that a certificate is the right record of an interaction, that a declared finite
carrier is the relevant one, or that a result is significant. Many theorems of the
certificate library are short consequences of the definitions of their records (main
paper, \Cref{sec:sep-definitional}). Two statements of the main paper are argued on paper
only: the elementary remark on $a\oplus b$ and the first clause of the no-free-join
refusal (main paper, \Cref{sec:lean}).
