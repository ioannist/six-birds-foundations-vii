\section{Certificate fields}
\label{supp:fields}

The main paper summarizes the certificates by cluster (main paper,
\Cref{sec:certificates}). \Cref{tab:certificates} lists each certificate with what it
records and its validity predicate. \Cref{tab:fields} lists the Lean structure that
carries each one, the number of its fields and their names, read from the Lean sources.
Validity predicates are separate definitions next to each structure.
