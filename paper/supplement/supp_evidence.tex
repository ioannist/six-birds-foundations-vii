\section{Finite evidence}
\label{supp:evidence}

\subsection{Scenarios, countermodels and the detector contract}

Twenty-four scenarios and twenty-seven named countermodels are declared against the reference
world (main paper, \Cref{sec:ttw}). A scenario exercises a certificate and carries the status it
is expected to produce; a countermodel is built to refute one named inference and carries the
reason it is rejected. The separations of \Cref{tab:separations} are the twenty-seven
countermodels. A detector contract records append-only evidence, non-empty lists of signal, null
and falsifier cases, non-empty lists of controls of four kinds (same source, no contact,
rescheduling and relabelling) and a false-positive cost above the false-negative cost. Over the
24 scenarios and 27 countermodels the contract is well formed and complete, and every scenario's
and countermodel's expected status is decided by the finite evaluator; this is a decidable
statement checked by evaluation. Canonical counts below are smaller than raw counts only where a
declared symmetry, such as exchanging the two parents or renaming sources, is a genuine symmetry
of the property checked; the accepted and rejected counts record what each carrier contains and
are not evidence for or against a law.

\subsection{The 43 assays}

\Cref{tab:assays} lists every assay with its raw, canonical and accepted cardinalities
and the family it enumerates. The totals are $284{,}524$ raw, $214{,}636$ canonical,
$70{,}915$ accepted and $143{,}721$ rejected cases. Each count holds for its declared
carrier only.
