\section{The certificate records}
\label{supp:certificates}

This section defines the certificate records that the main paper summarizes in its section on
the certificate language (main paper, \Cref{sec:certificates}). The strict-join certificate is
defined there.

\subsection{Access and admission}

The access record is defined in the main paper (\Cref{def:access}).

Admission is modelled as a typed transition system with five operations: admission,
expiry, revocation, retraction and rollback. Each requires source, budget, guard and audit
witnesses and has a typed effect on the admissibility coordinate. The four non-admitting
transitions matter as much as admission: a system that can only admit cannot distinguish
a claim that was withdrawn from one that was never made. An admission regime records
whether its family of extensions is closed, which seeds it has admitted, which generators
are reachable and which extensions are provisioned from outside. A first extension is
\emph{authorized} when there is an admitted seed, a reachable generator or open external
provision; \Cref{prop:bootstrap} is the resulting refusal.

\subsection{Contact and join}

\begin{definition}[Contact surface and contact witness]
\label{def:contact}
A \emph{contact surface} records two theories, their interfaces, a crossing relation and
an admissibility flag; it is well formed when the two theories differ and the crossing
relation is named. A \emph{contact witness} records a surface, an event, a source, whether
the crossing occurred, a description of the payload and an audit; it is well formed when
its surface is well formed and admissible, the crossing occurred, the payload is described
and the audit is non-empty. Separately, channel evidence licenses one of five contact
modes (rendezvous, mediation, sequential, shared carrier, shared budget) by a direct
endpoint match, a certified mediator, a recorded sequence, a certified shared carrier or a
certified shared budget.
\end{definition}

Contact is weaker than join. Two theories may share a carrier, exchange a record or
transport a payload without any joint theory coming into existence.

\begin{definition}[Join status]
\label{def:join-status}
Within a declared finite family of interfaces, a join attempt receives one of seven
statuses: no evidenced contact, evidenced contact, common refinement, lawful composite,
strict join, typed obstruction, or certified non-interaction.
\end{definition}

The classifier assigns exactly one status to every attempt in the declared family, which is
checked by enumeration over its carriers. Nothing is claimed about attempts outside the
declared family.

The join budget prices interface access, parent retention, joint novelty, observer and
instrument occupancy, and cross-costs, with refunds. An entry is credited when its amount
is covered by payments and refunds, or when its amount is zero on a certified zero-cost
channel. A formation earns \emph{native} credit, credit as a product of the
join's own sources rather than of bridged or external ones, only when every observer's
occupancy is charged in full. The ledger is local
accounting over declared resources, not a conservation law.

\subsection{Enablement and descent}

\begin{definition}[Enablement record]
\label{def:enablement}
An \emph{enablement record} identifies an operation made available, its source, whether it
was executed, the target before and after, budgets, an attribution and an audit. An
\emph{attributed} enablement adds attribution evidence (a typed source, a witnessed
execution, accounted budget and audit, and no hidden executor), requires execution, and
requires the record's source to match the evidence. Enablement is \emph{endogenous} when
the enabling generator is carried, reachable, executed, audited and budgeted within a
closed boundary, neither a theorist nor an observer executor is hidden, and any
environmental input is accounted.
\end{definition}

An \emph{enablement separation profile} stores independent Boolean flags. Two of them
record claims: that an enablement is \emph{load-bearing}, that is, the record asserts that
the enabled operation depends on it; and that \emph{descent} holds, that is, the record
asserts that the enabled content descends from the enabling theory, which then also
determines it. No semantic definition of dependence or descent is formalized; the flags
are recorded assertions. \Cref{tab:separations} contains a profile whose load-bearing flag
is set and whose descent flag is clear.

A transmission record carries a direction (upward, downward or peer), a payload, what was
lost, what is ambiguous and a source. An operational profile records whether a rule is
sound, executable, guard-active, reachable, fired and occurrent; it is coherent when an
executable rule is sound, a reachable one is guard-active and executable, a fired one is
reachable and an occurrent one has fired. A negative-evidence record states the force of a negative result: a point null, a
bounded search, an exhaustive search of a finite family, and an unrestricted universal
claim have four different forces, and the record states which one is claimed.

\subsection{Dynamics and residue}

\begin{definition}[Route comparison and holonomy]
\label{def:route}
A \emph{route comparison} records two route results, each with a target, a list of
members, order and bracketing codes, a predictive value, an audit value and a legality
flag. It has \emph{route residue} when both results are legal, they share target and
members, their order or bracketing codes differ, and their predictive or audit values
differ. The catalog calls residue with differing predictive values \emph{interaction
holonomy}. The name is used in a declared structural sense; no bundle, connection or
geometric phase is involved \citep{Berry1984}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Drive certificate]
\label{def:drive}
A \emph{drive certificate} records five flags: a drive, a path asymmetry, a failed
reversal, budgeting and auditing. It is valid when all five are set. An \emph{arrow claim} is eligible only with a valid drive certificate.
Cross-time contact additionally requires a synchronization or partial-order witness.
\end{definition}

The fluctuation theorem relates forward and reverse path probabilities to entropy
production for stochastic, microscopically reversible dynamics \citep{Crooks1999}; it is a
comparison for the drive and reversal fields, not an interpretation of them.

The residual ledger types what an interaction leaves undischarged: inaccessible,
unadmitted, expired, failed and join-created residuals. Each entry may record a discharge
route (ascent, peer join, refinement or declared termination), and the ledger is
append-only, so no residual disappears silently.
