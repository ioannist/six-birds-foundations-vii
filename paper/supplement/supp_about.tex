\section{What this supplement holds}
\label{supp:about}

This supplement accompanies \emph{Six Birds Foundations VII: Lawful Theory Interaction}.
The main paper states and proves every result; its numbered items are cited here by their
numbers there. The supplement gives the Lean counterpart of each numbered item
(\Cref{supp:concordance}), the field lists of the certificates (\Cref{supp:fields}), the
register of the 36 catalog entries with their refusals and cross-family corollaries
(\Cref{supp:register}), the finite evidence and the named countermodels
(\Cref{supp:evidence}), the formal status of the Lean development (\Cref{supp:status}) and
the commands that rebuild it (\Cref{supp:replay}). The certificate language builds on the
series' earlier papers \citep{STsiokosFoundationsI,STsiokosFoundationsII,STsiokosFoundationsIII,
STsiokosFoundationsIV,STsiokosFoundationsV,STsiokosFoundationsVI}.

Every declaration listed is present in the Lean sources at the file and line given.
