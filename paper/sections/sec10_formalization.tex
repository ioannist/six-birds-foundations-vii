\section{Formalization}
\label{sec:lean}

The results are accompanied by a development in Lean~4.28.0 that uses no external library.
It has two parts.

\paragraph{The certificate library.} The certificate language and its reference world
comprise \publicdecls{} public declarations, of which \publicthms{} are theorems. An axiom
report was recorded for every one of these theorems. None depends on the axiom of choice
or on an unfinished proof; the others use at most propositional extensionality and
quotient soundness, and the counts are in \Cref{supp:status}. Most of these theorems
concern records and are short consequences of their definitions, as
\Cref{sec:sep-definitional} describes; the kernel confirms that each follows, not that it
is deep. One record-level construction, a commuting square used to record faithful
transmission between layers, is stated for types in Lean's lowest universe only.

\paragraph{The semantic modules.} \Cref{sec:strict-join,sec:admission} are formalized in
three further modules, and the propositions of \Cref{sec:sep-semantic} in five of six further modules; their axiom
reports are listed in \Cref{supp:status}. Newman's
lemma and the uniqueness of normal forms depend on no axioms. The axiom of choice enters
in four places: in choosing a representative of each fibre in
\Cref{thm:split-criterion}; in choosing a successor when a terminating relation reaches a
normal state; in the proof of the finite reachability check (\Cref{prop:finite-peaks}),
which uses the set of reachable states classically although the check itself is
computable; and through the standard library's lemmas on removing an element from a
list. The flags in \Cref{thm:bridge} are truth values of propositions and are defined
classically. These modules are universe-polymorphic; they contain no unfinished proof, no
added axiom and no proof by compiled evaluation. The library builds with them included.

\paragraph{What is not formalized.} Two statements are argued on paper only: that
$a\oplus b$ factors on its image through the pairing but through neither parent
(\Cref{sec:strict-join}), and the relation of this work to the external literature
(\Cref{sec:related}). The formalization also cannot
establish that the definitions capture what they are meant to capture. The kernel checks
that each encoded statement follows from its encoded hypotheses
\citep{DeMouraUllrich2021,Pollack1998}; whether a certificate is the right record of an
interaction is argued in the paper.

The supplement gives the Lean counterpart of every numbered item
(\Cref{supp:concordance}), the axiom reports (\Cref{supp:status}) and the commands that
rebuild the library, the finite evidence and the reports (\Cref{supp:replay}).
