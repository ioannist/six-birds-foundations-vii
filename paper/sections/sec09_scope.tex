The catalog's 36 entries are 36 theorems. & Most are definitions, consequences of definitions or refusals (\Cref{sec:setting-kinds,sec:sep-definitional}); the theorems are those of \Cref{sec:strict-join,sec:admission}. \\
A valid strict-join certificate shows that a strict join exists. & The certificate's flags are recorded assertions. \Cref{thm:bridge} shows they can be earned by a semantic strict join proved not to factor through a declared refinement and schedule; objecthood, parent retention, independence and budget remain assertions. \\
A strict observable is new relative to every description of the join. & Strictness is tested against the parents and their pairing only (\Cref{rem:refinement-limit}). \\
An observable that neither parent determines is strict. & It may factor on its image through the pairing, as $a\oplus b$ does (\Cref{sec:strict-join}). \\
Admission order never matters. & It does not matter when admission can only enable, which is sufficient but not shown necessary (\Cref{thm:admission}); one disabling guard gives two terminal states (\Cref{ex:disabling}). \\
Local confluence gives confluence. & Not without further hypotheses: with termination it does (\Cref{thm:newman}), and without it it can fail (\Cref{ex:four-state}). \\
Every item is eventually admitted. & Items on a cycle of prerequisites are never enabled (\Cref{thm:admission}). \\
The catalog's critical-pair criterion proves confluence. & It restates a predicate on critical-pair records (\Cref{sec:sep-definitional}). \\
Joining theories reduces obstructions. & The residual ledger records residuals created at the composite interface; the record is an attribution, not a derived fact (\Cref{sec:sep-definitional}). \\
Contact, a shared origin or a shared carrier is interaction. & Each is separated from shared access, transport and join (\Cref{tab:separations}). \\
Enablement explains what it enables. & Enablement is separated from descent and from sufficiency (\Cref{tab:separations}). \\
Order dependence, or a strict join, gives an arrow of time. & An arrow requires a drive certificate (\Cref{def:drive}). \\
A join is a categorical product. & No categorical statement is made; only the set-level pairing appears (\Cref{rem:deferral-categorical}). \\
No quantity is conserved under contact. & Two particular scalars disagree; nothing universal is proved (\Cref{rem:degree-refuted}). \\
A negative search result is universal. & Its force is that of its search family, horizon and detector (\Cref{sec:sep-definitional}). \\
The finite assays prove the laws. & They are exhaustive over their declared carriers only (\Cref{sec:ttw}). \\
The results apply to particular real theories. & No instantiation is carried out; each would need its own regime, carriers, witnesses and audit. \\
\end{longtable}
