\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Combining and comparing theories.}
Several mature literatures address neighbouring, more specialized problems. Nelson--Oppen
combination gives conditions under which decision procedures for quantifier-free
fragments of suitably separated first-order theories cooperate \citep{NelsonOppen1979}.
Institution theory and algebraic specification study signatures, sentences, models,
satisfaction and morphisms across logical systems, and build structured specifications
from them \citep{GoguenBurstall1992,SannellaTarlecki2012}. Interface automata and
contract-based design make compatibility, refinement, environment assumptions and
composition explicit \citep{DeAlfaroHenzinger2001,AbadiLamport1993,BenvenisteEtAl2018}.
Theories can also be compared without being composed: Morita and categorical equivalence
give criteria for when two formulations are the same theory, and show how much structure
a criterion preserves \citep{BarrettHalvorson2016,Weatherall2021}. These are the nearest
comparison classes. None of them is used here as a representation theorem for the join
of \Cref{sec:strict-join}, whose content is the elementary statement on factorization over the reached image in
Theorem~A.

\paragraph{Rewriting.}
Newman's lemma \citep{Newman1942} and the critical-pair method \citep{KnuthBendix1970,
Huet1980} are the classical tools for proving that the order of rewriting steps does not
matter. \Cref{sec:admission} applies them to admission orders.

\paragraph{Provenance, causation and enablement.}
Database provenance tracks how outputs depend on source data; causal models separate
common causes from stronger causal claims; organizational-closure work treats constraints
as acting on processes while depending on one another for their maintenance
\citep{GreenEtAl2007,Pearl2009,MontevilMossio2015}. The certificate language keeps these
apart: its source-independence field is a record of distinct ancestry, not statistical
independence, and its enablement record is not a causal or biological closure model. The
reachability and horizon fields are informed by the distinction between safety and
liveness and by bounded model checking \citep{AlpernSchneider1985,ClarkeEtAl2018,
BiereEtAl1999}, in particular by the warning that a bounded search establishes a bounded
result. Preregistration in scientific method motivates the prospective-commitment record
\citep{NosekEtAl2018}.

\paragraph{The Six Birds series.}
Foundations~I supplies the notion of a theory package: a carrier with a lens, a
completion endomap and an audit \citep{TsiokosFoundationsI}. Foundations~II supplies the
vocabulary of admissible claims, of which the admission records here are a
specialization \citep{TsiokosFoundationsII}. Foundations~III supplies an audited
interaction calculus within a fixed setting \citep{TsiokosFoundationsIII}; this paper asks
the prior question of when interaction across a boundary is lawful at all.
Foundations~IV supplies the catalog form and the access quotient
\citep{TsiokosFoundationsIV}. Foundations~V uses the word \emph{join} for the repair join,
a lattice operation on quotients of one carrier \citep{TsiokosFoundationsV}; the join of
theories here is a different object and gets a different symbol, $\tjoin$ rather than
$\repairjoin$. Foundations~V and~VI provide formal libraries that the Lean development
builds on \citep{TsiokosFoundationsV,TsiokosFoundationsVI}. Individual certificates
sharpen results of earlier papers in the series.
