\section{The reference world and finite evidence}
\label{sec:ttw}

The catalog is anchored by one finite model, the \emph{Two-Theory World}: two theory packages
with access records, admission transitions, contact surfaces, join entries, enablement chains,
budget ledgers and audits, small enough to enumerate and rich enough that every certificate has
an instance in it. Named scenarios exercise each certificate, and named countermodels each refute
one tempting inference; the record-level separations of \Cref{tab:separations} are among them. A
detector contract, with signal, null and falsifier cases and controls for same source, no contact,
rescheduling and relabelling, fixes how each certificate is tested, and finite assays enumerate
declared carriers exhaustively. All of this shows how the certificates read, not that they are the
right ones or that any law holds; the scenarios, countermodels, contract and assay counts are in
\Cref{supp:evidence}.
