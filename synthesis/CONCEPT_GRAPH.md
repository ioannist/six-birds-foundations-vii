The machine-readable graph is `concept_graph.graphml`. Nodes marked `existing` are established corpus objects or registries; nodes marked `VII-gap` are provisional targets distilled from the wish lists. An edge records a dependency, constraint, or explicit non-implication—not a new theorem.
