# Canonical SBT synthesis after Step 1

## 1. Controlling interpretation

Six Birds Theory is a theory of **how objects, layers, laws, records, and regimes become available and acquire lawful standing under bounded access**. Its basic unit is not a scale label or an informal pattern. It is a typed theory package with declared carrier, interface, definability, packaging/closure, and audit structure.

Audit is constitutive: without provenance, visibility, source, bridge, cost, replay, and nonclaim records, a candidate conclusion is not lawfully attributable. But SBT is not merely an audit framework. Its mathematical content includes fixed-point object formation, quotient-relative access, nonfactorization and strict extension, obstruction and repair, route mismatch and memory, endogenous reclosure, run-level currencies and liveness, and exact impossibility theorems.

The canonical package notation used in this repository is

\[
\mathcal T=(Z,f,\Sigma_f,E,A),
\]

where `Z` is the carrier, `f` the declared lens/interface, `Σ_f` the distinctions expressible through that interface, `E` the packaging/completion operation, and `A` the audit/provenance apparatus. Individual papers enrich this tuple with instruments, ledgers, witnesses, source records, dynamics, or realization fields. Those enrichments are typed additions, not permission to erase the base roles.

## 2. The three certificates

Every strong emergence or extension claim must keep three obligations separate.

### 2.1 Objecthood / stability

A candidate must be a fixed point, approximate fixed point, or otherwise formed object under the declared packaging operation, with its formation and audit conditions included. A syntactically named candidate is not an object merely because it is useful or persistent in one demonstration.

### 2.2 Novelty / strictness

A new layer must carry a distinction, operation, or predictive capacity not factoring through the old interface. In finite quotient language, there must be an old-indistinguishable pair separated by the new readout, or an equivalent nonfactorization witness. Strictness is instrument-relative and does not itself imply closure, promotion acceptance, endogeny, realization, or drive.

### 2.3 Directionality

An arrow claim requires a P6-side drive audit—such as non-exact affinity or forward/reversed path-law asymmetry—that survives the declared observation and protocol. Route dependence, noncommutation, order residue, or P3 holonomy can force memory without proving a time arrow. The no-go data-processing and protocol-trap results are mandatory controls.

The three certificates are logically independent. Foundations VII must repeat this separation for contact and join claims: joint novelty is not joint objecthood, and order-dependent joining is not an interaction arrow.

## 3. Canonical primitive-role architecture

Foundations II controls the meanings of P1–P6, including corrections to looser Foundations-I language.

| Role | Canonical meaning | Required red line |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | Descent/update-versus-quotient compatibility and its obstruction | Generic semantics or route data is not P1 |
| P2 | Representability/realizability of a declared macro specification | Not every predicate or constraint is a P2 activation |
| P3 | Enriched route/coherence mismatch or holonomy | P3 is not directionality; `P6_drive` is separate |
| P4 | Stage, order, transition, refinement, sector, or gluing architecture | Change structure alone does not prove novelty or closure |
| P5 | Quotient packaging/identification with validity or universal-property data | Generic paths are not P5; package strictness is separate |
| P6 | Audit, provenance, source, replay, dependency, cost, and checkability | P6 is broader than drive; passive logs are insufficient |

Each role has behavioral, verification/certificate, and structural levels. Lifts among levels are selected and data-bearing, not automatic. A six-channel description can have inactive, below-threshold, collapsed, absent-with-record, or out-of-scope channels.

The scoped exact-six theorem is an existential, non-unique exhaustion result over FATCD under a recognition hypothesis. It is not a universal six-symbol algebra, a uniqueness theorem, a claim that all six channels are active, or an empirical statement that all systems realize six mechanisms.

## 4. The finite audited interaction calculus

Foundations III supplies `BirdInt_fin^aud`, whose controlling body declaration has nineteen fields:

`P, Lev, Host, Cont, Instr, Pkg, Prof, Wit, Upd, Def, Judg, Status, Gate, Dep, Audit, Vis, Source, Nonclaim, Real`.

An appendix's older sixteen-component shorthand omits `Vis`, `Source`, and `Real`; this repository records that as a legacy inconsistency and treats the body declaration as normative.

The calculus separates:

- directed cells, where an actor consumes an informant witness through a typed update;
- unordered pair observables, which require joint-source and branch-compatibility records;
- promotion bridges;
- claims, gates, squares, and their distinct status families;
- internal records from model realization.

A real cell does not make a pair observable real. A strict promotion does not prove target closure or drive. Fallback evidence is not native evidence. Rotating audit is useful but not final same-level self-certification.

BirdInt is a finite typed calculus and record schema, not already a category, algebra, closure operator, or general theory-join operation. Foundations VII should reuse its typing and provenance discipline while introducing new judgment families for contact, admission, join, reachability, and occurrence.

## 5. Four strata of established law

### 5.1 Static structural laws — Foundations IV

The 52 F-laws classify access, stability, transport, symmetry, status, records, self-reference, and limits for fixed packages or declared finite families. The most load-bearing VII precedents are:

- **F2** descent-repair normal form: obstruction, source refinement, and minimal target coarsening;
- **F4** local-global obstruction normal form;
- **F5** recombination comparison;
- **F10–F12** access quotient, no-overread, and no-free-distinction;
- **F17** common public quotient/objectivity;
- **F18** lawhood descent;
- **F22** interface mediation;
- **F28** composability;
- **F35** scale descent;
- **F37** complementarity/non-joint access;
- **F38** arrow/record monotonicity;
- **F49** common-source/nonlocal correlation;
- **F51** unification/common refinement;
- **F53** closure–reality anchor.

F51 is not a strict join theorem. F49 is not source independence. F17 is not shared access. F5 is not evidenced contact. These laws constrain VII but do not preempt it.

### 5.2 Endogenous/carried laws — Foundations V

Foundations V makes instruments, ledgers, repair generators, boundary records, and re-audit loops part of the carrier. Its D1 repair join is a coarsest common refinement of quotients that supplies distinctions only; it is not a join of formed theories and says nothing by itself about source, cost, access, occurrence, or utility.

D3–D6 define carried instruments, endogenous closure systems, closed-loop scope, and finite probe economies. A carried operation's existence is separate from permission, lawful reachability, on-kernel execution, occurrence, and re-audit. P6 remains governance/audit rather than being folded into the P1–P5 repair generator.

The E1–E16 laws cover internalization, bounded reflexivity, self-maintaining reclosure, repair compilation, collapse, attention, alarm, control price, curiosity, cognitive demarcation, institutional rewrite, individuation, repair transport, reconsolidation, offline reclosure, and adaptability.

### 5.3 Dynamical/run-level laws — Foundations VI

The G1–G13 laws quantify over runs rather than fixed states. They treat amortized and transfinite currencies, moving covers, carry horizons, endogenous needle generation, adversarial mobility, odometer abelianization, defect evacuation, lossy boundary persistence, anti-symmetry, witness radiation, and thin-orbit saturation.

Static existence must not be flattened into liveness. `reachable`, `eventually reached`, `observed by horizon H`, and `guaranteed on every lawful run` require distinct quantifiers. The Collatz flagship is conditional on unproved bridge hypotheses and is not a Collatz proof.

### 5.4 No-go boundaries

The eight no-go fronts are carrier-specific:

`NG_ARROW_DPI`, `NG_PROTOCOL_TRAP`, `NG_FORCE_FOREST`, `NG_FORCE_NULL`, `NG_MACRO_CLOSURE_DEFICIT`, `NG_OBJECT_CONTRACTIVE`, `NG_LADDER_IDEM`, and `NG_LADDER_BOUNDED_INTERFACE`.

Their exact carrier and escape routes are part of the theorem. A Markov support graph is not a process-connection graph; a bounded-interface no-go does not forbid growth after interface change; and a protocol trap hides drive rather than proving its absence. The format to inherit for VII is: objects first, hypotheses second, impossibility third, escape routes fourth, application bridge last.

## 6. Philosophy and metatheory

The metatheory papers sharpen how established mathematics, methodology, and philosophical purchase must be separated.

- **AOR (P006)** supplies scoped audit completion, a structural-defect hierarchy, a thirteen-type residual atlas, and discharge cascades. Audit closure is not runtime reachability or occurrence.
- **Reflexive SBT (P023)** treats audit as bounded capacity bookkeeping and gives conditional anti-localization results. It does not yield universal capacity decay or free observer standing.
- **Incompleteness (P034)** proves fixed-package saturation and necessity of package change for persistent strict growth. It is a precursor to bootstrap obstruction, not the finished reachability theorem.
- **ZFC (P053)** separates finite/auditable construction from purchased commitments. It provides an assumption-ledger precedent, not a direct theory-join law.
- **A Mathematics Is a Theory (P046)** is a closure-audit method with diagnostics and explicit target-theorem nonclaims.
- **A Physics Is a Theory (P041)** gives controlled total-lens instantiations and mechanized/numerical evidence. Total-lens results cannot be transferred silently to partial self-owned access.
- **The SAU family (P040/P058)** proves a vertical promoted-use pattern: hidden non-descent plus audited boundary descent. Usable answers can descend without literalizing the promoted object. This is not horizontal joint formation.
- **Reality as Closure (P056)** offers a conditional ontology and explicitly purchases Ontic Closure Completeness and Substrate Indifference. It is not a source that overrides formal theorem grades.

## 7. Cognition and social pressure tests

The cognition/social papers are not extra foundations, but they expose distinctions VII must survive.

- **Consciousness (P005):** eight structural clauses and four claim lanes; component certificates do not imply recognition or realization.
- **Agents (P055):** agenthood/viability is distinct from causal agency/intervention.

These applications support status and bridge discipline. They do not establish a universal join, enablement, or birth law.

## 9. What the corpus does not yet supply

After the full Step-1 survey, the missing object is clear. The corpus does not yet provide a general theory of **partially accessible, separately formed theory packages making witnessed, lawful contact**. In particular, it lacks a unified formal treatment of:

- founding and changing accessible domains;
- admission mechanisms and bootstrap obstruction;
- expressible/present/reachable/occurrent status;
- contact surfaces and contact witnesses;
- coincidence, common refinement, strict join, obstructed join, fake join, and pending join;
- source/ancestry independence versus behavioral nonfactorization;
- join existence, obstruction, anti-product evidence, cost, and capacity;
- interaction without join and certified non-interaction;
- theorist-triggered versus carried/endogenous enablement;
- bottom-up versus top-down construction reachability;
- inter-theory order, confluence, holonomy, simultaneity, and arrow audits;
- exposure, recoverability, operational admissibility, observer occupancy, and residuals;
- interaction-specific no-gos and detector/null/falsifier obligations.

This is the legitimate Foundations VII problem surface. It is narrower than 'all interaction' and stronger than a new audit vocabulary.

## 11. Formalization-aware authority rule

The imported proof scaffold adds a second anti-smuggling boundary. “Present in a paper,” “reported as mechanized,” “source file locally available,” “statically indexed,” “import-resolved,” “kernel-compiled here,” and “finite tests rerun here” are distinct statuses. Statement fidelity is independent: a typed mirror, record projection, conditional classification, narrowed theorem, guarded wrapper, and direct derivation do not license the same prose.

The controlling reconciliation is:

- Foundations I supplies a locally present but intentionally minimal closure/package core.
- Foundations II supplies a locally present typed harness and malformed-case rejection surface, not an independent exact-six proof.
- Foundations III supplies locally present BirdInt modules, but this repository has not rerun the exact original manifest validator.
- Foundations IV reports 52 Lean rows; only 14 are in the supplied archive, and guarded/anchor statuses remain visible.
- Foundations V supplies the complete local D1-D6 / E1-E16 theorem base. The upstream E16 root/manifest omission is repaired only in VII-owned adapters, and paper-side conditionality remains controlling.
- Foundations VI supplies the complete local G-law/evidence surface, with theorem/schema/calibration grades preserved.
- The no-go paper discloses only a narrow formalized component for its contraction front; no blanket eight-theorem mechanization is inferred.

The exact vocabulary and current evidence states are defined in `synthesis/FORMALIZATION_EVIDENCE_LADDER.md`. Later work may say “already mechanized” only after naming the exact declaration, fidelity, trust/host premises, and local verification state.
