# Interaction, access, enablement, and budget taxonomy

The matrices keep status ladders typed and prevent familiar collapses. Source claim IDs are navigation pointers; the frozen TeX remains authoritative.

## Access State Matrix

Machine-readable table: `access_state_matrix.csv` (8 rows).

- **EXPRESSIBLE:** A well-typed sentence, rule, or operation can be formed in the declared language. **Boundary:** Does not imply that the referenced object or operation is present on the carrier.
- **PRESENT:** The relevant carrier object, rule, record, or operation exists in the package. **Boundary:** Does not imply exposure, executable access, or use.
- **EXPOSED:** A declared instrument can distinguish, display, or invoke the item through an interface. **Boundary:** Exposure may be lossy and does not imply recoverability or admissible execution.
- **RECOVERABLE:** The item can be reconstructed to declared fidelity from exposed data. **Boundary:** Recoverability does not imply permission, reachability, or occurrence.
- **ADEQUATE:** The exposed/recovered representation meets the declared task or probe family. **Boundary:** Adequacy is probe-relative and does not imply complete access.
- **ADMISSIBLE:** The item passes current type, source, budget, and audit gates. **Boundary:** A sound/admissible rule can remain operationally unreachable.
- **REACHABLE:** An executable finite path from an admitted current state reaches the item under declared resources. **Boundary:** Existence of a path does not witness actual firing.
- **OCCURRENT:** A run/event record witnesses that the transition, contact, or join actually fired. **Boundary:** Occurrence alone does not establish novelty, closure, or directionality.

## Admission Kind Matrix

Machine-readable table: `admission_kind_matrix.csv` (8 rows).

- **RULE_TEXT:** A declarative admission rule exists. **Boundary:** Not necessarily executable.
- **EXECUTABLE_OPERATION:** A carried operation is available to the runtime. **Boundary:** May still be unreachable.
- **NATIVE_RECORD:** Evidence is produced by the target package's own declared source. **Boundary:** May still fail relevance, budget, or quality gates.
- **BRIDGED_RECORD:** Evidence comes from another package through an explicit translation. **Boundary:** Must not be relabeled native.
- **PROSPECTIVE_COMMITMENT:** A timestamped, preregistered, budgeted rule reserves later admission conditions. **Boundary:** Post-hoc predicates do not qualify.
- **EXTERNAL_PROVISION:** An allowed seed, tool, or record is supplied from outside. **Boundary:** Cannot be credited as endogenous.
- **ENDOGENOUS_GENERATION:** The package carries and executes its own admissible generator. **Boundary:** Theorist execution is not enough.
- **RETRACTION_OR_ROLLBACK:** A previously admitted item is lawfully withdrawn or reverted. **Boundary:** Deletion is not automatically lawful rollback.

## Contact Join Status Matrix

Machine-readable table: `contact_join_status_matrix.csv` (9 rows).

- **NO_EVIDENCED_CONTACT:** No admissible contact witness has been supplied. **Boundary:** Not equivalent to certified non-interaction.
- **POTENTIAL_CONTACT:** Interfaces are type-compatible in principle. **Boundary:** Compatibility is not evidence of access or execution.
- **EVIDENCED_CONTACT:** A native or honestly bridged record crosses a declared contact surface. **Boundary:** Does not imply a joint object or joint novelty.
- **PEER_TRANSPORT:** Certified content is transported through an explicit peer map. **Boundary:** Does not imply formation of a joint theory.
- **COMMON_REFINEMENT:** A carrier/package projects lawfully to both parents. **Boundary:** May be a fork or product rather than a strict join.
- **COMPOSITE_PACKAGE:** Both parents are retained in one auditable package. **Boundary:** Retention is not anti-product novelty.
- **STRICT_JOIN:** A composite is formed and has an anti-product/nonfactorization witness relative to both parents and their mere product/refinement. **Boundary:** Does not by itself establish a P6 arrow.
- **OBSTRUCTED_JOIN:** A typed obstruction blocks source, budget, compatibility, gluing, retention, or novelty. **Boundary:** A failed gate must not be relabeled generic non-interaction.
- **CERTIFIED_NON_INTERACTION:** A coverage- and power-qualified audit establishes irrelevance or impossibility within a declared family. **Boundary:** Does not universalize beyond the covered family.

## Enablement Attribution Matrix

Machine-readable table: `enablement_attribution_matrix.csv` (7 rows).

- **THEORIST:** The analyst declares or executes the enabling operation. **Boundary:** Not endogenous.
- **CARRIER:** Substrate dynamics supplies the enabling transition. **Boundary:** Carrier causation need not imply theory-level descent.
- **OTHER_THEORY:** A peer package supplies a bridged operation or record. **Boundary:** Must retain peer provenance.
- **OBSERVER_INSTRUMENT:** An observer/instrument creates exposure or admissibility. **Boundary:** Observer resources are not free.
- **ENVIRONMENT:** An environmental input triggers or maintains the condition. **Boundary:** Environmentally sustained is not closed-loop endogenous.
- **ENDOGENOUS_SYSTEM:** The system carries, reaches, executes, and audits the generator itself. **Boundary:** Theorist-triggered execution does not qualify.
- **MIXED:** Multiple sources are load-bearing. **Boundary:** Mixed provenance must not be simplified to a single source.

## Order Holonomy Matrix

Machine-readable table: `order_holonomy_matrix.csv` (7 rows).

- **COMMUTING:** Declared routes agree on the audited target readout. **Boundary:** Agreement at one readout need not imply equality at a richer predictive quotient.
- **ORDER_SENSITIVE:** Two admissible operation orders produce distinguishable outputs. **Boundary:** Order sensitivity alone is not memory or directionality.
- **HOLONOMY:** A closed protocol loop has nontrivial route residue relative to the declared quotient/readout. **Boundary:** Holonomy does not imply P6 arrow.
- **MEMORY_REQUIRED:** Current quotient equality hides a route difference relevant to future probes. **Boundary:** Memory need not create a new object or sustained drive.
- **CONFLUENT:** All admissible reduction/interaction orders join to an equivalent audited result. **Boundary:** Confluence does not imply absence of transient route information.
- **NONCONFLUENT:** Admissible orders lead to inequivalent terminal or predictive statuses. **Boundary:** Nonconfluence is not automatically a temporal arrow.
- **P6_DRIVE_OR_ARROW:** A separate drive/affinity or path-space asymmetry certificate establishes directionality. **Boundary:** Cannot be inferred from P3 holonomy alone.

## Currency Budget Matrix

Machine-readable table: `currency_budget_matrix.csv` (7 rows).

- **ACCESS_COST:** Resources consumed to expose or invoke an interface. **Boundary:** No access credit when exposure fails.
- **BLIND_SPOT_RESIDUAL:** Task-relevant distinctions remaining inaccessible or inadequate. **Boundary:** Residual remains on the ledger until repaired or scope is narrowed.
- **COMMITMENT_BUDGET:** Capacity reserved prospectively for later admission or testing. **Boundary:** Post-hoc choice receives no prospective-credit refund.
- **OBSERVER_OCCUPANCY:** Instrument/observer resources occupying a channel, state, or budget slot. **Boundary:** Unpriced observer work cannot be credited as free endogeny.
- **PROVENANCE_DEBT:** Unresolved source or translation obligations attached to a record. **Boundary:** Unresolved debt blocks native or exact-bridge credit.
- **NOVELTY_BUDGET:** New distinctions actually earned relative to the current interface/package. **Boundary:** Relabeling or product composition earns no strict-novelty credit.
- **FAILURE_REFUND:** Explicit reversal of credit when a gate, detector, or bridge fails. **Boundary:** Refund restores honest status but does not erase the failure record.

## Retention Absorption Matrix

Machine-readable table: `retention_absorption_matrix.csv` (5 rows).

- **PARENT_RETAINED:** Each parent remains recoverable with source identity inside the child/composite. **Boundary:** Retention alone does not give strict join novelty.
- **PARTIAL_RETENTION:** Only declared parent readouts survive. **Boundary:** Must not be described as full parent preservation.
- **ABSORPTION:** A parent loses independent recoverability or identity in the child. **Boundary:** Absorption is not automatically failure if the claim permits it.
- **ERASURE:** A parent distinction or provenance record is silently or explicitly removed. **Boundary:** Silent erasure invalidates retention claims.
- **RECOMBINATION:** Parent/branch content is reorganized under a richer comparison quotient. **Boundary:** Branchwise equality need not determine recombination equality.
