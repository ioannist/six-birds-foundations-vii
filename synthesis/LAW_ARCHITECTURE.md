| Stratum | Primary quantified object | Registry | Count | Typical question |

## Import order for a future VII law

A candidate VII law should declare, in order:

1. the theory packages and partial domains;
2. instruments/interfaces and what they expose;
3. contact/admission/join operation and its runtime semantics;
4. source, ancestry, and bridge records;
5. objecthood and strictness obligations;
6. cost, capacity, and observer occupancy;
7. static versus run-level quantifiers;
8. detector, null, falsifier, and nearest countermodel;
9. inherited F/E/G/no-go results with explicit object maps;
10. claim grade and nonclaims.

## High-risk law neighborhoods

### Access

F10–F12 establish quotient-relative access and no-overread, but do not define self-owned partial domains or operational reachability. P030 adds carried instruments and probe economy, but carried presence is not firing. P034 and the ladder no-gos constrain bootstrap, but a VII theorem still needs an admission transition system and allowed seed class.

### Joining

F51 gives common refinement; F17 gives a common public quotient; F5 compares recombination quotients; P016 supplies anti-product-style finite evidence; P015 supplies gluing obstruction. A strict theory join needs additional contact, source, closure, anti-product, cost, and occurrence records.

### Direction and order

P3/F3/P012/P045 supply route/order residue and confluence tools. P031/P032 supply arrow audits and protocol controls. Interaction holonomy must be measured first as order residue and only later tested for a driven arrow.

### Enablement

P030 supplies carried operations and P055 separates agenthood from agency. P038/P043 supply application-side enablement/birth precedents. VII must still define the enablement relation and state when it is carried, sufficient, compositional, or merely theorist-triggered.

### Observer and detector

P023 supplies capacity constraints; P030 supplies finite probe economies; BirdInt supplies visibility/source/audit records. VII must add observer occupancy, detector competence, and explicit null/falsifier obligations.

## Identifier preservation

F, E, G, and existing no-go identifiers are never renumbered. Candidate VII laws must receive a new namespace until the paper fixes its own canonical series. P040/P058 are one unresolved version family and cannot provide two independent votes for the same SAU claim.
