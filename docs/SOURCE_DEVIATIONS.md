Rule: the Lean tree, receipts and registries are frozen and authoritative. Wherever the text
said more than the corresponding declaration or proof establishes, the **text** was weakened
to what is established; wherever the text misdescribed what the development contains, the
text was corrected to match. No Lean file, receipt or registry was touched. Every entry names
the frozen source it was checked against.

Severity: **S** = text stronger than source (weakened); **M** = text misdescribes source
(corrected, neither stronger nor weaker); **N** = fidelity note added to the text (no change
of claim). Phase 1 entries are L-*; later phases append C-* (claim discipline) and D-*
