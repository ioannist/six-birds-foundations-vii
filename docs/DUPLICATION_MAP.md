# Duplication map — Foundations VII results against the earlier corpus

Method. For each of the 36 entries, the six apparatus definitions, the 11 no-go fronts and the 27
countermodels, the candidate's recorded source trace (`science/registry/final_candidate_closure.jsonl`,
field `source_trace`: bridged corpus claims `Pxxx-Cnnnn`, related law IDs), the 30 exact inherited
Lean declarations (`final_adapters.jsonl`, FT01–FT20) and the terminology memo `paper_prep/PP-03`
were read against the vendored corpus (`source/papers/`, 78 papers; catalog IDs from
`config/paper_catalog.csv`). Theorem numbers of the earlier papers were recovered by compiling scratch
copies outside the repository. The canonical home of each recovered result is the **earliest** corpus
statement found; where an external result underlies it, that is listed under `docs/EXTERNAL_ANCHORS.md`.
The map records the label(s) assigned, the canonical source(s), and the one-sentence relation printed
in the paper. Every corpus paper cited is vendored in the repository; no "canonical source not in repo"
flag was needed.

Label vocabulary: RECOVERED STANDARD · RECOVERED SBT · ELEMENTARY · SHARPENED · NEW.
