# Working synthesis of Six Birds Theory

This is a planning-level synthesis, not a replacement for the papers.

## Core package

The canonical starting point is a theory package of the form

`T = (Z, f, Σ_f, E, A)`

with a carrier, an observational/definability lens, the induced definability algebra, a completion or packaging operation, and an audit. The exact signatures vary by paper and must be versioned rather than flattened.

## Three separate certificates

1. **Objecthood/stability:** the candidate survives the declared completion or packaging operation; fixed-point/idempotence ideas are central.
2. **Novelty/strictness:** the promoted interface distinguishes or supports something not factorizable through the lower interface. Iterating a fixed idempotent closure does not by itself create open-ended novelty.
3. **Directionality:** an audit such as path-reversal asymmetry or non-exact affinity must survive the protocol and data-processing constraints. Holonomy or route mismatch alone is not an arrow certificate.

## Corrected six-role discipline

Foundations II and III must control later readings of P1–P6:

- P1: update-versus-quotient compatibility / descent obstruction.
- P2: representability, gating, feasibility and constraint.
- P3: route/coherence mismatch or holonomy; diagnostic, not directionality by itself.
- P4: stage, order and refinement structure.
- P5: quotient packaging and object formation.
- P6: audit, accounting and provenance; the affinity-carrying `P6_drive` face is reserved for arrow content.

The exact-six result is scoped to typed role channels in the declared finite audited domain. Channels may be inactive, decompositions need not be unique, and the result is not a claim that every natural-language phenomenon visibly uses all six.

## Architectural progression

- **Foundations I:** emergence calculus and the basic package/certificates.
- **Foundations II:** admissibility meta-theory, corrections and exact-six scope.
- **Foundations III:** finite audited interaction calculus (`BirdInt_fin^aud`) with typed cells, pair observables, promotions, dependencies, claims, gates and nonclaims.
- **Foundations IV:** static layer-agnostic F-series laws.
- **Foundations V:** endogenous/carried closure apparatus for living, cognitive and social systems.
- **Foundations VI:** run-level G-series laws for liveness, moving obstructions, amortized currencies, adversaries, transport and saturation.
- **No-Go:** exact impossibility boundaries for arrow, protocol traps, force/affinity, closure deficit, contraction and bounded-interface ladders.

## Foundations VII pressure point

The wish lists agree that the missing object is not “another closure law” in isolation. It is an audited account of how partially accessible theory domains can—or cannot—make contact, admit evidence, enable one another, form a genuine joint object, preserve child records, and acquire directionality without smuggling information or mistaking order effects for an arrow.

## Non-collapse rules that the reading program must enforce

- interaction is not composition;
- a common source is not common access;
- a common refinement is not automatically a strict join;
- a directed cell is not automatically a real pair observable;
- strict promotion is not closure or drive;
- P3 holonomy is not P6-drive directionality;
- structural downward influence is not automatically a top-down causal channel;
- enablement is not descent;
- soundness is not reachability;
- reachability is not occurrence;
- a negative census is not a universal no-go without quantified hypotheses.
